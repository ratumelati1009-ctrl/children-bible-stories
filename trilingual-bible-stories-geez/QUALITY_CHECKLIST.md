# ✅ Pre-Publishing Quality Checklist

Do these before you list the ebook for sale. This is what protects your reviews and refund rate — the single biggest driver of long-term sales.

## 1. Language accuracy (do this first — non-negotiable)
- [ ] **Native Tigrinya editor** reads all 10 stories aloud and corrects spelling, grammar, and word choice.
- [ ] **Native Amharic editor** does the same.
- [ ] **Ge'ez / liturgical reviewer** (ideally an Orthodox deacon, priest, or seminary student) confirms each Ge'ez verse is accurate, correctly spelled, and appropriately reverent. The verses in this draft are approximate — treat them as placeholders until confirmed.
- [ ] Confirm scripture references match the wording used (some verses are paraphrased "cf." — either confirm or adjust the citation).
- [ ] Check that the Fidel renders correctly on Kindle, phone, and PDF (embed an Ethiopic font such as *Noto Serif Ethiopic* or *Abyssinica SIL*).

## 2. Theological / doctrinal review
- [ ] A pastor or church leader reviews the stories for doctrinal soundness for your target audience (Orthodox Tewahedo, Protestant, Catholic — pick your lane and be consistent).
- [ ] Confirm nothing is offensive or misrepresented across denominations you want to sell to.

## 3. Kid-appropriateness (ages 5–13)
- [ ] Read aloud to a few children in your target age range; note where they lose interest.
- [ ] Confirm difficult topics (the cross, the flood) are handled gently.

## 4. Design & production
- [ ] Add **illustrations** — this is critical for a children's book and for sales. Budget for an illustrator or use consistent AI-generated art you have rights to sell.
- [ ] Professional cover design (the #1 factor in click-through on any store).
- [ ] Export tested formats: **EPUB** (reflowable, for Kindle/Kobo/Apple Books) and **PDF** (fixed layout, for print-on-demand and direct download).
- [ ] Embed fonts in the PDF so Fidel always displays.
- [ ] Proofread page numbers, table of contents links, and copyright page.

## 5. Legal & rights
- [ ] Confirm you own or have licensed all artwork and fonts for commercial use.
- [ ] Scripture translations: modern published translations are copyrighted. Use public-domain sources or your own translation, or license the text.
- [ ] Add a copyright page and ISBN (free/low-cost depending on country; required by some stores).

## 6. Store setup
- [ ] Title + subtitle with searchable keywords (see marketing plan).
- [ ] Strong description with the trilingual + Ge'ez angle front and center.
- [ ] 3–5 preview/sample pages showing the four-language layout.
- [ ] Correct categories: Children's Religious, Bilingual, Ethiopian/Eritrean.
