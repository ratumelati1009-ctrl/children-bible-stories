Deborah the Judge

ደቦራ ፈራዲት

Deborah was a wise woman who served as a judge and leader of Israel. She sat under a palm tree and people came to her for guidance. God told her that Israel would defeat the army of Sisera. She called Barak to lead the army and promised that God would give them victory. Israel won the battle because Deborah trusted God.

ደቦራ ኣብ እስራኤል ከም ፈራዲትን መራሒትን ዘገልገለት ጥበበኛ ሰበይቲ ነበረት። ኣምላኽ እስራኤል ንሰራዊት ሲሰራ ከም ዝስዕሩ ነገራ። ንባራቕ ሰራዊት ክመርሕ ጸውዐት ኣምላኽ ዓወት ከም ዝህቦም ድማ ተስፋ ሃበት። እስራኤል ውግእ ሰዓሩ።

Gideon's Calling

ጽውዓ ጊድኦን

Israel was being attacked by the Midianites. God sent an angel to a man named Gideon who was hiding while threshing wheat. The angel called him a mighty warrior. Gideon was surprised because he felt weak and unimportant. God promised to be with him and give him strength to save Israel from their enemies.

እስራኤል ብሚድያናውያን ትጥቃዕ ነበረት። ኣምላኽ ናብ ጊድኦን ዝብሃል ሰብ መልኣኽ ሰደደ። መልኣኽ ሓያል ተዋጋኢ ኢሉ ጸውዖ። ጊድኦን ድኹምን ኣገዳስነት ዘይብሉን ኮይኑ ስለ ዝተሰምዖ ተገረመ። ኣምላኽ ምስኡ ከም ዝህሉ ተስፋ ሃቦ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

God sees our potential, not our weakness.

ኣምላኽ ድኽመትና ዘይኮነ ዓቕምና ይርኢ።

Gideon's Small Army

ንእሽቶ ሰራዊት ጊድኦን

Gideon gathered a large army, but God said there were too many soldiers. God reduced the army from 32,000 to only 300 men. Each soldier carried a trumpet and a jar with a torch inside. At night they surrounded the enemy camp, blew their trumpets and smashed their jars. The confused enemy fled in terror.

ጊድኦን ዓቢ ሰራዊት ኣኪቡ ኣምላኽ ግና ብዙሕ ወተሃደራት ኣለዉ በለ። ኣምላኽ ሰራዊት ካብ 32,000 ናብ 300 ጥራይ ኣጉደሎ። ነፍሲ ወከፍ ወተሃደር መለኸትን ጥሩምባ ዘለዎ ዕትሮን ሓዘ። ብለይቲ ንጸላኢ ከበብዎ መለኸት ነፊሖም ዕትሮ ሰባሪ ጸላኢ ብራዕዲ ሃደመ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

God can win with few when they trust Him.

ኣምላኽ ብውሑዳት ክስዕር ይኽእል ምስ ዝኣምንዎ።

Samson Is Born

ሳምሶን ይውለድ

An angel appeared to a woman who could not have children and promised her a son. The boy would be special and set apart for God from birth. His hair was never to be cut as a sign of his dedication to God. The boy was named Samson and God blessed him with extraordinary strength as he grew up.

መልኣኽ ናብ ቆልዓ ክትወልድ ዘይትኽእል ሰበይቲ ተራእዩ ወዲ ተስፋ ሃባ። እቲ ቆልዓ ፍሉይ ካብ ልደቱ ንኣምላኽ ዝተፈልየ ክኸውን ነበሮ። ጸጉሩ ከቶ ክቑረጽ ኣይነበሮን። ቆልዓ ሳምሶን ተባህለ ኣምላኽ ድማ ብፍሉይ ሓይሊ ባረኾ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

God gives special gifts for special purposes.

ኣምላኽ ንፍሉይ ዕላማ ፍሉይ ውህብቶ ይህብ።

Samson's Great Strength

ዓቢ ሓይሊ ሳምሶን

Samson grew to be incredibly strong. He killed a lion with his bare hands. He fought the Philistines who oppressed Israel many times. Once he defeated a thousand soldiers with just a donkey's jawbone. His strength came from God's Spirit. As long as Samson kept his promise to God, his strength remained.

ሳምሶን ኣዝዩ ሓያል ኮይኑ ዓበየ። ኣንበሳ ብጥራይ ኢዱ ቀተለ። ንፍልስጥኤማውያን ብዙሕ ግዜ ተዋግኦም። ሓንሳብ ብመንጋጋ ኣድጊ ጥራይ ሽሕ ወተሃደራት ስዒሩ። ሓይሉ ካብ መንፈስ ኣምላኽ ይመጽእ ነበረ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

True strength comes from God.

ሓቀኛ ሓይሊ ካብ ኣምላኽ ይመጽእ።

Samson and Delilah

ሳምሶንን ደሊላን

Samson fell in love with a woman named Delilah. The Philistine rulers paid her to discover the secret of his strength. She asked him many times until he finally told her: his strength was in his uncut hair. While he slept, she had his hair cut. Samson's strength left him and the Philistines captured and blinded him.

ሳምሶን ደሊላ ዝብሃል ሰበይቲ ፈተወ። መራሕቲ ፍልስጥኤም ምስጢር ሓይሉ ክትፈልጥ ከፈልዋ። ብዙሕ ግዜ ሓተተቶ ኣብ መወዳእታ ነገራ ሓይሉ ኣብ ዘይተቖረጸ ጸጉሩ ምዃኑ። እንዳ ደቀሰ ጸጉሩ ኣቖረጸቶ። ሓይሉ ገደፎ ፍልስጥኤማውያን ሒዞም ዓወርዎ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

Guard the gifts God gives you.

Samson's Final Victory

ናይ መወዳእታ ዓወት ሳምሶን

The Philistines put Samson in chains and made him entertain them in their temple. But his hair began to grow back. Samson prayed one last time for strength. He pushed against the two main pillars of the temple with all his might. The temple collapsed, defeating more enemies in his death than in his whole life.

ፍልስጥኤማውያን ንሳምሶን ኣሲሮም ኣብ ቤት መቕደሶም ከዘናግዖም ገበሩ። ጸጉሩ ግና ክበቅል ጀመረ። ሳምሶን ንመወዳእታ ግዜ ሓይሊ ጸለየ። ነተን ክልተ ዋና ኣዕኑድ ቤት መቕደስ ደፍኤን ቤት መቕደስ ፈረሰት።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

It is never too late to turn back to God.

ናብ ኣምላኽ ንምምላስ ከቶ ኣይደንጕን።

Ruth and Naomi

ሩትን ንኣሚን

Naomi lost her husband and two sons in a foreign land. She decided to return home to Bethlehem. Her daughter-in-law Ruth refused to leave her. Ruth said, 'Where you go, I will go. Your people will be my people, and your God my God.' Ruth's loyalty to Naomi showed true love and faithfulness.

ንኣሚ ኣብ ጓና ምድሪ ሰብኣያን ክልተ ደቃን ሞቱ። ናብ ቤት ልሔም ክትምለስ ወሰነት። ሰበይቲ ወዳ ሩት ክትገድፋ ኣበየት። ሩት ናብ ዝኸድኪ ክኸይድ ህዝብኺ ህዝበይ ኣምላኽኺ ኣምላኸይ በለት።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

True love means loyalty and commitment.

ሓቀኛ ፍቕሪ ማለት ተወፋይነትን ቅንዕናን ማለት እዩ።

Ruth Finds Kindness

ሩት ለውሃት ትረክብ

In Bethlehem, Ruth worked hard gathering leftover grain in the fields to feed herself and Naomi. She happened to work in the field of a kind man named Boaz. Boaz noticed Ruth's hard work and loyalty to Naomi. He told his workers to leave extra grain for her and to protect her. God was providing for Ruth through Boaz's kindness.

ኣብ ቤት ልሔም ሩት ንርእሳን ንንኣሚን ንምምጋብ ኣብ ሜዳ ዝተረፈ ስርናይ ብጻዕሪ ትእክብ ነበረት። ኣብ ሜዳ ቦኣዝ ዝብሃል ለዋህ ሰብ ሰርሐት። ቦኣዝ ጻዕሪ ሩትን ንንኣሚ ተወፋይነታን ኣስተብሃለ። ተወሳኺ ስርናይ ክገድፉላ ነገሮም።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

Hard work and kindness are noticed by God.

ጻዕርን ለውሃትን ብኣምላኽ ይርአ።

Hannah's Prayer

ጸሎት ሃና

Hannah was very sad because she could not have children. She went to God's temple and prayed with all her heart, crying and making a promise. She told God that if He gave her a son, she would give him back to serve God. God heard Hannah's prayer and she had a baby boy named Samuel, which means 'God heard.'

ሃና ቆልዓ ክትወልድ ስለ ዘይከኣለት ኣዝያ ሓዚና ነበረት። ናብ ቤት መቕደስ ኣምላኽ ከይዳ ብምሉእ ልባ ጸለየት እናበኸየት ተስፋ ሃበት። ኣምላኽ ጸሎት ሃና ሰሚዑ ሳሙኤል ዝስሙ ወዲ ሃባ ትርጉሙ ኣምላኽ ሰሚዑ ማለት እዩ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

God hears the prayers of a sincere heart.

Samuel's Calling

ጽውዓ ሳሙኤል

Young Samuel served in the temple with the priest Eli. One night, God called Samuel's name. Samuel thought it was Eli calling. After the third time, Eli realized God was speaking to the boy. He told Samuel to say, 'Speak Lord, your servant is listening.' Samuel obeyed and became a great prophet of God.

ንእሽቶ ሳሙኤል ኣብ ቤት መቕደስ ምስ ካህን ኤሊ የገልግል ነበረ። ሓደ ለይቲ ኣምላኽ ስም ሳሙኤል ጸውዖ። ሳሙኤል ኤሊ ዝጽውዖ ዘሎ መሰሎ። ኤሊ ኣምላኽ ንቖልዓ ከም ዝዛረቦ ፈለጠ ተዛረብ ጐይታ ኣገልጋሊኻ ይሰምዕ ኣሎ ክብል ነገሮ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

God speaks to those who are willing to listen.

Saul Becomes King

ሳኦል ንጉስ ይኸውን

The people of Israel wanted a king like other nations. God told Samuel to anoint Saul, a tall, handsome young man from the tribe of Benjamin. Samuel poured oil on Saul's head, making him Israel's first king. At first Saul was humble, but God warned that having a king would bring challenges.

ህዝቢ እስራኤል ከም ካልኦት ህዝብታት ንጉስ ደለዩ። ኣምላኽ ንሳሙኤል ንሳኦል ክቐብኦ ነገሮ። ሳሙኤል ኣብ ርእሲ ሳኦል ዘይቲ ኣፍሲሱ ቀዳማይ ንጉስ እስራኤል ገበሮ። መጀመርታ ሳኦል ትሑት ነበረ ኣምላኽ ግና ንጉስ ምህላው ብድሆታት ከም ዝስዕብ ኣጠንቀቐ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

Leadership requires humility before God.

መራሕነት ኣብ ቅድሚ ኣምላኽ ትሕትና ይሓትት።

David the Shepherd Boy

ዳዊት ጓሳ ቆልዓ

God rejected Saul and sent Samuel to anoint a new king from Jesse's family. Jesse presented his older, stronger sons, but God chose the youngest, David, a shepherd boy. God told Samuel that He does not look at outward appearance but at the heart. Samuel anointed David, and God's Spirit came upon him.

ኣምላኽ ንሳኦል ነጺግዎ ሳሙኤል ካብ ስድራ እሴይ ሓድሽ ንጉስ ክቐብእ ሰደዶ። እሴይ ዓበይቲ ደቁ ኣቕረበ ኣምላኽ ግና ንዝነኣሰ ጓሳ ቆልዓ ዳዊት መረጸ። ኣምላኽ ደጋዊ ትርኢት ዘይኮነ ልቢ ከም ዝርኢ ነገረ። ሳሙኤል ንዳዊት ቀብኦ።

Lesson / ትምህርቲ

God looks at the heart, not outward appearance.

David and Goliath

ዳዊትን ጎልያድን

A giant warrior named Goliath challenged the Israelites every day for forty days. All the soldiers were terrified. Young David came to the battlefield and was not afraid. He said God would help him fight the giant. With just a sling and a stone, David struck Goliath on the forehead. The giant fell and Israel won the battle.

Malachi's Promise

ተስፋ ሚልክያስ

Malachi was the last prophet before four hundred years of silence. He promised that God would send a messenger to prepare the way for the Lord. He spoke of a Sun of Righteousness rising with healing in His wings. These promises pointed to John the Baptist and Jesus. God's people waited with hope for these promises to come true.

The Faith of Abraham's Servant

Abraham sent his servant to find a wife for Isaac. The servant prayed and asked God for a specific sign: the woman who offered water to him and his camels would be the right one.
