# ወርቂ ሓረግ (Golden Thread) — A Long-Form Story in Tigrinya

An original, 30+ page story written in **Tigrinya** (ትግርኛ), following the life
journey of **ሳባ (Saba)** — a curious girl from a small highland village who,
through education, perseverance, and her family's sacrifice, becomes a doctor
and returns to transform her community.

## 🖨️ The PDF

A ready-made **[`ወርቂ-ሓረግ.pdf`](./ወርቂ-ሓረግ.pdf)** (32 pages, A4) is included. The Ge'ez
font is **embedded** in the file, so the Tigrinya renders correctly on any device
or viewer — no font installation needed. Just open or download it.

It was generated by embedding *Noto Sans Ethiopic* into a self-contained HTML
(`build_pdf_html.py`) and printing it to PDF with headless Chromium. To rebuild:

```bash
python3 build_pdf_html.py   # produces _print.html with fonts embedded
chrome --headless --no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf=ወርቂ-ሓረግ.pdf _print.html
```

Prefer to make your own PDF? Open **`ወርቂ-ሓረግ.html`** in a browser and choose
**Print → Save as PDF**.

## ✍️ Themes

Education as empowerment, perseverance through hardship, family sacrifice, and
the idea that true wealth lies in lifting others — the "golden thread"
(ወርቂ ሓረግ) passed from one generation to the next.
