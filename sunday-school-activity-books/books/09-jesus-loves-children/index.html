Lesson Aim & Teacher Preparation

Lesson aim: Children will learn that Jesus loves and welcomes every child, including them.

Learning objectives

  • Retell how Jesus welcomed the children.
  • Explain that Jesus loves each child.
  • Recite the memory verse (Mark 10:14).
  • Say one way they know Jesus loves them.

You will need

  • Bibles or printed story pages
  • Crayons, paper, scissors, glue
  • A mirror or hand-mirror craft materials
Teacher tip: Affirm each child personally — this lesson is about belonging and being loved.

The Bible Story — Part 1

Crowds Follow Jesus

Everywhere Jesus went, crowds followed to hear him teach and to be healed.

Parents wanted Jesus to bless their children, so they brought them close to him.

The Bible Story — Part 2

The Disciples Say No

The disciples thought Jesus was too busy and important for little children.

They tried to send the children away. 'Don't bother Jesus,' they said.

The Bible Story — Part 3

Let the Children Come

But Jesus was not too busy. He said, 'Let the little children come to me. Do not stop them!'

Jesus welcomed every child with open arms. Children were important and precious to him.

The Bible Story — Part 4

Jesus Blesses the Children

Jesus took the children in his arms, placed his hands on them, and blessed them.

Jesus said God's kingdom belongs to those who come to him like a child. Jesus loves you very much!

Talk About It

Discussion questions for the group

Sit in a circle. Let everyone share. There are no wrong answers when we talk about God's Word!

1. Why did the disciples try to send the children away?

2. What did Jesus say instead?

3. How do you think the children felt when Jesus welcomed them?

4. How do we know Jesus loves us?

5. How can we welcome others the way Jesus welcomed the children?

Act It Out!

Retell the story with a simple drama

Teacher tip: Give children simple roles and props. Acting the story helps them remember it.

Roles & steps

  1. Choose Jesus, parents, children and disciples.
  2. Parents bring children forward; disciples try to stop them.
  3. Jesus opens his arms and says, 'Let the children come to me!'
  4. Jesus 'blesses' each child with a gentle hand on the head.

Game: Come to Jesus Circle

A fun game for the whole group

You need: just the group

How to play

  1. One child stands as a friendly 'welcomer' in the middle.
  2. They call, 'Come to me!' and wave friends in.
  3. Each child joins the circle with a happy wave.
  4. Talk about how Jesus welcomes everyone.
Teacher tip: Make sure every child is chosen and welcomed so no one feels left out.

Craft: 'Jesus Loves Me' Mirror

See how loved you are!

You need: paper or card, foil or a small mirror, crayons, glue, scissors
  1. Cut a circle or heart frame from card.
  2. Glue foil (or a small mirror) in the middle.
  3. Write 'Jesus loves me!' around the frame.
  4. Look in it and remember: Jesus loves YOU!
