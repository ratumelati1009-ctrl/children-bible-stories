Lesson Aim & Teacher Preparation

Lesson aim: Children will learn to show kindness and mercy, loving their neighbour as themselves.

Learning objectives

  • Retell the story of the Good Samaritan.
  • Explain who our 'neighbour' is.
  • Recite the memory verse (Luke 10:27).
  • Name one kind act they can do this week.

You will need

  • Bibles or printed story pages
  • Crayons, coloured paper, scissors, glue
  • Optional: bandages as props
Teacher tip: Have children brainstorm kind acts before the craft to inspire their kindness chain.

Opening Prayer & Gathering

Welcome circle

Gather in a circle. Ask children about a time someone was kind to them.

Dear God, thank you for loving us. Help us learn to be kind and to love others, like the Good Samaritan. Amen.

Icebreaker question

What is a kind thing someone did for you this week?

The Bible Story — Part 1

A Question for Jesus

One day a man asked Jesus, 'Who is my neighbour?' Jesus answered by telling a wonderful story.

'A man was travelling on a lonely road,' Jesus began, 'when robbers attacked him and left him hurt.'

The Bible Story — Part 2

Two Men Pass By

A priest came down the road. He saw the hurt man, but he crossed to the other side and walked past.

Then a Levite came. He also saw the man, but he too passed by without helping.

The Bible Story — Part 3

The Kind Samaritan

Next, a Samaritan came along. He felt sorry for the hurt man and stopped to help.

He cleaned and bandaged the wounds, lifted the man onto his own donkey, and took him to an inn to rest.

The Bible Story — Part 4

Go and Do the Same

The Samaritan even paid the innkeeper to care for the man. He was a true neighbour.

Jesus asked, 'Which one was a good neighbour?' Then he said, 'Go and do the same.' We are neighbours to anyone who needs kindness.

Memory Verse

“Love your neighbour as you love yourself.”Luke 10:27 (ICB)
Read the verse together three times. Then trace it and say it from memory!

Love your neighbour as you love yourself.

Teacher tip: Point to a neighbour, then to yourself, as you say the verse to make it stick.

Talk About It

Discussion questions for the group

Sit in a circle. Let everyone share. There are no wrong answers when we talk about God's Word!

1. Why do you think the priest and Levite did not stop?

2. What made the Samaritan a good neighbour?

3. Who are the 'neighbours' around you?

4. Why can helping others cost us something?

5. How can you show kindness this week?

Living It Out

Being a kind neighbour like the Samaritan

Read each situation. Circle the choice that pleases God. Talk about it together.

1. You see someone fall and drop their things.

a) Stop and help them up.   b) Walk past.

2. A child no one likes needs a game partner.

a) Leave them out.   b) Be their kind partner.

3. Someone is sad and sitting alone.

a) Sit with them and be kind.   b) Ignore them.

Act It Out!

Retell the story with a simple drama

Teacher tip: Give children simple roles and props. Acting the story helps them remember it.

Roles & steps

  1. Choose the traveller, robbers (gentle miming!), a priest, a Levite and the Samaritan.
  2. The traveller is 'hurt'; the priest and Levite pass by.
  3. The Samaritan stops, 'bandages' the man and helps him onto a 'donkey'.
  4. End by asking, 'Who was the good neighbour?'

Game: Kindness Pass-It-On

A fun game for the whole group

You need: a soft ball or beanbag

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle and pass the ball.
  2. Whoever holds it names one kind act.
  3. No repeating ideas!
  4. See how many kind acts the group can name together.
Teacher tip: Write the ideas on the board and encourage children to try one this week.

Craft: Kindness Heart Chain

Make a chain of good deeds!

You need: coloured paper, scissors, glue, a pen
  1. Cut several paper hearts.
  2. On each heart, write one kind thing you can do.
  3. Glue the hearts into a chain.
  4. Do one kind act each day. Love your neighbour!
