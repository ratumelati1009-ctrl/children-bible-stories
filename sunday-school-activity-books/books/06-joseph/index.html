Lesson Aim & Teacher Preparation

Lesson aim: Children will learn to forgive others and trust that God works everything for good.

Learning objectives

  • Retell Joseph's story.
  • Explain what forgiveness means.
  • Recite the memory verse (Genesis 50:20).
  • Name one person they can forgive.

You will need

  • Bibles or printed story pages
  • Crayons, coloured paper, scissors, glue
  • Optional: a plain coat or cloth
Teacher tip: Talk gently about forgiveness; some children may find this topic personal.

Opening Prayer & Gathering

Welcome circle

Gather in a circle. Ask children how it feels to be forgiven after doing something wrong.

Icebreaker question

The Bible Story — Part 1

The Colourful Coat

Joseph was one of twelve brothers. His father loved him and gave him a beautiful coat of many colours.

Joseph also had special dreams from God. But his brothers became very jealous of him.

The Bible Story — Part 2

Sold by His Brothers

The jealous brothers sold Joseph to traders going to Egypt and told their father he was gone.

In Egypt, Joseph worked hard and stayed honest. Even when treated unfairly, he trusted God.

The Bible Story — Part 3

From Prison to Palace

God helped Joseph understand dreams. He warned Pharaoh of coming hungry years and how to prepare.

Pharaoh put Joseph in charge of all Egypt to store food. Joseph saved many people from starving.

The Bible Story — Part 4

Joseph Forgives

When his brothers came to buy food, Joseph knew them. Instead of anger, he forgave them and wept for joy.

'You meant to harm me, but God used it for good,' Joseph said. The whole family was joyfully together again.

Memory Verse

“You planned to harm me, but God planned it for good.”Genesis 50:20 (ICB)
Read the verse together three times. Then trace it and say it from memory!

You planned to harm me, but God planned it for good.

Teacher tip: Use a frown-to-smile motion to show how God turns bad things into good.

Talk About It

Discussion questions for the group

Sit in a circle. Let everyone share. There are no wrong answers when we talk about God's Word!

1. Why were Joseph's brothers jealous?

2. How did Joseph stay faithful in hard times?

3. Why is forgiving others hard, and why is it good?

4. How can God bring good from hard times?

5. Who is someone you need to forgive?

Act It Out!

Retell the story with a simple drama

Teacher tip: Give children simple roles and props. Acting the story helps them remember it.

Roles & steps

  1. Choose Joseph, his father, the brothers and Pharaoh.
  2. The brothers 'sell' Joseph; Joseph works and rises in Egypt.
  3. The brothers come for food and don't recognise Joseph.
  4. Joseph reveals himself and forgives them — a joyful family hug!

Game: Coat of Colours Relay

A fun game for the whole group

You need: coloured paper strips, two baskets

How to play

  1. Two teams line up with a basket of colour strips.
  2. Each child adds one strip to a 'coat' on the wall.
  3. First team to build a full colourful coat wins.
  4. Talk about how God had a colourful plan for Joseph.
Teacher tip: Keep strips large and taped lightly so it's quick and mess-free.

Craft: Coat of Many Colours

Make a colourful coat!

You need: paper, crayons or coloured paper scraps, scissors, glue
  1. Cut out a coat shape from paper.
  2. Glue on colour strips or colour in bright stripes.
  3. Add buttons and patterns.
  4. Remember: God had a good plan for Joseph, and for you too!
