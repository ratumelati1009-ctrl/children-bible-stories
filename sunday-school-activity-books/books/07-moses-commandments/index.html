Lesson Aim & Teacher Preparation

Lesson aim: Children will learn that God gave good rules to help us love him and love others.

Learning objectives

  • Retell how Moses received the commandments.
  • Explain why God's rules are good.
  • Recite the memory verse (Exodus 20:6).
  • Name a rule that shows love to God or others.

You will need

  • Bibles or printed story pages
  • Crayons, card, scissors, glue
  • Grey/brown paper for stone tablets
Teacher tip: Explain the commandments simply: love God, and love people.

Opening Prayer & Gathering

Welcome circle

Gather in a circle. Ask why we have rules at home and school, and how they help us.

Dear God, thank you for giving us good rules to keep us safe and loving. Help us to obey them. Amen.

Icebreaker question

What is one good rule at your home? Why does it help?

The Bible Story — Part 1

God's People at the Mountain

After God rescued his people from Egypt, Moses led them to a great mountain called Sinai.

God wanted to give his people good rules to help them love him and live well together.

The Bible Story — Part 2

Moses Meets God

Moses climbed up the mountain to meet with God. There was thunder, smoke and a loud trumpet sound.

God spoke to Moses and gave him the rules to share with all the people.

The Bible Story — Part 3

The Ten Commandments

God gave ten commandments written on two stone tablets. The first ones teach us to love God: worship only him, and keep his day holy.

The others teach us to love people: honour your parents, do not steal, do not lie, and be kind and truthful.

The Bible Story — Part 4

Rules That Show Love

God's rules were not to make life hard — they were a gift to help his people live in love and peace.

When we obey God's good rules, we show that we love him and we care for the people around us.

Memory Verse

“I show my love to those who love me and obey my commands.”Exodus 20:6 (ICB)
Read the verse together three times. Then trace it and say it from memory!

I show my love to those who love me and obey my commands.

Teacher tip: Split the verse: 'love me' (hands on heart) and 'obey' (hands together) to help memory.

Talk About It

Discussion questions for the group

Sit in a circle. Let everyone share. There are no wrong answers when we talk about God's Word!

1. Why did God give his people rules?

2. Which commandments teach us to love God?

3. Which teach us to love people?

4. How do good rules keep us safe?

5. What is one rule you can obey to show love this week?

Act It Out!

Retell the story with a simple drama

Teacher tip: Give children simple roles and props. Acting the story helps them remember it.

Roles & steps

  1. Choose Moses and children to be the people at the mountain.
  2. Make 'thunder' by stamping and 'trumpet' sounds with voices.
  3. Moses 'climbs' up and returns holding two paper tablets.
  4. The people cheer for God's good rules and promise to obey.

Game: Simon Says (God's Rules)

A fun game for the whole group

You need: just the group

How to play

  1. Play 'Simon Says' but say 'God says'.
  2. Give kind, loving actions: 'God says help a friend'.
  3. If you act without 'God says', you sit for a round.
  4. Talk about obeying God's good words.
Teacher tip: Use the game to reinforce that obeying is about love, not just following orders.

Craft: Stone Tablet Cards
