Lesson Aim & Teacher Preparation

Lesson aim: Children will learn to obey God and that God lovingly gives second chances.

Learning objectives

  • Retell Jonah's adventure.
  • Explain why obeying God is best.
  • Recite the memory verse (Jonah 2:2).
  • Name someone they can forgive or a fresh start they can make.

You will need

  • Bibles or printed story pages
  • Crayons, scissors, glue
  • Paper plate for the fish craft
  • Optional: a blue sheet for the 'sea'
Teacher tip: Practise the 'storm' sounds (clapping, stamping) so children can join in the story.

Opening Prayer & Gathering

Welcome circle

Gather in a circle. Ask about a time someone gave them another chance and how it felt.

Dear God, thank you that you always hear us and give us second chances. Help us to obey you. Amen.

Icebreaker question

Have you ever tried to run away from doing something? What happened?

The Bible Story — Part 1

Jonah Runs Away

God told Jonah, 'Go to the big city of Nineveh and tell the people to stop doing wrong.' But Jonah did not want to go.

Instead, Jonah got on a ship going the opposite way, trying to run away from God.

The Bible Story — Part 2

The Big Storm

God sent a huge storm. The wind roared and the waves crashed. The sailors were terrified.

Jonah knew it was because he had run away. He said, 'Throw me into the sea, and it will grow calm.'

The Bible Story — Part 3

Inside the Big Fish

When they threw Jonah in, the storm stopped. Then God sent a great big fish that swallowed Jonah whole.

Jonah was safe inside for three days. There, he prayed and told God he was sorry.

The Bible Story — Part 4

A Second Chance

God heard Jonah and had the fish bring him safely to dry land. 'Go to Nineveh,' God said again.

This time Jonah obeyed. The people of Nineveh listened and turned to God, who kindly forgave them.

Act It Out!

Retell the story with a simple drama

Teacher tip: Give children simple roles and props. Acting the story helps them remember it.

Roles & steps

  1. Choose Jonah, sailors, and children to be the 'sea' and the 'big fish'.
  2. Sailors 'rock' the boat during the storm.
  3. The 'fish' (children in a circle) surrounds Jonah, who prays.
  4. Jonah is set free and hurries off to Nineveh — the people cheer and turn to God!

Game: Storm & Calm

A fun game for the whole group

You need: space to move

How to play

  1. When the leader says 'Storm!', children sway and make wind sounds.
  2. When the leader says 'Calm!', everyone freezes still and quiet.
  3. Anyone who moves during 'Calm' sits for a round.
  4. Talk about how God calmed the real storm.
Teacher tip: Alternate quickly between storm and calm to keep everyone laughing and listening.

Craft: Paper-Plate Big Fish

Make Jonah's big fish!

You need: a paper plate, crayons, scissors, glue

  2. Colour the fish and add scales.
  3. Add a big eye and a friendly smile.
  4. Remember: God can rescue us, just like he rescued Jonah!
