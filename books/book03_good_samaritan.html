ENGLISH: Dear God, thank You for teaching us what it means to truly love our neighbor. Help us to have eyes that see people in need, hands that are ready to help, and hearts full of Your love. Help us not to walk past people who are hurting but to stop and show kindness. Make us like the Good Samaritan every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.
