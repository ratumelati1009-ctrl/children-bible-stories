Scene 1
[PICTURE: The mighty city of Babylon with towering walls, hanging gardens, and grand palaces. A huge golden statue being constructed in the distance. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In the powerful city of Babylon, King Nebuchadnezzar ruled over everything. He was very proud and wanted everyone to worship him.
ኣብ ሓያል ከተማ ባቢሎን ንጉስ ነቡካድነጻር ኣብ ኩሉ ይገዝእ ነበረ። ኣዝዩ ትዕቢተኛ ነበረ ኩሉ ሰብ ክሰግደሉ ድማ ይደሊ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: Ab hayal ketema Babilon ngus Nebukadnetsar ab kulu ygze' nebere. Azyu t'ibiteNa nebere kulu seb ksegedelu dma ydeli nebere.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: A gigantic golden statue towering over the plain, shining in the sunlight. Workers putting finishing touches. It's enormous - 90 feet tall! East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king built a giant golden statue — 90 feet tall! It stood on a wide plain for everyone to see. He wanted all people to bow down and worship it.
ንጉስ ዓባይ ናይ ወርቂ ምስሊ — 90 እግሪ ቁመት ሰርሐ! ኣብ ገፊሕ ሜዳ ኩሉ ሰብ ክርእያ ቆመት። ኩሉ ሰብ ተንበርኪኹ ክሰግደላ ደለየ።
(Pronunciation: Ngus 'abay nay werqi msli — 90 igri qumet serhe! Ab gefih meda kulu seb kr'eya qomet. Kulu seb tenberkiku ksgedela deleye.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Three young men - Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego - standing together looking brave and determined. They wear Babylonian official clothing but have faithful hearts. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Three young men — Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego — were friends who loved God. They were from Israel but served as officials in Babylon. They would never worship anyone but God!
ሰለስተ ኣባጽሕ — ሻድራቕ ሜሻቕ ኣብድነጎ — ንኣምላኽ ዘፍቅሩ ኣዕሩኽ ነበሩ። ካብ እስራኤል ነበሩ ኣብ ባቢሎን ግና ከም ሰበስልጣን ይግልግሉ ነበሩ። ብዘይ ኣምላኽ ንዝኾነ ክሰግዱ ኣይምተረፈሉን!
(Pronunciation: Seleste abatsh — Shadraq Meshaq Abdnego — n'Amlak zefqiru a'erukh neberu. Kab Israel neberu ab Babilon gna kem sebesselTan yglglu neberu. Bzey Amlak nzkhone ksgdu aymitefrelun!)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: A herald/announcer standing before a massive crowd with the golden statue behind him. Musicians with instruments ready to play. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king's messenger announced: "When the music plays, everyone must bow down to the golden statue! Anyone who refuses will be thrown into a fiery furnace!"
ልኡኽ ንጉስ ኣወጀ፡ "ሙዚቃ ምስ ተሰምዐ ኩሉ ሰብ ነታ ናይ ወርቂ ምስሊ ክሰግድ ኣለዎ! ዝኣቢ ኩሉ ናብ እቶን ሓዊ ክድርበ'ዩ!"
(Pronunciation: L'ukh ngus aweje: "Muziqa ms tesem'e kulu seb neta nay werqi msli ksgd alewo! Z'abi kulu nab iton hawi kdrbe'yu!")
Scene 5
[PICTURE: A massive crowd of people all bowing down flat on the ground before the golden statue. Music playing. But three figures remain standing tall in the crowd. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The music played and EVERYONE bowed down — except Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego! They stood tall and brave. They would only worship the true God!
ሙዚቃ ተሰምዐ ኩሉ ሰብ ተንበርኪኹ ሰገደ — ብዘይ ሻድራቕ ሜሻቕን ኣብድነጎን! ነዊሖምን ጀጋኑን ኮይኖም ደው በሉ። ነቲ ሓቀኛ ኣምላኽ ጥራይ'ዮም ዝሰግዱ!
(Pronunciation: Muziqa tesem'e kulu seb tenberkiku segede — bzey Shadraq Meshaqn Abdnegon! Newihomn jeganun koynom dew belu. Neti haqeNa Amlak Teray'yom zsgdu!)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Angry officials pointing at the three young men and reporting to the king. Jealous expressions on their faces. The king's throne room. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Some jealous officials told the king: "Those three men refuse to bow! They don't obey your command!" The king became furious!
ገለ ቅኑኣት ሰበስልጣን ንንጉስ ነገሩ፡ "እቶም ሰለስተ ሰባት ክሰግዱ ኣቢዮም! ትእዛዝካ ኣይሰምዑን!" ንጉስ ኣዝዩ ተቖጠዐ!
(Pronunciation: Gele qnu'at sebesselTan nngus negeru: "Itom seleste sebat ksgdu abiyom! T'izzazka aysem'un!" Ngus azyu teqoTe'e!)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: King Nebuchadnezzar on his throne, face red with anger, pointing at the three young men who stand calmly before him. Guards on either side. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king brought the three friends before him. "Is it true you won't bow to my statue?" he roared. "I'll give you one more chance! If you refuse, into the fire you go!"
ንጉስ ነቶም ሰለስተ ኣዕሩኽ ኣብ ቅድሚኡ ኣቕረቦም። "ንምስለይ ክትሰግዱ ምእባይኩም ሓቂ ድዩ?" ጨደረ። "ሓንቲ ዕድል ተወሳኺ ክህበኩም! ምስ እትኣብዩ ናብ ሓዊ ክትኣትዉ ኢኹም!"
(Pronunciation: Ngus netom seleste a'erukh ab qdmi'u aqrebom. "Nmsley ktsgdu mi'baykum haqqi dyu?" chedere. "Hanti 'idl tewesaki khbekum! Ms it'abiyu nab hawi ktatwu ikhum!")
Scene 8
[PICTURE: The three friends standing together bravely before the angry king, their faces calm and peaceful. They hold hands showing unity and faith. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The three friends answered bravely: "Our God CAN save us from the fire. But even if He doesn't, we will NEVER bow to your statue. We worship only God!"
ሰለስተ ኣዕሩኽ ብትብዓት መለሱ፡ "ኣምላኽና ካብ ሓዊ ከድሕነና ይኽእል'ዩ። ግና ዘየድሕነና'ውን እንተ ኾነ ንምስልኻ ፈጺምና ኣይንሰግደላን። ንኣምላኽ ጥራይ ኢና ንሰግድ!"
(Pronunciation: Seleste a'erukh bt'b'at melesu: "Amlakna kab hawi kedhnnena ykhel'yu. Gna zeyedhnnena'wn inte khone nmsslkha fetsimna aynsgdelan. N'Amlak Teray ina nsgd!")
Scene 9
[PICTURE: The king in a rage, shouting orders to make the furnace seven times hotter. Workers stoking enormous flames. The furnace glowing orange-red. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king was SO angry that his face changed! "Heat the furnace SEVEN times hotter!" he screamed. The fire blazed so hot that even the soldiers nearby felt the heat!
ንጉስ ሕራኑ ክሳብ ገጹ ዝቕየር ተቖጠዐ! "ነቲ እቶን ሸውዓተ ዕጽፊ ኣውዕልዎ!" ጨደረ። ሓዊ ኣዝዩ ሙቐት ክሳብ ኣብ ቀረባ ዝነበሩ ወተሃደራት'ውን ሙቐት ዝስመዖም!
(Pronunciation: Ngus hranu ksab getsu zqeyer teqoTe'e! "Neti iton shew'ate 'itsfi awl'wo!" chedere. Hawi azyu muqet ksab ab qereba zneberu wetehadeerat'wn muqet zsme'om!)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Strong soldiers tying up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego with ropes. The three friends look calm and peaceful despite the danger, trusting God completely. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Soldiers tied up the three friends tightly. But Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego were not afraid! They knew God was with them, no matter what happened.
ወተሃደራት ነቶም ሰለስተ ኣዕሩኽ ብጽኑዕ ኣሰሩዎም። ግና ሻድራቕ ሜሻቕ ኣብድነጎ ኣይፈርሑን! እንታይ'ውን ይኹን ኣምላኽ ምሳታቶም ምዃኑ ይፈልጡ ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: Wetehadeerat netom seleste a'erukh bts'nu' aseruwom. Gna Shadraq Meshaq Abdnego ayferHun! Intay'wn ykhun Amlak msatatom mkwanu yfelTu neberu.)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: The three friends being thrown into the enormous blazing furnace. Flames leaping high. The soldiers who threw them in falling back from the intense heat. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The soldiers threw them into the fiery furnace! The fire was so hot that the soldiers who threw them in were killed by the flames! But God had a plan.
ወተሃደራት ናብ እቶን ሓዊ ደርበዩዎም! ሓዊ ኣዝዩ ምውዕ ነበረ ነቶም ዝደርበዩዎም ወተሃደራት ብሃልሃልታ ሞቱ! ግና ኣምላኽ ውጥን ነበሮ።
(Pronunciation: Wetehadeerat nab iton hawi derbeyuwom! Hawi azyu mw' nebere netom zderbeyuwom wetehadeerat bhalhalta motu! Gna Amlak wuTn nebero.)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: King Nebuchadnezzar jumping up from his throne in shock, leaning forward to look into the furnace. His advisors behind him looking equally stunned. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king looked into the furnace and jumped up in shock! "Didn't we throw THREE men in?" he asked his advisors. "Yes!" they answered. "But I see FOUR! And the fourth looks like a son of the gods!"
ንጉስ ናብ እቶን ጠሚቱ ብምግራም ተንሰአ! "ሰለስተ ሰባት ኣይኮኑን ድዮም ዝደርበናዮም?" ንኣማኸርቱ ሓተተ። "እወ!" መለሱ። "ኣርባዕተ ግና ይረኣዩኒ! እቲ ራብዓይ ከም ወዲ ኣማልኽቲ ይመስል!"
(Pronunciation: Ngus nab iton Temitu bmgraam tense'a! "Seleste sebat aykhonun dyom zderbenaayom?" n'amakhertu hatete. "Iwe!" melesu. "Arba'te gna yre'ayuni! Iti rab'ay kem wedi amalkhti ymesl!")
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Inside the furnace - the three friends walking freely, unharmed, with a glowing fourth figure beside them (an angel/divine being). Flames all around but not touching them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Inside the furnace, the three friends were walking around freely! They were not burned at all! A fourth person — an angel of God — was walking with them, protecting them!
ኣብ ውሽጢ እቶን ሰለስተ ኣዕሩኽ ብነጻ ይዘዋወሩ ነበሩ! ፈጺሞም ኣይነደዱን! ራብዓይ ሰብ — መልኣኽ ኣምላኽ — ምሳታቶም ይዘዋወር ይሕልዎም ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Ab wshTi iton seleste a'erukh bnetsa yzewaweru neberu! Fetsimom aynededun! Rab'ay seb — mel'ak Amlak — msatatom yzewawer yhlwom nebere!)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: King Nebuchadnezzar calling out to the three men at the door of the furnace. He looks amazed and frightened. Flames visible inside but the men standing calmly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king called out, "Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego — servants of the Most High God — come out!" And they walked right out of the fire!
ንጉስ "ሻድራቕ ሜሻቕ ኣብድነጎ — ኣገልገልቲ ልዑል ኣምላኽ — ውጹ!" ኢሉ ጸውዓ። ካብ ሓዊ ብቐጥታ ወጹ!
(Pronunciation: Ngus "Shadraq Meshaq Abdnego — agelgelti l'ul Amlak — wuts'u!" ilu tsew'a. Kab hawi bqeTta wets'u!)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: The three friends stepping out of the furnace completely unharmed. Officials examining them closely in amazement. Not a single burn mark or smell of smoke. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Everyone crowded around to look! Not a single hair was burned! Their clothes were not damaged! They didn't even smell like smoke! God had completely protected them!
ኩሉ ሰብ ክርኢ ተጸጊዑ! ሓንቲ ጸጉሪ'ውን ኣይነደደትን! ክዳውንቶም ኣይተበላሸወን! ሽታ ትኪ'ውን ኣይነበሮምን! ኣምላኽ ምሉእ ብምሉእ ሓልዩዎም!
(Pronunciation: Kulu seb kr'i tetsegiou! Hanti tseguri'wn aynededetn! Kdawnntom aytbelashewen! Shta tki'wn ayneberomn! Amlak mlu'e bmlu'e halyuwom!)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: King Nebuchadnezzar bowing his head in respect, declaring God is great. The three friends standing proud and relieved. Courtiers watching in wonder. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king declared, "Praise the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego! He sent His angel to save them! No other god can save like this!"
ንጉስ ኣወጀ "ንኣምላኽ ሻድራቕ ሜሻቕ ኣብድነጎ ይመስገን! መልኣኹ ሰዲዱ ኣድሒኑዎም! ካልእ ኣምላኽ ከምዚ ከድሕን ዝኽእል የለን!"
(Pronunciation: Ngus aweje "N'Amlak Shadraq Meshaq Abdnego ymesgen! Mel'akhu sedidu adhinuwom! Kal'e Amlak kemzi kedhn zkhel yelen!")
Scene 17
[PICTURE: The king giving Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego higher positions in the kingdom. They receive official robes and medallions. Celebration in the palace. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king promoted the three friends to even higher positions in Babylon! Their faith in God was rewarded. Standing up for what's right always leads to blessing!
ንጉስ ነቶም ሰለስተ ኣዕሩኽ ኣብ ባቢሎን ዝለዓለ ስልጣን ሃቦም! እምነቶም ኣብ ኣምላኽ ተሸሊሙ። ንቕኑዕ ነገር ምቕዋም ኩሉ ግዜ ናብ በረኸት ይመርሕ!
(Pronunciation: Ngus netom seleste a'erukh ab Babilon zle'ale sslTan habom! Imnetom ab Amlak teshelimu. Nqnu' neger mqwam kulu gize nab bereket ymerh!)
Scene 18
[PICTURE: The three friends praying together in thanksgiving after their rescue. Kneeling with hands raised. Warm divine light around them. Joy and gratitude on their faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The three friends thanked God for saving them. They knew their faith had been tested and God was faithful! He had never left them alone in the fire.
ሰለስተ ኣዕሩኽ ንኣምላኽ ስለ ዘድሓኖም ኣመስገኑ። እምነቶም ተፈቲኑ ኣምላኽ ድማ እሙን ምዃኑ ፈለጡ! ኣብ ሓዊ ፈጺሙ በይኖም ኣይገደፎምን።
(Pronunciation: Seleste a'erukh n'Amlak sle zedhanom amesgenu. Imnetom tefetinu Amlak dma imun mkwanu feleTu! Ab hawi fetsimu beynom aygedefomn.)
Scene 19
[PICTURE: People all over Babylon talking about the miracle. Market scenes with people amazed, pointing toward the palace. The news spreading everywhere. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Everyone in Babylon heard about the miracle! The story of God's power spread everywhere. Many people learned about the true God because of their bravery.
ኩሉ ሰብ ኣብ ባቢሎን ብዛዕባ ተኣምር ሰምዐ! ዛንታ ሓይሊ ኣምላኽ ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ ተዘርግሐ። ብዙሓት ሰባት ብጅግንነቶም ብዛዕባ ሓቀኛ ኣምላኽ ተመሃሩ።
(Pronunciation: Kulu seb ab Babilon bza'ba te'amr sem'e! Zanta hayli Amlak ab kulu bota tezerghe. Bzuhat sebat bjgnnnetom bza'ba haqeNa Amlak temeharu.)
Scene 20
[PICTURE: The king writing a new law on a scroll declaring that no one should speak against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. Royal seal being pressed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king made a new law: "No one in my kingdom may say anything bad about the God of these three men! Their God is the most powerful of all!"
ንጉስ ሓድሽ ሕጊ ኣውጸአ፡ "ኣብ መንግስተይ ዝኾነ ሰብ ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ናይ'ዞም ሰለስተ ሰባት ሕማቕ ክዛረብ የብሉን! ኣምላኾም ካብ ኩሉ ዝሕይል'ዩ!"

🌟 Moral of the Story 🌟

English: Standing up for God and doing what's right takes courage, but God is always with us. Even in the hardest situations, God never leaves us. He protects those who are faithful to Him!

ትግርኛ: ንኣምላኽ ምቕዋምን ቅኑዕ ምግባርን ትብዓት ይሓትት ግና ኣምላኽ ኩሉ ግዜ ምሳና'ዩ። ኣብ ዝኸፍአ ኩነታት'ውን ኣምላኽ ፈጺሙ ኣይገድፈናን። ንኣኡ ንዝእመኑ ይሕልዎም!
