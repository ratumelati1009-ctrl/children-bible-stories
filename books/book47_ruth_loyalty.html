Famine gripped the land of Judah like a slow, choking death. The fields that once overflowed with grain lay cracked and barren. Elimelech, a man from Bethlehem — whose name ironically means "my God is King" — made a fateful decision: he would take his wife Naomi and their two sons, Mahlon and Chilion, and emigrate to Moab, a foreign land with foreign gods. It was a desperate choice born of survival, yet it meant leaving behind the community of faith, the promises of God, and the land of their inheritance.
ጥሜት ንምድሪ ይሁዳ ከም ዝዝሕል ሞት ዘንቀጠ ሓዞ። እቶም ሓደ ግዜ ብእኽሊ ዝውሕዙ ዝነበሩ ግራውቲ ተሰንጢቖም ጠሪዞም ተረኽቡ። ኤሊሜሌክ ሓደ ናይ ቤትልሄም ሰብኣይ — ስሙ ብዘገርም "ኣምላኸይ ንጉስ'ዩ" ዝብል ትርጉም ዘለዎ — ዕጫ ዝውስን ውሳኔ ወሰነ፡ ሰበይቱ ናኦሚን ክልተ ደቁ ማሕሎንን ክልዮንን ሒዙ ናብ ሞኣብ ባዕዳዊት ምድሪ ምስ ባዕዳውያን ኣማልኽቲ ክሰድድ። ካብ ድሕነት ዝተወልደ ተስፋ ዝቖረጸ ምርጫ ነበረ ግን ማሕበረሰብ እምነት፡ ተስፋታት ኣምላኽን ናይ ውርሻ ምድሮምን ምግዳፍ ማለት ነበረ።
(Timet nimidri Yihuda kem zizihhil mot zeniqete hazo. Itom hade gize bi'ikhli ziwihizu zineberu grawthi tesenitiqom terizom terekhibu. Kab dihhinet zitewelde tesfa ziqoretse mircha nebere gin mahbereseb imnet, tesfatat Amlak'n nay wirsha midrom'n migdaf malet nebere.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: The family settled in Moab — a modest home surrounded by Moabite architecture and culture. Elimelech lies gravely ill on a bed while Naomi holds his hand, weeping. In Moab, Elimelech died. The text offers no explanation — just the stark, devastating fact. Naomi, now a widow in a foreign land without the social protections a husband provided in that era, was left with her two sons. They were her only security, her only connection to the future. The boys grew up and married Moabite women — Orpah and Ruth — crossing ethnic and religious boundaries that most Israelites would have condemned. For about ten years, they built lives together in this foreign land.
ኣብ ሞኣብ ኤሊሜሌክ ሞተ። እቲ ጽሑፍ ምንም ማብራህታ ኣይህብን — ዝጭብጥ ዘስደምም ሓቂ ጥራይ። ናኦሚ ሕጂ ኣብ ባዕዳዊት ምድሪ ሰብኣይ ዝህቦ ማሕበራዊ ሓለዋ ብዘይብላ መበለት ምስ ክልተ ደቃ ተረፈት። ንሳቶም ጥራይ'ዮም ድሕንነታን ምስ መጻኢ ዘራኽባ ነገርን ዝነበሩ። እቶም ኣወዳት ዓበዩ ናይ ሞኣብ ኣንስቲ — ዖርጳን ሩትን — ኣእተዉ ብዙሓት ኣስራኤላውያን ዝኹንንዎ ናይ ዓሌትን ሃይማኖትን ዶባት ሰጊሮም። ንኣስታት ዓሰርተ ዓመታት ኣብ'ዛ ባዕዳዊት ምድሪ ብሓደ ሂወት ሃነጹ።
(Ab Mo'ab Elimelekh mote. Iti tsihuf minim mabrahita ayihibin — zichbiit zisdemim haqi tiray. Na'omi hiji ab ba'idawit midri seb'ay zihibbo mahberawi halewa bizeyibla mebelet mis kilte deqa terefet. Nisatom tiray'yom dihinnetaa'n mis metsai zerakiba neger'n zineberu. Itom awedat abeyu nay Mo'ab anisti — Orpa'n Rut'n — a'itewu bizuhat Asra'elawiyan zikhininwo nay alit'n haymanot'n dobat segirom. Ni'astat asirte ametat ab'za ba'idawit midri bihade hiwet hanetsu.)
Scene 3

Then both Mahlon and Chilion died. In the span of a decade, Naomi lost everything — her husband, both sons, and with them, any hope of grandchildren, financial security, or social standing. In the ancient world, a childless widow was among the most vulnerable people in society. Naomi was not just grieving; she was destitute, stranded in a foreign country with no male protector, no income, and no future she could see. Sometimes life strips away everything, and we are left with nothing but God — and even He can feel distant.
ድሕሪኡ ማሕሎንን ክልዮንን ክልቲኦም ሞቱ። ኣብ ውሽጢ ዓሰርተ ዓመታት ናኦሚ ኩሉ ስኣነት — ሰብኣያ፡ ክልቲኦም ደቃ ምስኦም ድማ ዝኾነ ተስፋ ደቂ ደቂ፡ ገንዘባዊ ድሕንነት ወይ ማሕበራዊ ደረጃ። ኣብ ጥንታዊ ዓለም ቆልዓ ዘይብላ መበለት ኣብ ማሕበረሰብ ካብ ዝበለጹ ተጠቃዕቲ ሰባት ነበረት። ናኦሚ ትሓዝን ጥራይ ዘይኮነት — ኣብ ባዕዳዊት ሃገር ብዘይ ወድ ተባዕታይ ሓላዊ፡ ብዘይ እቶት ዝረአ መጻኢ ብዘይብላ ድኽይቲ ነበረት። ሓደ ሓደ ግዜ ሂወት ኩሉ ነገር ይቕንጥጠልካ ብዘይ ኣምላኽ ሓንቲ'ውን ኣይትተርፈካን — ንሱ'ውን ርሑቕ ኮይኑ ክስምዓካ ይኽእል።
(Dihri'u Mahhlon'n Kiliyon'n kiltom motu. Ab wishti asirte ametat Na'omi kulu si'anet — seb'aya, kilti'om deqa misom dima zikhone tesfa deqi deqi, genzebawi dihinnet wey mahberawi dereja. Ab tintawi alem qol'a zeyibla mebelet ab mahbereseb kab zibelitsu tetqa'iti sebat neberet. Na'omi tihazin tiray zeyikonet — ab ba'idawit hager bizey wed teba'itay halawi, bizey itot zir'e metsai bizeyibla dikhyiti neberet. Hade hade gize hiwet kulu neger yiqinititelka bizey Amlak hanti'wun ayitirfekaan — nisu'wun rihuq koynu kismi'aka yikhil.)
Scene 4

Word reached Naomi that the Lord had visited His people in Bethlehem — the famine was over, and bread had returned to the "house of bread." This news stirred something in Naomi's devastated heart. Despite her bitterness and grief, she decided to return home. It was not hope that drove her — it was simply the absence of any reason to stay. Sometimes we return to God not in triumph but in desperation, not with full hearts but with empty hands. And God welcomes us either way.
እግዚኣብሄር ንህዝቡ ኣብ ቤትልሄም ከም ዝበጽሐ — ጥሜት ከም ዝተወድአ ናብ "ቤት ኅብስቲ" ኅብስቲ ከም ዝተመልሰ ዜና ናብ ናኦሚ በጽሐ። እዚ ዜና ኣብ ዝተሰብረ ልቢ ናኦሚ ገለ ነገር ቀስቀሰ። ዋላ'ኳ ምረትን ሓዘንን እንተ ነበራ ናብ ገዛኣ ክትምለስ ወሰነት። ተስፋ ዘይኮነ'ዩ ዝደፋፈኣ — ንኽትጸንሕ ምኽንያት ምስኣን ጥራይ'ዩ ዝነበረ። ሓደ ሓደ ግዜ ናብ ኣምላኽ ንምለስ ብዓወት ዘይኮነ ብተስፋ ምቑራጽ፡ ብመሊኡ ልቢ ዘይኮነ ብጥራይ ኢድ'ዩ። ኣምላኽ ድማ ብኽልቲኡ መገዲ ይቕበለና።
(Igzi'abher nihizbu ab Betlehem kem zibetsihe — timet kem zitewede'a nab "bet khibisti" khibisti kem zitemeliise zena nab Na'omi betsihe. Izi zena ab zitesebere libi Na'omi gele neger qesqese. Wala'kwa miret'n hazin'n inte nebera nab gezaa kitimelis wesenet. Tesfa zeyikone'yu zidefefi'a — nikitseenih mikiniyat mis'an tiray'yu zinebere. Hade hade gize nab Amlak nimiles bi'awet zeyikone bitesfa miqurats, bimeli'u libi zeyikone bitiray id'yu. Amlak dima bikilti'u megedi yiqbelena.)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Naomi on a dusty road, turning to face her two daughters-in-law. She holds their hands, tears streaming down her face, urging them to go back to their mothers' homes in Moab. Orpah and Ruth both weep and cling to her. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
As they set out on the road to Judah, Naomi stopped and turned to her daughters-in-law with a speech that reveals her broken heart: "Go back, each of you, to your mother's home. May the Lord show you kindness, as you have shown kindness to your dead husbands and to me. May the Lord grant that you find rest in the home of another husband." Naomi was releasing them from any obligation. She believed she had nothing left to offer — no sons for them to marry, no future, no hope. In her pain, she couldn't imagine that God still had plans unfolding.
ናብ ይሁዳ ኣብ ዝወስድ መገዲ ክብገሳ ከለዋ ናኦሚ ደው ኢላ ንሰብኡተን ናብ ጸኒዐን ዝተሰብረ ልባ ዝገልጽ ዘረባ ተዛረበተን፡ "ተመለሳ ነፍሲ ወከፍክን ናብ ቤት ኣዴኽን ኪዳ። እግዚኣብሄር ከም'ቲ ንዝሞቱ ሰብኡትክንን ንዓይን ዝገበርክኒ ለውሃት የርእኽን። እግዚኣብሄር ኣብ ቤት ካልእ ሰብኣይ ዕረፍቲ ክትረኽባ ይሃብክን።" ናኦሚ ካብ ዝኾነ ግዴታ ትፈትሐን ነበረት። ክትህበን ዝተረፋ ሓንቲ'ውን ከም ዘይብላ ኣመነት — ዝምርዓወን ኣወዳት የልቡን፡ መጻኢ የልቦን፡ ተስፋ የልቦን። ኣብ ቃንዛኣ ኣምላኽ ገና ዝግለጹ መደባት ከም ዘለውዎ ክትሓስብ ኣይከኣለትን።
(Nab Yihuda ab ziwesid megedi kibgesa kelewa Na'omi dew ila niseb'uten nab tsenni'en zitesebere liba zigelits zereba tezarebeten; "Temelisa nefsi wekefkin nab bet adekin kida. Igzi'abher kem'ti nizimutu seb'utkin'n ni'ay'n zigeberkinni lewhat yeri'khin. Igzi'abher ab bet kal'e seb'ay irefti kitrekhibba yihabkin." Na'omi kab zikhone gideta tifetihen neberet. Kithiben ziterefa hanti'wun kem zeyibla amenet — zimir'awen awedat yelbun, metsai yelbon, tesfa yelbon. Ab qanzaa Amlak gena zigelitsu medebat kem zilewwo kithassib ayke'aletin.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Orpah kissing Naomi goodbye with tears, then turning back toward Moab. In contrast, Ruth clings tightly to Naomi, refusing to let go. The road forks — one path leads back to Moab's familiar hills, the other stretches toward unknown Judah. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Both women wept and insisted on going with Naomi. But Naomi pressed them again — more firmly this time, with painful logic: "Return home. Why would you come with me? Am I going to have any more sons to become your husbands? I am too old. Even if I had a husband tonight and bore sons, would you wait until they grew up?" Orpah, with tears and a kiss, made the reasonable choice — she turned back to her people and her gods. No one could blame her. Ruth, however, clung to Naomi and would not let go. Two women, same circumstances, radically different choices.
ክልቲአን ኣንስቲ በኸያ ምስ ናኦሚ ክኸዳ ኣጽኒዐን ሓተታ። ግን ናኦሚ ከም ብሓድሽ ጸቐጠተን — ሕጂ ብዝያዳ ብጽኑዕ ብዘቐንዝው ሎጂክ፡ "ተመለሳ። ስለምንታይ ምሳይ ትመጻ? ሰብኡትክን ክኾኑ ደቂ ክውለዱለይ'ዶ'ዮም? ኣሪገ'የ። ዋላ ሎሚ ለይቲ ሰብኣይ ነይሩኒ ኣወዳት ወሊደ'ኳ ክሳብ ዝዓብዩ ምተጸበኽኒ'ዶ?" ዖርጳ ብንብዓትን ስዕመትን ቅኑዕ ምርጫ ገበረት — ናብ ህዝባን ኣማልኽታን ተመልሰት። ማንም ክኽሰሳ ኣይክእልን። ሩት ግን ኣብ ናኦሚ ጸኒዓ ሓዘት ክትገድፋ ኣበየት። ክልተ ኣንስቲ ሓደ ዓይነት ኩነታት ብቐጻሊ ዝተፈላለየ ምርጫ።
(Kilti'an anisti bekeya mis Na'omi kikheda atsinni'en hateta. Gin Na'omi kem bihadish tseqetetien — hiji biziyada bitsinu'e bizeqiniziw lojik, "Temelisa. Silemintay misay timetsaa? Seb'utkin kikhonu deqi kiwleduley'do'yom? Arige'ye. Wala lomi leyti seb'ay neyiruni awedat welide'kwa kisab zi'abiyu mitetsebkhinni'do?" Orpa binib'at'n si'imet'n qinu'e mircha geberet — nab hizba'n amalkhta'n temeliset. Manim kikhisesa ayikhiln. Rut gin ab Na'omi tsenni'a hazet kitgedifa abeyet. Kilte anisti hade aynet kunetat biqetsali zitefelaleye mircha.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Ruth holding both of Naomi's hands, looking directly into her eyes with fierce determination and love. Her words seem to hang in the air between them like a sacred vow. The light behind Ruth creates a halo effect as she makes her declaration. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Ruth spoke words that have echoed through three thousand years of human history — words so powerful they are still recited at weddings today: "Don't urge me to leave you or to turn back from you. Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God. Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. May the Lord deal with me, be it ever so severely, if even death separates you and me." This was not romantic emotion — it was a covenant commitment. Ruth was choosing permanent exile from her homeland, her family, her gods, and her culture for the sake of love.

The Moral of the Story

Loyalty and love can transcend cultural and ethnic boundaries — God's family has no borders. God deliberately includes outsiders in His story, proving that belonging is not about heritage but about faithfulness. Small, daily acts of service — done with humility and excellence — lead to extraordinary outcomes that only God can orchestrate. God redeems empty seasons and transforms loss into legacy; no chapter of suffering is the final chapter. True love is not merely emotion; it is sacrificial commitment that perseveres when there is no visible reward, choosing another's good above your own comfort.

Discussion Questions / ሕቶታት ምይይጥ

1. Ruth left everything familiar — her homeland, culture, religion, and family — to follow Naomi into an uncertain future. Have you ever had to leave something comfortable behind to do the right thing? What made it difficult, and what gave you the courage to move forward?

1. ሩት ዝፈልጣ ኩሉ ገዲፋ — ዓዳ፡ ባህላ፡ ሃይማኖታ ስድራቤታ — ንናኦሚ ናብ ዘይርጉጽ መጻኢ ሰዓበታ። ቅኑዕ ነገር ንምግባር ድሕሪ ምቹ ነገር ገዲፍካ ትፈልጥ'ዶ? እንታይ'ዩ ኣጸጊምዎ እንታይ ድማ ንቕድሚት ክትኸይድ ትብዓት ሂቡካ?

2. Naomi was brutally honest about her pain — she told God and others that her life was bitter. Do you think it's okay to be angry or honest with God about suffering? How does Naomi's story show that expressing pain and having faith are not opposites?

2. ናኦሚ ብዛዕባ ቃንዛኣ ብጨካን ቅንዕና ተዛሪባ — ንኣምላኽን ንኻልኦትን ሂወታ ከም ዝመረረ ነጊራ። ብዛዕባ ስቓይ ምስ ኣምላኽ ምሕራቕ ወይ ቅንዕቲ ምዃን ጽቡቕ'ዩ ኢልካ ትሓስብ'ዶ? ዛንታ ናኦሚ ቃንዛ ምግላጽን እምነት ምህላውን ተጻረርቲ ከም ዘይኮኑ ብኸመይ ተርኢ?

3. Ruth didn't wait for rescue — she immediately went to work in the fields, doing humble labor to provide for herself and Naomi. 4. Boaz used his wealth and position to protect and provide for Ruth rather than exploit her vulnerability. In your life, how can you use whatever privilege or advantage you have to uplift others rather than benefit only yourself? What does it mean to be a "kinsman-redeemer" in today's world?

4. ቦዓዝ ሃብቱን ስልጣኑን ንሩት ንምሕላውን ንምስናይን ተጠቒሙ ተጠቃዕነታ ንምምዝማዝ ዘይኮነ። ኣብ ሂወትካ ዝኾነ ፍሉይ መሰል ወይ ቅድምና ንርእስኻ ጥራይ ዘይኮነ ንኻልኦት ንምልዕዓል ብኸመይ ክትጥቀም ትኽእል? ኣብ ሎሚ ዓለም "ዋሓስ" ምዃን እንታይ ማለት'ዩ?
