Jesus Calms the Storm

የሱስ ነቲ ማዕበል ኣህዲእዎ

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 10

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 10

For all the children who sometimes feel scared - Jesus is always with you.

ንኹሎም ሓደ ሓደ ግዜ ዝፈርሑ ቆልዑ - የሱስ ኩሉ ግዜ ምሳኹም'ዩ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: Jesus teaching large crowds all day by the Sea of Galilee, looking tired but kind, the sun setting in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus had a very long and busy day. He taught many people and healed the sick. By evening, He was very tired. His body needed rest.
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ኣዝዩ ነዊሕን ጻዕራዊን መዓልቲ ነይሩዎ። ንብዙሓት ሰባት ኣምሂሩ ሕሙማት ፈዊሱ። ኣብ ምሸት ኣዝዩ ደኸመ። ኣካላቱ ዕረፍቲ ደለየ።
(Pronunciation: ደኸመ = dekheme = tired, ዕረፍቲ = 'irefti = rest, ምሸት = mishet = evening)

Page 2

[PICTURE: Jesus talking to his disciples by a wooden boat at the shore of the lake, pointing across the water. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus said to His disciples, "Let us go to the other side of the lake." So they got into a boat together. It was a calm, peaceful evening.
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ንደቀ መዛሙርቱ "ናብ'ቲ ሓደ ገጽ ቀላይ ንሕለፍ" በሎም። ብሓባር ድማ ናብ ጃልባ ደየቡ። ህዱእን ሰላማውን ምሸት ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ቀላይ = qelay = lake, ጃልባ = jalba = boat, ንሕለፍ = niHilef = let us cross)

(Pronunciation: ደመናታት = demenat = clouds, ንፋስ = nifas = wind, ማዕበላት = ma'ibelat = waves)

(Pronunciation: ይድቅስ = yidqis = sleeping, ነጎዳ = negoda = thunder, ብሰላም = biselam = peacefully)
