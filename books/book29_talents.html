Once there was a kind and wealthy master who was going on a long journey to a faraway land.

ግን እቲ ሳልሳይ ኣገልጋሊ ፈሪሑ። ነቲ ሓንቲ ታለንቱ ጠሚቱ "እንተ ኣጥፊአያ ኸ? እንተ ወዲቐ ኸ?" ኢሉ ተሻቐለ።

The Moral of the Story

God gives everyone gifts and abilities. Use what God gave you! Don't hide your talents out of fear. Be faithful with what you have, and God will bless you with even more.
