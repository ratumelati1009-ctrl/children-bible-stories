Scene 1
[PICTURE: A young Jewish girl named Hadassah (Esther) living in a modest Persian home with her older cousin Mordecai. She is studious and graceful, reading scrolls by lamplight while Mordecai watches proudly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In the vast Persian Empire, during the reign of King Xerxes, there lived a young Jewish orphan named Hadassah — known by her Persian name, Esther. Her parents had died when she was young, and her older cousin Mordecai had taken her in, raising her as his own daughter with deep love and devotion to God.
ኣብ'ታ ሰፊሕ ናይ ፋርስ መንግስቲ፡ ኣብ ግዜ ዝነገሰ ንጉስ ኣሕሽዌሮስ፡ ሃዳሳ ዝስማ ሓንቲ ናይ ኣይሁድ ዘኽታም ነበረት — ብፋርሳዊ ስማ ኤስቴር ትፍለጥ ነበረት። ወለዳ ንእሽቶ ከላ ሞይቶም፡ ዓቢ ሓዉ ሞርድኬ ብዓሚቕ ፍቕርን ተወፋይነት ንኣምላኽን ከም ጓሉ ኣዕበያ።
(Ab'ta sefiH nay Faris mengisti, ab gize zineges nigus AHishweros, Hadasa zisma Hanti nay Ayhud zekhtam neberet — biFarsawi sima Ester tifleT neberet. Weleda ni'ishto kela moytom, abi Hawu Mordeke bi'amiq fiqirin tewefaynet ni'Amlakhin kem gualu a'ibeya.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: The magnificent Persian palace of Susa with towering columns and hanging gardens. King Xerxes sits on an elevated golden throne, looking troubled and displeased. Courtiers whisper nervously in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
King Xerxes ruled from his throne in the citadel of Susa. After Queen Vashti refused to appear at his banquet, the king was furious and banished her. His advisors suggested a search throughout all provinces for a new queen — someone beautiful, wise, and worthy of the royal crown.
ንጉስ ኣሕሽዌሮስ ካብ ዝፋኑ ኣብ ግምቢ ሱሳ ይገዝእ ነበረ። ንግስቲ ዋሽቲ ኣብ ድግሱ ክትቀርብ ምስ ኣበየት፡ ንጉስ ተቖጢዑ ሰጎጋ። ኣማኸርቱ ኣብ ኩለን ኣውራጃታት ሓዳስ ንግስቲ ክደልዩ ሓሳብ ኣቕረቡ — ጽብቕቲ፡ ጥበበኛን ንናይ ንጉሳዊ ዘውዲ ዝበቅዕን።
(Nigus AHishweros kab zifanu ab gimbi Susa yigez'i nebere. Nigisti Washti ab digsu kitqerib mis abeyet, nigus teqoTi'u segoga. Amakhertu ab kulen awrajat Hadas nigisti kidelyu Hasab aqrebu — tsibiqti, Tibebenyain ninay nigusawi zewdi zibeqi'in.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Mordecai speaking privately with young Esther, his hand on her shoulder, his face serious and caring. He speaks urgently while she listens intently. A window shows Persian soldiers gathering young women outside. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When the king's decree reached their city, Mordecai knew Esther would be taken to the palace. He pulled her aside and spoke urgently: "You must not reveal your Jewish identity to anyone. Keep our people's heritage hidden in your heart, but never forget who you truly are — a daughter of the God of Abraham."
ኣዋጅ ንጉስ ናብ ከተማኦም ምስ በጽሐ፡ ሞርድኬ ኤስቴር ናብ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ከም ትውሰድ ፈለጠ። ፈልዩ "ናይ ኣይሁድ መንነትኪ ንማንም ኣይትግለጺ። ውርሻ ህዝብና ኣብ ልብኺ ሓብእዮ፡ ግን መን ምዃንኪ ኣይትረስዒ — ጓል ኣምላኽ ኣብርሃም ኢኺ" ብህጹጽ ነገራ።
(Awaj nigus nab ketema'om mis bets'He, Mordeke Ester nab beyte-mengisti kem tiwsed feleThe. Felyu "nay Ayhud mennetki nimanum aytgiletsi. Wirsha hizibna ab libki Hab'iyo, gin men mukhanki aytiresii — gual Amlak Abrham ikhi" bihitsuTs negeraa.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Esther among many beautiful young women in the palace women's quarters. She stands out with her quiet grace and humility. A kind eunuch named Hegai presents her with special oils and foods. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Esther was taken to the palace along with many other young women from across the empire. Hegai, the keeper of the women, was immediately impressed by her grace and character. He provided her with the best beauty treatments and seven chosen maids. Yet Esther remained humble, winning favor not through arrogance but through genuine kindness.
ኤስቴር ካብ ምሉእ መንግስቲ ምስ ብዙሓት ካልኦት መንእሰያት ኣንስቲ ናብ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ተወስደት። ሄጌ ሓላዊ ኣንስቲ ብጸጋኣን ባህርያታን ብቕጽበት ተመሰጠ። ዝበለጸ ናይ ጽባቐ ክንክንን ሸውዓተ ዝተመርጻ ገረዳትን ኣቕረበላ። ኤስቴር ግን ትሑት ኮይና፡ ብሓቀኛ ለውሃት'ምበር ብትዕቢት ሞገስ ረኸበት።
(Ester kab milu'e mengisti mis buzuHat kal'ot men'eseyat anisti nab beyte-mengisti tewsedet. Hege Halawi anisti bitsega'an bahriyatan biqits'bet temeseTe. Zibeletse nay tsibaqe kinikiniin shew'ate zitemerTsa geredatin aqrebela. Ester gin tiHut koyna, biHaqenya lewhat'mber bit'ibit moges rekhebet.)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: King Xerxes placing a royal crown on Esther's head in a grand throne room. His face shows genuine admiration and joy. Esther looks dignified yet humble, wearing beautiful royal robes. Courtiers watch in amazement. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When Esther's turn came to appear before the king, she asked for nothing more than what Hegai advised. The king was captivated — not merely by her beauty, but by her wisdom and grace. He set the royal crown upon her head, declaring her queen above all others. A great banquet was held in her honor throughout the empire.
ግዜ ኤስቴር ኣብ ቅድሚ ንጉስ ክትቀርብ ምስ በጽሐ፡ ሄጌ ካብ ዝመኸራ ንላዕሊ ኣይሓተተትን። ንጉስ ተማረኸ — ብጽባቐኣ ጥራይ ዘይኮነ ብጥበባን ጸጋኣን። ንጉሳዊት ዘውዲ ኣብ ርእሳ ኣቐሚጡ ካብ ኩለን ንላዕሊ ንግስቲ ኣወጃ። ዓቢ ድግሲ ኣብ ምሉእ መንግስቲ ንኽብራ ተኻየደ።
(Gize Ester ab qidmi nigus kitqerib mis bets'He, Hege kab zimekera nil'ali ayHatetetin. Nigus temarekhe — bitsibaqe'a Teray zeyikone biTibebain tsega'ain. Nigusawit zewdi ab ri'isa aqemiTu kab kulen nil'ali nigisti aweja. Abi digsi ab milu'e mengisti nikhbra tekayede.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Mordecai sitting faithfully at the palace gate day after day, wearing simple Jewish clothing. He overhears two guards whispering a plot against the king. His face shows alertness and concern. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mordecai faithfully sat at the king's gate each day, watching over Esther from afar. One day, he overheard two royal guards — Bigthana and Teresh — plotting to assassinate King Xerxes. Mordecai immediately reported the conspiracy through Esther, and the traitors were executed. The event was recorded in the royal chronicles, though Mordecai received no reward.
ሞርድኬ ነፍሲ ወከፍ መዓልቲ ኣብ ደገ ንጉስ ብእምንነት ኮፍ ኢሉ ንኤስቴር ብርሑቕ ይሕልዋ ነበረ። ሓደ መዓልቲ ክልተ ናይ ንጉስ ሓለውቲ — ቢግታናን ተሬሽን — ንንጉስ ኣሕሽዌሮስ ክቐትልዎ ኪውጠኑ ሰምዐ። ሞርድኬ ብቕጽበት ብኤስቴር ኣቢሉ ነቲ ውዲት ሓበረ፡ እቶም ከዳዓት ድማ ተቐትሉ። ኣብ ናይ ንጉሳዊ ዜና መዓልቲ ተጻሕፈ፡ ሞርድኬ ግን ሽልማት ኣይተዋህቦን።
(Mordeke nefsi wekef me'alti ab dege nigus bi'iminnet kof ilu ni'Ester birHuq yiHilwa nebere. Hade me'alti kilte nay nigus Halewti — Bigtana'n Teresh'n — ninigus AHishweros kiqetl'wo kiwTenu sem'e. Mordeke biqits'bet bi'Ester abilu neti widit Habere, itom keda'at dima teqetlu. Ab nay nigusawi zena me'alti tetsaHfe, Mordeke gin shilmat aytewahbon.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Haman, a proud and arrogant official in luxurious robes, walking through the palace gate. All people bow before him except Mordecai who stands upright with quiet dignity. Haman's face burns with rage. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After these events, King Xerxes elevated Haman the Agagite to the highest position in the empire. All royal officials were commanded to bow before him. But Mordecai refused to kneel — not out of stubbornness, but because as a Jew, he would bow only before God. Haman noticed this act of defiance, and fury consumed his heart.
ድሕሪ'ዚ ፍጻመታት ንጉስ ኣሕሽዌሮስ ንሃማን ኣጋጋዊ ናብ'ታ ዝለዓለት ስልጣን ኣብ'ታ መንግስቲ ኣዕበዮ። ኩሎም ናይ ንጉስ ሰበስልጣን ኣብ ቅድሚኡ ክስግዱ ተኣዘዙ። ሞርድኬ ግን ክስገድ ኣበየ — ብትሪ ዘይኮነ ከም ኣይሁዳዊ ንኣምላኽ ጥራይ ስለ ዝስገድ። ሃማን ነዚ ጸረ-ትእዛዝ ርእዩ ቁጥዓ ልቡ መልኦ።
(DiHri'zi fits'ametat nigus AHishweros niHaman Agagawi nab'ta zile'alet silTan ab'ta mengisti a'ibeyoo. Kulom nay nigus sebesilTan ab qidmi'u kisigdu te'azezu. Mordeke gin kisged abeye — bitri zeykone kem Ayhudawi ni'Amlak Teray sile zisged. Haman nezi tsere-ti'izaz ri'iyu quTi'a libu mel'o.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Haman in a dark room casting lots (Pur dice) on a table, his face twisted with hatred and scheming. Shadows emphasize his evil intentions. A map of the Persian Empire hangs on the wall behind him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Haman's hatred grew beyond one man. He learned Mordecai was Jewish, and decided that punishing one person was not enough — he would destroy all the Jews in the entire Persian Empire. He cast lots, called "Pur," to determine the date for this massacre. The lot fell on the twelfth month, Adar, giving him nearly a year to execute his genocidal plan.
ጽልኢ ሃማን ካብ ሓደ ሰብ ሓሊፉ ዓበየ። ሞርድኬ ኣይሁዳዊ ምዃኑ ምስ ፈለጠ፡ ንሓደ ሰብ ምቕጻዕ ኣይኣክልን ኢሉ ውሰነ — ኣብ ምሉእ ናይ ፋርስ መንግስቲ ንኹሎም ኣይሁድ ከጥፍእ ደለየ። ነቲ ናይ ጥፍኣት መዓልቲ ንምውሳን "ጲር" ዝብሃል ዕጫ ደርበየ። ዕጫ ኣብ መበል ዓሰርተ ክልተ ወርሒ ኣዳር ወደቐ፡ ነቲ ናይ ህዝቢ-ጽንተት ውጥኑ ንምግባር ክሳብ ሓደ ዓመት ሃበ።
(Tsilii Haman kab Hade seb Halifu abeye. Mordeke Ayhudawi mukhanu mis feleThe, niHade seb miqTs'a'i ayi'akilin ilu wesene — ab milu'e nay Faris mengisti nikulum Ayhud keTif'i deleye. Neti nay Tif'at me'alti nimwisan "Pur" zibhal itsHa derbeye. ItsHa ab mebel aserte kilte werHi Adar wedeqe, neti nay hizbi-tsintet wiTnu nimgibar kisab Hade amet habe.)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Haman kneeling before King Xerxes, presenting a scroll with his decree. He offers bags of silver, looking persuasive and cunning. The king looks thoughtful, holding his royal ring. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Haman approached the king with poisonous words: "There is a certain people scattered throughout your kingdom whose customs differ from all others. They do not obey the king's laws. It is not in your interest to tolerate them." He offered ten thousand talents of silver to finance their destruction. The king, not knowing these were Esther's people, gave Haman his signet ring to seal the decree.
ሃማን ብመርዛም ቃላት ናብ ንጉስ ቀረበ፦ "ኣብ ምሉእ መንግስትኻ ዝተበተኑ ልምድታቶም ካብ ኩሉ ዝተፈልየ ህዝቢ ኣሎ። ሕጊ ንጉስ ኣይእዘዙን። ክትጻወሮም ንዓኻ ኣይጠቅምን።" ንጥፍኣቶም ንምምዋል ዓሰርተ ሽሕ ታለንት ብሩር ኣቕረበ። ንጉስ እዚኣቶም ናይ ኤስቴር ህዝቢ ምዃኖም ከይፈለጠ ነቲ ኣዋጅ ንምሕታም ናይ ማሕተም ቀለቤቱ ንሃማን ሃቦ።
(Haman bimerzam qalat nab nigus qerebe: "Ab milu'e mengistikha zitebetenu limiditathom kab kulu zitefeleye hizbi alo. Higi nigus ayi'izezun. Kittsawerom ni'akha ayTeqimin." Scene 10
[PICTURE: Throughout the city of Susa, Jewish families weep and mourn in the streets. Mordecai tears his clothes and puts on sackcloth and ashes, crying out in grief at the palace gate. The city is in confusion and despair. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The decree was dispatched to every province: on the thirteenth day of Adar, all Jews — young and old, women and children — were to be destroyed. When Mordecai learned of the edict, he tore his clothes, dressed in sackcloth and ashes, and went into the city crying with a loud and bitter cry. Throughout the empire, Jews mourned with fasting, weeping, and wailing.
ኣዋጅ ናብ ኩለን ኣውራጃታት ተላእከ፦ ኣብ መበል ዓሰርተ ሰለስተ መዓልቲ ኣዳር ኩሎም ኣይሁድ — መንእሰይን ኣረጋውያንን ኣንስትን ቆልዑን — ክጠፍኡ ነበሮም። ሞርድኬ ነቲ ኣዋጅ ምስ ፈለጠ ክዳኑ ቀደደ፡ ሸሓኒ ለቢሱ ብሓሙኽሽቲ ተሸፊኑ ብዓውታን መሪርን ብኽያት ናብ ከተማ ወጸ። ኣብ ምሉእ መንግስቲ ኣይሁድ ብጾምን ብኽያትን ዋይዋይታን ሓዘኑ።
(Awaj nab kulen awrajat tela'ike: ab mebel aserte seleste me'alti Adar kulom Ayhud — men'esey'n aregawiyan'n anistin qol'un — kiTef'u neberom. Scene 11
[PICTURE: Esther's maid Hathak going back and forth between Esther in her royal chambers and Mordecai at the gate, carrying messages. Esther looks distressed upon receiving the news, holding a copy of the decree. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When Esther's maids reported that Mordecai was in sackcloth at the gate, she was deeply distressed. She sent her servant Hathak to learn why. The Moral of the Story / ትምህርቲ ዛንታ

God sovereignly places us in positions and seasons for His divine purposes — nothing in our lives is accidental. True courage is not the absence of fear, but choosing to act faithfully despite being terrified. Standing up for others may cost us everything, yet silence in the face of injustice costs even more. God works powerfully behind the scenes even when His name isn't mentioned and His hand isn't visible. Our identity and heritage are not burdens to hide but foundations that give us strength and purpose when the moment of testing arrives.

ኣምላኽ ብልዑል ስልጣኑ ንመለኮታዊ ዕላማኡ ኣብ ቦታታትን ግዜያትን የቐምጠና — ኣብ ህይወትና ብኣጋጣሚ ዝኾነ ነገር የለን። ሓቀኛ ጅግንነት ፍርሒ ዘይምህላው ኣይኮነን ግን ብፍርሒ ተሸፊንካ ብእምነት ክትገብር ምምራጽ'ዩ። ንኻልኦት ምቕዋም ኩሉ ከኽፍለና ይኽእል ግን ኣብ ቅድሚ ዓመጽ ምስቓጥ ዝገደደ ይኽፍል። ኣምላኽ ስሙ ዘይጥቀስን ኢዱ ዘይርአን'ኳ ምስ ኮነ ብድሕሪ መድረኽ ብሓይሊ ይሰርሕ። መንነትናን ውርሻናን ክንሓብኦ ዘሎና ጾር ኣይኮነን ግን ናይ ፈተና ህሞት ክበጽሕ ከሎ ሓይልን ዕላማን ዝህበና መሰረት'ዩ።

Teen Discussion Questions / ሕቶታት ምይይጥ ንመንእሰያት

1. Mordecai told Esther that deliverance would come "from another place" if she stayed silent. What does this reveal about God's sovereignty versus human responsibility? Can God accomplish His purposes without us, and if so, why does He choose to use us?

1. ሞርድኬ ንኤስቴር ስቕ እንተበለት ምድሓን "ካብ ካልእ ቦታ" ከም ዝመጽእ ነገራ። እዚ ብዛዕባ ልዑል ስልጣን ኣምላኽ ኣንጻር ሓላፍነት ሰብ እንታይ ይገልጽ? ኣምላኽ ብዘይ ንዓና ዕላማኡ ከውቅዕ ይኽእል'ዶ፡ ከምኡ እንተኾይኑ ድማ ስለምንታይ ክጥቀመና ይመርጽ?

2. Esther had to hide her Jewish identity to survive in the Persian court. Have you ever felt pressure to hide part of who you are — your faith, culture, or values — to fit in? How did that feel, and what did you learn from it?

2. ኤስቴር ኣብ ናይ ፋርስ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ንኽትድሕን ናይ ኣይሁድ መንነታ ክትሓብእ ነበራ። ክትሰማማዕ ኢልካ ክፋል መንነትካ — እምነትካ፡ ባህልኻ ወይ ሰናያት — ክትሓብእ ጸቕጢ ተሰሚዑካ'ዶ ይፈልጥ? ከመይ ተሰሚዑካ ካብኡ'ኸ እንታይ ተማሂርካ?

3. Esther used strategic wisdom — she didn't rush to expose Haman but waited for the right moment through two banquets. How does patience combined with courage differ from impulsive bravery? When in your life might strategic timing matter as much as bold action?

3. ኤስቴር ስትራተጂያዊ ጥበብ ተጠቒማ — ንሃማን ከተቃልዖ ኣይተቓለዐትን ግን ብኽልተ ድግስታት ቅኑዕ ግዜ ተጸብየት። ትዕግስቲ ምስ ጅግንነት ክጥመር ከሎ ካብ ህውከታዊ ትብዓት ብኸመይ ይፍለ? ኣብ ህይወትካ ስትራተጂያዊ ግዜ ከም ደፋር ተግባር ኣገዳሲ ዝኾነሉ መዓስ ክኸውን ይኽእል?

4. The Book of Esther is unique in the Bible because God's name is never directly mentioned. Yet His presence is undeniable in every twist of the story. Can you identify moments in your own life where God seemed silent or invisible, yet looking back, you can see His hand at work?

4. መጽሓፍ ኤስቴር ስም ኣምላኽ ብቐጥታ ስለ ዘይጥቀስ ኣብ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ፍሉይ'ዩ። ግን ኣብ ነፍሲ ወከፍ ለውጢ ዛንታ ህላውነቱ ዘይክሓድ'ዩ። ኣብ ህይወትካ ኣምላኽ ስቕ ዝበለ ወይ ዘይርአ ዝመሰለ ግን ተመሊስካ ክትርኢ ከሎኻ ኢዱ ትሰርሕ ከም ዝነበረት ትርእየሉ ህሞታት ተለልዩ'ዶ ትኽእል?

5. Esther said "If I perish, I perish" — she accepted that doing the right thing might cost her everything. What causes or people in your life would you be willing to sacrifice for? What does it look like for a teenager today to take courageous stands that might be costly?

5. ኤስቴር "እንተጠፋእኩ ጠፋእኩ" በለት — ቅኑዕ ምግባር ኩሉ ከኽፍላ ከም ዝኽእል ተቐበለት። ኣብ ህይወትካ ንኸመይ ዝኣመሰሉ ጠንቅታት ወይ ሰባት ክትስውኣ ትኽእል? ሎሚ ንሓደ መንእሰይ ዋጋ ከኽፍል ዝኽእል ደፋር ቅዋም ምውሳድ ከመይ ይመስል?

Prayer / ጸሎት

Almighty God, thank You for the story of Esther and the reminder that You place us exactly where we need to be for Your purposes. Give us the courage to stand up for what is right, even when it costs us something. Help us to trust that You are always working behind the scenes, even in seasons of silence. Strengthen our identity in You so that when our moment of testing comes, we will not shrink back in fear but step forward in faith. Teach us the wisdom to know when to speak and when to wait, and the boldness to say "If I perish, I perish" when justice demands it. May we never be silent when others are suffering. Use us, Lord, for such a time as this. Amen.

ኩሉ ዝኽእል ኣምላኽ፡ ብዛንታ ኤስቴርን ንዕላማኻ ኣብ ዘድልየና ቦታ ብልክዕ ከም ተቐምጠና ብዘዘኻኽር ነመስግነካ። ቅኑዕ ነገር ንምግባር ዋጋ'ኳ ዘኽፍል ምስ ኮነ ክንቆውም ጅግንነት ሃበና። ኣብ ናይ ስቕታ ግዜያት'ውን ኩሉ ግዜ ብድሕሪ መድረኽ ትሰርሕ ከም ዘሎኻ ክንኣምን ሓግዘና። ናይ ፈተና ህሞትና ክመጽእ ከሎ ብፍርሒ ከይንሕቆል ግን ብእምነት ንቕድሚት ክንስጉም ኣብ ዓኻ ዘሎና መንነት ኣጽንዓና። መዓስ ክንዛረብን መዓስ ክንጽበይን ጥበብ፡ ፍትሒ ክሓትት ከሎ "እንተጠፋእኩ ጠፋእኩ" ንምባል ድፍረት ምሃረና። ካልኦት ክሳቐዩ ከለዉ ስቕ ከይንብል። ጎይታ ንኸምዚ ዝኣመሰለ ግዜ ተጠቐመና። ኣሜን።
