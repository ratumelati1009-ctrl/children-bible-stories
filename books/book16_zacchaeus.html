Zacchaeus the Tax Collector

ዘኬዎስ እቲ ቀራጽ

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 16

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 16

For all the children who know that Jesus loves everyone and can change any heart.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ የሱስ ንኹሉ ሰብ ከም ዝፈትዎን ዝኾነ ልቢ ክቕይር ከም ዝኽእልን ዝፈልጡ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A very short man named Zacchaeus in fine rich clothes, counting coins at a table in the city of Jericho. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
In the city of Jericho, there lived a man named Zacchaeus. He was very rich but very short. He was a tax collector who took too much money from people.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ከተማ ያሪኮ ዘኬዎስ ዝስሙ ሰብ ይነብር ነበረ። ኣዝዩ ሃብታም ግን ኣዝዩ ሓጺር ነይሩ። ካብ ሰባት ዝያዳ ገንዘብ ዝወስድ ቀራጽ ነይሩ።
(Pronunciation: ዘኬዎስ = ZeKe'os = Zacchaeus, ቀራጽ = qerats' = tax collector, ሓጺር = Hats'ir = short)

Page 2

[PICTURE: People turning away from Zacchaeus, looking angry and annoyed, nobody wanting to be his friend. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Nobody liked Zacchaeus. People called him a cheat and a thief. He had no friends. Even though he was rich, he was very lonely and sad inside.
TIGRINYA:
ሓደ'ኳ ንዘኬዎስ ኣይፈትዎን ነበረ። ሰባት ጠባሪን ሰራቕን ይብሉዎ ነበሩ። መሓዙት ኣይነበሩዎን። ሃብታም'ኳ እንተነበረ ብውሽጡ ኣዝዩ በይናውን ሓዚንን ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ጠባሪ = t'ebari = cheat, ሰራቕ = seraq = thief, በይናዊ = beynawi = lonely)

Page 3

[PICTURE: People in the streets of Jericho buzzing with excitement, telling each other that Jesus is coming to their city. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
One day, exciting news spread through Jericho! "Jesus is coming to our city!" everyone said. People rushed to the main road to see Him. Everyone wanted to see Jesus!
TIGRINYA:
ሓደ መዓልቲ ኣብ ያሪኮ ዘሐጉስ ዜና ተዘርግሐ! "የሱስ ናብ ከተማና ይመጽእ ኣሎ!" ኩሎም በሉ። ሰባት ንኽርእዩዎ ናብ ዓባይ ጽርግያ ጎየዩ። ኩሎም ንየሱስ ክርእዩ ደለዩ!
(Pronunciation: የሱስ = Yesus = Jesus, ዜና = zena = news, ጽርግያ = ts'irgya = road)

Page 4

[PICTURE: Zacchaeus trying to see over the crowd but he is too short, jumping up and down, people blocking his view. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Zacchaeus really wanted to see Jesus too! But he was too short. He jumped and jumped but could not see over the tall crowd. Everyone was in his way!
TIGRINYA:
ዘኬዎስ'ውን ንየሱስ ክርኢ ኣዝዩ ደለየ! ግን ኣዝዩ ሓጺር ነበረ። ዘለለን ዘለለን ግን ኣብ ልዕሊ ነዊሓት ሰባት ክርኢ ኣይከኣለን። ኩሎም ኣብ መንገዱ ነበሩ!
(Pronunciation: ሓጺር = Hats'ir = short, ዘለለ = zelele = jumped, ነዊሓት = newiHat = tall)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Zacchaeus getting a clever idea, spotting a big sycamore tree near the road, starting to run toward it. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Then Zacchaeus had an idea! He saw a big sycamore tree by the road. "If I climb that tree, I can see Jesus!" he thought. So he ran ahead to the tree.
TIGRINYA:
ድሕሪኡ ዘኬዎስ ሓሳብ ኣተዎ! ኣብ ጥቓ ጽርግያ ዓባይ ኦም ሳግላ ረኣየ። "ነታ ኦም እንተ ደዪበያ ንየሱስ ክርኢ ይኽእል!" ሓሰበ። ስለዚ ናብታ ኦም ቀዲሙ ጎየ።
(Pronunciation: ኦም = om = tree, ሳግላ = sagla = sycamore, ደዪበ = deyibe = climb)

Page 6

[PICTURE: Zacchaeus climbing up the sycamore tree, his fine robes getting tangled, determined to get to the top branches. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Zacchaeus climbed up the tree as fast as he could! His fancy clothes got dirty but he did not care. He climbed higher and higher until he had a perfect view of the road.
TIGRINYA:
ዘኬዎስ ብዝተኻእሎ ቅልጣፈ ነታ ኦም ደየባ! ጽቡቕ ክዳኑ ተረኺሱ ግን ግድን ኣይነበሮን። ንጽርግያ ፍጹም ዝረአሉ ቦታ ክሳብ ዝረክብ ልዕሊ ልዕሊ ደየበ።
(Pronunciation: ደየባ = deyeba = climbed it, ቅልጣፈ = qilt'afe = fast, ልዕሊ = li'ili = higher)

Page 7

[PICTURE: Zacchaeus sitting happily on a branch of the sycamore tree, looking down at the road, waiting excitedly for Jesus. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Zacchaeus sat on a branch and waited. From up high, he could see everything! His heart was beating fast. He was so excited to see Jesus walking this way.
TIGRINYA:
ዘኬዎስ ኣብ ጨንፈር ኮፍ ኢሉ ጸነሐ። ካብ ላዕሊ ኩሉ ክርኢ ይኽእል ነበረ! ልቡ ብቕልጡፍ ትሃረመ። የሱስ ናብዚ ክመጽእ ከሎ ክርኢ ኣዝዩ ተሓጒሱ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ጨንፈር = chenfer = branch, ላዕሊ = la'ili = up high, ልቡ = libu = his heart)

Page 8

[PICTURE: Jesus walking along the road with a crowd following Him, approaching the sycamore tree where Zacchaeus is hiding. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus came walking down the road with many people following Him. He smiled and talked to everyone. Then He stopped right under Zacchaeus's tree!
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ብዙሓት ሰባት እናሰዓቡዎ ኣብ ጽርግያ ክኸይድ መጸ። ፍሽኽ እናበለ ምስ ኩሎም ይዛረብ ነበረ። ድሕሪኡ ኣብ ትሕቲ ኦም ዘኬዎስ ደው በለ!
(Pronunciation: ክኸይድ = kikeyd = walking, ፍሽኽ = fishikh = smiled, ደው በለ = dew bele = stopped)

Page 9

[PICTURE: Jesus looking up into the tree, smiling directly at Zacchaeus with warm loving eyes, everyone watching in surprise. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus looked up into the tree and smiled! He knew Zacchaeus was there! "Zacchaeus!" Jesus called. "Come down quickly! I want to visit your house today!"
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ናብ ላዕሊ ናብታ ኦም ረኣየ ፍሽኽ ድማ በለ! ዘኬዎስ ኣብኡ ምህላዉ ፈሊጡ ነይሩ! "ዘኬዎስ!" የሱስ ጸውዐ። "ቀልጢፍካ ውረድ! ሎሚ ገዛኻ ክበጽሕ ደልየ!"
(Pronunciation: ጸውዐ = ts'ew'e = called, ቀልጢፍካ = qelt'ifka = quickly, ውረድ = wured = come down)

Page 10

[PICTURE: Zacchaeus looking shocked and overjoyed up in the tree, pointing at himself in disbelief that Jesus knows his name. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Zacchaeus could not believe it! Jesus knew his name! Jesus wanted to come to HIS house! Nobody ever wanted to visit Zacchaeus before. His heart was so happy!
TIGRINYA:
ዘኬዎስ ክኣምን ኣይከኣለን! የሱስ ስሙ ፈሊጡዎ! የሱስ ናብ ገዝኡ ክመጽእ ደልዩ! ቅድም ክብ'ዚ ሓደ'ኳ ንዘኬዎስ ክበጽሕ ዝደለየ ኣይነበረን። ልቡ ኣዝዩ ተሓጒሱ!
(Pronunciation: ስሙ = simu = his name, ገዝኡ = gez'u = his house, ልቡ = libu = his heart)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Zacchaeus scrambling down the tree quickly with excitement, nearly falling in his hurry to reach Jesus. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Zacchaeus hurried down from the tree as fast as he could! He almost fell because he was so excited! He ran to Jesus with the biggest smile on his face.
TIGRINYA:
ዘኬዎስ ብዝተኻእሎ ቅልጣፈ ካብ ኦም ወረደ! ብኣዝዩ ሓጎስ ክወድቕ ዳርጋ ነይሩ! ኣዝያ ዓባይ ፍሽኽታ ኣብ ገጹ ሒዙ ናብ የሱስ ጎየ።
(Pronunciation: ወረደ = werede = came down, ሓጎስ = Hagos = excitement, ጎየ = goye = ran)

Page 12

[PICTURE: The crowd looking angry and whispering to each other, upset that Jesus would visit a sinful tax collector. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
But the crowd was angry! "Why would Jesus visit that terrible man?" they whispered. "He is a cheater and a sinner! He does not deserve Jesus's time!"
TIGRINYA:
ግን እቲ ህዝቢ ሓሪቑ! "የሱስ ስለምንታይ ነዚ ሕማቕ ሰብ ይበጽሖ?" ንሓድሕዶም ኣሕሹሹ። "ንሱ ጠባሪን ሓጢኣተኛን'ዩ! ግዜ የሱስ ኣይግብኦን!"
(Pronunciation: ሓሪቑ = Hariqu = angry, ኣሕሹሹ = aHshushu = whispered, ሓጢኣተኛ = Hat'i'atenya = sinner)

Page 13

ENGLISH:
But Jesus did not listen to the angry people. He went to Zacchaeus's house happily. Jesus knew that Zacchaeus needed love and kindness, not anger and hate.

(Pronunciation: ሓጎስ = Hagos = happily, ፍቕሪ = fiqri = love, ለውሃት = lewhat = kindness)
