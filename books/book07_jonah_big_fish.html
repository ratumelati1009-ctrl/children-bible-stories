Jonah and the Big Fish

ዮናስን ዓቢ ዓሳን

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 7

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 7

For all the children who learn that God's plans are always best.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ ናይ ኣምላኽ መደባት ኩሉ ግዜ ዝበለጹ ምዃኖም ዝመሃሩ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A kind-looking man named Jonah standing in an ancient village, wearing simple robes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
A long time ago, there was a man named Jonah. He loved God very much. God had a special job for Jonah to do.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ቅድሚ ግዜ ዮናስ ዝስሙ ሰብኣይ ነይሩ። ንኣምላኽ ኣዝዩ ይፈትዎ ነይሩ። ኣምላኽ ንዮናስ ፍሉይ ስራሕ ነይሩዎ።
(Pronunciation: ዮናስ = Yonas = Jonah, ኣምላኽ = Amlak = God, ፍሉይ = fluuy = special)

Page 2

[PICTURE: God's voice coming from the sky as bright golden light shining down on Jonah who looks up. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
God said to Jonah, "Go to the big city of Nineveh. Tell the people there to stop doing bad things." It was an important message.
TIGRINYA:
ኣምላኽ ንዮናስ "ናብ ዓባይ ከተማ ነነወ ኪድ። ነቶም ሰባት ሕማቕ ነገራት ንኽገድፉ ንገሮም" በሎ። ኣገዳሲ መልእኽቲ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ከተማ = ketema = city, ነነወ = Nenewe = Nineveh, መልእኽቲ = mel'ikhti = message)

Page 3

[PICTURE: Jonah looking scared and worried, shaking his head, turning away from the direction of Nineveh. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:

TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: ፈሪሑ = ferihu = afraid, ክሃድም = kihadm = to run away, ወሰነ = wesene = decided)

(Pronunciation: ወደብ = wedeb = port, መርከብ = merkeb = ship, ገንዘብ = genzeb = money)

(Pronunciation: ባሕሪ = bahri = sea, ደቀሰ = deqese = slept, ክሕባእ = kihibae = to hide)
