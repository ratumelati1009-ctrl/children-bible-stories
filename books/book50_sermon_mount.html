The crowds had been growing for weeks. Word spread across Galilee that a rabbi from Nazareth was healing every disease, casting out demons, and speaking with an authority no one had ever heard. People traveled from Syria, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, and beyond the Jordan to find Him. Now, seeing the multitudes, Jesus climbed a hillside, sat down — the posture of a rabbi about to deliver something significant — and opened His mouth to teach. What followed would become the most revolutionary speech in human history.
ህዝቢ ንሳምንታት ክበዝሕ ጸንሐ። ካብ ናዝሬት ዝመጸ ረቢ ንኹሉ ሕማም ከም ዝፍውስ ኣጋንንቲ ከም ዝሰጉግ ዝኾነ ሰብ ሰሚዕዎ ዘይፈልጥ ስልጣን ከም ዝዛረብ ዜና ኣብ ምሉእ ገሊላ ተዘርግሐ። ሰባት ካብ ሶርያ፡ ደቃጱሊስ፡ ኢየሩሳሌም፡ ማዕዶ ዮርዳኖስ ክረኽብዎ መጹ። ሕጂ ነቲ ብዙሕ ህዝቢ ርእዩ የሱስ ናብ ከረን ደየበ፡ ተቐመጠ — ኣገዳሲ ነገር ክምህር ዝቐረበ ረቢ ኣቀማምጣ — ኣፉ ኸፊቱ ክምህር ጀመረ። ዝስዕብ ዝነበረ ኣብ ታሪኽ ሰብ ኣዝዩ ሰውራዊ ዝኾነ ንግግር ክኸውን'ዩ ዝነበሮ።

"Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven." With these opening words, Jesus turned everything the world believed about success completely upside down. In that culture — and in ours — blessing meant wealth, health, power, and status. But Jesus declared that God's kingdom belongs to those who recognize their spiritual emptiness, who know they have nothing to offer God and desperately need Him. This isn't about material poverty — it's about the death of pride, the recognition that we are spiritually bankrupt without God.
"ብመንፈስ ድኻታት ብጹኣን እዮም ምኽንያቱ መንግስተ ሰማያት ናታቶም'ያ።" በዘን ናይ መኽፈቲ ቃላት የሱስ ዓለም ብዛዕባ ዓወት ዝኣመነቶ ኩሉ ነገር ፍጹም ገልበጦ። ኣብቲ ባህሊ — ኣብ ናትና'ውን — በረኸት ማለት ሃብቲ፡ ጥዕና፡ ስልጣንን ደረጃን'ዩ ዝነበረ። ግን የሱስ መንግስቲ ኣምላኽ ናይቶም መንፈሳዊ ባዶነቶም ዝኣፍልጡ ንኣምላኽ ዝህብዎ ዋላ ሓንቲ ከም ዘየብሎም ዝፈልጡ ብተስፋ ምቑራጽ ዘድልዮም ምዃና ኣወጀ። እዚ ብዛዕባ ናይ ንዋት ድኽነት ኣይኮነን — ብዛዕባ ሞት ትዕቢት'ዩ ብዘይ ኣምላኽ መንፈሳዊ ጥፉኣት ምዃንና ምእማን'ዩ።

Scene 3
[PICTURE: Jesus continuing to teach the Beatitudes. In the crowd, a mourning woman wipes tears, a gentle man helps a fallen child, and a person with a pure expression gazes upward. Each Beatitude is shown through the people listening. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted." Jesus declared that grief — over sin, over injustice, over brokenness — is not weakness but a doorway to God's comfort. "Blessed are the meek, for they will inherit the earth." Not the aggressive, not the powerful who seize what they want, but the meek — those who have strength under control, who refuse to use their power to dominate others. The world says the aggressive inherit the earth. Jesus says the exact opposite. "Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled." God satisfies those who crave justice and holiness the way a starving person craves bread.
"ዝሓዝኑ ብጹኣን'ዮም ምኽንያቱ ክጸናንዑ'ዮም።" የሱስ ሓዘን — ብሰንኪ ሓጢኣት ብዘይፍትሓውነት ብስብራት — ድኻም ዘይኮነ ናብ ምጽንናዕ ኣምላኽ ዝመርሕ ማዕጾ ምዃኑ ኣወጀ። "ለዋሃት ብጹኣን'ዮም ምኽንያቱ ንምድሪ ክወርስዋ'ዮም።" ነቶም ጨካናት ስልጣን ሒዞም ዝደለይዎ ዝውስዱ ዘይኮነ ለዋሃት — ሓይሎም ብቑጽጽር ዝሕዙ ንኻልኦት ክገዝኡ ስልጣኖም ክጥቀሙ ዝኣብዩ'ዮም። ዓለም ጨካናት ንምድሪ ይወርስዋ ትብል። የሱስ ፍጹም ኣንጻሩ ይብል። "ንጽድቂ ዝጠምዩን ዝጸምኡን ብጹኣን'ዮም ምኽንያቱ ክጸግቡ'ዮም።" ኣምላኽ ነቶም ከም ዝጠመየ ሰብ ንእንጀራ ዝብህጎ ንፍትሕን ቅድስናን ዝብህጉ የጸግቦም።
(Zihaznu bitsuan'yom mikiniyatu kitsenani'u'yom. Yesus hazen — bisenki hati'at bizeyifitihawnet bisibrat — dikhham zeyikone nab mitsinina'e Amlak zimirih ma'itsho mkhwanu aweje. Lewahat bitsuan'yom mikiniyatu nimidri kiwerswa'yom. Netom chekanat siltan hizom zideleyw ziwisdu zeyikone lewahat — haylom biqutstsir zihizu nikhal'ot kigez'u siltanom kitiqemu zi'abiyu'yom. "Blessed are the merciful, for they will be shown mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God. Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God." Jesus valued mercy over vengeance, purity of motive over outward performance, and active peacemaking over passive neutrality. Then came the most shocking declaration: "Blessed are those who are persecuted for righteousness' sake." Being hated for following Jesus is not a curse — it's a confirmation that you belong to His kingdom. The world's rejection is heaven's approval.
"ምሕረት ዝገብሩ ብጹኣን'ዮም ምኽንያቱ ምሕረት ክረኽቡ'ዮም። ብልቢ ንጹሃት ብጹኣን'ዮም ምኽንያቱ ንኣምላኽ ክርእይዎ'ዮም። ሰላም ዝገብሩ ብጹኣን'ዮም ምኽንያቱ ደቂ ኣምላኽ ክበሃሉ'ዮም።" የሱስ ካብ ሕነ ምሕረት ካብ ግዳማዊ ፍጻመ ንጽህና ዕላማ ካብ ተዃል ገለልተኝነት ንጡፍ ምስራሕ ሰላም ኣኽበረ። ድሕሪኡ እቲ ኣዝዩ ዘደንጽ ኣዋጅ መጸ፡ "ስለ ጽድቂ ዝስደዱ ብጹኣን'ዮም።" ንየሱስ ብምስዓብ ምጽላእ ርግማን ኣይኮነን — ናይ መንግስቱ ምዃንካ ምርግጋጽ'ዩ። ዓለም ምንጻግ ናይ ሰማይ ምቕባል'ዩ።

Yesus kab hine mihret kab gidamawi fitsame nitsihina ilama kab tekwal gelelitenynet nituf misrah selam akhibere. Dihri'u iti aziyu zidenits awaj metse, "Sile tsidqi zisdedu bitsuan'yom." Scene 5
[PICTURE: Jesus holding up a handful of salt crystals in one hand and gesturing toward a clay oil lamp glowing brightly on a stand with the other. The crowd watches intently. Behind them, a dark village is visible, waiting for light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"You are the salt of the earth," Jesus declared. "But if the salt loses its saltiness, how can it be made salty again? It is no longer good for anything except to be thrown out." Salt preserves and adds flavor. Believers are meant to slow the decay of the world around them, to bring out the true flavor of life. Then Jesus added: "You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden." We are not called to blend in with darkness but to shine so distinctly that people cannot ignore the difference. A faith that nobody notices has lost its purpose.
"ንስኻትኩም ጨው ምድሪ ኢኹም" ኢሉ የሱስ ኣወጀ። "ግን ጨው ጨውነቱ እንተ ኣጥፊኡ ብኸመይ ክጨውን ይኽእል? ንዋላ ሓንቲ ዘይብቑዕ ደኣ ክድርበ ናብ ደገ ጥራይ'ዩ።" ጨው ይዕቅብን ጣዕሚ ይህብን። ኣመንቲ ኣብ ከባቢኦም ንዘሎ ብልሽውና ከደንጉይዎ ናይ ሂወት ሓቀኛ ጣዕሚ ክህቡ'ዮም ዝተዓደሙ። ድሕሪኡ የሱስ ወሰኸ፡ "ንስኻትኩም ብርሃን ዓለም ኢኹም። ኣብ ከረን ዝተሃንጸት ከተማ ክትሕባእ ኣይትኽእልን።" ምስ ጸልማት ክንሕወስ ዘይኮነ ሰባት ነቲ ፍልልይ ከሸልብይዎ ዘይክእሉ ክሳብ ዝኾነ ብንጹር ክንበርህ ኢና ዝተጸዋዕና። ዝኾነ ሰብ ዘየስተውዓላ እምነት ዕላማኣ ስኢና'ያ።

"Do not think that I have come to abolish the Law or the Prophets," Jesus said with absolute authority. "I have not come to abolish them but to fulfill them." This was critical. Jesus wasn't erasing the Old Testament or starting from scratch. He was completing what it always pointed toward. Every prophecy, every sacrificial lamb, every commandment — all of it found its ultimate meaning in Him. He raised the standard, not lowered it. "Until heaven and earth disappear, not the smallest letter will disappear from the Law until everything is accomplished."
"ሕጊ ወይ ነቢያት ክፍርስ ከም ዝመጻእኩ ኣይትሕሰቡ" የሱስ ብፍጹም ስልጣን በለ። "ክፍርሶም ዘይኮነ ክፍጽሞም'የ ዝመጻእኩ።" እዚ ወሳኒ ነበረ። የሱስ ብሉይ ኪዳን ይድምስስ ወይ ካብ ባዶ ይጅምር ኣይነበረን። ካብ ቀደም ዘመልከቶ ክፍጽም'ዩ ዝነበረ። ኩሉ ትንቢት ኩሉ ናይ መስዋእቲ ገንሸል ኩሉ ትእዛዝ — ኩሉ ናይ መወዳእታ ትርጉሙ ኣብኡ'ዩ ዝረኽቦ። ንደረጃ ኣዕበዮ'ምበር ኣይተንከዮን። "ሰማይን ምድርን ክሳብ ዝሓልፍ ኩሉ ክሳብ ዝፍጸም ካብ ሕጊ እቲ ዝነኣሰ ፊደል ኣይሓልፍን'ዩ።"

Scene 7
[PICTURE: Jesus teaching with intensity, pointing to His heart. Behind Him, a visual split — on one side, a man outwardly calm but with a dark storm cloud forming inside his chest representing hidden anger; on the other side, two brothers embracing in reconciliation near a temple altar. "You have heard that it was said, 'You shall not murder.' But I tell you that anyone who is angry with a brother or sister will be subject to judgment." The crowd must have gasped. Jesus was saying that the commandment against murder isn't just about physical violence — it reaches into the heart. Anger, contempt, hateful words — these are murder of the soul. Calling someone worthless, nursing a grudge, refusing to reconcile — these poison us from within. Jesus demanded that before you even offer worship to God, if someone has something against you, go and make it right first. Relationships matter more than rituals.

Moral of the Story / ትምህርቲ ዛንታ

Jesus' kingdom operates on completely different values than the world. The world says "blessed are the powerful, the proud, the aggressive." Jesus says "blessed are the poor in spirit, the meek, the peacemakers." True righteousness is a matter of the heart, not just outward behavior — anger is heart-murder, lust is heart-adultery, and religious performance without genuine love for God is worthless. The Christian life is radical, countercultural, and impossible without God's help. We are called to be different — salt and light — not to blend in with the darkness around us. We are called to love enemies, forgive freely, trust God with tomorrow, and build our lives on obedience, not just knowledge. Hearing God's word without obeying it is building on sand — it may look impressive for a time, but it will not survive life's storms.

Discussion Questions / ሕቶታት ምይይጥ

1. The Beatitudes reverse the world's definition of success and blessing. Which Beatitude challenges you the most personally? What would it look like to actually live by "blessed are the meek" or "blessed are the peacemakers" in your school, friend group, or online interactions?

1. ብጹኣንነታት ናይ ዓለም ኣበሃህላ ዓወትን በረኸትን ይገልብጡ። ብውልቂ ኣየነይቲ ብጹኣንነት ኣዝያ ትብድሃካ? ኣብ ቤት ትምህርትኻ ኣብ ጉጅለ ኣዕሩኽትኻ ወይ ኣብ ኦንላይን "ለዋሃት ብጹኣን" ወይ "ሰላም ዝገብሩ ብጹኣን" ብሓቂ ምንባር ከመይ ምመሰለ?

2. Jesus taught that anger is "murder of the heart" and lust is "adultery of the heart." Why does God care so much about what happens internally, even when no one else sees it? How does this change the way you think about your thought life, your social media consumption, or your private habits?

2. የሱስ ቁጥዓ "ናይ ልቢ ቅትለት" ፍትወት ድማ "ናይ ልቢ ዝሙት" ከም ዝኾነ ኣስተምሃረ። ማንም ከይርእየካ ከሎ'ውን ኣምላኽ ብውሽጥኻ ንዝኸውን ስለምንታይ'ዩ ክንዲ'ዚ ዝግደስ? እዚ ብዛዕባ ናይ ሓሳብ ሂወትካ ናይ ማሕበራዊ ሚዲያ ምጥቃምካ ወይ ናይ ብሕቲ ልምድታትካ ንሓሳብካ ብኸመይ ይቕይሮ?

3. Jesus commands us to "love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you." Think of someone who has hurt you, excluded you, or spoken against you. What would it actually look like to pray for them and genuinely desire their good? Why is this so difficult, and why does Jesus consider it so important?

3. የሱስ "ንጸላእትኹም ፍቶ ንዝሰድዱኹም ጸልዩሎም" ኢሉ ይእዝዝ። ዝሃሰየካ ዝነጸገካ ወይ ኣንጻርካ ዝተዛረበ ሰብ ሕሰብ። ንኣኣቶም ምጽላይን ጽቡቕ ምድላይን ብሓቂ ከመይ ምመሰለ? ስለምንታይ ኣዝዩ ይኸብድ ስለምንታይ'ከ የሱስ ኣዝዩ ኣገዳሲ ገይሩ ይርእዮ?

4. Jesus warns against practicing righteousness "to be seen by others." In an age of social media, where everything is performed for an audience, how do you guard against turning your faith into a performance? 4. የሱስ ጽድቂ "ሰባት ክርእይኹም" ምግባር ኣጠንቂቑ። ኩሉ ነገር ንተዓዛቢ ኣብ ዝግበረሉ ናይ ማሕበራዊ ሚዲያ ዘመን እምነትካ ናብ ፍጻመ ከይትቕይር ብኸመይ ትሕሊ? ሓቀኛ ብሕታዊ ንኣምላኽ ተወፋይነት ንዓኻ ከመይ ይመስል? ኣብ መንጎ ብሓቂ እምነትካ ምክፋልን ምትርኣይን ፍልልይ ብኸመይ ትፈልጥ?

5. The parable of the wise and foolish builders teaches that hearing God's word without obeying is like building on sand. What would it look like to move from merely hearing to actively building on the rock of obedience?
