The Pharisees were furious. Jesus — a rabbi who claimed to speak for God — was eating with tax collectors and sinners. In their world, sharing a meal meant acceptance, endorsement, even intimacy. "This man welcomes sinners and eats with them," they muttered with contempt. They believed holiness meant separation from impure people. Jesus heard their grumbling and responded not with a theological argument, but with three stories. The third — the longest and most devastating — would expose that the Pharisees themselves were just as lost as the sinners they despised. It is known as the Parable of the Prodigal Son, but it should be called the Parable of Two Lost Sons.

"There was a man who had two sons," Jesus began. The younger son approached his father with a request that would have shocked every listener: "Father, give me my share of the estate." In that culture, this was unspeakable. The inheritance was only distributed after the father's death. By asking for it now, the younger son was essentially saying, "I wish you were dead. You are worth more to me as a corpse than as a living father. Give me what I'm owed and get out of my way." Every person in Jesus' audience would have expected the father to strike his son, disown him, or drive him away in fury. But the father did something no one anticipated.
"ክልተ ኣወዳት ዝነበርዎ ሓደ ሰብ ነበረ" ኢሉ የሱስ ጀመረ። እቲ ንእሽቶ ወዲ ንኣቦኡ ንኹሉ ሰማዒ ዘስንብድ ሕቶ ሒዙ ቀረበ፡ "ኣቦ ካብ ንብረት ዝግባኣኒ ክፍለይ ሃበኒ።" ኣብቲ ባህሊ እዚ ዘይብሃል'ዩ ነበረ። ውርሻ ድሕሪ ሞት ኣቦ ጥራይ'ዩ ዝዕደል። ሕጂ ብምሕታቱ እቲ ንእሽቶ ወዲ ብሓቂ "ሞይትካ እንተ ትኸውን ምደለኹ። ከም ህያው ኣቦ ዘይኮነ ከም ሬሳ ዝያዳ ትጠቕመኒ። ዝግባኣኒ ሃበኒ ካብ መንገደይ ውጻእ" ይብል ነበረ። ኣብ ተዓዘብቲ የሱስ ዝነበረ ኩሉ ሰብ ኣቦ ንወዱ ክወቕዖ ከባርሮ ወይ ብቑጥዓ ክሰጎ ተጸቢዩ ነይሩ ክኸውን ኣለዎ። ግን ኣቦ ዝኾነ ሰብ ዘይተጸበዮ ገበረ።

The father divided his property between them. He gave the younger son his share — roughly one-third of everything he owned. No argument. No lecture. No conditions. This reveals something profound about the heart of God: He respects human freedom, even when that freedom is used to reject Him. God does not force anyone to stay. He does not manipulate, guilt, or coerce. Love, by its very nature, must allow the beloved to walk away. A love that imprisons is not love at all — it is control. The father's silence was not indifference; it was the most painful expression of love imaginable. He released his son into the consequences of his own choices, knowing full well what was coming.

"Not long after that, the younger son got together all he had, set off for a distant country." He didn't just leave — he went as far as possible. The "distant country" represents more than geography; it represents the human desire to escape accountability, to be free from any authority, to write our own rules. Every teenager who has felt suffocated by family expectations understands this impulse. The allure is intoxicating: no curfew, no rules, no one watching, no one judging. Total autonomy. But what feels like freedom is actually the beginning of a prison. Autonomy without wisdom is simply a longer leash attached to self-destruction.
"ድሕሪ ቁሩብ ግዜ እቲ ንእሽቶ ወዲ ኩሉ ዝነበሮ ኣኪቡ ናብ ርሑቕ ሃገር ተጓዕዘ።" ክኸይድ ጥራይ ዘይኮነ ክሳብ ዝኸኣለ ርሒቑ ከደ። "ርሑቕ ሃገር" ካብ ጂኦግራፊ ንላዕሊ ትውክል'ያ፣ ካብ ሓላፍነት ምህዳም ካብ ዝኾነ ስልጣን ናጻ ምዃን ናይ ባዕልና ሕጊ ምጽሓፍ ዝብል ናይ ሰብ ድሌት ትውክል'ያ። ብትጽቢታት ስድራ ዝተሓነቐ ዝተሰምዖ ኩሉ መንእሰይ ነዚ ድርኺት'ዚ ይርድኦ'ዩ። ስሕበቱ ኣዝዩ ሓያል'ዩ፡ ዋላ ሓደ ናይ ምምላስ ግዜ የለን ሕጊ የለን ዝዕዘብ የለን ዝፈርድ የለን። ምሉእ ርእሰ-ምሕደራ። ግን ከም ናጽነት ዝስምዖ ብሓቂ ናይ ቤት ማእሰርቲ መጀመርታ'ዩ። ብዘይ ጥበብ ርእሰ-ምሕደራ ኣብ ርእሰ-ምጥፋእ ዝተኣሰረ ነዊሕ ገመድ ጥራይ'ዩ።

"There he squandered his wealth in wild living." The Greek word for "squandered" means to scatter, to throw to the wind. He didn't invest poorly or make a few bad decisions — he deliberately, recklessly poured out everything his father had spent a lifetime building. Wild living: parties, excess, people who loved his money but cared nothing for his soul. This is the seduction of sin — it never advertises its end. Addiction doesn't show you the hospital bed. Lust doesn't show you the shattered relationships. Greed doesn't show you the emptiness at the top. Sin always promises freedom and delivers chains. Every "friend" at the party was actually a vulture, waiting for the money to run out.
"ኣብኡ ሃብቱ ብዘይ ስርዓት ኣነባብራ በተኖ።" እቲ ናይ ግሪኽ ቃል "በተነ" ማለት ምብታን ንንፋስ ምድርባይ ማለት'ዩ። ብኽፉእ ኣይወፈረን ወይ ቁሩብ ሕማቕ ውሳኔታት ኣይወሰደን — ኣቦኡ ምሉእ ዕድሜኡ ክሃንጾ ዝጸንሐ ኩሉ ብፍላጥ ብዘይ ጥንቃቐ ኣፍሰሶ። ዘይስሩዕ ኣነባብራ፡ ባዕልታት ምልዕዋል ገንዘቡ ዝፈትዉ ግን ንነፍሱ ዋላ ሓንቲ ዘይግደሱ ሰባት። እዚ ናይ ሓጢኣት ምስሓብ'ዩ — ፍጻሜኣ ፈጺማ ኣይተውስውስን'ያ። ሱስ ነቲ ናይ ሆስፒታል ዓራት ኣይተርእየካን'ያ። ፍትወት ነቶም ዝተሰባበሩ ርክባት ኣይተርእየካን'ያ። ስስዐ ነቲ ኣብ ላዕሊ ዘሎ ባዶነት ኣይተርእየካን'ያ። ሓጢኣት ኩሉ ግዜ ናጽነት ተተስፍው ሰንሰለት ተረክብ። ኣብ ባዕልቲ ዝነበረ ኩሉ "ዓርኪ" ብሓቂ ገንዘብ ክውዳእ ዝጽበ ኣሞራ'ዩ ነይሩ።

"After he had spent everything, there was a severe famine in that whole country, and he began to be in need." Notice the sequence: first he emptied himself, then the external crisis hit. When you have destroyed your inner resources — your character, your relationships, your integrity — you have nothing left when life's inevitable storms arrive. The famine didn't cause his problem; it exposed it. He had been spiritually bankrupt long before he became financially bankrupt. And where were all those "friends" now? Gone. Every single one. The people who drank his wine and praised his generosity vanished the moment his usefulness ended. Sin always leaves you alone.

"When he came to his senses, he said, 'How many of my father's hired servants have food to spare, and here I am starving to death!'" This is the turning point — "he came to his senses." The original Greek literally means "he came to himself." Sin takes us out of ourselves; repentance is coming home to who we truly are. He finally saw reality clearly: even the lowest servants in his father's house lived better than this. This moment of honest self-assessment is where all genuine transformation begins. Not at an emotional altar call, not under pressure from others, but in the quiet devastation of hitting bottom and finally admitting the truth: "I did this to myself. The life I chose is destroying me."

"I will set out and go back to my father and say to him: Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you. I am no longer worthy to be called your son; make me like one of your hired servants." Notice what he's doing — rehearsing a transaction. He's preparing a business proposal: "I'll work for my food. I'll earn my way back. Just give me a job." This reveals he still doesn't understand his father's heart. He thinks love must be earned, that acceptance requires performance, that he must prove himself worthy before receiving anything. He's planning to negotiate, not to simply receive grace. Most of us approach God exactly this way — "I'll be better, I'll try harder, I'll make it up to You." But grace cannot be earned. That's what makes it grace.

"He was filled with compassion for him; he ran to his son, threw his arms around him and kissed him." In ancient Middle Eastern culture, a dignified patriarch never ran. Running required hiking up your robes, exposing your legs — it was humiliating for an elderly man of status. But this father didn't care about his dignity. His love for his son obliterated every social convention. He ran. The word "compassion" in Greek means his guts churned — it's visceral, physical love that cannot contain itself. The father is waiting. And when He sees you coming — no matter how far you've wandered or how long you've pretended — He will run.

The Moral / ትምህርቲ

God's love is not transactional — it cannot be earned through performance or lost through failure. There are two ways to be lost: through open rebellion (rejecting God entirely) and through religious performance (serving God for reward rather than love). Both the rebel and the religious person miss the father's heart. True repentance is not proving yourself worthy — it is simply coming home, trusting that the Father's love will meet you before you arrive. Grace scandalizes the self-righteous because it is given freely to the undeserving. God's feast is for the broken and honest, not the polished and proud. The Father is always watching, always waiting, and always ready to run.

Discussion Questions / ሕቶታት ምይይጥ

1. The younger son's request for his inheritance was culturally equivalent to wishing his father dead. Have you ever treated God or your parents as merely a source of resources — wanting what they provide while rejecting the relationship itself? What does it look like to value people for who they are rather than what they give you?

1. ናይ ንእሽቶ ወዲ ናይ ውርሻ ሕቶ ባህላዊ ንኣቦኡ ሞይቱ ምትምናይ ማዕረ'ዩ ነይሩ። ንኣምላኽ ወይ ንወለድኻ ከም ናይ ጸጋ ምንጪ ጥራይ — ዝህብዎ ትደሊ ግን ርክብ ባዕሉ ትነጽግ ገይርካ ኣለኻ'ዶ? ሰባት ብዝህብኻ ዘይኮነ ብዝኾንዎ ምቕባል ከመይ ይመስል?

2. The older brother served his father for years but described it as "slaving." He kept score and felt entitled to reward. Examine your own faith honestly: do you serve God out of genuine love, or are you keeping a mental tally of what you think God owes you? How can you tell the difference between serving from love and serving for reward?

2. ዓቢ ሓው ንዓመታት ንኣቦኡ ኣገልጊሉ ግን "ባርያ ኮይነ" ኢሉ ገለጾ። ነጥቢ ይሕዝ ዓስቢ ይግብኦ ኢሉ ይኣምን ነይሩ። ናይ ባዕልኻ እምነት ብቕንዕና መርምር፡ ንኣምላኽ ብሓቀኛ ፍቕሪ ተገልግሎ ወይስ ኣምላኽ ዝኸፍለካ ዝመስለካ ናይ ኣእምሮ ሕሳብ ትሕዝ? ኣብ መንጎ ብፍቕሪ ምግልጋልን ንዓስቢ ምግልጋልን ፍልልይ ብኸመይ ትፈልጥ?

3. The father ran to his son — something undignified and culturally shameful for an elderly patriarch. What does it tell you about God's character that He is willing to sacrifice His own dignity to reach you? Have you ever felt that God's grace was "too good to be true"? Why is it so hard for us to accept unconditional love?

3. ኣቦ ናብ ወዱ ጎየየ — ንኣረጋዊ ኣቦ ክብሪ ዘይብሉ ባህላዊ ውርደት ዝኾነ ተግባር። ኣምላኽ ክረኽበካ ናይ ባዕሉ ክብሪ ክስውእ ፍቓደኛ ምዃኑ ብዛዕባ ባህሪ ኣምላኽ እንታይ ይነግረካ? ጸጋ ኣምላኽ "ሓቂ ክኸውን ኣዝዩ ጽቡቕ" ኮይኑ ተሰሚዑካ ይፈልጥ'ዶ? ስለምንታይ'ዩ ብዘይ ቅድመ-ኩነት ፍቕሪ ምቕባል ክንድኡ ዝኸብደና?

4. Jesus deliberately left the parable unfinished — we never learn whether the older brother went inside to the feast. Why do you think Jesus ended it this way? If you were the older brother, what would it take for you to swallow your pride and join the celebration for someone you felt didn't deserve it? Think of a real situation where someone received grace that felt unfair to you.

4. የሱስ ብዕላማ ንምስላ ዘይተወድአ ገደፎ — ዓቢ ሓው ናብ ባዕልቲ ኣትዩ ዶ ኣይኣተወን ፈጺምና ኣይንፈልጥን። የሱስ ስለምንታይ ከምዚ ኣቋሪጹ ዝወድኦ ይመስለካ? ዓቢ ሓው እንተ ትኸውን ትዕቢትካ ውሒጥካ ዘይግብኦ ዝመሰለካ ሰብ ናይ ምሕጐስ በዓል ክትጽንበር እንታይ ክኸውን ነይሩዎ? ንዓኻ ዘይፍትሓዊ ዝኾነ ጸጋ ሓደ ሰብ ዝረኸበሉ ሓቀኛ ኩነታት ሕሰብ።

5. The parable shows two ways to be far from God: rebellion and religion. Which tendency do you lean toward — running away from God's rules entirely, or trying to earn His love through perfect behavior? How does understanding that grace is free (not earned) change the way you relate to God, to failure, and to other people who mess up?

5. ምስላ ካብ ኣምላኽ ርሒቕካ ክልተ መንገዲ ተርኢ፡ ዕልወትን ሃይማኖትን። ናብ ኣየናይ ዝንባለ ኣለካ — ካብ ሕጊ ኣምላኽ ብምሉኡ ምሃዳም ወይ ብፍጹም ባህሪ ፍቕሩ ክትረክብ ምፍታን? ጸጋ ናጻ (ዘይስረሓሉ) ምዃኑ ምርዳእ ምስ ኣምላኽ ምስ ውድቀት ምስ ዝጋገዩ ካልኦት ሰባት ርክብካ ብኸመይ ይቕይሮ?

Prayer / ጸሎት

Thank You that You are the kind of Father who sacrifices His dignity to reach His children. We come home to You today — not with a polished speech, but with empty hands and honest hearts. In Jesus' name, Amen.
