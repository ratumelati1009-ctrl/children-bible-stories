Creation - God Made Everything Beautiful

ፍጥረት - ኣምላኽ ኩሉ ጽቡቕ ፈጢሩ

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 13

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 13

For all the children who see God's beauty in everything around them.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ ኣብ ኩሉ ኣብ ዙርያኦም ዘሎ ነገራት ጽባቐ ኣምላኽ ዝርእዩ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: Complete darkness everywhere, nothing at all, just empty black space with no light or shapes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
In the very beginning, before anything existed, there was only darkness. No light, no land, no animals, no people. Nothing at all! But God was there.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ፈለማ ፈለማ ቅድሚ ዝኾነ ነገር ምህላው ጸልማት ጥራይ ነይሩ። ብርሃን የለ ምድሪ የለ እንስሳ የለ ሰባት የለ። ምንም ኣይነበረን! ግን ኣምላኽ ነይሩ።
(Pronunciation: ፈለማ = felema = beginning, ጸልማት = ts'elmat = darkness, ኣምላኽ = Amlak = God)

Page 2

[PICTURE: A burst of brilliant golden light breaking through the darkness, God's power creating light for the first time. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
On Day One, God said, "Let there be light!" And suddenly, beautiful bright light appeared! God separated the light from the darkness. He called them day and night.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ቀዳማይ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ "ብርሃን ይኹን!" በለ። ብድንገት ጽቡቕ ደሚቕ ብርሃን ተራእየ! ኣምላኽ ንብርሃን ካብ ጸልማት ፈለዮ። መዓልትን ለይትን ኢሉ ሰመዮም።
(Pronunciation: ብርሃን = birhan = light, መዓልቲ = me'alti = day, ለይቲ = leyti = night)

Page 3

[PICTURE: Beautiful blue sky forming above with white fluffy clouds, water below reflecting the sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
On Day Two, God made the sky! He put water above and water below. The sky stretched out blue and beautiful with fluffy white clouds. How wonderful!
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ካልኣይ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ ሰማይ ፈጠረ! ማይ ኣብ ላዕልን ማይ ኣብ ታሕትን ኣቐመጠ። ሰማይ ሰማያዊን ጽቡቕን ኮይኑ ምስ ጻዕዳ ደመናታት ተዘርገሐ። ክንደይ ደስ ዘብል!
(Pronunciation: ሰማይ = semay = sky, ማይ = may = water, ደመናታት = dementat = clouds)

Page 4

[PICTURE: Dry land rising from the waters, mountains forming, green grass and colorful flowers sprouting everywhere. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
On Day Three, God said, "Let dry land appear!" Mountains rose up and oceans filled in. Then God made grass, flowers, and trees with yummy fruits!
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ሳልሳይ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ "ንቑጽ መሬት ይረአ!" በለ። ኣኽራን ተለዓሉ ውቅያኖሳት ድማ ተመልኡ። ድሕሪኡ ኣምላኽ ሳዕሪ ዕንባባታትን ኣእዋም ምስ ጥዑም ፍረታትን ፈጠረ!
(Pronunciation: መሬት = meret = land, ኣኽራን = akran = mountains, ኣእዋም = a'iwam = trees)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Beautiful garden with many kinds of trees bearing different fruits - mangoes, bananas, oranges - lush green everywhere. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
God made so many amazing plants! Tall palm trees, tiny flowers, big bushes with berries. Every plant had seeds to grow more plants. God said, "It is good!"
TIGRINYA:
ኣምላኽ ብዙሓት ዘደንቑ ተኽልታት ፈጠረ! ነዊሓት ኣእዋም ተምሪ ንኣሽቱ ዕንባባታት ዓበይቲ ጉንዲታት ምስ ፍረታት። ኩሉ ተኽሊ ዝያዳ ንምብቋል ዘርኢ ነበሮ። ኣምላኽ "ጽቡቕ'ዩ!" በለ።
(Pronunciation: ተኽልታት = tekhltat = plants, ዕንባባታት = 'inbababat = flowers, ዘርኢ = zer'i = seeds)

Page 6

[PICTURE: A brilliant golden sun shining in the sky, a silvery moon, and twinkling stars scattered across a deep blue sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
On Day Four, God made the sun to shine in the daytime! He made the moon and stars to sparkle at night. They helped mark days, months, and seasons.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ራብዓይ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ ንመዓልቲ ክትበርህ ጸሓይ ፈጠረ! ንለይቲ ክጻባረቑ ወርሕን ኮኾብትን ፈጠረ። መዓልታት ኣዋርሕን ወቕትታትን ንኽፍለጡ ሓገዙ።
(Pronunciation: ጸሓይ = ts'eHay = sun, ወርሒ = werHi = moon, ኮኾብቲ = koKobti = stars)

Page 7

[PICTURE: Colorful fish swimming in blue ocean waters - big fish, tiny fish, dolphins jumping, coral reefs below. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
On Day Five, God filled the oceans with fish! Big whales, tiny seahorses, colorful fish with stripes and spots. The waters were full of life!
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ሓምሻይ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ ነቲ ውቅያኖሳት ብዓሳ መልኦ! ዓበይቲ ዓሳ ንኣሽቱ ዓሳ ሕብራውያን ዓሳታት ምስ ሕንጻጽን ነጠብጣብን። ማያት ብህይወት መልኡ!
(Pronunciation: ዓሳ = 'asa = fish, ውቅያኖስ = wiqyanos = ocean, ህይወት = hiyiwet = life)

[PICTURE: Many beautiful birds flying in the sky - eagles, parrots, hummingbirds, flamingos, all different colors and sizes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:

(Pronunciation: ኣዕዋፍ = a'iwaf = birds, ንስሪ = nisri = eagle, ደርፊ = derfi = song)

Page 9

[PICTURE: God blessing the fish and birds, them multiplying and filling the seas and skies, vibrant and alive. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
God blessed the fish and birds. "Have many babies!" He said. "Fill the oceans and fill the skies!" God smiled at everything He made. It was good!
TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: ባረኾም = bareKom = blessed them, ውለዱ = wuledu = have babies, ፍሽኽ = fishikh = smiled)

(Pronunciation: እንስሳ = inssa = animals, ሓርማዝ = Harmaz = elephant, ቀጣን = qet'an = giraffe)
