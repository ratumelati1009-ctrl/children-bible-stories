Jesus Heals Blind Bartimaeus

የሱስ ንዓይነ-ስዉር ባርጢሜዎስ ፈዊሱ

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 19

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 19

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

For all the children who never give up calling on Jesus, no matter what.

ንኹሎም ብዘየገድስ ንየሱስ ምጽዋዕ ፈጺሞም ዘይሓድጉ ቆልዑ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A blind man named Bartimaeus sitting alone by the dusty roadside near Jericho, wearing old torn clothes, with a begging bowl. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Near the city of Jericho, there was a blind man named Bartimaeus. He could not see anything at all. Every day he sat by the road and begged for money to buy food.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ጥቓ ከተማ ያሪኮ ባርጢሜዎስ ዝስሙ ዓይነ-ስዉር ሰብ ነበረ። ፈጺሙ ሓንቲ ነገር ክርኢ ኣይክእልን ነበረ። ኩሉ መዓልቲ ኣብ ጥቓ ጽርግያ ኮፍ ኢሉ ምግቢ ዝገዝኣሉ ገንዘብ ይልምን ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ባርጢሜዎስ = Bart'ime'os = Bartimaeus, ዓይነ-ስዉር = 'ayne-siwur = blind, ይልምን = yilmin = begs)

Page 2

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus sitting in darkness (shown from his perspective), hearing sounds around him - birds, footsteps, voices. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Bartimaeus lived in darkness. He could not see the blue sky, the green trees, or the faces of people. He could only hear sounds. His life was very hard and lonely.
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ኣብ ጸልማት ይነብር ነበረ። ሰማያዊ ሰማይ ቆጽሊ ኦም ገጽ ሰባት ክርኢ ኣይክእልን ነበረ። ድምጽታት ክሰምዕ ጥራይ ይኽእል ነበረ። ህይወቱ ኣዝያ ኣጸጋሚትን በይናዊትን ነበረት።
(Pronunciation: ጸልማት = ts'elmat = darkness, ሰማይ = semay = sky, ድምጽታት = dimts'itat = sounds)

Page 3

[PICTURE: People walking past Bartimaeus ignoring him, some dropping small coins in his bowl, others looking away. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Most people walked right past Bartimaeus. Some gave him a small coin, but many ignored him completely. He wished so much that someone would help him see again.
TIGRINYA:
መብዛሕትኦም ሰባት ንባርጢሜዎስ ሓሊፎምዎ ይኸዱ ነበሩ። ገሊኦም ንእሽቶ ሳንቲም ይህቡዎ ግን ብዙሓት ፈጺሞም ይረስዕዎ ነበሩ። ሓደ ሰብ ከም ብሓድሽ ክርኢ ዝሕግዞ እንተ ዝረክብ ኣዝዩ ተመነየ።
(Pronunciation: ሓሊፎም = Halifom = walked past, ሳንቲም = santim = coin, ተመነየ = temenneye = wished)

Page 4

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus hearing a growing commotion, many footsteps and excited voices approaching on the road. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
One day, Bartimaeus heard something different. He heard many footsteps and excited voices coming closer. A big crowd was walking down the road! Something special was happening!
TIGRINYA:
ሓደ መዓልቲ ባርጢሜዎስ ዝተፈልየ ነገር ሰምዐ። ብዙሕ ስጉምትን ዝሕጎሱ ድምጽታትን እናቐረቡ ሰምዐ። ዓቢ ህዝቢ ኣብ ጽርግያ ይኸይድ ነበረ! ፍሉይ ነገር ይፍጸም ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: ስጉምቲ = sigumti = footsteps, ህዝቢ = hizbi = crowd, ፍሉይ = filuy = special)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus turning his head toward the noise, asking a person nearby what is happening, looking hopeful. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
"What is happening?" Bartimaeus asked someone near him. "Jesus of Nazareth is passing by!" they told him. Bartimaeus's heart began to beat fast. He had heard about Jesus!
TIGRINYA:
"እንታይ ይፍጸም ኣሎ?" ባርጢሜዎስ ኣብ ጥቓኡ ንዝነበረ ሰብ ሓተተ። "የሱስ ናዝሬታዊ ይሓልፍ ኣሎ!" ነገሩዎ። ልቢ ባርጢሜዎስ ብቕልጡፍ ክሃርም ጀመረ። ብዛዕባ የሱስ ሰሚዑ ነይሩ!
(Pronunciation: ይፍጸም = yifts'em = happening, ናዝሬታዊ = Nazretawi = of Nazareth, ይሓልፍ = yiHalif = passing by)

Page 6

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus opening his mouth wide and shouting with all his might, people around him looking startled. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Bartimaeus began to shout as loudly as he could! "Jesus! Son of David! Have mercy on me!" His voice rang out above all the noise of the crowd.
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ብዝኽእሎ ጽንዓት ክጭድር ጀመረ! "የሱስ! ወዲ ዳዊት! ራህርሃለይ!" ድምጹ ካብ ኩሉ ዕግርግር ህዝቢ ንላዕሊ ተሰምዐ።
(Pronunciation: ክጭድር = kich'idir = to shout, ወዲ ዳዊት = wedi Dawit = Son of David, ራህርሃለይ = raHrihaley = have mercy on me)

Page 7

[PICTURE: People in the crowd turning to Bartimaeus angrily, putting fingers to their lips, telling him to be quiet. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The people in the crowd turned to Bartimaeus. "Be quiet!" they scolded him. "Stop shouting! You are bothering everyone! Jesus is too busy for you!" But Bartimaeus did not listen to them.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ህዝቢ ዝነበሩ ሰባት ናብ ባርጢሜዎስ ተገልበጡ። "ስቕ በል!" ገንሑዎ። "ምጭዳር ግደፍ! ንኩሉ ሰብ ተሸግር ኣለኻ! የሱስ ንዓኻ ግዜ የብሉን!" ግን ባርጢሜዎስ ኣይሰምዖምን።
(Pronunciation: ስቕ በል = siq bel = be quiet, ገንሑዎ = genHuwo = scolded him, ምጭዳር = mich'idar = shouting)

Page 8

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus shouting even louder than before, face lifted up, desperate and determined, not giving up. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Bartimaeus shouted even LOUDER! "JESUS! SON OF DAVID! HAVE MERCY ON ME!" He was not going to give up. This might be his only chance to be healed! He believed Jesus could help him!
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ዝያዳ ብዝበለጸ ጨደረ! "የሱስ! ወዲ ዳዊት! ራህርሃለይ!" ክሓድግ ኣይኮነን። ክሓዊ ዝኽእለሉ እንኮ ዕድሉ ክኸውን ይኽእል! የሱስ ክሕግዞ ከም ዝኽእል ኣሚኑ!
(Pronunciation: ዝያዳ = ziyada = more, ጨደረ = chedere = shouted, ዕድል = 'idil = chance)

Page 9

[PICTURE: Jesus stopping in the middle of the road, turning His head toward Bartimaeus's voice, the crowd falling silent. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus stopped walking! He heard Bartimaeus's cries. "Bring him to me," Jesus said. The crowd became silent. Jesus wanted to see the blind man who was calling so loudly!
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ምኻድ ደው በለ! ጭድሪ ባርጢሜዎስ ሰምዐ። "ናባይ ኣምጽኡዎ" የሱስ በለ። እቲ ህዝቢ ስቕ በለ። የሱስ ነቲ ብጽንዓት ዝጽውዖ ዝነበረ ዓይነ-ስዉር ሰብ ክርእዮ ደለየ!
(Pronunciation: ደው በለ = dew bele = stopped, ጭድሪ = ch'idri = cries, ኣምጽኡዎ = amts'i'uwo = bring him)

Page 10

[PICTURE: People now helping Bartimaeus up, telling him with excitement that Jesus is calling him, Bartimaeus throwing off his cloak. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
"Cheer up! Get up! He is calling you!" people told Bartimaeus. Bartimaeus threw off his cloak, jumped up, and walked toward Jesus as fast as he could, even though he could not see!
TIGRINYA:
"ተሓጎስ! ተንስእ! ይጽውዓካ ኣሎ!" ሰባት ንባርጢሜዎስ ነገሩዎ። ባርጢሜዎስ ኣልባሱ ደርብዩ ዘሊሉ ክርኢ'ኳ ዘይክእል ብዝተኻእሎ ቅልጣፈ ናብ የሱስ ከደ!
(Pronunciation: ተሓጎስ = teHagos = cheer up, ተንስእ = tenis' = get up, ደርብዩ = derbyu = threw off)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus standing before Jesus, face turned upward with blind eyes, Jesus looking at him with compassion and love. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Bartimaeus stood before Jesus. He could feel Jesus close to him even though he could not see Him. Jesus looked at Bartimaeus with great love and compassion.
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ኣብ ቅድሚ የሱስ ደው በለ። ክርእዮ'ኳ ዘይክእል የሱስ ኣብ ጥቓኡ ምህላዉ ተሰሚዕዎ። የሱስ ንባርጢሜዎስ ብዓቢ ፍቕርን ርሕራሐን ጠመቶ።
(Pronunciation: ቅድሚ = qidmi = before, ርሕራሐ = riHraHe = compassion, ጠመቶ = t'emeto = looked at him)

Page 12

[PICTURE: Jesus leaning toward Bartimaeus, asking him a gentle question, the crowd watching quietly all around them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus asked Bartimaeus a kind question: "What do you want me to do for you?" Jesus already knew, but He wanted Bartimaeus to say it with his own mouth and believe.
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ንባርጢሜዎስ ለዋህ ሕቶ ሓተቶ: "እንታይ ክገብረልካ ትደሊ?" የሱስ ፈሊጡ ነይሩ ግን ባርጢሜዎስ ባዕሉ ብኣፉ ክዛረቦን ክኣምንን ደሊዩ።
(Pronunciation: ክገብረልካ = kigebrelka = to do for you, ትደሊ = tideli = you want, ክኣምን = ki'amin = to believe)

Page 13

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus speaking with passion and faith, hands reaching toward Jesus, saying he wants to see. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
"Rabbi! I want to see!" Bartimaeus said with all his heart. His voice was full of faith and hope. He believed with everything in him that Jesus could give him his sight!
TIGRINYA:
"ረቢ! ክርኢ ደልየ!" ባርጢሜዎስ ብምሉእ ልቡ በለ። ድምጹ ብእምነትን ተስፋን መሊኡ ነበረ። የሱስ ምርኣይ ክህቦ ከም ዝኽእል ብኩሉ ዘለዎ ኣሚኑ!
(Pronunciation: ረቢ = rebi = Rabbi/Teacher, ክርኢ = kir'i = to see, እምነት = imnet = faith)

Page 14

[PICTURE: Jesus smiling warmly and speaking words of healing to Bartimaeus, a golden light beginning to glow around Bartimaeus's eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus smiled and said, "Go! Your faith has healed you." At that very moment, something wonderful happened! Light began to fill Bartimaeus's eyes!
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ፍሽኽ ኢሉ "ኪድ! እምነትካ ኣሕዊያትካ" በለ። ኣብ'ቲ ህሞት ግሩም ነገር ተፈጸመ! ብርሃን ኣብ ኣዒንቲ ባርጢሜዎስ ክመልእ ጀመረ!
(Pronunciation: እምነትካ = imnetka = your faith, ኣሕዊያትካ = aHwiyatka = has healed you, ብርሃን = birhan = light)

Page 15

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus blinking his eyes open for the first time, seeing blurry shapes becoming clear, an expression of pure wonder. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Bartimaeus could SEE! For the first time in his life, the world was not dark anymore! Colors, shapes, faces - everything was clear and beautiful! He could see!
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ክርኢ ከኣለ! ንመጀመርታ ግዜ ኣብ ህይወቱ ዓለም ጸልማት ኣይነበረትን! ሕብርታት ቅርጽታት ገጻት - ኩሉ ንጹርን ጽቡቕን ነበረ! ክርኢ ይኽእል!
(Pronunciation: ክርኢ ከኣለ = kir'i ke'ale = could see, ሕብርታት = Hibritat = colors, ንጹር = nits'ur = clear)

Page 16

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus looking at Jesus's face for the first time, tears of joy streaming down, seeing the loving eyes of Jesus. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The very first thing Bartimaeus saw was Jesus's face! He saw Jesus's kind, loving eyes looking at him. Tears of joy ran down his face. "I can see You!" he cried happily.
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ንመጀመርታ ዝረኣዮ ገጽ የሱስ'ዩ ነይሩ! ለዋሃትን ፈቃራትን ኣዒንቲ የሱስ ክጥምቱዎ ረኣየ። ንብዓት ሓጎስ ካብ ገጹ ፈሰሰ። "ክርእየካ ይኽእል'የ!" ብሓጎስ ኣእወየ።
(Pronunciation: ገጽ = gets' = face, ንብዓት ሓጎስ = nib'at Hagos = tears of joy, ክርእየካ = kir'iyeka = I can see You)

Page 17

[PICTURE: Bartimaeus looking all around in wonder - at the blue sky, green trees, colorful flowers, seeing everything for the first time. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Bartimaeus looked all around in wonder! He saw the blue sky, the green trees, the bright sun. Everything was so beautiful! He had never seen any of these things before!
TIGRINYA:
ባርጢሜዎስ ብምግራም ናብ ኩሉ ኣቕጣጫ ጠመተ! ሰማያዊ ሰማይ ቆጽሊ ኦም ብሩህ ጸሓይ ረኣየ። ኩሉ ኣዝዩ ጽቡቕ ነበረ! ቅድሚ ሕጂ ካብ'ዚኦም ሓደ'ኳ ሪኢዩ ኣይፈልጥን ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: ምግራም = migiram = wonder, ሰማያዊ = semayawi = blue, ብሩህ = biruh = bright)

Page 18

[PICTURE: The crowd cheering and celebrating, people hugging Bartimaeus, everyone amazed at the miracle. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
