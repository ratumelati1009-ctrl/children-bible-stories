Scene 1
[PICTURE: A young woman named Mary in a simple home in Nazareth, praying peacefully. Soft blue and white clothing. Humble room with clay walls. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In the town of Nazareth, there lived a young woman named Mary. She loved God very much and prayed every day. She was kind and gentle.
ኣብ ከተማ ናዝሬት ማርያም ዝስማ መንእሰይ ሰበይቲ ትነብር ነበረት። ንኣምላኽ ኣዝያ ተፍቅሮ ነበረት ኩሉ መዓልቲ ድማ ትጽሊ ነበረት። ለዋህን ልዙብን ነበረት።
(Pronunciation: Ab ketema Nazret Maryam zsma men'esey sebeyti tneber neberet. N'Amlak azya tefqiro neberet kulu me'alti dma tsli neberet. Lewahon lzubon neberet.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: The angel Gabriel appearing to Mary in brilliant white light. Mary looking up in wonder and awe. Golden rays surrounding the angel. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One day, the angel Gabriel came to Mary! "Don't be afraid, Mary!" he said. "God has chosen you for something very special! You will have a baby boy — the Son of God!"
ሓደ መዓልቲ መልኣኽ ገብሪኤል ናብ ማርያም መጸ! "ኣይትፍርሒ ማርያም!" በላ። "ኣምላኽ ንፍሉይ ነገር ሓሪዩኪ! ወዲ ክትወልዲ ኢኺ — ወዲ ኣምላኽ!"
(Pronunciation: Hade me'alti mel'ak Gebri'el nab Maryam metse! "Aytfrhi Maryam!" bela. "Amlak nfluye neger hariyuki! Wedi ktweldi ikhi — Wedi Amlak!")
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Mary with her hands on her heart, accepting God's plan with humble joy. A gentle glow around her. Peaceful expression on her face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mary said, "I am God's servant. Let everything happen just as you said." She trusted God completely, even though she didn't understand everything.
ማርያም "ኣነ ኣገልጋሊት ኣምላኽ'የ። ከም ዝበልካዮ ኩሉ ይኹን" በለት። ኩሉ ነገር ዘይተረድኣ'ኳ ንኣምላኽ ምሉእ ብምሉእ ኣመነቶ።
(Pronunciation: Maryam "Ane agelgalit Amlak'ye. Kem zbelkayo kulu ykhun" belet. Kulu neger zeyterd'a'kwa n'Amlak mlu'e bmlu'e ameneto.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Joseph the carpenter in his workshop, looking worried. An angel appearing to him in a dream, reassuring him with warm light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Joseph was going to marry Mary. He was worried until an angel spoke to him in a dream. "Don't be afraid! The baby is from God. Name him Jesus — He will save His people!"
ዮሴፍ ንማርያም ከእትዋ ነበሮ። ተሻቒሉ ነበረ ክሳብ መልኣኽ ብሕልሚ ዝዛረቦ። "ኣይትፍራሕ! እዚ ቆልዓ ካብ ኣምላኽ'ዩ። የሱስ ኣውጽኣሉ — ንህዝቡ ከድሕን'ዩ!"
(Pronunciation: Yosef n'Maryam ke'etwa nebero. Teshaqilu nebere ksab mel'ak bhlmi zzarebo. "Aytfrah! Izi qol'a kab Amlak'yu. Yesus awts'a'alu — nhzbu kedhn'yu!")
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Mary and Joseph traveling on a road, Mary riding a donkey while Joseph walks beside her. Rolling hills and a distant town. Mary's belly showing she's expecting. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The king ordered everyone to go to their hometown to be counted. Mary and Joseph had to travel far — all the way to Bethlehem. Mary was almost ready to have her baby!
ንጉስ ኩሉ ሰብ ናብ ዓዱ ኪዱ ተቖጸሩ ኢሉ ኣዘዘ። ማርያምን ዮሴፍን ርሑቕ ክጎዓዙ ነበሮም — ክሳብ ቤትልሔም። ማርያም ክትወልድ ቀሪባ ነበረት!
(Pronunciation: Ngus kulu seb nab 'adu kidu teqotseru ilu azeze. Maryamn Yosefn rhuq kgo'azu neberom — ksab Betlhem. Maryam ktweld qeriba neberet!)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Mary and Joseph arriving at Bethlehem, a crowded busy town with many travelers. They look tired from the journey. Evening sky with orange and purple colors. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When they arrived in Bethlehem, the town was very crowded! People were everywhere. Mary was so tired from the long journey on the donkey.
ቤትልሔም ምስ በጽሑ እታ ከተማ ኣዝያ ተጻዒና ነበረት! ሰባት ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ ነበሩ። ማርያም ካብ ነዊሕ ጉዕዞ ኣብ ኣድጊ ኣዝያ ደኺማ ነበረት።
(Pronunciation: Betlhem ms betshu ita ketema azya tets'ina neberet! Sebat ab kulu bota neberu. Maryam kab newih gu'zo ab adgi azya dekhima neberet.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Joseph knocking on inn doors at night, being turned away by innkeepers shaking their heads. Mary waiting on the donkey behind him, looking weary. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Joseph knocked on many doors. "Is there room for us?" he asked. But everyone said, "No! We are full!" Joseph kept trying. He had to find a place for Mary.
ዮሴፍ ብዙሕ ኣፍደገ ኣንኳሕኳሕ ገበረ። "ቦታ ኣለኩም ድዩ?" ሓተተ። ግና ኩሎም "ኣይፋልን! መሊኢና!" በሉ። ዮሴፍ ቀጸለ። ንማርያም ቦታ ክረኽበላ ነበሮ።
(Pronunciation: Yosef bzuh afdege ankwahkwah gebere. "Bota alekum dyu?" hatete. Gna kulom "Ayfaln! Meli'ina!" belu. Yosef qetsele. N'Maryam bota krkhbela nebero.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: A kind innkeeper pointing to a humble stable/cave behind his inn. A warm glow comes from inside. Straw and animals visible. Joseph looks grateful. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Finally, a kind innkeeper said, "I have no rooms, but you can use my stable where the animals sleep." Joseph thanked him. At least it was warm and safe!
ኣብ መወዳእታ ሓደ ለዋህ ኣስተናጋዲ "ክፍሊ የብለይን ግና እንስሳታት ዝድቅሱሉ ማሕረስይ ክትጥቀሙ ትኽእሉ" በለ። ዮሴፍ ኣመስገኖ። ብውሑዱ ምውቕን ውሑስን ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Ab meweda'eta hade lewah astenagadi "Kifli yebleyn gna insesatat zdeqsulu mahresiy kttqemu tkhelu" bele. Yosef amesgeno. Bwhudu mwqon whuson nebere!)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Inside the humble stable with hay, a donkey, an ox, and sheep. Mary settling down on fresh straw. Joseph making it comfortable for her. Lantern light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Joseph made the stable as comfortable as he could. He laid fresh straw for Mary. The animals were quiet and gentle. It was humble but full of love.
ዮሴፍ ነቲ ማሕረስ ብዝኽእሎ ምቹእ ገበሮ። ንማርያም ሓድሽ ሳዕሪ ኣንጸፈላ። እንስሳታት ህዱኣትን ልዙባትን ነበሩ። ትሑት ግና ብፍቕሪ ዝመልአ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: Yosef neti mahres bzkhelo mchu'e gebero. N'Maryam hadsh sa'ri antsefela. Insesatat hdu'atoon lzubatn neberu. Thut gna bfqri zmle'a nebere.)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Baby Jesus born! Mary holding the newborn baby wrapped in cloths, her face glowing with pure love and joy. Soft divine golden light surrounding them. Joseph kneeling beside. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
That special night, Baby Jesus was born! Mary wrapped him in soft cloths and held him close. He was the most precious baby in all the world — God's own Son!
ኣብታ ፍልይቲ ለይቲ ህጻን የሱስ ተወልደ! ማርያም ብልስሉስ ዓለባ ጠቕሊላ ናብኣ ኣቕረበቶ። ኣብ ኩላ ዓለም ዝኸበረ ህጻን ነበረ — ወዲ ኣምላኽ ባዕሉ!
(Pronunciation: Abta flyti leyti htsan Yesus tewelde! Maryam blslus 'aleba teqlila nab'a aqrebeto. Ab kula 'alem zkhbere htsan nebere — Wedi Amlak ba'lu!)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Baby Jesus lying in a manger filled with straw. Animals gently looking over at the baby. Soft starlight coming through the stable opening. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mary laid Baby Jesus in a manger — a feeding box for animals. There was no fancy crib, but it was perfect. The animals watched the baby with gentle eyes.
ማርያም ንህጻን የሱስ ኣብ መብልዒ — ናይ እንስሳታት ናውቲ መግቢ ኣደቀሰቶ። ግሩም ዓራት ኣይነበረን ግና ፍጹም ነበረ። እንስሳታት ብልዙብ ኣዒንቲ ነቲ ህጻን ይርእዩዎ ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: Maryam n'htsan Yesus ab mebl'i — nay insesatat nawti megbi adeqeseto. Grum 'arat ayneberen gna ftsum nebere. Insesatat blzub a'inti neti htsan yr'eyuwo neberu.)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Shepherds on a hillside at night with their sheep, a brilliant angel appearing in the dark sky above them. The shepherds look startled and afraid. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
That same night, shepherds were watching their sheep on a hillside nearby. Suddenly, a bright angel appeared in the sky! They were very afraid!
ኣብታ ለይቲ እታ ጓሶት ኣብ ቀረባ ኩርባ ኣባጊዖም ይጓስዩ ነበሩ። ብድንገት ብሩህ መልኣኽ ኣብ ሰማይ ተራእየ! ኣዝዮም ፈርሑ!
(Pronunciation: Abta leyti ita gwasot ab qereba kurba abagi'om ygwasyu neberu. Bdnget bruh mel'ak ab semay tera'eye! Azyom ferhu!)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: The angel speaking to the shepherds with arms open wide, radiant with heavenly glory. The sheep calm around them. Stars twinkling. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"Don't be afraid!" said the angel. "I bring you wonderful news! Today, a Savior has been born in Bethlehem! He is Christ the Lord! You will find him lying in a manger."
"ኣይትፍርሑ!" በለ መልኣኹ። "ዘደንቕ ዜና ኣምጻኣልኩም! ሎሚ ኣብ ቤትልሔም መድሓኒ ተወሊዱ! ንሱ ክርስቶስ ጎይታ'ዩ! ኣብ መብልዒ ደቂሱ ክትረኽቡዎ ኢኹም።"
(Pronunciation: "Aytfrhu!" bele mel'akhu. "Zedenq zena amtsa'alkum! Lomi ab Betlhem medhani tewelidu! Nsu Kristos Goyta'yu! Ab mebl'i deqisu ktrekhbuwo ikhum.")
Scene 14
[PICTURE: The entire sky filled with hundreds of angels singing and praising God! Brilliant golden light fills the darkness. The shepherds looking up in complete wonder. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then the whole sky filled with angels singing! "Glory to God in the highest! Peace on earth to those He loves!" It was the most beautiful music ever heard!
ድሕሪኡ ምሉእ ሰማይ ብዝዝምሩ መላኽቲ መልአ! "ክብሪ ንኣምላኽ ኣብ ላዕሊ! ሰላም ኣብ ምድሪ ንዘፍቅሮም!" ካብ ዝኾነ ግዜ ዝጽብቐ ሙዚቃ ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Dhri'u mlu'e semay bzzimru melakhti mel'a! "Kbri n'Amlak ab la'li! Selam ab mdri nzefqirom!" Kab zkhone gize ztsbqe muziqa nebere!)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: The shepherds running excitedly toward Bethlehem, leaving their sheep behind. Joy and excitement on their faces. The town visible below with one stable glowing. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The shepherds said to each other, "Let's go to Bethlehem and see this baby!" They ran as fast as they could, their hearts full of joy and excitement!
ጓሶት ንሓድሕዶም "ናብ ቤትልሔም ንኺድ ነዚ ህጻን ንርአ!" በሉ። ብዝኽእሉ ቅልጣፈ ጎየዩ ልቦም ብሓጎስን ተስፋን ዝመልአ!
(Pronunciation: Gwasot nhadhdom "Nab Betlhem nkhid nezi htsan nr'a!" belu. Bzkhelu qlTafe goyeyu lbom bhagoson tesfan zmle'a!)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: The shepherds arriving at the stable, peering inside with wonder. They see Mary, Joseph, and baby Jesus in the manger, just as the angel said. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The shepherds found Baby Jesus just as the angel said — lying in a manger! They knelt down in wonder. This tiny baby was the Savior of the world!
ጓሶት ንህጻን የሱስ መልኣኹ ከም ዝበሎም — ኣብ መብልዒ ደቂሱ ረኸቡዎ! ብምግራም ተምበርከኹ። እዚ ንእሽተይ ህጻን መድሓኒ ዓለም ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Gwasot n'htsan Yesus mel'akhu kem zbelom — ab mebl'i deqisu rekhbuwo! Bmigraam temberkeKhu. Izi n'ishteye htsan medhani 'alem nebere!)
Scene 17
[PICTURE: A brilliant star shining brightly over the stable in Bethlehem. The star is larger and brighter than all others. The town sleeps peacefully below. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
High above, a special star shone brighter than all the others. It was God's sign pointing to where Jesus lay. Far away, wise men noticed this star!
ኣብ ላዕሊ ፍልይቲ ኮኾብ ካብ ኩለን ንላዕሊ ደመቐት። የሱስ ኣበይ ከም ዘሎ ዝሕብር ምልክት ኣምላኽ ነበረት። ርሑቕ ዘለዉ ጠቢባን ነዛ ኮኾብ ተዓዘቡዋ!
(Pronunciation: Ab la'li flyti kokhob kab kulen nla'li demeqet. Yesus abey kem zelo zhbr mlkt Amlak neberet. Rhuq zelewu Tebiban neza kokhob te'azebuwa!)
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Three wise men on camels crossing a desert at night, following the bright star. They carry beautiful gift boxes. Silhouettes against starry sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Wise men from the East followed the star for many days. They brought precious gifts for the new King: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
ጠቢባን ካብ ምብራቕ ንብዙሕ መዓልታት ነታ ኮኾብ ተኸተሉዋ። ነቲ ሓድሽ ንጉስ ክቡራት ህያባት ኣምጽኡ፡ ወርቂ፡ ዕጣን፡ ከርበን።
(Pronunciation: Tebiban kab mbraq nbzuh me'altat neta kokhob teketeluwa. Neti hadsh ngus kburat hyabat amts'u: werqi, 'itan, kerben.)
Scene 19
[PICTURE: The wise men bowing before baby Jesus and Mary, presenting their beautiful gifts. Gold, frankincense and myrrh in ornate boxes. Joy and reverence on their faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The wise men found Jesus and bowed down to worship Him. They opened their treasures and gave their gifts. They knew this baby was truly special — a King!
ጠቢባን ንየሱስ ረኺቦም ክሰግዱ ተምበርከኹ። ሓጺናቶም ከፊቶም ህያባቶም ሃቡ። እዚ ህጻን ብሓቂ ፍሉይ ምዃኑ ፈለጡ — ንጉስ!
(Pronunciation: Tebiban n'Yesus reKhibom ksgdu temberkeKhu. Hatsinatom kefitom hyabatom habu. Izi htsan bhaqqi fluye mkwanu feleTu — ngus!)
Scene 20
[PICTURE: The shepherds telling everyone in the town about what they saw. People gathered around listening in amazement. Morning market scene in Bethlehem. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The shepherds told everyone about the angels and the baby! People were amazed by their story. God's good news was spreading — the Savior had come!
ጓሶት ንኩሉ ሰብ ብዛዕባ መላኽትን ህጻንን ነገሩ! ሰባት ብዛንታኦም ተገረሙ። ጽቡቕ ዜና ኣምላኽ ይዝርጋሕ ነበረ — መድሓኒ መጺኡ!
(Pronunciation: Gwasot nkulu seb bza'ba melakhton htsanon negeru! Sebat bzanta'om tegeremu. Tsbuq zena Amlak yzrgah nebere — medhani mets'iu!)
Scene 21
[PICTURE: Mary sitting peacefully, holding baby Jesus, looking at him with deep love. She is treasuring every moment in her heart. Soft warm golden light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mary treasured all these things in her heart. She held her baby and smiled. God had kept His promise. The Savior of the world was here!

English: God's greatest gift to the world was His Son Jesus. He came not as a powerful king in a palace, but as a humble baby, showing us that God's love reaches everyone, no matter who they are or where they come from.
