Abraham and Isaac

ኣብርሃมን ይስሓቕን

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 17

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 17

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

For all the children who are learning to trust God even when things are hard.

ንኹሎም ነገራት ኣጸጋሚ ኣብ ዝኾነሉ'ውን ንኣምላኽ ክኣምኑ ዝመሃሩ ቆልዑ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: Abraham, an old man with a white beard, sitting outside his tent looking at the starry sky, with his young son Isaac playing nearby. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham loved God very much. God had given him a special son named Isaac. Abraham had waited many, many years for this child. Isaac was his greatest joy!
TIGRINYA:
ኣብርሃም ንኣมላኽ ኣዝዩ ይፈትዎ ነበረ። ኣምላኽ ይስሓቕ ዝስሙ ፍሉይ ወዲ ሂቡዎ ነበረ። ኣብርሃም ነዚ ቆልዓ ብዙሕ ብዙሕ ዓመታት ተጸቢዩ ነበረ። ይስሓቕ ዝዓበየ ሓጎሱ ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: ኣብርሃም = Abraham, ይስሓቕ = Yis'Haq = Isaac, ፍሉይ = filuy = special, ሓጎስ = Hagos = joy)

Page 2

[PICTURE: Abraham and young Isaac laughing together, Abraham teaching Isaac about God under a big tree. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham taught Isaac about God every day. They prayed together and laughed together. Isaac loved his father and his father loved him more than anything in the world.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብርሃም ኩሉ መዓልቲ ንይስሓቕ ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ይምህሮ ነበረ። ብሓባር ይጽልዩ ብሓባር ይስሕቁ ነበሩ። ይስሓቕ ንኣቡኡ ይፈትዎ ኣቡኡ ድማ ካብ ኩሉ ኣብ ዓለም ንላዕሊ ይፈትዎ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ይምህሮ = yimhiro = teaches him, ብሓባር = biHabar = together, ኣቡኡ = abu'u = his father)

Page 3

[PICTURE: Abraham alone at night, looking troubled, hearing God's voice from the sky, a divine light shining down. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
One day, God spoke to Abraham. "Abraham!" God said. "Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love, and go to the mountain I will show you."
TIGRINYA:
ሓደ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ ንኣብርሃም ተዛረቦ። "ኣብርሃም!" ኣምላኽ በለ። "ንወድኻ እቲ በይኑ ወድኻ ይስሓቕ ንዝትፈትዎ ውሰዶ ናብቲ ዘርእየካ ከረን ድማ ኪድ።"
(Pronunciation: ወድኻ = wedKa = your son, በይኑ = beynu = only, ከረን = keren = mountain)

Page 4

[PICTURE: Abraham sitting alone with his head in his hands, looking very sad and confused, tears on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
God told Abraham to offer Isaac as a sacrifice on that mountain. This was the hardest test Abraham ever faced. His heart was breaking, but he trusted God completely.
TIGRINYA:
ኣምላኽ ንኣብርሃም ንይስሓቕ ኣብ'ቲ ከረን ከም መስዋእቲ ክቕርቦ ነገሮ። እዚ ኣብርሃም ዝጓነፎ ዝኸበደ ፈተና ነበረ። ልቡ ይሰባበር ነበረ ግን ብምሉእ ንኣምላኽ ኣሚኑ።
(Pronunciation: መስዋእቲ = meswa'iti = sacrifice, ፈተና = fetena = test, ኣሚኑ = aminu = trusted)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Early morning, Abraham loading wood onto a donkey, preparing for the journey, the sun just rising. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Early the next morning, Abraham got up. He cut wood and loaded it on the donkey. He called Isaac and two servants. They began the long journey to the mountain.
TIGRINYA:
ንጽባሒቱ ኣብርሃም ኣንጊሁ ተንስአ። ዕንጨይቲ ቆሪጹ ኣብ ኣድጊ ጸዐነ። ንይስሓቕን ክልተ ኣገልገልትን ጸውዖም። ነቲ ነዊሕ ጉዕዞ ናብ ከረን ጀመሩ።
(Pronunciation: ዕንጨይቲ = 'incheyti = wood, ኣድጊ = adgi = donkey, ጉዕዞ = gu'izo = journey)

Page 6

[PICTURE: Abraham, Isaac, and two servants walking along a dusty path through hills, on their three-day journey. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
They walked for three days. Abraham was quiet and sad inside, but he kept trusting God. He believed God had a plan even though he did not understand it.
TIGRINYA:
ሰለስተ መዓልቲ ተጓዕዙ። ኣብርሃም ስቕ ኢሉን ብውሽጡ ሓዚኑን ነበረ ግን ንኣምላኽ ምእማን ቀጸለ። ዘይርድኦ'ኳ እንተኾነ ኣምላኽ መደብ ከም ዘለዎ ኣሚኑ።
(Pronunciation: ሰለስተ = seleste = three, ተጓዕዙ = tegu'azu = traveled, መደብ = medeb = plan)

Page 7

[PICTURE: Abraham pointing to a mountain in the distance, telling the servants to wait while he and Isaac go ahead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
On the third day, Abraham saw the mountain. He told the servants, "Wait here with the donkey. The boy and I will go up there to worship, and then we will come back."
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ሳልሳይ መዓልቲ ኣብርሃም ነቲ ከረን ረኣዮ። ነቶም ኣገልገልቲ "ምስ ኣድጊ ኣብዚ ጽንሑ። ኣነን እቲ ቆልዓን ናብ ላዕሊ ክንሰግድ ኢና ከይድና ድማ ክንምለስ ኢና" በሎም።
(Pronunciation: ሳልሳይ = salsay = third, ከረን = keren = mountain, ክንሰግድ = kinisegd = to worship)

Page 8

[PICTURE: Isaac carrying bundles of wood on his back, walking up the mountain with Abraham who carries a knife and fire. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham put the wood on Isaac's back. He carried the fire and the knife himself. Together, father and son walked up the mountain. Abraham's heart was heavy.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብርሃም ነቲ ዕንጨይቲ ኣብ ሕቖ ይስሓቕ ኣቐመጠ። ባዕሉ ነቲ ሓዊን ካራን ሒዙ። ብሓባር ኣቦን ወድን ነቲ ከረን ደየቡ። ልቢ ኣብርሃም ከቢዱ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ዕንጨይቲ = 'incheyti = wood, ሕቖ = Hiqo = back, ሓዊ = Hawi = fire, ካራ = kara = knife)

Page 9

[PICTURE: Isaac looking up at Abraham with an innocent, curious face, asking a question as they walk together. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Isaac looked at his father and asked, "Father? I see the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for the sacrifice?" Isaac was curious and confused.
TIGRINYA:
ይስሓቕ ናብ ኣቡኡ ጠሚቱ "ኣቦ? ሓዊን ዕንጨይትን ይርኢ ኣለኹ ግን ንመስዋእቲ ዝኸውን ገንሸል ኣበይ'ሎ?" ኢሉ ሓተተ። ይስሓቕ ተገሪሙን ተደናጊሩን ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ገንሸል = genshel = lamb, መስዋእቲ = meswa'iti = sacrifice, ኣቦ = abo = father)

Page 10

[PICTURE: Abraham with tears in his eyes but a trusting expression, holding Isaac's hand, looking toward heaven. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham answered gently, "God Himself will provide the lamb for the sacrifice, my son." Abraham believed with all his heart that God would take care of everything.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብርሃም ብልኡም "ኣምላኽ ባዕሉ ንመስዋእቲ ዝኸውን ገንሸል ክቕርብ'ዩ ወደይ" ኢሉ መለሰ። ኣብርሃም ኣምላኽ ንኹሉ ከም ዝኣልዮ ብምሉእ ልቡ ኣሚኑ።
(Pronunciation: ባዕሉ = ba'ilu = Himself, ክቕርብ = kiqrib = will provide, ወደይ = wedey = my son)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Abraham building a stone altar on top of the mountain, carefully placing stones one by one. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
When they reached the top, Abraham built an altar with stones. He placed the wood carefully on top. His hands were shaking but his faith was strong.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ጫፍ ምስ በጽሑ ኣብርሃም ብእምኒ መሰውኢ ሰርሐ። ነቲ ዕንጨይቲ ብጥንቃቐ ኣብ ላዕሊ ኣቐመጠ። ኣእዳዉ ይንቀጥቀጣ ነበራ ግን እምነቱ ጽንዕቲ ነበረት።
(Pronunciation: መሰውኢ = mesew'i = altar, እምኒ = imni = stones, እምነቱ = imnet'u = his faith)

Page 12

[PICTURE: Abraham gently tying Isaac's hands, Isaac looking at his father with trust, both with tears in their eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Then Abraham tied Isaac and placed him on the altar on top of the wood. Isaac did not fight. He trusted his father just like Abraham trusted God.
TIGRINYA:
ድሕሪኡ ኣብርሃም ንይስሓቕ ኣሲሩ ኣብ ልዕሊ ዕንጨይቲ ኣብ መሰውኢ ኣቐመጦ። ይስሓቕ ኣይተቓወመን። ኣብርሃም ንኣምላኽ ከም ዝኣመነ ንሱ'ውን ንኣቡኡ ኣሚኑ።
(Pronunciation: ኣሲሩ = asiru = tied, መሰውኢ = mesew'i = altar, ኣይተቓወመን = ayteqawemen = did not fight)

Page 13

[PICTURE: Abraham raising his hand with the knife, looking up to heaven with tears streaming, the most difficult moment. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham raised his hand with the knife. Tears rolled down his face. This was the hardest thing he had ever done. But he trusted God with all his heart.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብርሃም ምስ ካራ ኢዱ ኣልዐለ። ንብዓት ካብ ገጹ ፈሰሰ። እዚ ዝገበሮ ዝኸበደ ነገር ነበረ። ግን ብምሉእ ልቡ ንኣምላኽ ኣሚኑ።
(Pronunciation: ካራ = kara = knife, ኣልዐለ = al'ele = raised, ንብዓት = nib'at = tears)

Page 14

[PICTURE: A glowing angel appearing from heaven, reaching down with one hand to stop Abraham, brilliant light all around. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Suddenly, an angel of the Lord called from heaven! "Abraham! Abraham! Stop! Do not hurt the boy! Now I know that you truly fear God because you did not hold back your son."
TIGRINYA:
ብድንገት መልኣኽ እግዚኣብሄር ካብ ሰማይ ጸውዐ! "ኣብርሃም! ኣብርሃም! ደው በል! ነቲ ቆልዓ ኣይትጉድኦ! ሕጂ ንወድኻ ስለ ዘይሓቆፍካ ብሓቂ ንኣምላኽ ከም ትፈርሆ ፈሊጠ።"
(Pronunciation: መልኣኽ = mel'ak = angel, ሰማይ = semay = heaven, ደው በል = dew bel = stop)

Page 15

[PICTURE: Abraham dropping the knife in relief, looking up to heaven with joy and gratitude, untying Isaac. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham dropped the knife immediately! His heart was filled with so much joy and relief! He quickly untied Isaac and hugged him tightly. God had stopped him just in time!
TIGRINYA:
ኣብርሃም ብቕጽበት ነቲ ካራ ኣውደቖ! ልቡ ብብዙሕ ሓጎስን ዕረፍትን መልአ! ቀልጢፉ ንይስሓቕ ፈቲሑ ጽኑዕ ሓቜፎ። ኣምላኽ ብግዜኡ ደው ኣቢሉዎ!
(Pronunciation: ኣውደቖ = awdeqo = dropped it, ዕረፍቲ = 'irefti = relief, ሓቜፎ = Haqufo = hugged him)

Page 16

[PICTURE: Abraham looking over and seeing a ram caught by its horns in a bush nearby, light shining on it. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Then Abraham looked up and saw a ram caught by its horns in a bush! God had provided a ram to sacrifice instead of Isaac! God always provides what we need.

(Pronunciation: ድቤላ = dibela = ram, ቐርኑ = qernu = its horns, ቊጥቋጥ = qut'qwat' = bush)

Page 17

[PICTURE: Abraham and Isaac kneeling together in prayer on the mountain, thanking God with the altar nearby. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Abraham sacrificed the ram instead of Isaac. Then he and Isaac knelt down and thanked God together. Abraham named that place "The Lord Will Provide."
TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: ሰውዐ = sew'e = sacrificed, ኣመስገኑ = amesgenu = thanked, ይቕርብ = yiqrib = will provide)

Page 18

[PICTURE: The angel speaking again from heaven with golden light, blessing Abraham with a beautiful promise. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The angel spoke again: "Because you obeyed God and did not hold back your son, God will bless you greatly! Your family will be as many as the stars in the sky!"

(Pronunciation: ተኣዘዝካ = te'azezka = obeyed, ክባርኸካ = kibarKeka = will bless you, ከዋኽብቲ = kewakibti = stars)

Page 19

[PICTURE: Abraham and Isaac walking back down the mountain together happily, holding hands, the sun shining brightly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
