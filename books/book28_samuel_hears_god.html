Samuel Hears God's Voice

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A beautiful woman named Hannah kneeling in prayer at the temple entrance, tears streaming down her face, her hands raised toward heaven. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
There was a woman named Hannah who loved God very much. She wanted a baby more than anything in the world, but she had no children.
ሓና ዝስማ ሓንቲ ሰበይቲ ንኣምላኽ ኣዝያ ትፈትዎ ነበረት። ካብ ኩሉ ንላዕሊ ቆልዓ ትደሊ ነበረት፡ ግን ውሉድ ኣይነበራን።
Hana zisma hanti sebeyti n'Amlak azya tifetwo neberet. Kab kulu nilaeli qol'a tideli neberet, gin wulud ayneberan.
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Hannah inside the temple, praying with deep emotion, her lips moving silently, the temple pillars visible around her. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Hannah went to the temple to pray. She cried and prayed to God with all her heart. "Please God, give me a son," she whispered.
ሓና ናብ ቤተ መቕደስ ክትጽሊ ከደት። ብምሉእ ልባ ናብ ኣምላኽ በኸየት ጸለየት። "በጃኻ ኣምላኸይ ወዲ ሃበኒ" ብሕሹኽ በለት።
Hana nab bete meqdas kititsli kedet. Bmlu'e lba nab Amlak bekheyet tseleyet. "Bejakha Amlakhey wedi habeni" bihshukh belet.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Hannah making a solemn promise to God, her hands clasped together, a soft divine light shining from above in the temple. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Hannah made a special promise to God. "If you give me a son, I will give him back to you. He will serve you all his life," she said.
ሓና ንኣምላኽ ፍሉይ መብጽዓ ኣተወት። "ወዲ እንተ ሂብካኒ ንዕኡ ናባኻ ክመልሶ እየ። ምሉእ ህይወቱ ከገልግለካ እዩ" በለት።
Hana n'Amlak fluy mebts'a atewet. "Wedi inte hibkani ni'u nabakha kmelso iye. Mlu'e hiyowetu kegeligleka iyu" belet.
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Eli the old priest watching Hannah pray from his seat near the temple door, looking concerned at first then understanding. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Eli the priest saw Hannah praying. At first he did not understand, but then he said, "Go in peace. May God give you what you asked for."
ኤሊ ካህን ንሓና ክትጽሊ ረኣያ። መጀመርታ ኣይተረድኦን፡ ድሕሪኡ ግን "ብሰላም ኪዲ። ኣምላኽ ዝለመንክዮ ይሃብኪ" በላ።
Eli kahin niHana kititsli re'aya. Mejemerta ayteredeon, dihri'u gin "Bselam kidi. Amlak zilemenkiyo yihabki" bela.
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Hannah holding a newborn baby boy wrapped in soft cloth, her face full of joy and gratitude, warm light filling the room. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God answered Hannah's prayer! She had a beautiful baby boy. She named him Samuel, which means "God heard me." Hannah was so happy!
ኣምላኽ ጸሎት ሓና መለሰ! ጽቡቕ ወዲ ተወልደላ። ሳሙኤል ኢላ ሰመየቶ፡ ትርጉሙ "ኣምላኽ ሰሚዑኒ" ማለት እዩ። ሓና ኣዝያ ተሓጎሰት!
Amlak tselot Hana melese! Tsbukh wedi teweldela. Samuel ila semeyeto, tirgumo "Amlak semi'uni" malet iyu. Hana azya tehagoset!
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Hannah lovingly caring for young toddler Samuel at home, teaching him about God, a warm domestic scene. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Hannah took care of little Samuel with so much love. She told him about God every day. She wanted him to know how special God's love is.
ሓና ንንእሽቶ ሳሙኤል ብብዙሕ ፍቕሪ ተኸናኸነቶ። ኩሉ መዓልቲ ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ትነግሮ ነበረት። ፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ክንደይ ፍሉይ ምዃኑ ክፈልጥ ትደሊ ነበረት።
Hana nin'ishto Samuel bizbuh fiqri tekhenakheneto. Kulu me'alti biza'ba Amlak tinegro neberet. Fiqri Amlak kindey fluy mkhanu kifelT tideli neberet.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Hannah walking with young Samuel toward the temple, holding his small hand, both dressed in simple robes, the temple visible ahead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When Samuel was old enough, Hannah remembered her promise to God. She took Samuel to the temple to live there and serve God, just as she had promised.
ሳሙኤል ምስ ዓበየ ሓና መብጽዓኣ ንኣምላኽ ዘከረት። ንሳሙኤል ኣብ ቤተ መቕደስ ክነብርን ንኣምላኽ ከገልግልን ወሰደቶ፡ ከምቲ ዝተመባጽዐት።
Samuel mis abeye Hana mebts'a'a n'Amlak zekeret. NiSamuel ab bete meqdas kinebrin n'Amlak kegelgiln wesedet, kemti zitemebaTs'et.
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Hannah kneeling before young Samuel at the temple, saying goodbye with tears of love, Eli the priest standing nearby. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
It was hard for Hannah to leave Samuel, but she trusted God. "I am giving him to the Lord," she told Eli. "For his whole life, he belongs to God."
ንሓና ንሳሙኤል ገዲፋ ምኻድ ከቢድ ነበረ፡ ግን ኣብ ኣምላኽ ትኣምን ነበረት። "ንእግዚኣብሄር እህቦ ኣለኹ" ንኤሊ በለቶ። "ምሉእ ህይወቱ ናይ ኣምላኽ እዩ።"
NiHana niSamuel gedifa mkhad kebid nebere, gin ab Amlak ti'amin neberet. "Ni'Egzi'abher ihbo alekhu" niEli belet. "Mlu'e hiyowetu nay Amlak iyu."
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Young Samuel in a simple linen robe helping sweep the temple floor, looking cheerful and dedicated. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel lived in the temple with Eli the priest. Even though he was young, Samuel helped with many tasks. He served God faithfully every day.
ሳሙኤል ምስ ኤሊ ካህን ኣብ ቤተ መቕደስ ይነብር ነበረ። ንእሽቶ እኳ እንተ ነበረ ብብዙሕ ስራሕ ይሕግዝ ነበረ። ኩሉ መዓልቲ ንኣምላኽ ብእምነት የገልግል ነበረ።
Samuel mis Eli kahin ab bete meqdas yinebir nebere. Ni'ishto ikua inte nebere bizbuh srah yihigiz nebere. Kulu me'alti n'Amlak bi'imnet yegelgil nebere.
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Samuel lighting the temple lamps carefully, standing on tiptoe to reach them, the golden lampstand glowing warmly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel helped keep the temple lamps burning. He opened the temple doors each morning. He was a good and obedient helper to old Eli.
ሳሙኤል ቀንዴላት ቤተ መቕደስ ከይጠፍኣ ይሕልዎም ነበረ። ኩሉ ጽባሕ ማዕጾ ቤተ መቕደስ ይኸፍት ነበረ። ንዓቢይ ኤሊ ጽቡቕን ተኣዛዝን ሓጋዚ ነበረ።
Samuel qendelat bete meqdas keytef'a yihl'wom nebere. Kulu tsbah ma'tso bete meqdas yikheft nebere. Ni'abiy Eli tsbukhin te'azazin hagazi nebere.
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Hannah visiting Samuel at the temple, bringing him a new little robe she made, both smiling with joy at seeing each other. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Every year, Hannah came to visit Samuel. She brought him a new coat she had made. She loved him so much and was proud he was serving God.
ኩሉ ዓመት ሓና ንሳሙኤል ክትበጽሖ ትመጽእ ነበረት። ባዕላ ዝሰፈየቶ ሓድሽ ክዳን ተምጽኣሉ ነበረት። ኣዝያ ትፈትዎ ነበረት፡ ንኣምላኽ ብምግልጋሉ ድማ ትሕበን ነበረት።
Kulu amet Hana niSamuel kitbets'ho timets'i neberet. Ba'la zisefeyeto hadish kidan temts'alu neberet. Azya tifetwo neberet, n'Amlak bmigilgalu dma tihben neberet.
Scene 12
[PICTURE: A nighttime scene of the temple interior, dark and quiet, with Samuel lying on his small bed near the Ark of God, a single lamp still flickering. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One night, Samuel was lying down in the temple where the Ark of God was kept. The lamp of God was still burning. Everything was quiet and still.
ሓደ ለይቲ ሳሙኤል ኣብ ቤተ መቕደስ ኣብቲ ታቦት ኣምላኽ ዘለዎ ተገምሲሉ ነበረ። ቀንዴል ኣምላኽ ገና ይነድድ ነበረ። ኩሉ ነገር ህዱእን ጸጥታን ነበረ።
Hade leyti Samuel ab bete meqdas abti tabot Amlak zelewo tegemisilu nebere. Qendel Amlak gena yinedid nebere. Kulu neger hidu'in tsettan nebere.
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Samuel sitting up suddenly in his bed, eyes wide with surprise, hearing his name called in the dark temple, a soft glow around him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then God called out, "Samuel! Samuel!" Samuel woke up. He thought it was Eli calling him. He did not yet know God's voice.
ሽዑ ኣምላኽ "ሳሙኤል! ሳሙኤል!" ኢሉ ጸውዓ። ሳሙኤል ተበራበረ። ኤሊ ዝጸውዓ ዘሎ መሲሉዎ። ድምጺ ኣምላኽ ገና ኣይፈልጦን ነበረ።
Shi'u Amlak "Samuel! Samuel!" ilu tsew'a. Samuel teberabere. Eli zitsew'a zelo mesiluwo. Dimtsi Amlak gena ayfelTon nebere.
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Young Samuel running to old Eli's bedside in the dark, Eli lying in bed looking sleepy and confused. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel ran to Eli and said, "Here I am! You called me." But Eli said, "I did not call you. Go back and lie down." So Samuel went back to bed.
ሳሙኤል ናብ ኤሊ ጐይዩ "እኔኹ! ጸዊዕካኒ" በለ። ኤሊ ግን "ኣይጸዋዕኩኻን። ተመለስ ደቅስ" በሎ። ሳሙኤል ድማ ናብ ዓራቱ ተመልሰ።
Samuel nab Eli goyyu "Inekhu! Tsewi'kani" bele. Eli gin "Aytsewa'kukhany. Temeles deqis" belo. Samuel dma nab aratu temelese.
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Samuel lying in bed again in the dark temple, then sitting up once more as he hears the voice calling his name a second time. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God called again, "Samuel!" Samuel got up and ran to Eli again. "Here I am, you called me," he said. Eli answered, "I did not call you, my son. Go back to sleep."
ኣምላኽ ከም ብሓድሽ "ሳሙኤል!" ኢሉ ጸውዓ። ሳሙኤል ተሲኡ ናብ ኤሊ ጐየየ። "እኔኹ ጸዊዕካኒ" በለ። ኤሊ "ኣይጸዋዕኩኻን ወደይ። ተመለስ ደቅስ" ኢሉ መለሰሉ።
Amlak kem bihadish "Samuel!" ilu tsew'a. Samuel tesi'u nab Eli goyyeye. "Inekhu tsewi'kani" bele. Eli "Aytsewa'kukhany wedey. Temeles deqis" ilu meleseilu.
Scene 16
[PICTURE: Samuel running to Eli a third time, and Eli now sitting up in bed with a look of realization dawning on his old face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God called Samuel a third time. Samuel went to Eli again and said, "Here I am, you called me." This time, Eli understood — it was God calling the boy!
ኣምላኽ ንሳሙኤል ንሳልሳይ ግዜ ጸውዓ። ሳሙኤል ናብ ኤሊ ከይዱ "እኔኹ ጸዊዕካኒ" በለ። ሕጂ ኤሊ ተረድኦ — ኣምላኽ ነቲ ቆልዓ ይጽውዖ ከም ዘሎ!
Amlak niSamuel nisalsay gize tsew'a. Samuel nab Eli keydu "Inekhu tsewi'kani" bele. Hiji Eli tered'o — Amlak neti qol'a yitsuw'o kem zelo!
Scene 17
[PICTURE: Eli gently placing his hands on Samuel's shoulders, speaking to him kindly, teaching him what to say when God calls again. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Eli told Samuel, "Go lie down. If He calls you again, say: 'Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.'" Samuel obeyed and went back to his place.
ኤሊ ንሳሙኤል "ኪድ ደቅስ። ከም ብሓድሽ እንተ ጸዊዑካ 'ተዛረብ ጐይታይ ባርያኻ ይሰምዕ ኣሎ' በል" በሎ። ሳሙኤል ተኣዚዙ ናብ ቦታኡ ተመልሰ።
Eli niSamuel "Kid deqis. Kem bihadish inte tsewi'uka 'Tezareb Goytay baryakha yisem'i alo' bel" belo. Samuel te'azizu nab botau temelese.
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Samuel lying still in bed, eyes open and alert, as a warm divine light fills the dark temple around him, God's presence drawing near. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God came and stood near Samuel. He called again, "Samuel! Samuel!" This time Samuel was ready. He knew what to say.
ኣምላኽ ናብ ሳሙኤል ቀሪቡ ደው በለ። ከም ብሓድሽ "ሳሙኤል! ሳሙኤል!" ኢሉ ጸውዓ። ሕጂ ሳሙኤል ድሉው ነበረ። እንታይ ከም ዝብል ይፈልጥ ነበረ።
Amlak nab Samuel qeribu dew bele. Kem bihadish "Samuel! Samuel!" ilu tsew'a. Hiji Samuel dilu'u nebere. Intay kem zibil yifelT nebere.
Scene 19
[PICTURE: Samuel kneeling on his bed with hands folded, speaking to God with a brave and reverent expression, golden light surrounding him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel answered, "Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening." How brave Samuel was! He was ready to hear whatever God wanted to tell him.
ሳሙኤል "ተዛረብ ጐይታይ ባርያኻ ይሰምዕ ኣሎ" ኢሉ መለሰ። ሳሙኤል ክንደይ ጅግና ነበረ! ኣምላኽ ክነግሮ ዝደለየ ኩሉ ክሰምዕ ድሉው ነበረ።
Samuel "Tezareb Goytay baryakha yisem'i alo" ilu melese. Samuel kindey jigna nebere! Amlak kinegro zideleye kulu kisem'i dilu'u nebere.
Scene 20
[PICTURE: Samuel listening intently with closed eyes and a peaceful face as God speaks to him, divine light streaming down gently in the quiet temple. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God gave Samuel an important message that night. Samuel listened carefully to every word. God trusted this young boy with His special words.
ኣምላኽ ነታ ለይቲ ንሳሙኤል ኣገዳሲ መልእኽቲ ሃቦ። ሳሙኤል ነፍሲ ወከፍ ቃል ብጥንቃቐ ሰሚዑ። ኣምላኽ ነዚ ንእሽቶ ቆልዓ ብፍሉይ ቃላቱ ኣሚኑዎ።
Amlak neta leyti niSamuel agedasi mel'ikhti habo. Samuel nefsi wekef qal biTinqaqe semi'u. Amlak nezi ni'ishto qol'a bifluy qalatu aminuwo.
Scene 21
[PICTURE: Morning light streaming into the temple, Samuel opening the large temple doors looking thoughtful but peaceful after his experience with God. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The next morning, Samuel got up and opened the doors of the temple as usual. He thought about God's message. He was a little afraid to tell Eli what God said.
ንጽባሒቱ ሳሙኤል ተሲኡ ከም ልሙድ ማዕጾ ቤተ መቕደስ ከፈተ። ብዛዕባ መልእኽቲ ኣምላኽ ሓሰበ። መልእኽቲ ኣምላኽ ንኤሊ ክነግሮ ቁሩብ ፈሪሑ ነበረ።
Nitsbahitu Samuel tesi'u kem limud ma'tso bete meqdas kefete. Biza'ba mel'ikhti Amlak hasebe. Mel'ikhti Amlak niEli kinegro qurub ferihu nebere.
Scene 22
[PICTURE: Eli asking Samuel about God's message, and Samuel bravely telling him everything, both sitting together on a bench in the temple courtyard. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Eli called Samuel and asked, "What did God say to you? Do not hide anything from me." Samuel was brave and told Eli everything God had said.
ኤሊ ንሳሙኤል ጸዊዑ "ኣምላኽ እንታይ ኢሉካ? ካባይ ሓደ ነገር ኣይትሕባእ" በሎ። ሳሙኤል ተባዒሱ ኣምላኽ ዝበሎ ኩሉ ንኤሊ ነገሮ።
Eli niSamuel tsewi'u "Amlak intay iluka? Kabay hade neger aytihba'e" belo. Samuel teba'isu Amlak zibelo kulu niEli negero.
Scene 23
[PICTURE: Samuel growing taller, now a young man, speaking God's words to groups of people gathered around him, respected by all. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel grew up and God was with him. Everyone knew Samuel was a true prophet of God. God continued to speak to Samuel, and Samuel shared God's words with the people.
ሳሙኤል ዓበየ፡ ኣምላኽ ድማ ምስኡ ነበረ። ኩሎም ሰባት ሳሙኤል ሓቀኛ ነብዪ ኣምላኽ ምዃኑ ይፈልጡ ነበሩ። ኣምላኽ ንሳሙኤል ክዛረቦ ቀጸለ፡ ሳሙኤል ድማ ቃል ኣምላኽ ነቶም ሰባት ኣካፈሎም።
Samuel abeye, Amlak dma mis'u nebere. Kulom sebat Samuel haqenya nebiy Amlak mkhanu yifelTu neberu. Amlak niSamuel kizarebo qetsele, Samuel dma qal Amlak netom sebat akafelom.
Scene 24
[PICTURE: Samuel as a wise prophet leading the people of Israel, standing tall with authority, people looking to him for guidance. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel became one of the greatest prophets in Israel. He helped the people follow God. It all started because one little boy was willing to listen to God's voice.
ሳሙኤል ካብ ዝዓበዩ ነቢያት እስራኤል ሓደ ኮነ። ነቶም ሰባት ንኣምላኽ ክስዕቡ ሓገዞም። ኩሉ ዝጀመረ ሓደ ንእሽቶ ቆልዓ ድምጺ ኣምላኽ ክሰምዕ ፍቓደኛ ብምዃኑ እዩ።
Samuel kab zi'abeyu nebiyat Israel hade kone. Netom sebat n'Amlak kis'ibu hagezom. Kulu zijemere hade ni'ishto qol'a dimtsi Amlak kisem'i fiqadenya bimkhanu iyu.
Scene 25
[PICTURE: A child kneeling by their bed at night in prayer, with a soft warm glow around them, inspired by Samuel's story to listen for God's voice. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God still speaks to children today! Just like Samuel, you can listen for God's voice. When you pray, read the Bible, or feel God's love, He is speaking to you. Will you listen?
ኣምላኽ ሎሚ'ውን ንቆልዑ ይዛረቦም እዩ! ከም ሳሙኤል ንስኻ'ውን ድምጺ ኣምላኽ ክትሰምዕ ትኽእል ኢኻ። ምስ ትጽሊ፡ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ምስ ተንብብ፡ ወይ ፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ምስ ትስምዓካ ይዛረበካ ኣሎ። ክትሰምዕ ዲኻ?
Amlak lomi'wun niqol'u yizarebom iyu! Kem Samuel niskha'wun dimtsi Amlak kitisem'i tikhil ikha. Mis titisli, metshaf qidus mis tenibib, wey fiqri Amlak mis tismI'aka yizarebeka alo. Kitisem'i dikha?

Moral of the Story / ትምህርቲ ዛንታ

God speaks to children too. We should listen for God's voice and be ready to obey. When God calls us, we should answer with willing hearts, just like Samuel did.
ኣምላኽ ንቆልዑ'ውን ይዛረቦም እዩ። ድምጺ ኣምላኽ ክንሰምዕን ክንእዘዝን ድሉዋት ክንከውን ኣለና። ኣምላኽ ምስ ዝጽውዓና ከም ሳሙኤል ብፍቓደኛ ልቢ ክንምልስ ኣለና።
Amlak niqol'u'wun yizarebom iyu. Dimtsi Amlak kinisem'in kini'azazin dilu'wat kinkewn alena. Amlak mis zitsuw'ana kem Samuel bifiqadenya libi kinimlis alena.
