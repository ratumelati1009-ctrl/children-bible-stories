Peter's Escape from Prison

ጴጥሮስ ካብ ቤት ማእሰርቲ ኣምሊጡ

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: King Herod sitting on a throne looking angry, pointing his finger and giving orders to soldiers. He wears a crown and royal robes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
King Herod was a mean king. He did not like the people who loved Jesus. He wanted to make the Jewish leaders happy, so he decided to hurt the believers.
ንጉስ ሄሮድስ ሕሱም ንጉስ ነበረ። ንየሱስ ዝፈትዉ ሰባት ኣይፈትዎምን ነበረ። ንመራሕቲ ኣይሁድ ክሕጉስ ስለ ዝደለየ፡ ነቶም ኣመንቲ ክጐድኦም ወሰነ።
Nigus Herodis hisoom nigus nebere. Niyesus zifet'wu sebat ayifet'womon nebere. Nimerahti Ayhud kihigus sile zideleye, ne'tom aminti kigod'om wesene.
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Soldiers grabbing Peter by the arms, arresting him in a street. Peter looks calm but concerned. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
King Herod sent his soldiers to arrest Peter! Peter was one of Jesus's closest friends and a leader of the church. The soldiers grabbed him and took him away.
ንጉስ ሄሮድስ ንጴጥሮስ ክሕዝዎ ወተሃደራቱ ለኣኸ! ጴጥሮስ ካብቶም ቀረባ ኣዕሩኽ የሱስን መራሒ ቤተ ክርስትያንን ነበረ። እቶም ወተሃደራት ሒዞም ወሰድዎ።
Nigus Herodis niPetros kihizwo wetehaderatu le'akhe! Petros kabtom qereba a'irukh Yesusn merahi bete kirstiyann nebere. Itom wetahederat hizom wesedwo.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Peter being led into a dark stone prison cell. Heavy iron door visible. Soldiers on either side of him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
They put Peter in prison! The prison was dark and cold. Herod planned to bring Peter before the people after the Passover feast. Things looked very bad for Peter.
ንጴጥሮስ ኣብ ቤት ማእሰርቲ ኣእተዉዎ! ቤት ማእሰርቲ ጸልማትን ዝሑልን ነበረ። ሄሮድስ ድሕሪ በዓል ፋሲካ ንጴጥሮስ ቅድሚ ህዝቢ ከቕርቦ ሓሰበ። ኩነታት ንጴጥሮስ ኣዝዩ ሕማቕ ይመስል ነበረ።
NiPetros ab biet ma'iserti a'itewuwo! Biet ma'iserti tselmatn zihuln nebere. Herodis dihri be'al Fasika niPetros qidmi hizbi keqirbo hasebe. Kunetat niPetros aziyu hima'q yimesil nebere.
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Peter chained between two sleeping soldiers inside the prison cell. Two more guards standing at the prison door. Heavy chains on Peter's wrists. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter was chained between two soldiers. Two more guards stood at the prison door. Four groups of four soldiers took turns watching him. There was no way to escape!
ጴጥሮስ ኣብ መንጎ ክልተ ወተሃደራት ብሰንሰለት ተኣሲሩ ነበረ። ካልኦት ክልተ ዘበኛታት ኣብ ማዕጾ ቤት ማእሰርቲ ደው ኢሎም ነበሩ። ኣርባዕተ ጉጅለ ናይ ኣርባዕተ ወተሃደራት ብተራ ይሕልዉዎ ነበሩ። ክትሃድም ዝከኣል ኣይነበረን!
Petros ab mengo kilte wetehaderat bisenselet te'asiru nebere. Kal'ot kilte zebenyatat ab ma'itso biet ma'iserti dew ilom neberu. Arba'ite gujle nay arba'ite wetahederat bitera yihilwuwo neberu. Kithadim zikehal ayeneberen!
Scene 5
[PICTURE: A group of believers kneeling in prayer inside a house, hands raised, some with tears on their faces. Oil lamp lighting the room. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But the church did not give up! All the believers gathered together and prayed very hard for Peter. They prayed all day and all night without stopping. They asked God to save Peter.
ግን ቤተ ክርስትያን ተስፋ ኣይቈረጸትን! ኩሎም ኣመንቲ ተኣኪቦም ንጴጥሮስ ብጽዒት ጸለዩ። ምሉእ መዓልትን ምሉእ ለይትን ከይዓረፉ ጸለዩ። ኣምላኽ ንጴጥሮስ ከድሕኖ ለመንዎ።
Gin bete kirstiyan tesfa ayqoretsetn! Kulom aminti te'akibom niPetros bits'it tseleyu. Milu'e me'altin milu'e leytin keyarefu tseleyu. Amlakh niPetros kedihno lemenwo.
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Peter sleeping peacefully between two soldiers in the dark prison cell on the night before his trial. Chains visible on his wrists. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The night before Herod planned to bring Peter to trial, Peter was sleeping. He was chained between two soldiers, sleeping so peacefully! Peter trusted God completely, even in prison.
ሄሮድስ ንጴጥሮስ ናብ ፍርዲ ከቕርቦ ዝሓሰበላ ለይቲ፡ ጴጥሮስ ደቂሱ ነበረ። ኣብ መንጎ ክልተ ወተሃደራት ብሰንሰለት ተኣሲሩ፡ ብሰላም ደቂሱ ነበረ! ጴጥሮስ ኣብ ቤት ማእሰርቲ እንከሎ እውን ንኣምላኽ ምሉእ ብምሉእ ይኣምኖ ነበረ።
Herodis niPetros nab firdi keqirbo zihasebelal leyti, Petros deqisu nebere. Ab mengo kilte wetehaderat bisenselet te'asiru, biselam deqisu nebere! Petros ab biet ma'iserti inkelo ewun niAmlakh milu'e bimilu'e yi'amino nebere.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: A bright glowing angel appearing in the dark prison cell, brilliant light filling the room. Peter still sleeping. The soldiers still asleep. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Suddenly, an angel of the Lord appeared! A bright light filled the dark prison cell. The angel shone so brightly! But the soldiers kept on sleeping — they didn't see anything!
ብድንገት መልኣኽ እግዚኣብሄር ተራእየ! ብሩህ ብርሃን ነታ ጸልማት ቤት ማእሰርቲ መልኣ። እቲ መልኣኽ ኣዝዩ ኣንጸባረቐ! ግን እቶም ወተሃደራት ደቂሶም ቀጸሉ — ገለ እውን ኣይረኣዩን!
Bidnget mel'akh Egzi'abher tera'iye! Biruh birhan neta tselmat biet ma'iserti mel'a. Iti mel'akh aziyu antsebareqe! Gin itom wetehaderat deqisom qetselu — gele ewun ayire'ayun!
Scene 8
[PICTURE: The angel gently tapping Peter on his side to wake him up. Peter opening his eyes in surprise, seeing the bright light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The angel tapped Peter on his side and woke him up. "Quick, get up!" the angel said. At that very moment, the heavy chains fell right off Peter's hands! Clank! Clank! They dropped to the floor!
እቲ መልኣኽ ንጴጥሮስ ኣብ ጐድኑ ደፊኡ ኣበራብሮ። "ቀልጢፍካ ተንስእ!" በሎ እቲ መልኣኽ። ኣብቲ ህሞት እቲ፡ እተን ከበድቲ ሰንሰለታት ካብ ኣእዳው ጴጥሮስ ወደቓ! ቸንቸን! ቸንቸን! ናብ ምድሪ ወደቓ!
Iti mel'akh niPetros ab godnu defi'u aberabro. "Qeltifka tinse'!" belo iti mel'akh. Ab'ti himot iti, iten kebedti senseletat kab a'idaw Petros wedeqa! Chenchen! Chenchen! Nab midri wedeqa!
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Peter looking down at his wrists in amazement, the broken chains lying on the prison floor. The angel standing beside him glowing brightly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter looked at his hands — the chains were gone! He couldn't believe it! The angel said, "Put on your sandals." Peter quickly put on his sandals, his hands shaking with surprise.
ጴጥሮስ ናብ ኣእዳዉ ጠመተ — እተን ሰንሰለታት የልያን! ክኣምን ኣይከኣለን! እቲ መልኣኽ "ሳእንካ ግበር" በሎ። ጴጥሮስ ቀልጢፉ ሳእኑ ገበረ፡ ኣእዳዉ ብምግራም ይንቀጥቀጣ ነበራ።
Petros nab a'idawu t'emete — iten senseletat yeliyan! Ki'amin ayke'alen! Iti mel'akh "sa'inka giber" belo. Petros qeltifu sa'inu gebere, a'idawu bimigiram yinqetqeta nebera.
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Peter wrapping his cloak around himself while the angel watches. The two soldiers still sleeping on the floor undisturbed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"Wrap your cloak around you and follow me," the angel told Peter. Peter obeyed right away. He put his cloak on and got ready to follow the angel. The soldiers didn't wake up at all!
"ክዳንካ ተኸደን ስዓበኒ" በሎ እቲ መልኣኽ ንጴጥሮስ። ጴጥሮስ ብኡ ንብኡ ተኣዘዘ። ክዳኑ ለቢሱ ነቲ መልኣኽ ክስዕብ ተዳለወ። እቶም ወተሃደራት ፈጺሞም ኣይተበራበሩን!
Kidanka tekeden si'abeni" belo iti mel'akh niPetros. Petros bi'u nibi'u te'azeze. Kidanu lebisu neti mel'akh kis'ib tedalewe. Itom wetehaderat fetsimon ayteneberuun!
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Peter following the glowing angel through a dark prison corridor. Peter looking confused, thinking this might be a dream. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter followed the angel out of the cell. But Peter didn't think it was real! He thought he was seeing a vision or having a wonderful dream. It all seemed too amazing to be true!
ጴጥሮስ ነቲ መልኣኽ ካብ ሳጹን ሰዓቦ። ግን ጴጥሮስ ሓቂ ኢዩ ኢሉ ኣይሓሰበን! ራእይ ይርኢ ኣሎ ወይ ድማ ጽቡቕ ሕልሚ ይሓልም ኣሎ ዝብል ሓሳብ ነበሮ። ኩሉ ሓቂ ክኸውን ዘይክእል ኮይኑ ተሰመዖ!
Petros neti mel'akh kab satsun se'abo. Gin Petros haqi iyu ilu ayhiseben! Ra'iy yir'i alo wey dima tsibiq hilmi yihalim alo zibil hasab nebero. Kulu haqi kikhewn zeykhi'il koynu teseme'o!
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Peter and the angel walking past the first set of guards who are standing still like statues with their eyes closed, completely unaware. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
They walked past the first guard post. The guards didn't move! They didn't see Peter or the angel at all. It was as if Peter and the angel were invisible! God was protecting them.
ነታ ቀዳመይቲ ሓለዋ ሓለፍዋ። እቶም ዘበኛታት ኣይተንቀሳቐሱን! ንጴጥሮስ ወይ ነቲ መልኣኽ ፈጺሞም ኣይረኣዩዎምን። ጴጥሮስን እቲ መልኣኽን ዘይረኣዩ ዝመስሉ ነበሩ! ኣምላኽ ይሕልዎም ነበረ።
Neta qedameyti halewa halefwa. Itom zebenyatat aytinqesaqesun! NiPetros wey neti mel'akh fetsimon ayire'ayuwomn. Petrosn iti mel'akhn zeyire'ayu zimeslu neberu! Amlakh yihilwom nebere.
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Peter and the angel passing a second guard post with more soldiers standing motionless. The corridor is dimly lit with torches on the walls. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
They passed the second guard post too! Again, the guards didn't notice a thing. Peter kept following the angel, step by step, through the dark prison hallways. God made them invisible to every guard!
ነታ ካልኣይቲ ሓለዋ እውን ሓለፍዋ! ከም ብሓድሽ እቶም ዘበኛታት ገለ እውን ኣየስተውዓሉን። ጴጥሮስ ነቲ መልኣኽ ስጉምቲ ብስጉምቲ ኣብተን ጸልማት መንገድታት ቤት ማእሰርቲ ሰዓቦ። ኣምላኽ ንኹሉ ዘበኛ ዘይረኣዩ ገበሮም!
Neta kal'ayti halewa ewun halefwa! Kem bihadish itom zebenyatat gele ewun ayestewal'un. Petros neti mel'akh sigumti bisigumti abten tselmat mengedtat biet ma'iserti se'abo. Amlakh nikulu zebenya zeyire'ayu geberom!
Scene 14
[PICTURE: A large iron gate at the end of the prison corridor starting to swing open by itself. Golden light shining through the opening. Peter and the angel approaching. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
They came to the big iron gate that led to the city. Nobody opened it — but it swung open all by itself! The heavy gate moved without anyone touching it. God's power opened the way!
ናብታ ናብ ከተማ ትወስድ ዓባይ ናይ ሓጺን ደገ መጹ። ዝኾነ ሰብ ኣይከፈታን — ግን ባዕላ ተኸፈተት! እታ ከብድቲ ደገ ዝኾነ ሰብ ከይተንከያ ተንቀሳቐሰት። ሓይሊ ኣምላኽ መንገዲ ከፈተ!
Nabta nab ketema tiwesid abay nay hatsin dege mets'u. Zikhone seb ayikefetan — gin ba'ila tekefetet! Ita kebditi dege zikhone seb keytinkeya tinqesaqeset. Hayli Amlakh mengedi kefete!
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Peter and the angel walking through the open iron gate onto a city street at night. Stars in the sky, cool night air. Peter's cloak flowing behind him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter and the angel walked through the gate and down one whole street together. The cool night air touched Peter's face. He was outside! He was free! But he still thought it was a dream.
ጴጥሮስን እቲ መልኣኽን ብደገ ሓሊፎም ሓንቲ ምሉእ ጐደና ብሓንሳብ ከዱ። ዝሑል ኣየር ለይቲ ገጽ ጴጥሮስ ተንከፎ። ኣብ ደገ ነበረ! ናጻ ነበረ! ግን ገና ሕልሚ ኢዩ ይብል ነበረ።
Petrosn iti mel'akhn bidege halifom hanti milu'e godena bihansab kedu. Zihul ayer leyti gets Petros tinkefo. Ab dege nebere! Natsa nebere! Gin gena hilmi iyu yibil nebere.
Scene 16
[PICTURE: The angel fading away into the night sky, becoming transparent with sparkles of light. Peter standing alone on the street looking up in wonder. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then suddenly, the angel disappeared! One moment the angel was there, and the next moment — gone! Peter stood alone on the quiet street, looking around. The angel had finished his job.
ድሕሪኡ ብድንገት እቲ መልኣኽ ጠፍአ! ሓንቲ ህሞት እቲ መልኣኽ ኣብኡ ነበረ፡ ኣብታ ዝቕጽል ህሞት ድማ — ጠፍአ! ጴጥሮስ ኣብታ ህድእቲ ጐደና በይኑ ደው ኢሉ፡ ናብ ኩሉ ገጹ ይጥምት ነበረ። እቲ መልኣኽ ስርሑ ወዲኡ ነበረ።
Dihri'u bidnget iti mel'akh tef'a! Hanti himot iti mel'akh ab'u nebere, ab'ta ziqtsil himot dima — tef'a! Petros ab'ta hid'iti godena beynu dew ilu, nab kulu getsu yit'mit nebere. Iti mel'akh sirhu wedi'u nebere.
Scene 17
[PICTURE: Peter standing on the street with his hands on his head in amazement, eyes wide open, realizing this is real and not a dream. Stars twinkling above. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Now Peter knew it was real! "It's true!" he said. "The Lord sent His angel and rescued me from Herod's power!" Peter was so happy and thankful. God had answered the prayers of his friends!
ሕጂ ጴጥሮስ ሓቂ ምዃኑ ፈለጠ! "ሓቂ ኢዩ!" በለ። "እግዚኣብሄር መልኣኹ ልኢኹ ካብ ስልጣን ሄሮድስ ኣድሒኑኒ!" ጴጥሮስ ኣዝዩ ሕጉስን ኣመስጋንን ነበረ። ኣምላኽ ነታ ጸሎት ኣዕሩኹ መሊሱላ ነበረ!
Hiji Petros haqi mikwanu felete! "Haqi iyu!" bele. "Egzi'abher mel'akhu li'ikhu kab siltan Herodis adhinuni!" Petros aziyu higusn amesgann nebere. Amlakh neta tselot a'irukhu melisulal nebere!
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Peter hurrying through narrow streets at night, heading toward a house with a warm light glowing from inside. The city is quiet and peaceful. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter hurried through the dark streets. He knew exactly where to go — to the house of Mary, the mother of John Mark. He knew his friends would be there, praying for him. He couldn't wait to tell them!
ጴጥሮስ ኣብተን ጸልማት ጐደናታት ተቐላጠፈ። ናበይ ከም ዝኸድ ብልክዕ ይፈልጥ ነበረ — ናብ ገዛ ማርያም ኣደ ዮሃንስ ማርቆስ። ኣዕሩኹ ኣብኡ ከም ዘለዉ ንእኡ ይጽልዩ ከም ዘለዉ ይፈልጥ ነበረ። ክነግሮም ሃንቀው ይብል ነበረ!
Petros abten tselmat godentat teqelat'efe. Nabey kem zikhde bilki'i yifelit' nebere — nab geza Maryam ade Yohanis Marqos. A'irukhu ab'u kem zilewu ni'u yitsiliyu kem zilewu yifelit nebere. Kingirom hanqew yibil nebere!
Scene 19
[PICTURE: Peter standing at a wooden door, knocking gently. The house has warm light coming from the windows. A courtyard gate is visible. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter arrived at Mary's house and knocked on the outer gate. Knock, knock, knock! Inside, many believers were still gathered together, praying with all their hearts for Peter's safety.
ጴጥሮስ ናብ ገዛ ማርያም በጺሑ ነታ ናይ ደገ ደገ ኣኳሕኰሐ። ኩሕ ኩሕ ኩሕ! ኣብ ውሽጢ ብዙሓት ኣመንቲ ገና ተኣኪቦም ብምሉእ ልቦም ንድሕነት ጴጥሮስ ይጽልዩ ነበሩ።
Petros nab geza Maryam betsiihu neta nay dege dege akwahkwohe. Kuh kuh kuh! Ab wishti bizuhat aminti gena te'akibom bimilu'e libom nidihinit Petros yitsiliyu neberu.
Scene 20
[PICTURE: A young servant girl named Rhoda running excitedly toward the gate, her face full of joy and surprise. She recognizes the voice from behind the door. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
A servant girl named Rhoda came to answer the door. When she heard Peter's voice, she was so excited and happy that she forgot to open the gate! Instead, she ran back inside to tell everyone!
ሮዳ ዝስማ ግልጅ ኣገልጋሊት ነታ ማዕጾ ክትመልስ መጸት። ድምጺ ጴጥሮስ ምስ ሰምዐት ክትከፍታ ረሲዓ ብሓጐስን ምግራምን መሊኣ! ኣብ ክንድኡ ናብ ውሽጢ ጐዪያ ንኹሎም ክትነግር ከደት!
Roda zisma gilij agelgalit neta ma'itso kitmelis mets'et. Dimtsi Petros mis sem'et kitkifta resi'a bihagosn migiramn meli'a! Ab kindU nab wishti goyiya nikulom kitnegir kedet!
Scene 21
[PICTURE: Rhoda inside the house telling the praying believers that Peter is at the door. The people looking at her with disbelief, some shaking their heads. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"Peter is at the door!" Rhoda shouted. But they didn't believe her! "You're crazy!" they said. "Maybe it's his angel." They had been praying for Peter, but they couldn't believe God answered so quickly!
"ጴጥሮስ ኣብ ማዕጾ ኣሎ!" ኢላ ሮዳ ጨደረት። ግን ኣይኣመንዋን! "ዓቂልኪ!" በልዋ። "ምናልባት መልኣኹ ይኸውን።" ንጴጥሮስ ይጽልዩ ነበሩ፡ ግን ኣምላኽ ክንድዚ ቀልጢፉ ከም ዝመለሰ ክኣምኑ ኣይከኣሉን!
"Petros ab ma'itso alo!" ila Roda chederet. Gin ayi'amenwaan! "Aqilki!" belwa. "Minalbat mel'akhu yikhewn." NiPetros yitsiliyu neberu, gin Amlakh kindizi qeltifu kem zimelese ki'amnu ayke'alun!
Scene 22
[PICTURE: Peter still standing outside knocking persistently on the gate. He is smiling, waiting patiently. The night sky is behind him with stars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Peter kept knocking! Knock, knock, knock! He didn't give up. He stood outside the gate, waiting for someone to let him in. He had such wonderful news to share with his friends!
ግን ጴጥሮስ ምኳሕኳሕ ቀጸለ! ኩሕ ኩሕ ኩሕ! ተስፋ ኣይቈረጸን። ኣብ ደገ ማዕጾ ደው ኢሉ ዝኾነ ሰብ ከእትዎ ተጸበየ። ኣዕሩኹ ክነግሮም ከንዲ ዝጽቡቕ ዜና ነበሮ!
Gin Petros mikwahkwah qetsele! Kuh kuh kuh! Tesfa ayqoretsen. Ab dege ma'itso dew ilu zikhone seb ke'itiwo tetsbeye. A'irukhu kingirom kendi zitsbuq zena nebero!
Scene 23
[PICTURE: The believers opening the gate and seeing Peter standing there. Their faces showing complete shock and joy. Some with mouths wide open, some reaching out to embrace him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Finally, they opened the gate — and there was Peter! They were completely amazed! Their mouths dropped open! They could not believe their eyes. Peter was really standing there, free and alive!
ኣብ መወዳእታ ነታ ደገ ከፈትዋ — ጴጥሮስ ድማ ኣብኡ ነበረ! ምሉእ ብምሉእ ተገረሙ! ኣፎም ተኸፈተ! ኣዒንቶም ክኣምና ኣይከኣላን። ጴጥሮስ ብሓቂ ኣብኡ ደው ኢሉ ነበረ፡ ናጻን ብህይወትን!
Ab meweda'ita neta dege kefetwa — Petros dima ab'u nebere! Milu'e bimilu'e tegeremu! Afom tekefete! A'intom ki'amna ayke'alan. Petros bihaqi ab'u dew ilu nebere, natsan bihiywetn!

[PICTURE: Peter inside the house, gesturing with his hands as he tells the whole group the amazing story of his escape. Everyone listening with wide eyes and joyful faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter motioned for them to be quiet. Then he told them the whole amazing story — how the angel came, how the chains fell off, how they passed the guards, and how the gate opened by itself! Everyone praised God!
ጴጥሮስ ስቕ ክብሉ ብኢዱ ኣመልከቶም። ድሕሪኡ ምሉእ እቲ ዘደንቕ ዛንታ ነገሮም — መልኣኽ ከመይ ከም ዝመጸ፡ ሰንሰለታት ከመይ ከም ዝወደቓ፡ ዘበኛታት ከመይ ከም ዝሓለፍዎም፡ ደገ ድማ ከመይ ባዕላ ከም ዝተኸፈተት! ኩሎም ንኣምላኽ ኣመስገኑ!
Petros siq kiblu bi'idu amelketom. Dihri'u milu'e iti zideniqi zanta negerom — mel'akh kemey kem zimet'se, senseletat kemey kem ziwodeqa, zebenyatat kemey kem zihalefwom, dege dima kemey ba'ila kem zitekefetet! Kulom niAmlakh amesgenu!
Scene 25
[PICTURE: The whole group of believers celebrating together with raised hands, some hugging Peter, children jumping with joy. Warm light filling the room. Big smiles on everyone's faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
What a celebration! God had heard their prayers and set Peter free! They learned that prayer is very powerful. When we pray together and don't give up, God listens and does amazing things. Never stop praying!
ከመይ ዝበለ በዓል! ኣምላኽ ጸሎቶም ሰሚዑ ንጴጥሮስ ናጻ ኣውጽኦ! ጸሎት ኣዝያ ሓያል ምዃና ተማህሩ። ብሓንሳብ ምስ እንጽሊ ተስፋ ምስ ዘይንቈርጽ ኣምላኽ ይሰምዕ ኣዝዩ ዘደንቕ ነገራት ድማ ይገብር። ካብ ምጽላይ ፈጺምኩም ኣይትቋረጹ!
Kemey zibele be'al! Amlakh tselotom semi'u niPetros natsa awts'o! Tselot azya hayal mikwana temahru. Bihansab mis intsili tesfa mis zeyniqorists Amlakh yisemi'i aziyu zidenqi negerat dima yigebir. Kab mitslay fetsimon aytiqwaretsu!

Moral of the Story / ትምህርቲ ዛንታ

Prayer is powerful! God hears our prayers and can do miracles. The believers prayed and prayed, and God sent an angel to free Peter from prison! When we pray together and don't give up, God listens. Never stop praying for others — God can do anything!
ጸሎት ሓያል ኢያ! ኣምላኽ ጸሎትና ይሰምዕ ተኣምራት ድማ ክገብር ይኽእል። እቶም ኣመንቲ ጸለዩን ጸለዩን ኣምላኽ ድማ ንጴጥሮስ ካብ ቤት ማእሰርቲ ከናግፎ መልኣኽ ለኣኸ! ብሓንሳብ ምስ እንጽሊ ተስፋ ምስ ዘይንቈርጽ ኣምላኽ ይሰምዕ። ንኻልኦት ካብ ምጽላይ ፈጺምኩም ኣይትቋረጹ — ኣምላኽ ኩሉ ነገር ክገብር ይኽእል!
Tselot hayal iya! Amlakh tselotna yisemi'i te'amrat dima kigebir yikhil. Itom aminti tseleyu'n tseleyu'n Amlakh dima niPetros kab biet ma'iserti kenagfo mel'akh le'akhe! Bihansab mis intsili tesfa mis zeyniqorists Amlakh yisemi'i. Nikhal'ot kab mitslay fetsimon ayitiqwaretsu — Amlakh kulu neger kigebir yikhil!

Let's Pray Together / ብሓንሳብ ንጸሊ

Dear God, thank You for hearing our prayers. Help us to never stop praying, even when things seem impossible. Thank You that You are powerful and can do miracles. Help us to pray for others just like the believers prayed for Peter. We trust You with all our hearts. Amen!
ዝኸበርካ ኣምላኽ፡ ጸሎትና ስለ ትሰምዕ ነመስግነካ። ነገራት ዘይከኣል ኮይኑ ምስ ዝስመዓና እውን ካብ ምጽላይ ከይነቋርጽ ሓግዘና። ሓያል ምዃንካን ተኣምራት ክትገብር ከም ትኽእልን ነመስግነካ። ከምቶም ኣመንቲ ንጴጥሮስ ዝጸለዩ ንኻልኦት ክንጽሊ ሓግዘና። ብምሉእ ልብና ንኣምነካ። ኣሜን!
