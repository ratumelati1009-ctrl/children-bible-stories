Elijah and the Fire from Heaven

ኤልያስን ሓዊ ካብ ሰማይን

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 15

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 15

For all the children who are brave enough to stand up for what is right.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ ንቕኑዕ ነገር ደው ክብሉ ትብዓት ዘለዎም።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A strong brave man named Elijah standing alone on a hilltop, wearing a rough cloak, looking determined. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
A long time ago in Israel, there was a brave man of God named Elijah. He was a prophet - someone who speaks God's messages to the people.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ቅድሚ ግዜ ኣብ እስራኤል ኤልያስ ዝስሙ ጅግና ናይ ኣምላኽ ሰብ ነይሩ። ንሱ ነቢይ ነይሩ - መልእኽቲ ኣምላኽ ንሰባት ዝነግር ሰብ።
(Pronunciation: ኤልያስ = Elyas = Elijah, ነቢይ = nebiy = prophet, ጅግና = jigna = brave)

Page 2

[PICTURE: Evil King Ahab and Queen Jezebel on thrones, with a golden idol of Baal beside them, looking cruel. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
But the king of Israel was a bad king named Ahab. His wife Jezebel was very wicked. They told the people to worship a false god called Baal instead of the true God.
TIGRINYA:
ግን ንጉስ እስራኤል ኣክኣብ ዝስሙ ሕማቕ ንጉስ ነይሩ። ሰበይቱ ኤልዛቤል ኣዝያ ኽፍእቲ ነበረት። ንሰባት ኣብ ክንዲ ሓቀኛ ኣምላኽ ንበዓል ዝብሃል ሓሶት ኣምላኽ ክሰግዱ ነገሩዎም።
(Pronunciation: ኣክኣብ = Ak'ab = Ahab, በዓል = Be'al = Baal, ሓቀኛ = Haqenya = true)

Page 3

[PICTURE: People of Israel bowing down to a golden idol of Baal, looking confused and sad, turning away from God. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Many people in Israel started worshiping Baal. They forgot about the true God who loved them. This made God very sad. The people were making a big mistake.
TIGRINYA:
ብዙሓት ሰባት ኣብ እስራኤል ንበዓል ክሰግዱ ጀመሩ። ነቲ ዝፈትዎም ሓቀኛ ኣምላኽ ረስዑዎ። እዚ ንኣምላኽ ኣዝዩ ኣሕዚኑዎ። እቶም ሰባት ዓቢ ጌጋ ይገብሩ ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ክሰግዱ = kisegdu = to worship, ረስዑዎ = res'uwo = forgot him, ጌጋ = gega = mistake)

Page 4

[PICTURE: Elijah standing bravely before King Ahab, pointing his finger, warning about no rain, Ahab looking angry. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Elijah went to King Ahab with a message from God. "There will be no rain in this land until I say so!" Elijah said bravely. Ahab was very angry!
TIGRINYA:
ኤልያስ ምስ መልእኽቲ ኣምላኽ ናብ ንጉስ ኣክኣብ ከደ። "ኣነ ክሳብ ዝብል ኣብ'ዛ ሃገር ዝናብ ኣይክህሉን!" ኤልያስ ብትብዓት በለ። ኣክኣብ ኣዝዩ ሓረቐ!
(Pronunciation: ዝናብ = zinab = rain, ብትብዓት = bit'ib'at = bravely, ሓረቐ = Hareqe = was angry)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Dry cracked ground, dead plants, people looking thirsty, showing the land without rain for a long time. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
And just as Elijah said, no rain came! For three whole years the land was dry. Plants died, rivers dried up, and people were very thirsty and hungry.
TIGRINYA:
ከምቲ ኤልያስ ዝበሎ ዝናብ ኣይመጸን! ንሰለስተ ምሉእ ዓመታት ምድሪ ነቐጸት። ተኽልታት ሞቱ ኣፍላጋት ነጸፉ ሰባት ድማ ኣዝዮም ጸምኡን ጠመዩን።
(Pronunciation: ዝናብ = zinab = rain, ነቐጸት = neqets'et = dried up, ዓመታት = 'ametat = years)

Page 6

[PICTURE: Elijah standing on a hilltop, sending a message to King Ahab, challenging him to meet on Mount Carmel. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
After three years, God told Elijah it was time! Elijah sent a message to King Ahab: "Meet me on Mount Carmel. Bring all 450 prophets of Baal!"
TIGRINYA:
ድሕሪ ሰለስተ ዓመት ኣምላኽ ንኤልያስ ግዜኡ ምዃኑ ነገሮ! ኤልያስ ንንጉስ ኣክኣብ መልእኽቲ ለኣኸ: "ኣብ ከረን ቀርሜሎስ ተራኸበኒ። ኩሎም 450 ነቢያት በዓል ኣምጽእ!"
(Pronunciation: ከረን = keren = mount, ቀርሜሎስ = Qermelos = Carmel, ነቢያት = nebiyat = prophets)

Page 7

[PICTURE: A huge crowd gathering on Mount Carmel - the people of Israel, 450 prophets of Baal, and Elijah standing alone on one side. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Everyone gathered on Mount Carmel - all the people, the 450 prophets of Baal, and brave Elijah all by himself. It was one man against 450!
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ከረን ቀርሜሎስ ኩሎም ተኣከቡ - ኩሎም ሰባት 450 ነቢያት በዓል ጅግና ኤልያስ ድማ በይኑ። ሓደ ሰብ ኣንጻር 450 ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: ተኣከቡ = te'akebu = gathered, በይኑ = beynu = alone, ኣንጻር = ants'ar = against)

Page 8

[PICTURE: Elijah speaking boldly to the crowd of Israelites, arms raised, asking them to choose between God and Baal. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Elijah shouted to the people, "How long will you go back and forth? If the Lord is God, follow Him! If Baal is god, follow him! You must choose!"
TIGRINYA:
ኤልያስ ንሰባት ጨደረ "ክሳብ መኣስ ናብዝን ናብትን ክትብሉ ኢኹም? ጎይታ ኣምላኽ ዲዩ ስዓቡዎ! በዓል ኣምላኽ ዲዩ ስዓቡዎ! ክትመርጹ ኣለኩም!"
(Pronunciation: ጨደረ = chedere = shouted, ስዓቡዎ = si'abuwo = follow him, ክትመርጹ = kitmerts'u = choose)

Page 9

[PICTURE: Elijah explaining the contest rules - two altars with wood and a bull, showing that the real God will send fire. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Elijah said, "Let us have a contest! We will each build an altar with wood and a sacrifice. The God who sends fire from heaven - He is the true God!"
TIGRINYA:
ኤልያስ "ውድድር ንግበር!" በለ። "ነፍሲ ወከፍና ምስ ዕንጨይትን መስዋእትን መሰውኢ ንሃንጽ። ካብ ሰማይ ሓዊ ዝልእኽ ኣምላኽ - ንሱ'ዩ ሓቀኛ ኣምላኽ!"
(Pronunciation: ውድድር = widdir = contest, መሰውኢ = mesew'i = altar, ሓዊ = Hawi = fire)

Page 10

[PICTURE: The 450 prophets of Baal dancing wildly around their altar, shouting and calling to Baal, but nothing happening. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The prophets of Baal went first. They built their altar and danced and shouted, "Baal, answer us! Send fire!" They danced from morning until noon. But nothing happened!
TIGRINYA:
ነቢያት በዓል ቅድም ጀመሩ። መሰውኢኦም ሃኒጾም ሳዕሲዖምን ጨዲሮምን "በዓል መልሰልና! ሓዊ ስደድ!" ካብ ንግሆ ክሳብ ቀትሪ ሳዕሰዑ። ግን ምንም ኣይኮነን!
(Pronunciation: ሳዕሲዖም = sa'isi'om = danced, ጨዲሮም = chedirom = shouted, ምንም = minim = nothing)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Elijah watching calmly with a slight smile, the prophets of Baal exhausted and frustrated, no fire on their altar. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Elijah teased them. "Shout louder! Maybe Baal is sleeping or on vacation!" The prophets shouted louder and louder but still nothing happened. Baal was not real!
TIGRINYA:
ኤልያስ ሰሓቖም። "ኣብሊጽኩም ጨድሩ! ምናልባት በዓል ደቂሱ ወይ ኣብ ዕረፍቲ ይኸውን!" ነቢያት ኣብሊጾም ጨደሩ ግን ገና ምንም ኣይኮነን። በዓል ሓቀኛ ኣይነበረን!
(Pronunciation: ሰሓቖም = seHaqom = teased them, ደቂሱ = deqisu = sleeping, ሓቀኛ = Haqenya = real)

Page 12

[PICTURE: Elijah building his altar carefully with twelve stones, placing wood and the sacrifice on top, people watching. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Now it was Elijah's turn! He built an altar with twelve stones - one for each tribe of Israel. He put wood and the sacrifice on top. Then he did something surprising!
TIGRINYA:
ሕጂ ግዜ ኤልያስ ኮነ! ብዓሰርተ ክልተ ኣእማን - ንነፍሲ ወከፍ ነገድ እስራኤል ሓደ - መሰውኢ ሃነጸ። ዕንጨይትን መስዋእትን ኣብ ልዕሊ ኣቐመጠ። ድሕሪኡ ዘገርም ነገር ገበረ!
(Pronunciation: ኣእማን = a'iman = stones, ነገድ = neged = tribe, ዘገርም = zegerm = surprising)

Page 13

[PICTURE: Workers pouring large jars of water over Elijah's altar, the wood getting completely soaked, water filling a trench around it. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Elijah said, "Pour water all over the altar!" They poured water once, twice, three times! The wood was soaking wet. Water filled a ditch all around it. Now fire would be impossible!
TIGRINYA:
ኤልያስ "ኣብ ልዕሊ መሰውኢ ማይ ኩዑ!" በለ። ሓደ ግዜ ክልተ ግዜ ሰለስተ ግዜ ማይ ኸዑ! ዕንጨይቲ ኣዝያ ጠልቂያ ነበረት። ማይ ኣብ ዙርያ ዘሎ ጎደጕዲ መልኤ። ሕጂ ሓዊ ዘይከኣል ነይሩ!
(Pronunciation: ማይ = may = water, ኩዑ = ku'u = pour, ጠልቂያ = t'elqiya = soaking wet)

Page 14

[PICTURE: Elijah standing before the wet altar, looking up to heaven with hands raised in prayer, a calm confident expression. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Then Elijah prayed a simple prayer. "Lord God of Israel, show these people that You are the one true God! Let them turn back to You today!"
TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: ጸለየ = ts'eleye = prayed, ሓቀኛ = Haqenya = true, ክምለሱ = kimlesu = turn back)

Page 15

[PICTURE: Enormous fire falling from heaven like a lightning bolt, hitting the altar with incredible power, flames everywhere. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
WHOOSH! Fire came down from heaven! It was so powerful! The fire burned up everything - the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, and even the water! God answered!
TIGRINYA:
ውውሽ! ሓዊ ካብ ሰማይ ወረደ! ኣዝዩ ሓያል ነይሩ! እቲ ሓዊ ንኩሉ ኣቃጸሎ - መስዋእቲ ዕንጨይቲ ኣእማን ንማይ'ውን ከይተረፈ! ኣምላኽ መሊሱ!
(Pronunciation: ሓዊ = Hawi = fire, ወረደ = werede = came down, ኣቃጸሎ = aqats'elo = burned it)

Page 16

[PICTURE: All the people falling on their faces in amazement and worship, the fire still burning on the altar, awe everywhere. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
All the people fell on their faces! They were amazed! They cried out, "The Lord, He is God! The Lord, He is God!" Everyone knew the truth now!
TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: ወደቑ = wedequ = fell down, ተገሪሞም = tegeriom = amazed, ሓቂ = Haqi = truth)

[PICTURE: The people of Israel standing up and celebrating, hugging each other, turning back to the true God with joy. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The people turned back to God that day! They were sorry for following Baal. They knew now that the Lord God of Israel is the only true and living God!

(Pronunciation: ተመልሱ = temelsu = turned back, ተጣዒሶም = tet'a'isom = sorry, ህያው = hiyaw = living)

Page 18

[PICTURE: Elijah kneeling in prayer on the mountaintop, looking toward the sky, asking God to send rain after three years. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Then Elijah prayed for rain. He bent down low and asked God to send water for the thirsty land. He prayed and prayed, trusting God completely.
TIGRINYA:
ድሕሪኡ ኤልያስ ንዝናብ ጸለየ። ጎቢጡ ንእታ ጸምኢት ምድሪ ማይ ክሰድድ ንኣምላኽ ሓተተ። ንኣምላኽ ብምሉእ እምነት ጸለየን ጸለየን።
(Pronunciation: ዝናብ = zinab = rain, ጸምኢት = ts'em'it = thirsty, እምነት = imnet = faith/trust)

Page 19

[PICTURE: A small cloud appearing in the distant sky over the sea, Elijah's servant pointing at it excitedly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Elijah sent his servant to look at the sky seven times. Finally, the servant said, "I see a tiny cloud!" That little cloud was God's answer - rain was coming!

(Pronunciation: ደመና = demena = cloud, ሸውዓተ = shew'ate = seven, ዝናብ = zinab = rain)

Page 20

[PICTURE: Dark rain clouds filling the sky, heavy rain pouring down on the dry land, people dancing and celebrating in the rain. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
