Paul's Shipwreck

ጳውሎስ መርከብ ተሰቢራቶ

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: Paul standing with his hands bound in chains, guarded by a Roman soldier on a busy dock. A large wooden sailing ship waits in the harbor. Other prisoners stand nearby. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Paul was a brave man who loved Jesus and told everyone about Him. But some people didn't like his message. Paul was arrested and made a prisoner. Now he was being taken to Rome by ship to stand before the emperor.
ጳውሎስ ንየሱስ ዘፍቅርን ንኹሉ ሰብ ብዛዕባኡ ዝነግርን ጅግና ሰብ ነበረ። ግን ገሊኦም ሰባት ንመልእኽቱ ኣይፈተዉን። ጳውሎስ ተኣሲሩ እሱር ኮነ። ሕጂ ኣብ ቅድሚ ንጉስ ንኽቐርብ ብመርከብ ናብ ሮማ ይውሰድ ነበረ።
(Pawlos niyesus zefqir'n nikhulu seb biza'iba'u zinegir'n jigna seb nebere. Gin geli'om sebat nimel'ikhtu ayfetwun. Pawlos te'asiru isur kone. Hiji ab qidmi nigus nikiqerib bimerkebi nab Roma yiwsed nebere.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Paul boarding a large wooden ship with other prisoners and Roman soldiers. A kind centurion named Julius walks beside Paul, treating him with respect. The harbor is busy with merchants and sailors. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
A Roman centurion named Julius was in charge of Paul and the other prisoners. Julius was kind to Paul and treated him with respect. They boarded a big ship with many other passengers and sailors.
ዩልዮስ ዝስሙ ሓለቓ ሚእቲ ሮማዊ ንጳውሎስን ነቶም ካልኦት እሱራትን ይሕልዎም ነበረ። ዩልዮስ ንጳውሎስ ለዋህ ነበረ ብኽብሪ ድማ ይሕዞ ነበረ። ምስ ብዙሓት ተሳፈርትን መርከበኛታትን ናብ ዓባይ መርከብ ደየቡ።
(Yulyos zismu haleqa mi'iti Romawi niPawlos'n netom kal'ot isuratni yihlwom nebere. Yulyos niPawlos lewah nebere bikhbri dima yihizo nebere. Mis buzuhat tesafert'n merkebenyatat'n nab abay merkebi deyebu.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: The ship sailing along the Mediterranean coast with strong winds blowing against it. The sails strain and the waves are choppy. Sailors pull on ropes trying to control the ship. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The ship sailed along the coast, but the winds were against them. The journey was slow and very difficult. The sailors had to work extra hard just to keep the ship moving forward.
እታ መርከብ ኣብ ገማግም ባሕሪ ተጓዕዘት፡ ግን ንፋስ ይጻረሮም ነበረ። እቲ ጉዕዞ ዝሕታልን ኣዝዩ ከቢድን ነበረ። መርከበኛታት ነታ መርከብ ንቕድሚት ንምውሳድ ብዙሕ ክጽዕሩ ነበሮም።
(Ita merkebi ab gemagim bahri tegwa'izet, gin nifas yitsarerom nebere. Iti gu'izo zihtal'n azyu kebid'n nebere. Merkebenyatat neta merkebi niqidmit nimiwsad buzuh kitsi'iru neberom.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Paul standing on the deck of the ship, speaking earnestly to a group of sailors and the centurion. He gestures with concern, pointing at the dark clouds gathering on the horizon. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When they stopped at a harbor, Paul warned everyone: "Friends, I can see that this voyage is going to be very dangerous. We could lose the cargo, the ship, and even our lives!" Paul had traveled by sea many times before and knew the danger.
ኣብ ወደብ ምስ ደው በሉ ጳውሎስ ንኹሉ ሰብ ኣጠንቀቐ፡ "ኣሕዋት፡ እዚ ጉዕዞ ኣዝዩ ሓደገኛ ክኸውን ምዃኑ ይርኣየኒ ኣሎ። ጽዕነት፡ መርከብ፡ ሂወትና'ውን ከይተረፈ ክንስእን ንኽእል ኢና!" ጳውሎስ ቅድሚ ሕጂ ብዙሕ ግዜ ብባሕሪ ተጓዒዙ ነቲ ሓደጋ ይፈልጦ ነበረ።
(Ab wedeb mis dew belu Pawlos nikhulu seb atenkeke: "Ahwat, izi gu'izo azyu hadegena kikhewn mika'nu yira'yeni alo. Tsi'inet, merkebi, hiwetna'wun keyterefe kinis'in nikhil ina!" Pawlos qidmi hiji buzuh gize bibahri tegwa'izu neti hadega yifelt'o nebere.)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: The ship's captain and the centurion huddled together, ignoring Paul's advice. They shake their heads dismissively while pointing toward the open sea. Paul watches from behind with a worried expression. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But the captain and the centurion did not listen to Paul. They thought the harbor was not good enough to stay in for winter. "We can make it to the next port!" they said. They decided to sail on, ignoring Paul's warning.
ግን እቲ ካፒቴንን ሓለቓ ሚእትን ንጳውሎስ ኣይሰምዕዎን። እቲ ወደብ ንክረምቲ ንምጽናሕ ጽቡቕ ኣይኮነን ኢሎም ሓሰቡ። "ናብቲ ዝቕጽል ወደብ ክንበጽሕ ንኽእል ኢና!" በሉ። ንመጠንቀቕታ ጳውሎስ ዘይሰምዑ ክጸድፉ ወሰኑ።
(Gin iti kapiten'n haleqa mi'it'n niPawlos aysemi'won. Iti wedeb nikremti nimitsnihi tsibuq aykonen ilom hasebu. "Nabti ziqtsil wedeb kinbetsihi nikhil ina!" belu. Nimetenkeqta Pawlos zeyisem'u kitsedf u wesenu.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: The sky turning dark and terrifying as enormous storm clouds roll in. The sea becomes wild with huge waves. The ship is tossed violently. Lightning flashes across the sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Soon after they set sail, a terrible storm hit! The wind howled and screamed. Huge waves crashed over the ship. The sky turned black with clouds. It was the most frightening storm anyone had ever seen!
ጸድፎም ድሕሪ ቑሩብ ግዜ ዘፍርሕ ማዕበል ወረዶም! ንፋስ ሓሪቑ ይጭድር ነበረ። ዓበይቲ ማዕበላት ነታ መርከብ ይዘብጥዋ ነበሩ። ሰማይ ብደበና ጸልመተ። ዝኾነ ሰብ ዝረኣዮ ኣዝዩ ዘፍርሕ ማዕበል ነበረ!
(Tsedfom dihri qurub gize zefrihi ma'ibel weredom! Nifas hariqu yichdir nebere. Abeyti ma'ibelat neta merkebi yizebitwa neberu. Semay bidebena tselmerete. Zikhone seb zire'ayo azyu zefrihi ma'ibel nebere!)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Sailors desperately tying ropes around the ship's hull to hold it together. Waves crash over the deck. Men slip and slide as the ship rocks violently back and forth. Rain pours down heavily. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The storm did not stop! Day after day, the wind howled and the waves crashed. The sailors tied ropes around the ship to keep it from breaking apart. Everyone was terrified as the ship was thrown around like a toy.
እቲ ማዕበል ኣይደውን! መዓልቲ ብድሕሪ መዓልቲ ንፋስ ይጭድር ማዕበላት ድማ ይጻፍዑ ነበሩ። መርከበኛታት ከይትሰባበር ነታ መርከብ ብገመድ ኣሰርዋ። እታ መርከብ ከም ጸዋር ክትድርበ ኸላ ኩሎም ኣዝዮም ፈርሑ።
(Iti ma'ibel aydewn! Me'alti bidihri me'alti nifas yichdir ma'ibelat dima yitsaf'u neberu. Merkebenyatat keytisebabeir neta merkebi bigemedaserwa. Ita merkebi kem tsewar kitdirbe khela kulom azyom ferhu.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Sailors and passengers throwing heavy boxes, barrels of cargo, and equipment overboard into the raging sea. Their faces show desperation. The ship rides dangerously low in the water. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The sailors threw the cargo overboard to make the ship lighter. They even threw the ship's equipment into the sea! They did everything they could think of to survive, but the storm just kept getting worse.
መርከበኛታት ነታ መርከብ ንምቅላል ጽዕነት ናብ ባሕሪ ደርበዩ። ናይ መርከብ ኣቑሑት'ውን ናብ ባሕሪ ደርበዩ! ንኽድሕኑ ዝሓሰብዎ ኩሉ ገበሩ፡ ግን እቲ ማዕበል ይገድድ ጥራይ ነበረ።
(Merkebenyatat neta merkebi nimiqilal tsi'inet nab bahri derbeyu. Nay merkebi aquhut'wun nab bahri derbeyu! Nikidihinnu zihasebwo kulu geberu, gin iti ma'ibel yigeded tiray nebere.)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Passengers huddled together below deck in darkness, looking hopeless and exhausted. Some cry, some stare blankly. No sun or stars visible through a small porthole - only dark clouds and rain. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
For many days, they could not see the sun or the stars. No one knew where they were. The people had not eaten for a long time. Everyone gave up hope of being saved. They thought they were all going to die.
ንብዙሓት መዓልታት ጸሓይ ወይ ከዋኽብቲ ክርእዩ ኣይከኣሉን። ኣበይ ከም ዘለዉ ዝፈልጥ ኣይነበረን። ሰባት ንነዊሕ ግዜ ኣይበልዑን ነበሩ። ኩሎም ናይ ምድሓን ተስፋ ቆረጹ። ኩሎም ክንሞት ኢና ኢሎም ሓሰቡ።
(Nibuzuhat me'altat tsehay wey kewakhbti kir'eyu ayke'alun. Abey kem zilewu zifelit ayneberen. Sebat ninewih gize aybelun neberu. Kulom nay midihan tesfa qoretsu. Kulom kinmot ina ilom hasebu.)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Paul standing up boldly among the frightened passengers below deck. A warm golden light surrounds him as he speaks with confidence. People look up at him with surprise and attention. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Paul stood up among them and said, "Men, you should have listened to me! But take courage! Not one of you will lose your life. Only the ship will be lost. I know this because God told me!"
ግን ጳውሎስ ኣብ ማእከሎም ተንሲኡ "ሰባት፡ ክትሰምዑኒ ነይሩኩም! ግን ተባዓቱ! ካባኹም ሓደ'ኳ ሂወቱ ኣይስእንን። መርከብ ጥራይ'ያ ክትጠፍእ። ኣምላኽ ስለ ዝነገረኒ ይፈልጥ'የ!" በለ።
(Gin Pawlos ab ma'ikelom tensii'u "Sebat, kitismi'uni neyrukum! Gin teba'atu! Kabakhum hade'kwa hiwetu ays'inn. Merkebi tiray'ya kititfif. Amlak sile zinegereni yifelit'ye!" bele.)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: A beautiful glowing angel appearing beside Paul in the dark ship at night. The angel radiates warm golden light. Paul kneels with his hands raised in awe and gratitude. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Paul told them what had happened: "Last night an angel of God came and stood beside me. The angel said, 'Don't be afraid, Paul! You must stand before Caesar. And God has graciously given you the lives of all who sail with you.'"
ጳውሎስ እንታይ ከም ዝኾነ ነገሮም፡ "ትማሊ ለይቲ መልኣኽ ኣምላኽ ኣብ ጥቓይ ደው ኢሉ። እቲ መልኣኽ 'ኣይትፍራሕ ጳውሎስ! ኣብ ቅድሚ ቄሳር ክትቐርብ ኣሎካ። ኣምላኽ ድማ ንኹሎም ምሳኻ ዝጸድፉ ብጸጋ ሂብካዮም'ዩ' በለኒ።"
(Pawlos intay kem zikhone negerom: "Timali leyti mel'ak Amlak ab tiqay dew ilu. Iti mel'ak 'Aytifrahi Pawlos! Ab qidmi Qesar kitqerib aloka. Amlak dima nikhulom misaka zitsedf u bitsega hibkayom'yu' beleni.")
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Paul standing with arms raised encouragingly, speaking to a large crowd of 276 people packed below deck. Some faces show hope returning. Paul's expression is full of faith and confidence. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"So keep up your courage, men!" Paul said with a strong voice. "I have faith in God that it will happen just as He told me. We will run aground on some island, but everyone will be safe!" There were 276 people on that ship, and Paul encouraged every one of them.
"ስለዚ ተባዓቱ ሰባት!" ጳውሎስ ብሓያል ድምጺ በለ። "ከምቲ ዝነገረኒ ክኸውን ኣብ ኣምላኽ እምነት ኣሎኒ። ኣብ ሓንቲ ደሴት ክንዓልብ ኢና፡ ግን ኩሉ ሰብ ክድሕን'ዩ!" ኣብታ መርከብ 276 ሰባት ነበሩ፡ ጳውሎስ ድማ ንኹሎም ኣተባብዖም።
(Silezi teba'atu sebat!" Pawlos bihayali dimtsi bele. "Kemti zinegereni kikhewn ab Amlak imnet aloni. Ab hanti deset kini'alib ina, gin kulu seb kidihin'yu!" Abta merkebi 276 sebat neberu, Pawlos dima nikhulom atebab'om.)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: The storm continuing to rage on day fourteen. The ship is battered and damaged. Sailors peer into the dark night, sensing they are approaching land. One sailor drops a rope with a weight to measure the water depth. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
On the fourteenth night of the storm, the sailors sensed they were getting close to land. They measured the water's depth and found it was getting shallower and shallower! Land must be near!
ኣብ መበል ዓሰርተ ኣርባዕተ ለይቲ ናይቲ ማዕበል መርከበኛታት ናብ ምድሪ ይቀርቡ ከም ዘለዉ ተሰመዖም። ዕምቈት ማይ ሰፍሮም እናሓጸረ ከም ዝኸይድ ረኸቡ! ምድሪ ቀረባ ክትከውን ኣለዋ!
(Ab mebel aserte arba'ite leyti nayti ma'ibel merkebenyatat nab midri yiqerbu kem zilewu teseme'om. Imqot may sefrom enihatere kem zikeyd rekebu! Midri qereba kitkewn alewa!)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Some sailors secretly trying to lower the lifeboat from the ship in the dark, planning to escape and leave the passengers behind. Paul notices them and urgently speaks to the centurion. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Some sailors tried to escape the ship secretly by lowering the lifeboat. But Paul saw them and told the centurion, "Unless these men stay with the ship, you cannot be saved!" So the soldiers cut the ropes and let the lifeboat fall away.
ገሊኦም መርከበኛታት ነታ ንእሽቶ ጃልባ ብምውራድ ብሕቡእ ካብ መርከብ ክሃድሙ ፈተኑ። ግን ጳውሎስ ረኣዮም'ሞ ንሓለቓ ሚእቲ "እዞም ሰባት ኣብ መርከብ እንተዘይተረፉ ክትድሕኑ ኣይትኽእሉን!" በሎ። ስለዚ ወተሃደራት ነቲ ገመድ ቆሪጾም ነታ ጃልባ ሓደግዋ።
(Geli'om merkebenyatat neta ni'ishto jalba bimiwrad bihbu'e kab merkebi kihadeemu fetenu. Gin Pawlos re'ayom'mo nihaleqa mi'iti "Izom sebat ab merkebi intezeyterfu kitdihin'u aytikhlun!" belo. Silezi wetehaderat neti gemed qoritsom neta jalba hadegwa.)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Paul standing before the exhausted passengers, holding up bread and giving thanks to God. People watch him with tired but hopeful eyes. Dawn light begins to show through the ship's openings. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Just before dawn, Paul urged everyone to eat. "You haven't eaten for fourteen days!" he said. "Please eat something — you need strength. Not one hair from your head will be lost!" Then Paul took bread, gave thanks to God, and began to eat.

The Moral of the Story

God is with us in every storm of life. Even when things seem hopeless, God keeps His promises. Trust God and encourage others!

ኣምላኽ ኣብ ኩሉ ማዕበል ሂወትና ምሳና'ዩ። ነገራት ተስፋ ዘይብሉ ኮይኑ ምስ ዝስመዓና'ውን ኣምላኽ ተስፋኡ ይሕሉ'ዩ። ንኣምላኽ ኣምኖ ንኻልኦት ድማ ኣተባባዕ!

(Amlak ab kulu ma'ibel hiwetna misana'yu. Negeerat tesfa zeybilu koynu mis zisme'ana'wun Amlak tesfa'u yihilu'yu. Ni'Amlak amino nikhal'ot dima atebaba'e!)
