Scene 1
[PICTURE: A wealthy, dignified man named Job standing before his vast estate — fields of crops, herds of livestock, and a large family home. He looks upward in prayer with hands raised, surrounded by his ten children gathered around him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In the ancient land of Uz, there lived a man named Job — a man whose integrity was unmatched in all the earth. He was enormously wealthy, owning thousands of sheep, camels, oxen, and donkeys. But what truly set Job apart was not his wealth; it was his unwavering devotion to God. Every morning, he rose early to offer sacrifices on behalf of his ten children, praying that their hearts would remain pure before the Lord.
ኣብታ ጥንታዊት ምድሪ ዑጽ፡ ኢዮብ ዝተበሃለ ሰብኣይ ይነብር ነበረ — ኣብ ኩላ ምድሪ ቅንዕንኡ ዘይወዳደር ሰብ። ኣዝዩ ሃብታም ነበረ፡ ኣሽሓት ኣባጊዕ፡ ኣግማል፡ ኣብዑርን ኣእዱግን ዝውንን። ግን ንኢዮብ ፍሉይ ዝገበሮ ሃብቱ ኣይኮነን፡ ዘይተናወጸ ውፉይነቱ ንኣምላኽ'ዩ። ኩሉ ንግሆ ቀልጢፉ ተንሲኡ ብስም ዓሰርተ ደቁ መስዋእቲ የቕርብ ነበረ፡ ልቦም ኣብ ቅድሚ እግዚኣብሄር ንጹህ ክኸውን ይጽሊ።
(Ab'ta tintawit midri Uts, Iyob zitebehale seb'ay yinebir nebere — ab kula midri qin'innu zeywedader seb. Azyu habtam nebere, ashihat abagi'e, agmal, ab'ur'n a'idug'n ziwinin. Gin ni'Iyob filuy zigebero habtu aykonien, zeytenawetse wifuyinetu ni'Amlak'yu. Kulu nigho qeltsifu tensiu bisim aserte dequ meswi'eti yeqrib nebere, libom ab qidmi Igzi'abher nitsuh kikhewn yitsli.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: A heavenly throne room with brilliant light. Angels stand before God's presence, and among them a dark, cunning figure — Satan — steps forward with a sly expression. The contrast between divine light and Satan's shadow is striking. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One day in the courts of heaven, the angels came to present themselves before God, and Satan came among them. The Lord asked Satan, "Have you considered my servant Job? There is no one on earth like him — blameless, upright, one who fears God and shuns evil." Satan sneered and replied, "Does Job fear God for nothing? You have put a hedge of protection around him. Take away everything he has, and he will surely curse you to your face."
ሓደ መዓልቲ ኣብ ኣደራሽ ሰማይ፡ መላእኽቲ ኣብ ቅድሚ ኣምላኽ ክቐርቡ ምስ መጹ፡ ሰይጣን ድማ ኣብ ማእከሎም ነበረ። እግዚኣብሄር ንሰይጣን "ንባርያይ ኢዮብ ኣስተብሂልካዮ'ዶ? ኣብ ምድሪ ከማኡ ዝበለ የልቦን — ንጹህ፡ ቅኑዕ፡ ንኣምላኽ ዝፈርሕን ካብ ክፉእ ዝርሕቕን" በሎ። ሰይጣን ብንዕቀት "ኢዮብ ብኸምኡ ንኣምላኽ ዝፈርሖ'ዶ? ዕርዲ ምሕላው ኣብ ዙርያኡ ኣቐሚጥካሉ። ኩሉ ዘለዎ ውሰድ እሞ ብርግጽ ኣብ ገጽካ ክረግመካ'ዩ" ኢሉ መለሰ።
(Hade me'alti ab aderash semay, mela'ikhti ab qidmi Amlak kiqerbu mis metsu, Seytan dima ab ma'iklalom nebere. Igzi'abher ni'Seytan "Ni'baryay Iyob astebhilkayo'do? Ab midri kemau zibele yelbon — nitsuh, qinu'e, ni'Amlak ziferih'n kab kifu'e zirhiq'n" belo. Seytan bini'eqet "Iyob bikemau ni'Amlak ziferiho'do? Irdi mihlaw ab zuryau aqemitikalu. Kulu zilewo wised imo birigts ab getska kiregmeka'yu" ilu melese.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: God on His throne, with a solemn expression, extending His hand in permission. Satan bows mockingly and retreats into shadow. A faint image of Job's peaceful estate appears below, about to be shattered. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God, in His sovereign wisdom, allowed the test. "Very well," He said to Satan. "Everything he has is in your power, but you must not lay a finger on Job himself." This was not because God was cruel or uncaring — it was because God knew the depth of Job's faith, even when Job himself did not yet understand how deep it ran. Sometimes God permits suffering not to destroy us, but to reveal what is truly inside us.
ኣምላኽ ብልዑል ጥበቡ ነቲ ፈተና ፈቐደ። "ጽቡቕ" ንሰይጣን በሎ። "ኩሉ ዘለዎ ኣብ ኢድካ'ዩ፡ ግን ንሱ ባዕሉ ኣይትንከኦ።" እዚ ኣምላኽ ጨካን ወይ ዘይግደስ ስለ ዝኾነ ኣይኮነን — ኣምላኽ ንዕምቈት እምነት ኢዮብ ይፈልጦ ነበረ፡ ኢዮብ ባዕሉ'ውን ክሳብ ክንደይ ዓሚቕ ምዃና ገና ኣይተረደኦን ነበረ። ሓደ ሓደ ግዜ ኣምላኽ ስቓይ ዝፈቅድ ንኸጥፍኣና ኣይኮነን፡ ብሓቂ ኣብ ውሽጥና ዘሎ ንኸርኢ ኢዩ።
(Amlak bil'ul tsibebu neti fetena feqede. "Tsibuq" ni'Seytan belo. "Kulu zilewo ab idka'yu, gin nisu ba'ilu aytinkeo." Izi Amlak chekkan wey zeygides sile zikhone aykonien — Amlak ni'imqot imnet Iyob yifelto nebere, Iyob ba'ilu'wun kisab kindey amiq mikwana gena ayteredeon nebere. Hade hade gize Amlak siqay zifeqid kiatifi'ana aykonien, bihaqi ab wishitna zelo nikheri'i iyu.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: A terrified messenger running toward Job, clothes torn, face filled with panic. Behind him in the distance, raiders are driving away oxen and donkeys while fire falls from the sky onto sheep in another field. Chaos and destruction everywhere. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then disaster struck with devastating speed. One messenger after another came running to Job with terrible news. "The Sabeans attacked and took your oxen and donkeys — your servants are dead!" Before he finished speaking, another arrived: "Fire fell from heaven and burned up your sheep and shepherds!" Then another: "The Chaldeans raided your camels and killed the servants!" Each blow came without warning, without reason, without mercy.
ድሕሪኡ ጥፍኣት ብኣስደማሚ ቅልጣፈ ወረደ። ሓደ ልኡኽ ድሕሪ ሓደ ናብ ኢዮብ እናጎየየ ዘስካሕክሕ ዜና ኣምጸአ። "ሰብኣውያን ኣጥቂዖም ኣብዑርን ኣእዱግን ወሲዶም — ኣገልገልትኻ ሞይቶም!" ምዝራብ ከይወድአ ካልእ በጺሑ፡ "ሓዊ ካብ ሰማይ ወዲቑ ኣባጊዕካን ጓሶትን ኣንዲዱ!" ድሕሪኡ ካልእ፡ "ከለዳውያን ኣግማልካ ዘሪፎም ኣገልገልቲ ድማ ቀቲሎም!" ነፍሲ ወከፍ ማህረምታ ብዘይ ማእከሮ፡ ብዘይ ምኽንያት፡ ብዘይ ርሕራሐ መጸ።
(Dihri'u tifi'at bi'asdimami qiltafe werede. Hade li'ukh dihri hade nab Iyob inagoyeye zeskahikihi zena amtse'a. "Seb'awyan atqi'om ab'ur'n a'idug'n wesidom — agelgeltkha moytom!" Mizrab keywede'a kal'e betsihiu, "Hawi kab semay wediqwu abagi'eka'n guasot'n andidu!" Dihri'u kal'e, "Keledawyan agmalka zerifom agelgelti dima qetilom!" Nefsi wekef mahremta bizey ma'ikero, bizey mikhinyat, bizey rihraha metse.)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: The final messenger arrives, weeping uncontrollably. Job's face turns from shock to absolute devastation. In the background, a collapsed house is shown — the ruins where his children had been feasting together. Dark storm clouds above. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But the worst blow was yet to come. A final messenger stumbled in, barely able to speak through his tears: "Your sons and daughters were feasting in your eldest son's house when a mighty wind swept in from the desert and collapsed the house upon them. They are all dead." In a single day, Job lost everything — his livestock, his servants, and all ten of his beloved children. The life he had built over decades was reduced to ashes in mere hours.
ግን እቲ ዝኸፍአ ማህረምታ ገና'ዩ ዝመጽእ ዝነበረ። ናይ መወዳእታ ልኡኽ እናተንበርከኸ ብብኽያቱ ዳርጋ ክዛረብ ኣይክእልን ነበረ፡ "ኣወዳትካን ኣዋልድካን ኣብ ቤት ዓብዪ ወድኻ ይበልዑ ነበሩ እሞ ሓያል ንፋስ ካብ በረኻ መጺኡ ነቲ ቤት ኣብ ልዕሊኦም ኣፍሪሱዎ። ኩሎም ሞይቶም።" ኣብ ሓንቲ መዓልቲ ኢዮብ ኩሉ ነገሩ ሰኣነ — ማሉ፡ ኣገልገልቱ፡ ኩሎም ዓሰርተ ፍቁራት ደቁ። እቲ ብዓሰርተታት ዓመታት ዝሃነጾ ሂወት ኣብ ውሑዳት ሰዓታት ናብ ሓሙኽሽቲ ተቐየረ።
(Gin iti zikhefi'a mahremta gena'yu zimets'e zinebere. Nay mewedi'eta li'ukh inatenberkekhe bib'khyatu darga kizareb aykhilin nebere, "Awedatka'n awalid'ka'n ab bet ab'yi wedkha yibel'u neberu imo hayal nifas kab berekha metsiu neti bet ab li'eliom afrisuwo. Kulom moytom." Ab hanti me'alti Iyob kulu negeru se'ane — malu, agelgeltu, kulom aserte fiquray dequ. Iti bi'asertetat ametat zihanetso hiwet ab wihiudat se'atat nab hamukishti teqeyere.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Job tearing his robes and shaving his head in grief. He falls to the ground on his knees, face toward the earth, but his posture is one of worship, not despair. Tears stream down his face, yet his hands are open in surrender. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Job's response in that moment of unimaginable grief reveals the depth of a faith forged over a lifetime. He tore his robe, shaved his head — the ancient signs of deepest mourning — and then he fell to the ground. But he did not fall in despair. He fell in worship. "Naked I came from my mother's womb," he said, "and naked I will depart. The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away. Blessed be the name of the Lord." In his darkest hour, Job chose to bless God rather than blame Him.
ኣብቲ ክሕሰብ ዘይክእል ሓዘን እቲ ምላሽ ኢዮብ ንዕምቈት ናይ ብምሉእ ዕድሚኡ ዝተሃንጸ እምነት ይገልጽ። ክዳኑ ቀደደ፡ ርእሱ ላጸየ — ናይ ጥንቲ ምልክት ዓሚቕ ሓዘን — ድሕሪኡ ናብ ምድሪ ወደቐ። ግን ብተስፋ ምቑራጽ ኣይወደቐን። ብኣምልኾ ወደቐ። "ካብ ከርሲ ኣደይ ዕሩቕ ወጻእኩ" በለ፡ "ዕሩቕ'ውን ክምለስ'የ። እግዚኣብሄር ሂቡ፡ እግዚኣብሄር'ውን ወሲዱ። ስም እግዚኣብሄር ብሩኽ ይኹን።" ኣብ ዝጸልመተ ሰዓቱ ኢዮብ ንኣምላኽ ካብ ምኽሳስ ክባርኾ መረጸ።
(Ab'ti kihiseb zeykhil hazen iti milash Iyob ni'imqot nay bimilu'e idmi'u zitehanitse imnet yigelits. Kidanu qedede, ri'isu latseye — nay tinti milkit amiq hazen — dihri'u nab midri wedeqe. Gin bitesfa miqurats aywdeqen. Bi'amlkho wedeqe. "Kab kersi adey iruq wetsa'iku" bele, "Iruq'wun kimles'ye. Igzi'abher hibu, Igzi'abher'wun wesidu. Sim Igzi'abher brukh yikhun." Ab zitselmete se'atu Iyob ni'Amlak kab mikhisas kibar'kho meretse.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Satan returning to God's throne room, looking frustrated and angry. God sits calmly on His throne with an expression of quiet confidence. Light radiates from God while Satan stands in shadow. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Satan returned to God's presence, defeated but not finished. God said, "Have you considered my servant Job? He still maintains his integrity, though you incited me against him to ruin him without reason." Satan snarled back, "Skin for skin! A man will give everything to save his own life. Strike his flesh and bones, and he will curse you." God permitted it: "He is in your hands. But you must spare his life." The battle for Job's soul was far from over.
ሰይጣን ናብ ቅድሚ ኣምላኽ ተመልሰ፡ ተሳዒሩ ግን ኣይወድአን። ኣምላኽ "ንባርያይ ኢዮብ ኣስተብሂልካዮ'ዶ? ብዘይ ምኽንያት ንኸጥፍኦ ኣላዒልካኒ'ኳ ገና ቅንዕንኡ ይሕዝ ኣሎ" በሎ። ሰይጣን ብቑጥዓ "ቆርበት ብቆርበት! ሰብ ንህይወቱ ኩሉ ነገሩ ይህብ። ስጋኡን ዓጽሙን ውቕዓዮ እሞ ክረግመካ'ዩ" ኢሉ መለሰ። ኣምላኽ ፈቐደ፡ "ኣብ ኢድካ'ዩ። ግን ህይወቱ ዕቀባ።" እቲ ውግእ ንነፍሲ ኢዮብ ገና ኣይወድአን ነበረ።
(Seytan nab qidmi Amlak temelise, tesa'iru gin aywede'an. Amlak "Ni'baryay Iyob astebhilkayo'do? Bizey mikhinyat nikhatfi'o ala'ilkani'kwa gena qin'innu yihiz alo" belo. Seytan biqut'a "Qorbet biqorbet! Seb nihyiwetu kulu negeru yihib. Siga'u'n atsmu'n wiq'ayo imo kiregmeka'yu" ilu melese. Amlak feqede, "Ab idka'yu. Gin hywetu iqeba." Iti wig'e ninefsi Iyob gena aywede'an nebere.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Job sitting among ashes on the ground, his body covered in terrible painful sores from head to toe. He holds a broken piece of pottery, scraping his wounds. His face shows immense physical agony, yet his eyes still look upward. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Satan struck Job with painful sores from the soles of his feet to the crown of his head. The man who had once been the greatest in all the East now sat among ashes, using a broken piece of pottery to scrape his oozing wounds. The pain was relentless, the humiliation complete. Yet even here — broken, diseased, stripped of everything that once defined him — Job did not sin. Physical suffering was added to emotional devastation, but his faith, though shaken, held firm.

The Moral / ትምህርቲ

Suffering does not always mean punishment — sometimes the most faithful people endure the deepest pain. God is sovereign even when we cannot see His plan or understand His purposes. True faith is not the absence of doubt or questions; it is the decision to trust God even in the darkest valleys. It is okay to question God honestly, to bring Him your raw grief and confusion — but in the end, we must trust His wisdom above our own. Our limited perspective cannot contain the fullness of God's purposes. And when we emerge from the fire, we will know Him not just by reputation, but by experience.

ስቓይ ኩሉ ግዜ መቕጻዕቲ ማለት ኣይኮነን — ሓደ ሓደ ግዜ ዝበለጸ እሙናት ሰባት ዝዓመቐ ቃንዛ ይጻወሩ። ንመደቡ ክንርእዮ ወይ ዕላማኡ ክንርደኦ ዘይንኽእል'ኳ ኣምላኽ ልዑል'ዩ። ሓቂ እምነት ጥርጣረን ሕቶታትን ዘይምህላው ኣይኮነን፡ ኣብ ዝጸልመቱ ሽንጭሮታት'ውን ንኣምላኽ ክንኣምን ምውሳን'ዩ። ንኣምላኽ ብቕንዕና ምሕታት፡ ጥረይ ሓዘንካን ምድንጋርካን ናብኡ ምምጻእ ጸገም የብሉን — ግን ኣብ መወዳእታ ንጥበቡ ካብ ናትና ንላዕሊ ክንኣምን ኣለና። ውሱን ኣረኣእያና ምሉእነት ዕላማ ኣምላኽ ክሕዝ ኣይክእልን'ዩ። ካብ ሓዊ ምስ ወጻእና ብስም ጥራይ ዘይኮነ ብተመክሮ ክንፈልጦ ኢና።

Discussion Questions / ሕቶታት ምይይጥ

1. Why do you think God allowed Satan to test Job, even though Job was faithful? What does this tell us about how God views suffering differently than we do?

1. ኢዮብ እሙን'ኳ ኣምላኽ ንሰይጣን ንኢዮብ ክፍትኖ ስለምንታይ ፈቐደ ይመስለካ? ኣምላኽ ንስቓይ ካባና ብኸመይ ፍልይ ኢሉ ከም ዝርእዮ እንታይ ይነግረና?

2. Job's friends assumed that all suffering is punishment for sin. Have you ever seen people make this same mistake today? How should we respond to someone who is suffering?

2. ኣዕሩኽ ኢዮብ ኩሉ ስቓይ መቕጻዕቲ ሓጢኣት'ዩ ኢሎም ገሚቶም። ሰባት ሎሚ'ውን ነዚ ጌጋ ክገብሩ ርኢኻ'ዶ ትፈልጥ? ንዝሳቐ ሰብ ብኸመይ ክንምልስ ኣለና?

3. God never explained to Job why he suffered. Why do you think God chose to reveal His power and sovereignty instead of giving a direct answer? Is that enough for you?

3. ኣምላኽ ንኢዮብ ስለምንታይ ከም ዝሳቐ ፈጺሙ ኣይገለጸሉን። ኣምላኽ ቀጥታ መልሲ ካብ ምሃብ ሓይሉን ልዑልነቱን ከርኢ ስለምንታይ ይመርጽ ይመስለካ? ንዓኻ እኹል ድዩ?

4. Job said, "My ears had heard of you but now my eyes have seen you." What is the difference between knowing about God and truly encountering Him? Have you experienced this shift in your own faith?

4. ኢዮብ "ብእዝነይ ብዛዕባኻ ሰሚዐ ነይረ ግን ሕጂ ዓይነይ ርእዩካ" በለ። ኣብ መንጎ ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ምፍላጥን ብሓቂ ምስኡ ምርኻብን ዘሎ ፍልልይ እንታይ'ዩ? ኣብ እምነትካ ነዚ ለውጢ ኣጋጢሙካ'ዶ ይፈልጥ?

5. How does the story of Job change the way you think about your own trials and hardships? What would you say to a friend who asks, "If God is good, why do bad things happen?"

5. ታሪኽ ኢዮብ ብዛዕባ ናይ ባዕልኻ ፈተናታትን ጸገማትን ዘለካ ኣተሓሳስባ ብኸመይ ይቕይሮ? "ኣምላኽ ሕያዋይ እንተ ኾይኑ ስለምንታይ ሕማቕ ነገራት ይኸውን?" ኢሉ ዝሓተካ ዓርኪ እንታይ ትብሎ?

Prayer / ጸሎት

Lord God, we confess that we do not always understand Your ways. Like Job, we sometimes face pain that makes no sense and silence that feels unbearable. Teach us to trust Your sovereignty even when our circumstances scream otherwise. Give us the courage to bring our honest questions before You, knowing You are big enough to handle our doubts. Help us to hold on to faith in the darkness, believing that You are working even when we cannot see it.
