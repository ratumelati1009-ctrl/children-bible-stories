Deborah the Brave Judge

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A land of Israel under dark clouds, with people looking sad and afraid, soldiers with swords threatening villagers. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Long ago, the people of Israel were in great trouble. A cruel king named Jabin ruled over them and made their lives very hard.
ቀደም ዘመን፡ ህዝቢ እስራኤል ኣብ ዓቢ ጸገም ነበሩ። ጨካን ንጉስ ያቢን ዝተባህለ ይገዝኦም ነበረ፡ ህይወቶም ድማ ኣጸጋሚ ገበረሎም።
Kedem zemen, hizbi Israel ab abi tsegem neberu. Chekan nigus Yabin zitebahle yigez'om nebere, hiywetomu dima atsegami geberalom.
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Iron chariots rolling through a village, with a fierce commander Sisera leading soldiers. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
King Jabin had a powerful commander named Sisera. He had nine hundred iron chariots and used them to frighten the Israelites for twenty years.
ንጉስ ያቢን ሓያል ሓለቓ ሰራዊት ሲሰራ ዝተባህለ ነበሮ። ትሽዓት ሚእቲ ሓጸላዊ ሰረገላ ነበሮ፡ ንዕስራ ዓመት ንእስራኤላውያን የፈራርሆም ነበረ።
Nigus Yabin hayal haleqa serawit Sisera zitebahle nebero. Tish'at mi'eti hatselawi seregela nebero, ni'isra amet ni'Israelawiyan yeferarihom nebere.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: A wise and strong woman, Deborah, sitting under a tall palm tree on a hillside, with people gathered around her. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But God had a special plan. There was a wise and brave woman named Deborah. She was a prophetess and a judge in Israel, and she sat under a palm tree where people came to her for help.
ግናኸ ኣምላኽ ፍሉይ መደብ ነበሮ። ጥበበኛን ጅግንቲን ሰበይቲ ድቦራ ትበሃል ነበረት። ንሳ ነብዪትን ፈራዲትን እስራኤል እያ ነበረት፡ ኣብ ትሕቲ ተምሪ ኮፍ ኢላ ህዝቢ ይመጽኣ ነበሩ።
Ginakhe Amlak filuy medeb nebero. Tbebeñan jigintiin sebeyti Debora tibehal neberet. Nisa nebiytin feraditin Israel iya neberet, ab tihti temri kof ila hizbi yimets'a neberu.
Scene 4
[PICTURE: People lining up under the palm tree — men, women, and children — bringing their problems to Deborah who listens kindly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Every day, people came from near and far to see Deborah. They trusted her wisdom because she listened to God and always gave fair answers.
ኵሉ መዓልቲ፡ ሰባት ካብ ቀረባን ርሑቕን ናብ ድቦራ ይመጹ ነበሩ። ንኣምላኽ ትሰምዕ ስለ ዝነበረት ፍትሓዊ መልሲ ትህብ ስለ ዝነበረት ይኣምንዋ ነበሩ።
Kulu me'alti, sebat kab kereban rihuk'in nab Debora yimetsu neberu. Ni'Amlak tismi' sile zineberet fitihawi melsi tihib sile zineberet yi'aminwa neberu.
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Deborah praying with her eyes closed and hands raised, a soft golden light surrounding her as God speaks to her heart. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One day, God spoke to Deborah's heart. He said, "It is time to free my people from King Jabin. I have a plan."
ሓደ መዓልቲ ኣምላኽ ናብ ልቢ ድቦራ ተዛረበ። "ንህዝበይ ካብ ንጉስ ያቢን ናጻ ናይ ምውጻእ ግዜ በጺሑ ኣሎ። መደብ ኣለኒ" በለ።
Hade me'alti Amlak nab libi Debora tezarebe. "Ni'hizbeyi kab nigus Yabin natsa nay miwts'a'e gize betsiihu alo. Medeb aleni" bele.
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Deborah sending a messenger to find Barak, pointing with authority as she gives instructions. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God told Deborah, "Send for a man named Barak. Tell him to gather ten thousand soldiers and go to Mount Tabor. I will give him victory over Sisera."
ኣምላኽ ንድቦራ "ባራቅ ዝተባህለ ሰብኣይ ጸውዕዮ። ዓሰርተ ሽሕ ሰራዊት ኣኪቡ ናብ ከረን ታቦር ክኸይድ ንገርዮ። ኣብ ልዕሊ ሲሰራ ዓወት ክህቦ እየ" በላ።
Amlak ni'Debora "Barak zitebahle seb'ay tsew'iyo. Aserte shih serawit akibu nab keren Tabor kikeyd nigeryo. Ab li'li Sisera awet kihbo iye" bela.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Barak, a strong warrior, standing before Deborah under the palm tree, looking uncertain and hesitant. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Deborah called for Barak and told him God's message. But Barak was afraid. He said, "I will only go if you come with me, Deborah."
ድቦራ ንባራቅ ጸዊዓ መልእኽቲ ኣምላኽ ነገረቶ። ግናኸ ባራቅ ፈሪሁ ነበረ። "ንስኺ ምሳይ እንተ ዘይመጺእኪ ኣይከይድን እየ ድቦራ" በለ።
Debora ni'Barak tsewi'a mel'ikhti Amlak negereto. Ginakhe Barak ferihu nebere. "Niski misay inte zeymets'iki aykeydin iye Debora" bele.
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Deborah standing tall and confident, agreeing to go with Barak, her expression showing courage and faith. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Deborah said, "I will surely go with you! But because of this, the honor of victory will not be yours — it will go to a woman." Deborah was not afraid because she trusted God.
ድቦራ "ብርግጽ ምሳኻ ክኸይድ እየ! ግናኸ በዚ ምኽንያት፡ ክብሪ ዓወት ናትካ ኣይክኸውንን — ናብ ሰበይቲ ክኸይድ እዩ" በለት። ድቦራ ኣብ ኣምላኽ ስለ ዝኣመነት ኣይፈርሐትን።
Debora "Birgits misaka kikeyd iye! Ginakhe bezi mikinyat, kibri awet natka aykilkewnin — nab sebeyti kikeyd iyu" belet. Debora ab Amlak sile zi'amenet ayferihatsin.
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Barak rallying soldiers from different tribes of Israel, men gathering with spears and shields on a hillside. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
So Barak went and gathered ten thousand brave men from the tribes of Naphtali and Zebulun. They were ready to fight for their freedom.
ስለዚ ባራቅ ከይዱ ዓሰርተ ሽሕ ጅግኑ ሰባት ካብ ነገድ ንፍታሌምን ዘብሎንን ኣኸበ። ንናጽነቶም ክዋግኡ ተዳሊዎም ነበሩ።
Silezi Barak keydu aserte shih jignu sebat kab neged Niftalemn Zeblonin akhebe. Ni'natsinetomu kiwag'u tedaliwomu neberu.
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Deborah and Barak leading the army of Israel up the green slopes of Mount Tabor, with determination on their faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Deborah and Barak led the army up to Mount Tabor. From the top of the mountain, they could see the wide valley below where Sisera's chariots waited.
ድቦራን ባራቅን ንሰራዊት ናብ ከረን ታቦር መርሕዎም። ካብ ርእሲ እቲ ከረን፡ ነቲ ሰፊሕ ለሰ ሲሰራ ሰረገላታቱ ዝጸንሕሉ ይርእይዎ ነበሩ።
Deboran Barak'in ni'serawit nab keren Tabor merhiwom. Kab ri'si iti keren, neti sefih lese Sisera seregelatattu zitsenihlu yir'iywo neberu.
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Deborah raising her hand boldly and calling out to Barak to go forward into battle, wind blowing her garments. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When the time was right, Deborah said to Barak, "Go! This is the day the Lord has given Sisera into your hands. The Lord goes ahead of you!"
ግዜኡ ምስ ኣኸለ ድቦራ ንባራቅ "ኪድ! እዚ እቲ እግዚኣብሄር ንሲሰራ ኣብ ኢድካ ዝሃበካ መዓልቲ እዩ። እግዚኣብሄር ቅድሜኻ ይኸይድ ኣሎ!" በለቶ።
Gize'u mis akhele Debora ni'Barak "Kid! Izi iti Egzi'abher ni'Sisera ab idka zihahbeka me'alti iyu. Egzi'abher kidmeka yikeyd alo!" beleto.
Scene 12
[PICTURE: The Israelite army charging down Mount Tabor with great energy and courage, Barak leading them with his sword raised. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Barak and his ten thousand men rushed down the mountain with great courage. They trusted God's promise and were no longer afraid.
ባራቅን ዓሰርተ ሽሕ ሰቡን ብዓቢ ጅግንነት ካብ ከረን ወረዱ። ናይ ኣምላኽ ተስፋ ኣሚኖም ድሕሪኡ ኣይፈርሁን።
Barak'in aserte shih sebun bi'abi jigninnet kab keren weredu. Nay Amlak tesfa aminomu dihri'u ayferihun.
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Dark storm clouds gathering over the valley, lightning flashing, and heavy rain beginning to pour down from the sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then God sent a mighty storm! Dark clouds filled the sky and heavy rain poured down into the valley. The river Kishon began to flood.
ድሕሪኡ ኣምላኽ ሓያል ማይ ወረደ ሰደደ! ጸሊም ደበና ንሰማይ መልኦ ከቢድ ዝናብ ድማ ናብ ለሰ ወረደ። ርባ ቂሾን ክውሕዝ ጀመረ።
Dihri'u Amlak hayal may werede sedede! Tselim debena ni'semay mel'o kebid zinab dima nab lese werede. Riba Kishon kiwihiz jemere.
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Sisera's iron chariots stuck deep in thick mud, horses struggling, wheels unable to turn, soldiers panicking. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The heavy rain turned the valley into thick mud. Sisera's nine hundred iron chariots got stuck! The wheels could not move. God had trapped the enemy!
እቲ ከቢድ ዝናብ ነቲ ለሰ ናብ ረግረግ ቀየሮ። ትሽዓት ሚእቲ ሓጸላዊ ሰረገላ ሲሰራ ተሸኹ! መንኰርኰር ክንቀሳቐስ ኣይከኣለን። ኣምላኽ ንጸላኢ ኣጻወዶ!
Iti kebid zinab neti lese nab regireg keyero. Tish'at mi'eti hatselawi seregela Sisera tesheKu! Menkorkor kink'esak'es ayke'alen. Amlak ni'tselai atsawedo!
Scene 15
[PICTURE: The Israelite soldiers bravely fighting Sisera's confused army in the muddy valley, with God's storm raging above. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The Israelite soldiers attacked with all their might. Sisera's army was confused and could not fight back. God was giving Israel the victory, just as He promised!
ሰራዊት እስራኤል ብዅሉ ሓይሎም ተዋግኡ። ሰራዊት ሲሰራ ተደናጊሮም ክምልሱ ኣይከኣሉን። ኣምላኽ ከምቲ ዝተመባጽዐ ንእስራኤል ዓወት ይህቦም ነበረ!
Serawit Israel bikulu hayilom tewag'u. Serawit Sisera tedenagirom kimelsu ayke'alun. Amlak kemti zitemebats'e ni'Israel awet yihbom nebere!
Scene 16
[PICTURE: Commander Sisera jumping off his chariot and running away on foot, looking terrified, leaving his army behind. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When Sisera saw that his army was losing, he jumped off his chariot and ran away on foot. The mighty commander was now running in fear!
ሲሰራ ሰራዊቱ ከም ዝስዕር ምስ ረኣየ፡ ካብ ሰረገላኡ ዘሊሉ ብእግሩ ሃደመ። እቲ ሓያል ሓለቓ ሰራዊት ሕጂ ብፍርሒ ይሃድም ነበረ!
Sisera serawitu kem zis'ir mis re'aye, kab seregela'u zeliilu bi'igru hademe. Iti hayal haleqa serawit hiji bifrihi yihadim nebere!
Scene 17
[PICTURE: The entire enemy army scattered and defeated across the muddy valley, with Israelite soldiers celebrating. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Not one of Sisera's soldiers was left. God had completely defeated the enemy army. The people of Israel were finally free from King Jabin's cruelty!
ካብ ሰራዊት ሲሰራ ሓደ እኳ ኣይተረፈን። ኣምላኽ ንሰራዊት ጸላኢ ብምሉኡ ሰዓሮ። ህዝቢ እስራኤል ኣብ መወዳእታ ካብ ጭካነ ንጉስ ያቢን ናጻ ወጹ!
Kab serawit Sisera hade ikwa ayterefen. Amlak ni'serawit tselai bimilu'u se'aro. Hizbi Israel ab mewed'eta kab chikane nigus Yabin natsa wetsu!
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Deborah and Barak standing together on a hilltop, looking over the peaceful valley after the battle, with grateful expressions. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After the battle, Deborah and Barak stood together and looked over the land. God had kept His promise. His people were safe and free.
ድሕሪ ውግእ፡ ድቦራን ባራቅን ሓቢሮም ደው ኢሎም ናብቲ ምድሪ ጠመቱ። ኣምላኽ ተስፋኡ ሓሊዩ። ህዝቡ ውሑሳትን ናጻን ኰኑ።
Dihri wig'i, Deboran Barak'in habiromu dew ilomu nabti midri temettu. Amlak tesfa'u haliyu. Hizbu wihusatin natsain konu.
Scene 19
[PICTURE: Deborah and Barak singing joyfully with raised hands, surrounded by happy Israelite people clapping and praising God. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Deborah and Barak sang a beautiful song to God. They praised Him for His mighty power and for saving Israel. Everyone joined in the celebration!
ድሕሪኡ ድቦራን ባራቅን ንኣምላኽ ጽቡቕ መዝሙር ዘመሩ። ብሓያል ሓይሉን እስራኤል ስለ ዘድሓነን ኣመስገንዎ። ኵሎም ኣብ ብዓል ተሓወሱ!
Dihri'u Deboran Barak'in ni'Amlak tsibuq mezmur zemeru. Bihayal hayilun Israel sile zedihanein amesgenwu. Kulom ab bi'al tehwesu!
Scene 20
[PICTURE: Musical instruments — tambourines, harps, and drums — being played by joyful women and men as they celebrate God's victory. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In their song, Deborah and Barak sang: "When the people offer themselves willingly, praise the Lord! The stars from heaven fought against Sisera!"
ኣብ መዝሙሮም ድቦራን ባራቅን "ህዝቢ ብፍቓዶም ምስ ዝወፍዩ ንእግዚኣብሄር ኣመስግኑ! ከዋኽብቲ ሰማይ ኣንጻር ሲሰራ ተዋግኡ!" ኢሎም ዘመሩ።
Ab mezmuromu Deboran Barak'in "Hizbi bifik'adom mis zewefyu ni'Egzi'abher amesginu! Kewakhbiti semay antsar Sisera tewag'u!" ilomu zemeru.
Scene 21
[PICTURE: Peaceful green fields and happy families working and playing in the land of Israel, with bright sunshine. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After this great victory, the land of Israel had peace for forty years. No enemy bothered them. The people could live, work, and worship God in safety.
ድሕሪ እዚ ዓቢ ዓወት፡ ምድሪ እስራኤል ንኣርብዓ ዓመት ሰላም ረኸበት። ዝዀነ ጸላኢ ኣይረበሾምን። ህዝቢ ብሰላም ክነብሩ፡ ክሰርሑ፡ ንኣምላኽ ክምለኹ ክኢሎም።
Dihri izi abi awet, midri Israel ni'arbi'a amet selam reKebet. Zikone tselai ayrebeshomin. Hizbi biselam kinebru, kiserhu, ni'Amlak kimleKu ki'ilomu.
Scene 22
[PICTURE: Young children sitting around an elder who tells them the story of Deborah, their eyes wide with wonder and inspiration. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The story of Deborah was told to children for many generations. She showed everyone that God can use anyone — whether man or woman, young or old — to do amazing things.
ዛንታ ድቦራ ንብዙሕ ወለዶታት ንቈልዑ ይንገር ነበረ። ኣምላኽ ንዝዀነ ሰብ — ሰብኣይ ኰነ ሰበይቲ፡ ንእሽቶ ኰነ ዓቢ — ንዘደንቕ ነገራት ክጥቀመሉ ከም ዝኽእል ኣርኣየት።
Zanta Debora ni'bizuh weledotat ni'kol'u yiniger nebere. Amlak ni'zikone seb — seb'ay kone sebeyti, ni'ishto kone abi — ni'zedenk'i negerat kitik'emelu kem zikhil ar'ayet.
Scene 23
[PICTURE: Deborah with a warm smile, holding a small child's hand, with a palm tree and peaceful sky behind them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Deborah's courage came from God. She was not strong because of swords or chariots — she was strong because she trusted the Lord with all her heart.
ጅግንነት ድቦራ ካብ ኣምላኽ ዝመጸ እዩ። ብሰይፊ ወይ ሰረገላ ኣይኰነትን ዝሓየለት — ብዅሉ ልባ ኣብ እግዚኣብሄር ስለ ዝኣመነት እያ ሓያል ነይራ።
Jigninnet Debora kab Amlak zimetshe iyu. Biseyfi wey seregela aykonetsin zihayelet — bikulu liba ab Egzi'abher sile zi'amenet iya hayal neyra.
Scene 24
[PICTURE: A group of diverse children — boys and girls — standing bravely together with light shining from above, inspired by Deborah's example. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
You too can be brave like Deborah! When you trust God and listen to His voice, He will give you wisdom and courage to do what is right — no matter how big the challenge.
ንስኻ'ውን ከም ድቦራ ጅግና ክትከውን ትኽእል ኢኻ! ንኣምላኽ ምስ ትኣምንን ቃሉ ምስ ትሰምዕን፡ ቅኑዕ ንምግባር ጥበብን ጅግንነትን ክህበካ እዩ — ብዘየገድስ ክንደይ ዓቢ ብድሆ ይኹን።
Niskha'win kem Debora jigna kitkewn tikhil ika! Ni'Amlak mis ti'aminin kalu mis tismi'in, kinue'i nimgibar tibebni jigninnetin kihbeka iyu — bizeygeddis kindey abi bidiho yikun.
Scene 25
[PICTURE: A beautiful sunrise over the land of Israel with a palm tree in the foreground, symbolizing peace, hope, and God's faithfulness. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Remember: God is always with those who trust Him. He can use you to do great things for His glory. Be brave, be wise, and always listen to God — just like Deborah!
ዘክሩ፡ ኣምላኽ ኵሉ ግዜ ምስቶም ዝኣምንዎ ኣሎ። ንኽብሩ ዓቢ ነገራት ንምግባር ክጥቀመልካ ይኽእል እዩ። ጅግና ኩን፡ ጥበበኛ ኩን፡ ከምቲ ድቦራ ዝገበረቶ ኵሉ ግዜ ንኣምላኽ ስምዓዮ!
Zekru, Amlak kulu gize mistomu zi'aminwo alo. Ni'kibru abi negerat nimgibar kitik'emelka yikhil iyu. Jigna kun, tbebeña kun, kemti Debora zigebereto kulu gize ni'Amlak simi'ayo!

Moral of the Story

God can use anyone — men or women, young or old — to do great things. Courage and wisdom come from God. When we trust Him and listen to His voice, He gives us the strength to overcome any challenge.
ኣምላኽ ንዝዀነ ሰብ — ሰብኣይ ኰነ ሰበይቲ፡ ንእሽቶ ኰነ ዓቢ — ንዓቢ ነገራት ክጥቀመሉ ይኽእል እዩ። ጅግንነትን ጥበብን ካብ ኣምላኽ ይመጹ። ምስ ንኣምኖን ቃሉ ምስ ንሰምዕን፡ ንዝዀነ ብድሆ ንምስዓር ሓይሊ ይህበና።
Amlak ni'zikone seb — seb'ay kone sebeyti, ni'ishto kone abi — ni'abi negerat kitik'emelu yikhil iyu. Jigninnetin tibebIn kab Amlak yimetsu. Mis ni'aminon kalu mis nismi'in, ni'zikone bidiho nimiS'ar hayili yihbena.
