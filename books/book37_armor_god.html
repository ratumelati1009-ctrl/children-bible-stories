The Armor of God

ኣጽዋር ኣምላኽ

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 37

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A kind, strong man named Paul sitting in a room writing a letter by candlelight. He has a warm smile and looks thoughtful. A scroll and ink pot are on the table. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
A long time ago, a man named Paul loved God very much. He wrote a special letter to his friends in a city called Ephesus. He wanted to teach them how to be strong and brave every day.
ቅድሚ ብዙሕ ዓመታት ጳውሎስ ዝተበሃለ ሰብኣይ ንኣምላኽ ኣዝዩ የፍቅሮ ነበረ። ንኣብ ኤፌሶን ዝነበሩ ኣዕሩኽቱ ፍሉይ ደብዳቤ ጸሓፈሎም። ኩሉ መዓልቲ ብኸመይ ሓያላትን ጀጋኑን ክኾኑ ከም ዝኽእሉ ክምህሮም ደለየ።
(Qidmi buzuh ametat Pawlos zitebehale seb'ay ni'Amlak azyu yefqiro nebere. Ni'ab Efeson zineberu a'erukhtu filuy debdabe tsehafalom. Kulu me'alti bikemey hayalat'n jeganu'n kikhonu kem zikhilu kimhirom deleye.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Paul holding up his letter with excitement, showing it to a group of children and families gathered around him. Behind him is a Roman soldier in full armor standing guard. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Paul said, "Be strong in the Lord and in His mighty power!" He told them that God's strength is much bigger than our own. When we trust God, we become very strong inside!
ጳውሎስ "ኣብ ጐይታን ኣብ ሓይሊ ጽንዓቱን ብርቱዓት ኩኑ!" በለ። ናይ ኣምላኽ ሓይሊ ካብ ናትና ኣዝዩ ከም ዝዓቢ ነገሮም። ንኣምላኽ ምስ ንኣምኖ ብውሽጥና ኣዝዩ ሓያላት ንኸውን!
(Pawlos "Ab Goyta'n ab hayli tsin'atu'n birtu'at kunu!" bele. Nay Amlak hayli kab natna azyu kem zi'abi negerom. Ni'Amlak mis ni'amino biwishitna azyu hayalat nikhewn!)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: A child looking worried as dark shadowy clouds swirl around, representing spiritual battles. But a bright golden light from above begins to push the darkness away. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Paul explained that sometimes we face hard things — mean words, temptation to do wrong, and lies that make us feel afraid. These are like invisible battles. But God gives us special armor to protect us!
ጳውሎስ ሓደ ሓደ ግዜ ኣጸገምቲ ነገራት ከም ዘጋጥሙና ገለጸ — ሕማቕ ቃላት፡ ጌጋ ንኽንገብር ፈተና፡ ከምኡ'ውን ከፍርሁና ዝገብሩ ሓሶታት። እዚኦም ከም ዘይረኣዩ ውግኣት እዮም። ግን ኣምላኽ ክከላኸለና ፍሉይ ኣጽዋር ይህበና!
(Pawlos hade hade gize atsegimti negeerat kem zigegatsmuna geletse — himaq qalat, giega nikningeber fetena, kemuwun kifrihuna zigebru hasotat. Izi'om kem zeyreaayu wig'at iyom. Gin Amlak kkelakhilena filuy atswar yihibena!)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: A Roman soldier in full shining armor standing tall and proud — with a belt, breastplate, boots, shield, helmet, and sword. A child stands beside him looking up in amazement. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Paul told his friends to "put on the full armor of God." Just like a soldier wears armor to be safe in battle, God gives us spiritual armor to keep us safe from evil every single day!
ጳውሎስ ንኣዕሩኽቱ "ምሉእ ኣጽዋር ኣምላኽ ልበሱ" በሎም። ከምቲ ወተሃደር ኣብ ውግእ ንምድሓን ኣጽዋር ዝለብስ፡ ኣምላኽ ኩሉ መዓልቲ ካብ ክፉእ ንኽሕልወና መንፈሳዊ ኣጽዋር ይህበና!
(Pawlos ni'a'erukhtu "milu'e atswar Amlak libisu" belom. Kemti wetehader ab wig'e nimidhan atswar zilibis, Amlak kulu me'alti kab kifu'e nikihilwena menfesawi atswar yihibena!)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: A child putting on a wide golden belt around their waist, standing straight and tall. The word "TRUTH" glows on the belt in golden letters. The child smiles with confidence. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The first piece of armor is the Belt of Truth! A belt holds everything together. When we always tell the truth and believe God's truth, it holds our whole life together and keeps us strong.
እቲ ቀዳማይ ክፋል ኣጽዋር ቅናት ሓቂ'ዩ! ቅናት ኩሉ ነገር ብሓደ ይሕዞ። ኩሉ ግዜ ሓቂ ምስ ንዛረብን ናይ ኣምላኽ ሓቂ ምስ ንኣምንን ምሉእ ሂወትና ብሓደ ሒዙ ሓያላት ይገብረና።
(Iti qedamay kifal atswar qinat haqi'yu! Qinat kulu neger bihade yihizo. Kulu gize haqi mis nizareb'n nay Amlak haqi mis ni'amin'n milu'e hiwetna bihade hizu hayalat yigebrena.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: A child at school telling the truth to a teacher, even though other children look nervous. The child's belt glows golden, showing they are wearing the belt of truth. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
How do we use the Belt of Truth? We tell the truth even when it's hard. We don't lie or pretend. When someone asks us a question, we answer honestly — just like God wants us to!
ነቲ ቅናት ሓቂ ብኸመይ ንጥቀመሉ? ኣጸጋሚ ኣብ ዝኾነሉ'ውን ሓቂ ንዛረብ። ኣይንሕሱን ወይ ዘይኮንና ኣይንመስልን። ሰብ ምስ ዝሓተና ብቕንዕና ንምልስ — ከምቲ ኣምላኽ ዝደልየና!
(Neti qinat haqi bikemey nitiqemelu? Atsegami ab zikhonelu'wun haqi nizareb. Aynihisun wey zeykon'na aynimesiln. Seb mis zihhatena biqin'ina nimlis — kemti Amlak zidelyena!)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: A child putting on a shining silver breastplate over their chest. The breastplate has a glowing heart shape in the center with the word "RIGHTEOUSNESS" engraved on it. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The second piece is the Breastplate of Righteousness! A breastplate protects your heart. Righteousness means doing what is right and good. When we choose to do good, our hearts are protected!
እቲ ካልኣይ ክፋል ጸታኔ ጽድቂ'ዩ! ጸታኔ ልብኻ ይከላኸል። ጽድቂ ማለት ቅኑዕን ጽቡቕን ምግባር ማለት'ዩ። ጽቡቕ ንምግባር ምስ ንመርጽ ልብና ይተሓለወልና!
(Iti kal'ay kifal tsetane tsidqi'yu! Tsetane libkha yikelakhel. Tsidqi malet qinu'e'n tsibuq'n migbar malet'yu. Tsibuq nimigbar mis nimerits libna yitehalewelna!)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: A child sharing their lunch with a lonely classmate who has no food. The giving child's chest glows with a warm silver light showing the breastplate of righteousness protecting their heart. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
How do we wear the Breastplate of Righteousness? We choose kindness over meanness. We share instead of being selfish. We help others and do what makes God happy — this protects our hearts!
ነቲ ጸታኔ ጽድቂ ብኸመይ ንለብሶ? ካብ ጭካነ ለውሃት ንመርጽ። ካብ ብርእስኻ ምሕሳብ ንካፈል። ንኻልኦት ንሕግዝ ንኣምላኽ ዘሕጉሶ ነገር ንገብር — እዚ ልብና ይሕልዎ!
(Neti tsetane tsidqi bikemey nilbiso? Kab chikane lewihat nimerits. Kab biri'iskha mihisab nikafel. Nikhal'ot nihigiz ni'Amlak zehiguso neger nigeber — izi libna yihlwo!)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: A child putting on beautiful glowing sandals. The sandals have wings of light on them and leave peaceful footprints of light wherever the child walks. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The third piece is the Shoes of the Gospel of Peace! Shoes help us walk and go places. These special shoes help us carry God's message of peace and love wherever we go!
እቲ ሳልሳይ ክፋል ኣሳእን ወንጌል ሰላም'ዩ! ኣሳእን ክንከይድን ናብ ቦታታት ክንከይድን ይሕግዛና። እዘን ፍሉያት ኣሳእን ናይ ኣምላኽ መልእኽቲ ሰላምን ፍቕርን ኣብ ዝኸድናዮ ኩሉ ክንሕዝ ይሕግዛና!
(Iti salisay kifal asa'in wengiel selam'yu! Asa'in kinkeyd'n nab botatat kinkeyd'n yihigzana. Izen filuyat asa'in nay Amlak mel'ikhti selam'n fiqri'n ab zikednaayo kulu kinhiz yihigzana!)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: A child walking between two friends who are arguing, gently placing a hand on each of their shoulders to help them make peace. Glowing footprints trail behind the peacemaker child. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
How do we wear the Shoes of Peace? We become peacemakers! When friends argue, we help them make up. We are kind to everyone and share God's love with our words and actions.
ነተን ኣሳእን ሰላም ብኸመይ ንለብሰን? ሰላም ገበርቲ ንኸውን! ኣዕሩኽ ምስ ዝበኣሱ ክዕረቑ ንሕግዞም። ንኹሉ ሰብ ለዋሃት ንኸውን ብቃላትናን ብተግባርናን ፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ነካፍል።
(Neten asa'in selam bikemey nilbisen? Selam geberti nikhewn! A'erukh mis zibe'asu ki'irequ nihigzom. Nikhulu seb lewahat nikhewn biqalatnan bitegibarnan fiqri Amlak nekafl.)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: A child holding up a large, beautiful golden shield that glows with warm light. Fiery arrows bounce off the shield and fall harmlessly to the ground. The child stands firm and brave. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The fourth piece is the Shield of Faith! Paul said to take up the shield of faith to stop all the fiery arrows of the evil one. Faith in God is like a big, strong shield that blocks every bad thing!
እቲ ራብዓይ ክፋል ዋልታ እምነት'ዩ! ጳውሎስ ኩሉ ናይ እቲ ኽፉእ ዝኾነ ዘሊሄብ ኵናት ምእንቲ ከተጥፍኡ ዋልታ እምነት ሐዙ በለ። ኣብ ኣምላኽ ዘላ እምነት ከም ዓባይ ሓያል ዋልታ'ያ ኩሉ ሕማቕ ነገር ትዓግት!
(Iti rab'ay kifal walta imnet'yu! Pawlos kulu nay iti khifu'e zikhone zelaheb kunat mi'inti keteffi'u walta imnet hazu bele. Ab Amlak zela imnet kem abay hayal walta'ya kulu himaq neger ti'agit!)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: A child holding up their shield of faith while other children tease them for praying. The mean words appear as small fiery arrows that bounce off the golden shield. The child smiles peacefully. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
How do we use the Shield of Faith? When we feel scared or when people say mean things, we remember that God loves us and is with us. We trust Him no matter what — and fear bounces right off!
ነቲ ዋልታ እምነት ብኸመይ ንጥቀመሉ? ምስ ንፈርሕ ወይ ሰባት ሕማቕ ምስ ዝብሉና ኣምላኽ ከም ዘፍቕረናን ምሳና ከም ዘሎን ንዝክር። ዝኾነ ይኹን ንኣምኖ — ፍርሒ ድማ ይጠፍእ!
(Neti walta imnet bikemey nitiqemelu? Mis nifrihi wey sebat himaq mis zibluna Amlak kem zefqirena'n misana kem zelo'n nizikir. Zikhone yikun ni'amino — firhi dima yitefie!)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: A child placing a magnificent shining helmet on their head. The helmet glows with a bright white light and has a cross symbol on the front. The child looks confident and safe. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The fifth piece is the Helmet of Salvation! A helmet protects your head and your mind. Salvation means God has saved us! When we remember this, our thoughts are protected from worry and doubt.
እቲ ሓምሻይ ክፋል ቁራዕ ድሕነት'ዩ! ቁራዕ ርእስኻን ኣእምሮኻን ይከላኸል። ድሕነት ማለት ኣምላኽ ኣድሒኑና ማለት'ዩ! እዚ ምስ ንዝክር ሓሳባትና ካብ ጭንቀትን ጥርጣረን ይተሓለወልና።
(Iti hamishay kifal qura'e dihnet'yu! Qura'e ri'iskha'n a'imrokha'n yikelakhel. Dihnet malet Amlak adihinuna malet'yu! Izi mis nizikir hasabaatna kab chinqet'n tirtare'n yitehalewelna.)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: A child lying in bed at night looking worried, but then smiling as they remember God's love. A glowing helmet appears around their head, and peaceful thoughts replace the worried ones. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
How do we wear the Helmet of Salvation? When bad thoughts come — like "I'm not good enough" or "God doesn't love me" — we remember that God saved us because He loves us SO much! Those bad thoughts go away!
ነቲ ቁራዕ ድሕነት ብኸመይ ንለብሶ? ሕማቕ ሓሳባት ምስ ዝመጽእ — ከም "ኣይፍቀረንን'ዩ" ወይ "ኣምላኽ ኣይፈትወንን" — ኣምላኽ ኣዝዩ ስለ ዘፍቕረና ከም ዘድሓነና ንዝክር! እቶም ሕማቕ ሓሳባት ይጠፍኡ!
(Neti qura'e dihnet bikemey nilbiso? Himaq hasabat mis zimets'e — kem "ayfiqerenn'yu" wey "Amlak ayfetiwenn" — Amlak azyu sile zefqirena kem zidhanena nizikir! Itom himaq hasabat yitef'u!)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: A child holding a glowing sword made of light. The sword has words from the Bible written along its blade in golden script. The child holds it up bravely with both hands. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The sixth piece is the Sword of the Spirit — which is the Word of God! A sword is used to fight. God's Word — the Bible — is our weapon! When we know Bible verses, we can fight lies with God's truth!
እቲ ሻድሻይ ክፋል ሰይፊ መንፈስ'ዩ — ንሱ ድማ ቃል ኣምላኽ'ዩ! ሰይፊ ንምውጋእ ይጥቀመሉ። ቃል ኣምላኽ — መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ — ናትና ኣጽዋር'ዩ! ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ጥቕስታት ምስ ንፈልጥ ሓሶታት ብሓቂ ኣምላኽ ንፋረቖም!
(Iti shadishay kifal seyfi menfes'yu — nisu dima qal Amlak'yu! Seyfi nimwig'e yitiqemelu. Qal Amlak — Metshaf Qidus — natna atswar'yu! Nay Metshaf Qidus tiqistat mis nifelit hasotat bihaqi Amlak nifareqom!)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: A child sitting with an open Bible on their lap, reading with joy. Bible verses float around them in glowing golden letters. The child looks excited to learn God's words. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
How do we use the Sword of the Spirit? We read our Bibles and learn God's words! When we feel tempted to do wrong, we remember what God says. Jesus used Bible verses to fight temptation — and so can we!
ነቲ ሰይፊ መንፈስ ብኸመይ ንጥቀመሉ? መጽሓፍ ቅዱስና ነንብብ ቃል ኣምላኽ ንመሃር! ጌጋ ንምግባር ምስ ንፍተን ኣምላኽ ዝበሎ ንዝክር። የሱስ ፈተና ንምውጋእ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ጥቕስታት ተጠቒሙ — ንሕና'ውን ንኽእል!
(Neti seyfi menfes bikemey nitiqemelu? Metshaf Qidusna ninbib qal Amlak nimhar! Giega nimigbar mis niften Amlak zibelo nizikir. Yesus fetena nimwig'e nay Metshaf Qidus tiqistat tetekimu — nihna'wun nikhil!)
Scene 17
[PICTURE: A child kneeling in prayer with hands folded and eyes closed. A beautiful golden glow surrounds them like a protective bubble. Angels stand watch in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

The Moral of the Story

God gives us everything we need to stand strong against evil. When we use God's armor every day, we are protected and brave!
