Scene 1
[PICTURE: The prophet Samuel arriving at the house of Jesse in Bethlehem, greeted by Jesse and his older sons who stand tall and impressive. A small shepherd boy (David) is absent, tending sheep on distant hills. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God sent the prophet Samuel to the house of Jesse in Bethlehem with a secret mission: to anoint the next king of Israel. When Samuel arrived, Jesse proudly presented his tall, strong older sons — warriors and men of impressive stature. Surely one of these mighty men would be God's chosen king.
ኣምላኽ ንነብዪ ሳሙኤል ናብ ቤት እሴይ ኣብ ቤትልሄም ብሚስጥራዊ ተልእኾ ሰደዶ፡ ነቲ ዝቕጽል ንጉስ እስራኤል ክቕብኦ። ሳሙኤል ምስ በጽሐ እሴይ ብኩርዓት ነቶም ነዋሕትን ሓያላትን ዓበይቲ ኣወዳቱ ኣቕረቦም — ተዋጋእትን ግርማ ዘለዎም ሰባትን። ብርግጽ ካብዞም ሓያላት ሰባት ሓደ ናይ ኣምላኽ ዝተመርጸ ንጉስ ክኸውን ነይርዎ።
(Amlak nineb'iy Samuel nab bet Isey ab Betlehem bimistirawi tel'ikho sededdo, neti ziqitsil nigus Israel kiqib'o. Samuel mis betshe Isey bikur'at netom newahtin hayalatn abeytii awedatu aqrebom — tewaga'itin girma zilewom sebatin. Birigts kabzom hayalat sebat hade nay Amlak zitemeretse nigus kikhewn neyrwo.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Samuel looking at Jesse's eldest son Eliab who is tall and handsome, but God's voice speaks to Samuel's heart. Samuel's expression shows he is listening to God internally while everyone watches expectantly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Samuel looked at Eliab, Jesse's firstborn — tall, commanding, kingly in appearance. "Surely this is the Lord's anointed," Samuel thought. But God spoke clearly to Samuel's heart: "Do not consider his appearance or his height. I have rejected him. The Lord does not look at the things people look at. People look at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart."
ሳሙኤል ንኤልያብ በኹሪ እሴይ ረኣዮ — ነዊሕ፡ ስልጡን፡ ብመልክዑ ንጉሳዊ። "ብርግጽ እዚ ናይ እግዚኣብሄር ቅቡእ'ዩ" ሳሙኤል ሓሰበ። ግን ኣምላኽ ንልቢ ሳሙኤል ብንጹር ተዛረቦ፡ "ኣብ መልክዑ ወይ ቁመቱ ኣይትጠምት። ነጺገዮ ኣለኹ። እግዚኣብሄር ሰባት ዝጥምትዎ ኣይጥምትን'ዩ። ሰባት ናብ ደጋዊ መልክዕ ይጥምቱ፡ ግን እግዚኣብሄር ናብ ልቢ ይጥምት።"
(Samuel ni'Eliyab bekhuri Isey re'ayo — newih, silitun, bimelki'u nigusawi. "Birigts izi nay Egzi'abher qibu'e'yu" Samuel hasebe. Gin Amlak nilbi Samuel binitsur tezarebo: "Ab melki'u wey qumetatu aytitumit. Netsigéyo alekhu. Egzi'abher sebat zitumitwo aytimit'n'yu. Sebat nab degawi melki'e yitimtu, gin Egzi'abher nab libi yitimit.")
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Young David, the youngest son, being called from the sheep fields. He runs toward the house with his shepherd's staff, ruddy-faced and bright-eyed, while sheep graze behind him on green hills. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Seven sons passed before Samuel, and God rejected every one. "Are these all the sons you have?" Samuel asked Jesse. "There is still the youngest," Jesse replied almost dismissively, "but he is tending the sheep." No one had even considered David worthy of an invitation. Yet God had chosen the one everyone else had overlooked — the forgotten shepherd boy with a heart full of worship.
ሸውዓተ ኣወዳት ቅድሚ ሳሙኤል ሓለፉ፡ ኣምላኽ ድማ ንኹሎም ነጸጎም። "እዚኦም ድዮም ኩሎም ኣወዳትካ?" ሳሙኤል ንእሴይ ሓተቶ። "ገና እቲ ዝነኣሰ ኣሎ" እሴይ ብዘይ ግዲ መለሰ "ግን ኣባጊዕ ይጓሲ ኣሎ።" ሓደ'ኳ ንዳዊት ናይ ዕድመ ብቑዕ ኣይገበሮን። ኣምላኽ ግን ነቲ ኩሉ ሰብ ዝረስዖ መሪጹ ነበረ — ነቲ ዝተረስዐ ጓሳ ቆልዓ ልቡ ብምስጋና ዝመልአ።
(Shew'ate awedat qidmi Samuel halefu, Amlak dima nikhulom netségom. "Izi'om diyom kulom awedatka?" Samuel ni'Isey hateto. "Gena iti zini'ase alo" Isey bizey gidi melese "gin abagi'e yigwasi alo." Hade'kwa niDawit nay idme biqu'e aygeberon. Amlak gin neti kulu seb ziresio meritsu nebere — neti ziteresie gwasa qol'a libu bimisigana zimel'a.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Samuel pouring anointing oil from a horn onto young David's bowed head, while his older brothers watch in stunned disbelief. Oil streams down David's hair. A divine light seems to rest upon David. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When David arrived, God said to Samuel, "Rise and anoint him; this is the one." In front of his astonished brothers, Samuel poured oil on the young shepherd's head. From that day forward, the Spirit of the Lord came powerfully upon David. He didn't look like a king, he didn't live in a palace, and no one expected greatness from him — but God saw what no one else could see.
ዳዊት ምስ መጸ ኣምላኽ ንሳሙኤል "ተንስእ ቅብኣዮ፤ እዚ'ዩ እቲ ዝተመረጸ" በሎ። ኣብ ቅድሚ ዝተገረሙ ኣሕዋቱ ሳሙኤል ኣብ ርእሲ'ቲ ንእሽቶ ጓሳ ዘይቲ ኣፍሰሰ። ካብ'ታ መዓልቲ ንደሓር መንፈስ እግዚኣብሄር ብሓይሊ ኣብ ዳዊት ወረደ። ከም ንጉስ ኣይመስልን ነበረ፡ ኣብ ቤተ መንግስቲ ኣይነበረን፡ ሓደ'ኳ ዕቤት ካብኡ ኣይተጸበየን — ግን ኣምላኽ ካልእ ሰብ ክርእዮ ዘይክእል ረኣየ።
(Dawit mis metse Amlak niSamuel "Tenis'e qib'ayo; izi'yu iti zitemeretse" belo. Ab qidmi zitegeremu ahwatu Samuel ab ri'isi'ti ni'ishto gwasa zeyti afisese. Kab'ta me'alti nidehar menfes Egzi'abher bihayili ab Dawit werede. Kem nigus aymesilín nebere, ab bete mengisti ayneberan, hade'kwa ibet kab'u aytetsebéyen — gin Amlak kal'e seb kiri'iyo zeykhil re'aye.)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: The giant Goliath in full armor standing in the valley between two armies, taunting the Israelite soldiers who cower in fear. Goliath towers over everyone, his spear like a weaver's beam. Dark storm clouds overhead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Years passed. Israel faced the Philistine army, and a champion named Goliath — over nine feet tall, armored in bronze — challenged Israel daily for forty days. Every soldier, including David's brothers, trembled with fear. Goliath didn't just threaten Israel's army; he mocked the living God. No one dared to answer his challenge, revealing a crisis not of military strength but of faith.
ዓመታት ሓለፉ። እስራኤል ንሰራዊት ፍልስጥኤማውያን ገጠመት፡ ጎልያድ ዝተባህለ ዋና ተዋጋኢ — ልዕሊ ትሽዓተ እግሪ ቁመት፡ ብነሃስ ዝተዓጠቐ — ንእስራኤል ኣርብዓ መዓልቲ ብየማዕልቱ ይብድሃ ነበረ። ነፍሲ ወከፍ ወታሃደር፡ ኣሕዋት ዳዊት ሓዊሱ፡ ብፍርሒ ይርዕድ ነበረ። ጎልያድ ንሰራዊት እስራኤል ጥራይ ኣየፈራርሐን — ንህያው ኣምላኽ ኣላገጸ። ሓደ'ኳ ንብድሆኡ ክምልስ ኣይደፈረን፡ ናይ ወታሃደራዊ ሓይሊ ዘይኮነ ናይ እምነት ቅልውላው ምዃኑ ኣርኣየ።
(Amettat halefu. Israel niserawit Filistiemawyan geTemet, Golyad zitebahle wana tewaga'i — li'ili tish'ate igri qumet, binhas zite'ateqe — ni'Israel arbi'a me'alti biyeme'altu yibdiha nebere. Nefsi wekef wetahader, ahwat Dawit hawisu, bifirihi yir'id nebere. Golyad niserawit Israel tiray ayeferarhen — nihiyaw Amlak alagétse. Hade'kwa nibidiho'u kiimlis aydeferen, nay wetahaderawi hayili zeyikone nay imnet qilwulaw mukhanu ar'aye.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Young David standing boldly before King Saul, refusing heavy armor that doesn't fit him. David holds his simple sling and picks smooth stones from a stream. His face shows calm determination while soldiers look on doubtfully. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
David, still just a teenager delivering food to his brothers, heard Goliath's mockery and was outraged — not for Israel's honor, but for God's. "Who is this uncircumcised Philistine that he should defy the armies of the living God?" David refused Saul's armor because his confidence wasn't in human weapons. He chose five smooth stones and his sling — tools he'd used as a shepherd. His faith wasn't reckless bravery; it was deep trust built through years of experiencing God's faithfulness.
ዳዊት ገና መንእሰይ ንኣሕዋቱ መግቢ ከብጽሕ ከሎ ናይ ጎልያድ ምልጋጽ ሰምዐ'ሞ ተቖጥዐ — ንኽብሪ እስራኤል ዘይኮነ ንኽብሪ ኣምላኽ። "እዚ ዘይተገዝረ ፍልስጥኤማዊ ንሰራዊት ህያው ኣምላኽ ክብድህ መን'ዩ?" ዳዊት ናይ ሳኦል ዕጥቂ ኣቢሑ ምኽንያቱ ኣብ ሰብኣዊ ኣጽዋር ኣይኣምንን ነበረ። ሓሙሽተ ልሙጻት እምንን ወንጭፍትን መረጸ — ከም ጓሳ ዝጥቀመሎም ናውቲ። እምነቱ ዓሻ ትብዓት ኣይነበረትን፤ ናይ ዓመታት ናይ ኣምላኽ ተኣማንነት ተመኩሮ ዝተሃንጸ ዓሚቕ ምእማን ነበረ።
(Dawit gena men'isey ni'ahwatu megbi kibitsih kelo nay Golyad milgats semi'e'mo teqot'e — nikhbri Israel zeykone nikhbri Amlak. "Izi zeytegezre Filistiemawi niserawit hiyaw Amlak kibdih men'yu?" Dawit nay Sa'ol itqi abihu mikiñyatu ab seb'awi atsiwar ay'amin'n nebere. Hamushte limutsatat imin'n wenchifiti'n meretse — kem gwasa zitiqemelom nawti. Imnetu asha tib'at ayneberetín; nay amettat nay Amlak te'amannet temekuro zitehantse amiq mi'iman nebere.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: David releasing a stone from his sling with powerful motion toward the giant Goliath. The stone flies through the air in a dramatic arc. Both armies watch from hillsides. David's face shows fierce concentration and faith. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
David ran toward the giant — not away from him. "You come against me with sword and spear," David declared, "but I come against you in the name of the Lord Almighty!" One stone, slung with precision born of years of practice, struck Goliath's forehead. The giant fell face-down. The battle was won not by superior weaponry or physical strength, but by a young man who understood that God's power is made perfect in human weakness.
ዳዊት ናብ'ቲ ግዙፍ ጎየ — ካብኡ ኣይሃደመን። "ንስኻ ብሴፍን ብዂናትን ትመጸኒ" ዳዊት ኣወጀ "ኣነ ግን ብስም እግዚኣብሄር ሰራዊት ይመጸካ ኣለኹ!" ሓንቲ እምኒ ብናይ ዓመታት ልምምድ ዝተፈጥረ ክእለት ተወንጪፋ ግንባር ጎልያድ ወቕዐት። እቲ ግዙፍ ብገጹ ወደቐ። ውግእ ብዝበለጸ ኣጽዋር ወይ ኣካላዊ ሓይሊ ዘይኮነ ሓይሊ ኣምላኽ ኣብ ድኻም ሰብ ፍጹም ከም ዝኸውን ዝተረድአ መንእሰይ'ዩ ስዒሩ።
(Dawit nab'ti gizuf goye — kab'u ayhademen. "Niskha bisefin bikuanatin timetseni" Dawit aweje "ane gin bism Egzi'abher serawit yimetséka alekhu!" Hanti imni binay amettat limimd zitefertre ki'ilet tewenchifa ginbar Golyad weqi'et. Iti gizuf bigetsu wedeqe. Wig'e bizibeletse atsiwar wey akalawi hayili zeykone hayili Amlak ab dikhiam seb fitsuma kem zikhewn ziteredí'e men'isey'yu si'iru.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: David playing a beautiful harp in King Saul's palace. Saul sits on his throne with a troubled expression that gradually softens as the music fills the room. Warm lamplight illuminates the scene. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After his victory, David entered Saul's service. When tormenting spirits troubled the king, David would play his harp, and peace would return to Saul's restless soul. David served faithfully — not seeking promotion or recognition, but honoring the king God had placed over him. This season of serving taught David patience, humility, and the discipline of honoring authority even when that authority was deeply flawed.
ድሕሪ ዓወቱ ዳዊት ናብ ኣገልግሎት ሳኦል ኣተወ። መናግስቲ መናፍስቲ ንንጉስ ምስ ዘጨንቕዎ ዳዊት ክናሩ ይጻወት ነበረ'ሞ ሰላም ናብ ዘይተረፍአ ነፍሲ ሳኦል ይምለስ ነበረ። ዳዊት ብእምነት ኣገልገለ — ዕብየት ወይ ኣፍልጦ ዘይደሊ ግን ነቲ ኣምላኽ ኣብ ልዕሊኡ ዝሓዞ ንጉስ ኣኽቢሩ። እዚ ወቕቲ ኣገልግሎት ንዳዊት ትዕግስቲ፡ ትሕትና፡ ስነ-ስርዓት ናይ ምኽባር ስልጣን ምሃሮ ሽግር'ኳ ይሃልዎ።
(Dihri awetu Dawit nab agelgilot Sa'ol atewe. Menagisti menafisti ninigus mis zijechiniqu'o Dawit kinaru yitsawet nebere'mo selam nab zeyterefi'a nefsi Sa'ol yimles nebere. Dawit bi'imnet agelgele — ibiyet wey afilito zeydeli gin neti Amlak ab li'ili'u zihiazo nigus akhbiru. Izi weqti agelgilot niDawit ti'igisti, tihtina, sine-sir'at nay mikbar silitan miharo shigir'kwa yihalwo.)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: King Saul on his throne with a dark, jealous expression, gripping a spear tightly. In the background, crowds of women dance and sing praises for David. Saul's face is twisted with envy and suspicion. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But jealousy is a poison that destroys from within. When the women sang, "Saul has slain his thousands, and David his tens of thousands," something broke inside the king. Saul began to view David not as a loyal servant but as a rival. Jealousy distorted his perception until he could see nothing but a threat in the young man who loved him most faithfully. This is what unchecked insecurity does — it turns friends into perceived enemies.

God looks at the heart, not outward appearance. Even great people fail, but true repentance brings restoration.
