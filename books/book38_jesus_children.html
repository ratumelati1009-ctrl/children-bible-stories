Jesus and the Children

የሱስን ቆልዑን

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A sunny day in ancient Galilee. Jesus is teaching a crowd of people under a large tree near a hillside. Families with young children can be seen at the edges of the crowd, watching and listening. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One beautiful day, Jesus was teaching people about God's love. Many families came from nearby villages to listen. Mothers and fathers brought their little children along, hoping to see the wonderful Teacher everyone was talking about.
ሓደ ጽቡቕ መዓልቲ የሱስ ንሰባት ብዛዕባ ፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ይምህር ነበረ። ብዙሓት ስድራ ቤታት ካብ ቀረባ ዓድታት ክሰምዑ መጹ። ኣደታትን ኣቦታትን ነቲ ኩሉ ሰብ ዝዛረበሉ ዘደንቕ መምህር ክርእዩ ተስፋ ብምግባር ንደቀን ሒዘን መጻ።
(Hade tsibuq me'alti Yesus nisebat biza'iba fiqri Amlak yimhir nebere. Buzuhat sidra betat kab qereba adtat kisem'u metsu. Adetat'n abotat'n neti kulu seb zizarebelu zidenq memhir kir'eyu tesfa bimigbar nideqen hizen metsa.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Several mothers holding babies and toddlers, walking along a dusty path toward where Jesus is teaching. Older children skip ahead excitedly. The mothers look hopeful and determined. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The parents wanted Jesus to touch their children and bless them. "If only Jesus would place His hands on our little ones and pray for them!" the mothers said to each other as they walked closer.
ወለዲ የሱስ ንደቆም ክትንክፎምን ክባርኾምን ይደልዩ ነበሩ። "የሱስ ኣብ ደቅና ኢዱ ኣንቢሩ እንተጸልየልና ጥራይ!" ኢለን ኣደታት ክቐርባ ከለዋ ንሓድሕደን ይዛራረባ ነበራ።
(Weledi Yesus nideqom kitinkifom'n kibarikhhom'n yidelyu neberu. "Yesus ab deqna idu anbiru intetsellyelna tiray!" ilen adetat kiqerba kelewa nihadihden yizarareba nebera.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: A group of excited children running toward Jesus with big smiles. Some carry wildflowers, others hold their parents' hands. A little girl waves happily. The crowd parts slightly as they approach. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The children were so excited! Some were tiny babies carried in their mothers' arms. Others were toddlers who could barely walk. And some were bigger children who ran ahead, eager to meet Jesus!
ቆልዑ ኣዝዮም ተሓጒሶም ነበሩ! ገሊኦም ኣብ ኢድ ኣዲኦም ዝተሓዙ ሕጻናት ነበሩ። ገሊኦም ብጭንቂ ዝኸዱ ንኣሽቱ ነበሩ። ገሊኦም ድማ ንየሱስ ክረኽቡ ሃንቀው ኢሎም ቀዲሞም ዝጎየዩ ዓበይቲ ቆልዑ ነበሩ!
(Qol'u azyom tehagwisom neberu! Geli'om ab id adi'om zitehazu hitsanat neberu. Geli'om bichiqi zikedu ni'ashtu neberu. Geli'om dima niyesus kirekhibu hanqew ilom qedimom zigoyeyu abeyti qol'u neberu!)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: The disciples standing in a line with stern faces, holding up their hands to stop the families from coming closer. They look serious and protective. The mothers look worried and disappointed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But when the disciples saw the families coming, they stepped forward and blocked their way! "Stop! Don't bother the Teacher!" they said firmly. "He is too busy and too important for children!"
ግን ደቀ መዛሙርቲ ነተን ስድራ ቤታት ክመጻ ምስ ረኣዩ ንቕድሚት ወጺኦም ዓገትወን! "ደው በሉ! ንመምህር ኣይትረብሽዎ!" ብጽኑዕ በሉ። "ንቆልዑ ኣዝዩ ጽሩይን ኣዝዩ ኣገዳስን'ዩ!"
(Gin deqe mezamurti neten sidra betat kimetsa mis re'ayu niqidmit wetsi'om agetwen! "Dew belu! Nimemhir aytirebshwo!" bitsinu'e belu. "Niqol'u azyu tsiruy'n azyu agedas'n'yu!")
Scene 5
[PICTURE: A mother holding her baby close, looking sad and turned away. A young boy tugs on his father's sleeve, looking confused and hurt. Other children look down at the ground sadly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The children felt sad. One little boy asked, "Why can't we see Jesus? Doesn't He like us?" A mother hugged her baby tightly, feeling disappointed. The families began to turn away sadly.
ቆልዑ ሓዘኑ። ሓደ ንእሽቶ ወዲ "ስለምንታይ ንየሱስ ክንርእዮ ዘይንኽእል? ኣየፍቅረናንዶ?" ኢሉ ሓተተ። ሓንቲ ኣደ ብተስፋ ምቝራጽ ንሕጻና ብጽኑዕ ሓቝፋ። ስድራ ቤታት ብሓዘን ክኸዳ ጀመራ።
(Qol'u hazenu. Hade ni'ishto wedi "Slemintay niyesus kinir'eyo zeynkhil? Ayefqirena'ndo?" ilu hatete. Hanti ade bitesfa miqurats nihitsana bitsinu'e haqufa. Sidra betat bihazen kikheda jemera.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Jesus looking up from teaching, His face showing strong displeasure toward the disciples. He stands up with authority, one hand raised. His eyes are full of compassion as He looks past the disciples toward the children. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Jesus saw what was happening and He was NOT happy with His disciples! He stood up and said firmly, "Stop! Do not send them away!" His voice was strong and clear. Everyone turned to listen.
ግን የሱስ ነቲ ዝኾነ ረኣዮ'ሞ ብደቀ መዛሙርቱ ኣይተሓጎሰን! ተንሲኡ ብጽኑዕ "ደው በሉ! ኣይትስጎጉወን!" በለ። ድምጹ ብርቱዕን ንጹርን ነበረ። ኩሎም ግልጽ ኢሎም ክሰምዑ ጀመሩ።
(Gin Yesus neti zikhone re'ayo'mo bideqe mezamurtu aytehagosen! Tensi'u bitsinu'e "Dew belu! Aytisgogwen!" bele. Dimtsu birtu'e'n nitsur'n nebere. Kulom gilts ilom kisem'u jemeru.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Jesus with arms wide open, beckoning the children to come to Him. His face is full of love and joy. A warm golden light surrounds Him. The disciples step back looking ashamed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Jesus opened His arms wide and said the most beautiful words: "Let the little children come to me! Do not stop them!" His voice was full of love. His smile was warm and welcoming.
ድሕሪኡ የሱስ ኣእዳዉ ዘርጊሑ ኣዝዩ ጽቡቕ ቃላት ተዛረበ፡ "ንንኣሽቱ ቆልዑ ናባይ ይምጹ ሕደግዎም! ኣይትኸልክልዎም!" ድምጹ ብፍቕሪ ዝመልአ ነበረ። ፍሽኽታኡ ውዑይን ኣቀባባልን ነበረ።
(Dihri'u Yesus a'idawu zergihu azyu tsibuq qalat tezarebe: "Nini'ashtu qol'u nabay yimtsu hidegwom! Aytikelkilwom!" Dimtsu bifiqri zimel'a nebere. Fishkita'u wu'uy'n aqebabal'n nebere.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Jesus speaking with authority to His disciples, pointing toward the children. The disciples look surprised and humbled. Jesus has a teaching expression on His face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus looked at His disciples and said something very important: "The kingdom of God belongs to such as these! Anyone who will not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it."
የሱስ ንደቀ መዛሙርቱ ጠሚቱ ኣዝዩ ኣገዳሲ ነገር ተዛረበ፡ "መንግስቲ ኣምላኽ ናይ ከምዚኣቶም'ያ! ንመንግስቲ ኣምላኽ ከም ንእሽቶ ቆልዓ ዘይቕበላ ፈጺሙ ኣይኣትዋን'ዩ።"
(Yesus nideqe mezamurtu temitu azyu agedasi neger tezarebe: "Mengisti Amlak nay kemzi'atom'ya! Nimengisti Amlak kem ni'ishto qol'a zeyqibela fetsimu ayi'atwaan'yu.")
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Children running joyfully toward Jesus with arms outstretched. A toddler waddles forward. An older girl leads her little brother by the hand. Flowers and grass surround the path. Parents smile in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When the children heard Jesus calling them, their faces lit up with joy! They ran toward Him as fast as their little legs could carry them. "He wants to see us! He wants US!" they shouted happily.
ቆልዑ የሱስ ክጽውዖም ምስ ሰምዑ ገጾም ብሓጎስ ኣብርሀ! ንእሽቶ ኣእጋሮም ብዝኽእሎ ቅልጣፈ ናብኡ ጎየዩ። "ክርእየና ይደሊ! ንዓና ይደሊ!" ኢሎም ብሓጎስ ጸርሑ።
(Qol'u Yesus kitsuw'om mis sem'u getsom bihagos abirhe! Ni'ishto a'igarom bizikhilo qiltafe nab'u goyeyu. "Kir'eyena yideli! Ni'ana yideli!" ilom bihagos tserhu.)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Jesus kneeling down to be at eye level with the children. A small boy reaches up to touch Jesus' face. Jesus smiles warmly with His hands gently on the child's shoulders. Other children gather around. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus knelt down so He could be at the same height as the children. He didn't stand tall above them — He came down to their level! A small boy gently touched Jesus' face, and Jesus smiled with so much love.
የሱስ ምስ ቆልዑ ማዕረ ቁመት ክኸውን ተምበርከኸ። ልዕሊኦም ደው ኣይበለን — ናብ ደረጃኦም ወረደ! ሓደ ንእሽቶ ወዲ ብልኡም ገጽ የሱስ ተንከፎ፡ የሱስ ድማ ብብዙሕ ፍቕሪ ፈገገ።
(Yesus mis qol'u ma'ire qumet kikhewn temberkekhhe. Li'ili'om dew aybelen — nab dereja'om werede! Hade ni'ishto wedi bili'um gets Yesus tenkefo, Yesus dima bibuzuh fiqri fegege.)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Jesus sitting on a large rock with a baby in His arms, gently cradling it. The baby looks peaceful and content. The mother stands nearby with tears of joy in her eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus took the little babies into His arms and held them close to His heart. He cradled them gently, just like the most loving father. The mothers cried happy tears watching Jesus hold their precious ones.
የሱስ ነቶም ንኡሳት ሕጻናት ኣብ ኢዱ ወሲዱ ናብ ልቡ ኣቕረቦም። ከም ኣዝዩ ፈቃር ኣቦ ብልኡም ሓቝፉዎም። ኣደታት የሱስ ንኽቡራት ደቀን ሒዙ ክርእያ ከለዋ ናይ ሓጎስ ንብዓት ኣፍሰሳ።
(Yesus netom ni'usat hitsanat ab idu wesidu nab libu aqrebom. Kem azyu feqar abo bili'um haqufuwom. Adetat Yesus nikhburat deqen hizu kir'eya kelewa nay hagos nib'at afsesa.)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Jesus placing His hands on a child's head in blessing. The child's eyes are closed peacefully with a sweet smile. A soft golden glow surrounds them. Other children wait their turn eagerly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One by one, Jesus placed His hands on each child's head and blessed them. He prayed for every single one. No child was forgotten. No child was too small. Each one received His love and blessing.
ሓደ ብሓደ የሱስ ኣብ ርእሲ ነፍሲ ወከፍ ቆልዓ ኢዱ ኣንቢሩ ባረኾም። ንነፍሲ ወከፍ ጸለየ። ዝተረሰዐ ቆልዓ ኣይነበረን። ዝነኣሰ ቆልዓ ኣይነበረን። ነፍሲ ወከፍ ፍቕሩን በረኸቱን ተቐበለ።
(Hade bihade Yesus ab ri'isi nefsi wekef qol'a idu anbiru barekhhom. Ninefsi wekef tseleye. Ziterese'e qol'a ayneberen. Zine'ase qol'a ayneberen. Nefsi wekef fiqru'n berekhetu'n teqebele.)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Jesus sitting cross-legged on the grass surrounded by children of different ages. He is telling a story with animated hand gestures. The children lean forward with wide eyes, completely fascinated. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Jesus sat down in the grass and began telling the children stories! He told them about birds that God feeds, about flowers that God dresses in beautiful colors, and about how much their Heavenly Father loves them.
ድሕሪኡ የሱስ ኣብ ሳዕሪ ኮፍ ኢሉ ንቆልዑ ዛንታታት ክነግሮም ጀመረ! ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ዝምግቦም ኣዕዋፍ፡ ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ብጽቡቕ ሕብሪ ዘልበሶም ዕምባባታት፡ ከምኡ'ውን ሰማያዊ ኣቦኦም ክንደይ ከም ዘፍቅሮም ነገሮም።
(Dihri'u Yesus ab sa'iri kof ilu niqol'u zantatat kinegrom jemere! Biza'iba Amlak zimgibom a'iwaf, biza'iba Amlak bitsibuq hibri zilebsom imbabatat, kemu'wun semayawi abo'om kindey kem zefqirom negerom.)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Children laughing and clapping their hands while Jesus tells a funny story. One boy rolls on the grass giggling. Jesus is laughing too, His eyes twinkling with joy. Butterflies flutter around them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The children laughed and clapped their hands! Jesus made them giggle with funny stories. He laughed with them too! Jesus was not serious and stern — He was joyful and full of fun with the little ones.
ቆልዑ ሰሓቑን ኣእዳዎም ኣጣቕዑን! የሱስ ብዘስሕቕ ዛንታታት ኣሰሓቖም። ንሱ'ውን ምሳታቶም ሰሓቐ! የሱስ ከቢድን ጽኑዕን ኣይነበረን — ምስ ንኣሽቱ ሕጉስን ብሓጎስ ዝመልአን ነበረ።
(Qol'u sehaqu'n a'idawom ataq'u'n! Yesus bizesihiq zantatat asehaqom. Nisu'wun misatatom sehaqqe! Yesus kebid'n tsinu'e'n ayneberen — mis ni'ashtu higus'n bihagos zimel'a'n nebere.)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: A curious little girl with braided hair raising her hand to ask Jesus a question. Jesus looks at her with full attention and delight, as if her question is the most important thing in the world. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The children asked Jesus many questions. "Does God really know my name?" asked one little girl. Jesus smiled and said, "Yes! God knows every hair on your head. He knows you and loves you more than you can imagine!"
ቆልዑ ንየሱስ ብዙሕ ሕቶታት ሓተቱ። "ኣምላኽ ብሓቂ ስመይ ይፈልጥዶ?" ሓንቲ ንእሽቶ ጓል ሓተተት። የሱስ ፈጊጉ "እወ! ኣምላኽ ኩሉ ጸጉሪ ርእስኺ ይፈልጥ'ዩ። ይፈልጠኪ'ዩ ካብ ትሓስብዮ ንላዕሊ ድማ የፍቅረኪ!" በለ።
(Qol'u niyesus buzuh hitotat hatetu. "Amlak bihaqi smey yifelit'do?" hanti ni'ishto gual hatetet. Yesus fegigu "Iwe! Amlak kulu tseguri ri'iski yifelit'yu. Yifeliteki'yu kab tihassbiyo nila'ili dima yefqireki!" bele.)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: A small boy sitting on Jesus' lap, looking up at Him with adoring eyes. Jesus has one arm around the boy protectively. Other children sit at Jesus' feet, some leaning against His legs. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
A little boy climbed onto Jesus' lap and asked, "Can I be Your friend?" Jesus hugged him and said, "You are already My friend! I love you so much. You are very precious to Me and to God."
ሓደ ንእሽቶ ወዲ ናብ ሕቝፊ የሱስ ደዪቡ "ዓርክኻ ክኸውን ይኽእል'ዶ?" ኢሉ ሓተተ። የሱስ ሓቝፉ "ድሮ ዓርከይ ኢኻ! ኣዝየ'የ ዘፍቅረካ። ንዓይን ንኣምላኽን ኣዝኻ ክቡር ኢኻ" በለ።
(Hade ni'ishto wedi nab hiqufi Yesus deyibu "Arikha kikhewn yikhil'do?" ilu hatete. Yesus haqufu "Diro arkey ikha! Azye'ye zefqireka. Ni'ay'n ni'Amlak'n azkha kibur ikha" bele.)
Scene 17

The Moral of the Story

Every child is precious to Jesus. You are never too young or too small for God's love. Jesus loves children and wants to be their friend!

ነፍሲ ወከፍ ቆልዓ ንየሱስ ክቡር'ዩ። ንፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ፈጺምኻ ኣይትንእስን ወይ ኣይትእኒስን። የሱስ ንቆልዑ የፍቅሮም'ዩ ዓርኮም ክኸውን ድማ ይደሊ!

(Nefsi wekef qol'a niyesus kibur'yu. Nifiqri Amlak fetsimkha aytin'is'n wey ayti'nis'n. Yesus niqol'u yefqirom'yu arkom kikhewn dima yideli!)
