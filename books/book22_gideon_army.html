Scene 1
[PICTURE: A young Israelite farmer named Gideon secretly threshing wheat in a winepress to hide from enemies. He looks afraid but determined. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Gideon was a young man who lived in Israel. The Midianites were mean enemies who stole their food. Gideon was hiding, threshing wheat secretly.
ጊድዮን ኣብ እስራኤል ዝነብር ኣባጽሕ ነበረ። ሚድያናውያን መግቦም ዝሰርቁ ጨካናት ጸላእቲ ነበሩ። ጊድዮን ብሕቡእ ስርናይ ይዐረግ ተሓቢኡ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: Gidyon ab Israel zneber abatsh nebere. Midyanawyan megbom zsrequ chekanat tsela'eti neberu. Gidyon bhbu'e srnaay y'ereg tehabiu nebere.)

[PICTURE: A glowing angel of the Lord appearing to Gideon under an oak tree. Gideon looking up in surprise and awe. Bright divine light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

[PICTURE: Gideon looking confused, gesturing around at the empty fields and destroyed crops. He questions why God hasn't helped. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

Scene 4
[PICTURE: The angel pointing at Gideon with authority, divine light emanating. Gideon standing taller, beginning to feel courage. Oak tree behind them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

[PICTURE: Gideon blowing a trumpet/shofar, calling men from all the tribes of Israel to gather for battle. Men running toward him from villages. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Gideon blew a trumpet and called men to fight! 32,000 men came to join his army. That seemed like a lot of soldiers!

(Pronunciation: Gidyon meleket nefihu sebat kwagu'u tsew'a! 32,000 sebat nab serawitu tetseneberu. Bzuhat wetehadeerat zmeslu neberu!)

But God said, "You have too many soldiers! If you win with so many, you might think you did it by yourselves. I want everyone to know I saved you!"

(Pronunciation: Gna Amlak "Bzuhat wetehadeerat alewukha! Bbzuhat ms its'er ba'elkhu kem zgeberkumo kthasbu tkhelu. Ane kem zedhanukukhum kulu kfelT edeli!" bele.)

[PICTURE: Many soldiers leaving the camp, walking away with their belongings. Gideon watching as thousands depart. Only some remain. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said, "Tell everyone who is afraid to go home." 22,000 men left! Now only 10,000 remained. But God said, "Still too many!"
ኣምላኽ "ዝፈርሐ ኩሉ ናብ ገዝኡ ክኸይድ ንገሮም" በለ። 22,000 ሰባት ከዱ! ሕጂ 10,000 ጥራይ ተረፉ። ግና ኣምላኽ "ገና ብዙሓት'ዮም!" በለ።
(Pronunciation: Amlak "Zferhe kulu nab gezu'u kkhyd ngerom" bele. 22,000 sebat kedu! Hji 10,000 Teray terefu. Gna Amlak "Gena bzuhat'yom!" bele.)

[PICTURE: Soldiers kneeling at a stream drinking water. Some cupping water with their hands to their mouths while watching, others putting faces down in water. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God told Gideon to take the men to the water. Some lapped water like dogs. Only 300 men scooped water with their hands while watching for danger. God chose these 300!
ኣምላኽ ንጊድዮን ነቶም ሰባት ናብ ማይ ውሰዶም በሎ። ገሊኦም ከም ኣኻልብ ሰትዮም። 300 ጥራይ ብኢዶም ማይ ቀዲሖም ንሓደጋ እናተኸታተሉ ሰተዩ። ኣምላኽ ነዞም 300 ሓረዮም!

[PICTURE: Gideon standing before his small group of 300 men, looking brave but also amazed at how few they are. Vast enemy camp visible in valley below. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
From 32,000 soldiers down to just 300! It seemed impossible! But God wanted to show His mighty power. With God, nothing is impossible!

English: God doesn't need big numbers or strong people to do great things. He uses those who trust Him with all their heart. When you feel small or weak, remember that God's power is made perfect in weakness!
