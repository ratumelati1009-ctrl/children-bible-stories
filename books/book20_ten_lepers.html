Jesus Heals Ten Lepers

የሱስ ዓሰርተ ለምጻማት ፈዊሱ

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 20

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 20

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

For all the children who remember to say "thank you" to God and to others.

ንኹሎም ንኣምላኽን ንኻልኦትን "ኣመስግነካ" ክብሉ ዝዝክሩ ቆልዑ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: Jesus walking along a road between two villages with His disciples, a peaceful countryside scene. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus was walking on His way to Jerusalem. He passed between two villages. His disciples walked with Him, talking and listening to His teachings along the way.
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ናብ ኢየሩሳሌም ኣብ መንገዲ ይኸይድ ነበረ። ኣብ መንጎ ክልተ ዓድታት ሓለፈ። ደቀ-መዛሙርቱ ምስኡ ይኸዱ ትምህርቱ ድማ ይዛረቡን ይሰምዑን ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ኢየሩሳሌም = Iyerusalem = Jerusalem, ዓድታት = 'aditat = villages, ደቀ-መዛሙርቲ = deqe-mezamurti = disciples)

Page 2

[PICTURE: Ten sick men standing far away from the road, looking miserable with skin disease, separated from everyone else. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
As Jesus came near a village, ten men stood far away. They had a terrible skin disease called leprosy. Because of their sickness, they had to stay away from everyone else.
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ናብ ሓንቲ ዓዲ ምስ ቀረበ ዓሰርተ ሰባት ርሒቖም ደው በሉ። ለምጺ ዝበሃል ዘፍርሕ ሕማም ቆርበት ነበሮም። ብሰንኪ ሕማሞም ካብ ኩሉ ሰብ ርሒቖም ክጸንሑ ነበሮም።
(Pronunciation: ዓሰርተ = 'aserte = ten, ለምጺ = lemts'i = leprosy, ሕማም = Himam = disease)

Page 3

[PICTURE: The ten lepers looking sad and alone, some with bandages, living in a group away from the village. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
These ten men were very sad. They could not hug their families. They could not play with their children. They could not go to the market or visit friends. They were all alone together.
TIGRINYA:
እዞም ዓሰርተ ሰባት ኣዝዮም ሓዚኖም ነበሩ። ንስድራኦም ክሓቁፉ ኣይክእሉን ነበሩ። ምስ ደቆም ክጻወቱ ኣይክእሉን ነበሩ። ናብ ዕዳጋ ክኸዱ ወይ መሓዙት ክበጽሑ ኣይክእሉን ነበሩ። ብሓባር በይኖም ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ስድራ = sidra = families, ክሓቁፉ = kiHaqufu = to hug, ዕዳጋ = 'idaga = market)

Page 4

[PICTURE: The ten lepers seeing Jesus approaching on the road, hope appearing on their faces for the first time. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
When the ten men saw Jesus coming, hope filled their hearts! They had heard that Jesus could heal sick people. Maybe He could heal them too! This was their chance!
TIGRINYA:
ዓሰርቱ ሰባት ንየሱስ ክመጽእ ምስ ረኣዩዎ ተስፋ ልቦም መልአ! የሱስ ሕሙማት ሰባት ከም ዝፍውስ ሰሚዖም ነበሩ። ንዓኦም'ውን ክፍውሶም ይኽእል'ዩ! እዚ ዕድሎም ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: ተስፋ = tesfa = hope, ሕሙማት = Himumat = sick people, ዕድል = 'idil = chance)

Page 5

[PICTURE: The ten lepers calling out to Jesus from a distance, hands raised, shouting together with desperation and hope. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The ten men called out loudly from far away: "Jesus! Master! Have pity on us! Please help us!" They could not come close, so they shouted as loud as they could.
TIGRINYA:
ዓሰርቱ ሰባት ካብ ርሑቕ ብዓውታ ጸውዑ: "የሱስ! መምህር! ራህርሃልና! በጃኻ ሓግዘና!" ክቐርቡ ስለ ዘይክእሉ ብዝኽእሉዎ ጨደሩ።
(Pronunciation: ብዓውታ = bi'awta = loudly, መምህር = memhir = Master, ራህርሃልና = raHrihalna = have pity on us)

Page 6

[PICTURE: Jesus turning toward the ten men with compassion, seeing their suffering, stopping to listen to their cries. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus heard their cries and looked at them with love. He saw their pain and their suffering. His heart was filled with compassion for these ten men who needed help so badly.
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ጭድሪኦም ሰሚዑ ብፍቕሪ ጠመቶም። ቃንዛኦምን ስቓዮምን ረኣየ። ልቡ ኣዝዩ ሓገዝ ንዘድልዮም ነዞም ዓሰርተ ሰባት ብርሕራሐ መልአ።
(Pronunciation: ጭድሪ = ch'idri = cries, ቃንዛ = qanza = pain, ርሕራሐ = riHraHe = compassion)

Page 7

[PICTURE: Jesus speaking to the ten men from the road, giving them instructions, pointing toward the village. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus called back to them: "Go and show yourselves to the priests!" This was a strange command. Usually only healed people went to the priests. But they were still sick!
TIGRINYA:
የሱስ ንዓኦም ጸውዐ: "ኪዱ ንገዛእ ርእስኹም ንኻህናት ኣርእዩ!" እዚ ዘገርም ትእዛዝ ነበረ። መብዛሕትኡ ግዜ ዝሓወዩ ሰባት ጥራይ ናብ ኻህናት ይኸዱ ነበሩ። ግን ገና ሕሙማት'ዮም ነይሮም!
(Pronunciation: ኻህናት = kaHnat = priests, ትእዛዝ = ti'izaz = command, ዝሓወዩ = ziHaweyu = healed)

Page 8

[PICTURE: The ten men looking at each other, deciding to trust Jesus, beginning to walk toward the village together. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The ten men looked at each other. Should they go? They were still sick! But they chose to trust Jesus and obey His words. Together, they turned and began walking toward the priests.
TIGRINYA:
ዓሰርቱ ሰባት ንሓድሕዶም ተጠማመቱ። ክኸዱ ኣለዎም'ዶ? ገና ሕሙማት'ዮም ነይሮም! ግን ንየሱስ ክኣምኑን ቃሉ ክእዘዙን መረጹ። ብሓባር ተጠውዮም ናብ ኻህናት ክኸዱ ጀመሩ።
(Pronunciation: ተጠማመቱ = tet'amametu = looked at each other, ክኣምኑ = ki'aminu = to trust, ክእዘዙ = ki'izezu = to obey)

Page 9

[PICTURE: The ten men walking along the path, suddenly looking at their hands and arms as their skin begins to heal and clear up. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
As they walked, something amazing happened! They looked at their hands and saw their skin becoming clean and healthy! The sores were disappearing! They were being healed as they walked!
TIGRINYA:
ክኸዱ ከለዉ ዘደንቕ ነገር ተፈጸመ! ናብ ኣእዳዎም ጠሚቶም ቆርበቶም ንጹህን ጥዑይን ይኸውን ከም ዘሎ ረኣዩ! ቁስልታት ይጠፍኡ ነበሩ! ክኸዱ ከለዉ ይሓውዩ ነበሩ!
(Pronunciation: ዘደንቕ = zedenq = amazing, ቆርበት = qorbet = skin, ንጹህ = nits'uH = clean, ይሓውዩ = yiHawyu = healing)

Page 10

[PICTURE: The ten men celebrating wildly - jumping, hugging each other, looking at their healed skin in disbelief and joy. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
"Look! My skin is clean!" "Mine too!" "We are healed!" The men jumped and hugged each other with joy! All ten of them were completely healed! Not one sore remained!
TIGRINYA:
"ርኣዩ! ቆርበተይ ንጹህ!" "ናተይ'ውን!" "ሓዊና!" ሰባት ዘሊሎም ብሓጎስ ተሓቛቘፉ! ኩሎም ዓሰርቲኦም ብምሉእ ሓዊዮም! ሓንቲ ቁስሊ'ኳ ኣይተረፈትን!
(Pronunciation: ቆርበተይ = qorbetey = my skin, ሓዊና = Hawina = we are healed, ተሓቛቘፉ = teHaquaqofu = hugged each other)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Nine of the men running excitedly toward the village, heading to see the priests, running away from Jesus. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Nine of the men were so excited that they kept running toward the village! They wanted to see the priests, go home, and see their families! They ran without looking back.
TIGRINYA:
ትሽዓቱ ሰባት ኣዝዮም ተሓጒሶም ናብ ዓዲ ምጉያይ ቀጸሉ! ንኻህናት ክርእዩ ናብ ገዝኦም ክኸዱ ንስድራኦም ክርእዩ ደለዩ! ንድሕሪት ከይጠመቱ ጎየዩ።
(Pronunciation: ትሽዓተ = tish'ate = nine, ጎየዩ = goyeyu = ran, ንድሕሪት = nidiHrit = back)

Page 12

[PICTURE: One man stopping in his tracks, turning around, looking back toward Jesus while the other nine keep running ahead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
But ONE man stopped. He looked at his healed hands and then looked back toward Jesus. "I must go back and thank Him!" he said. He turned around and ran back to Jesus.
TIGRINYA:
ግን ሓደ ሰብ ደው በለ። ናብ ዝሓወያ ኣእዳዉ ጠሚቱ ድሕሪኡ ናብ የሱስ ንድሕሪት ጠመተ። "ተመሊሰ ከመስግኖ ኣለኒ!" በለ። ተጠውዩ ናብ የሱስ ንድሕሪት ጎየ።
(Pronunciation: ሓደ = Hade = one, ዝሓወያ = ziHaweya = healed, ከመስግኖ = kemesgino = to thank Him)

[PICTURE: The one man running back to Jesus with arms outstretched, tears of gratitude on his face, full of thankfulness. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

(Pronunciation: ውዳሴ = widase = praise, ኣመስግነካ = amesgineka = thank You, ፈዊስካኒ = fewiskani = You healed me)

[PICTURE: Jesus looking down at the thankful man with a warm smile, then looking up at the road where the other nine disappeared. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:

Page 16

[PICTURE: Jesus helping the thankful man stand up, holding his hands, looking into his eyes with special love and blessing. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Jesus helped the man stand up. He looked at him with special love and said, "Rise and go. Your faith has made you well." This man received an extra blessing because he was thankful!

(Pronunciation: ተንስእ = tenis' = rise, እምነትካ = imnetka = your faith, በረኸት = bereket = blessing)

(Pronunciation: ረሲዖም = resi'om = forgot, ምስጋና = misgana = gratitude, ቀጸሉ = qets'elu = continued)
