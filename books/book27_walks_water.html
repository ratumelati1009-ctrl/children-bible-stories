Scene 1
[PICTURE: Jesus standing on a green hillside with a large crowd of people sitting around Him, finishing a meal of bread and fish. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus had just fed five thousand people with only five loaves of bread and two fish. Everyone was amazed!
የሱስ ብሓሙሽተ እንጌራን ክልተ ዓሳን ጥራይ ሓሙሽተ ሽሕ ሰባት ኣጽጊቡ ነበረ። ኩሎም ተገሪሞም!
Yesus biHamushte ingeran kilte asan teray Hamushte shiH sebat atsgibu nebere. Kulom teGerimo!
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Jesus gently directing His disciples toward a wooden boat at the shore of a lake, pointing across the water. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After the miracle, Jesus told His disciples to get into a boat and go ahead of Him to the other side of the lake.
ድሕሪ እቲ ተኣምር፡ የሱስ ንደቀ መዛሙርቱ ብጃልባ ቅድሚኡ ናብቲ ማዶ ባሕሪ ክሰግሩ ነገሮም።
DiHri iti te'amir, Yesus ndeqe mezamurtu bijalba qidmi'u nabti mado baHri kisegru negerom.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Jesus waving goodbye to the crowd of people who are walking away along a dusty path. The sun is beginning to set. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Jesus sent the crowd away. He wanted to be alone so He could pray to His Father in heaven.
ድሕሪኡ የሱስ ነቲ ህዝቢ ሰደዶም። ንበይኑ ኮይኑ ናብ ኣቦኡ ኣብ ሰማይ ክጽሊ ስለ ዝደለየ።
DiHri'u Yesus neti hizbi sededom. Nibeynu koynu nab Abo'u ab semay kitsli sle zideleye.
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Jesus kneeling alone on top of a mountain, hands raised in prayer, stars beginning to appear in the evening sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus climbed up the mountain by Himself. He knelt down and prayed to God. He talked to His Father for a long time.
የሱስ ንበይኑ ናብ ከረን ደየበ። ተምበርኪኹ ናብ ኣምላኽ ጸለየ። ንነዊሕ ግዜ ምስ ኣቦኡ ተዛረበ።
Yesus nibeynu nab keren deyebe. Temberkiku nab Amlak tseleye. Ninewih gize mis Abo'u tezarebe.
Scene 5
[PICTURE: A wooden boat with disciples rowing in calm waters under a darkening sky, the distant shore behind them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Meanwhile, the disciples were in the boat, rowing across the lake. The sky was getting darker and darker.
ብእዚ እዋን እቶም ደቀ መዛሙርቲ ኣብ ጃልባ ነቲ ባሕሪ ይቕድፉ ነበሩ። እቲ ሰማይ እናጸልመተ ከደ።
Bi'izi iwan itom deqe mezamurti ab jalba neti baHri yiqdifu neberu. Iti semay inatselimete kede.
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Strong winds blowing across the lake with dark clouds gathering, waves starting to form around the small boat. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Suddenly, a strong wind began to blow! The waves grew bigger and bigger, rocking the boat back and forth.
ብድንገት ሓያል ንፋስ ክነፍስ ጀመረ! ማዕበላት ዓብየን ዓብየን ነቲ ጃልባ ንየማንን ንጸጋምን ይወዝውዞ ነበረ።
Bidnget Hayal nifas kinefis jemere! Ma'ibelat abyen abyen neti jalba niyemanan nitsegaman yiwezwizo nebere.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Disciples struggling to row in the storm, waves splashing over the sides of the boat, their faces showing effort and worry. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The disciples rowed as hard as they could, but the wind pushed against them. They were far from shore and very tired.
ደቀ መዛሙርቲ ብዝኽእልዎ ይቕድፉ ነበሩ፡ ግን እቲ ንፋስ ይጻረሮም ነበረ። ካብ ገምገም ርሒቖም ኣዝዮም ድኹማት ነበሩ።
Deqe mezamurti bizikhi'liwo yiqdifu neberu, gin iti nifas yitsarerom nebere. Kab gemgem riHiqom azyom dikumat neberu.
Scene 8
[PICTURE: A completely dark night sky with the boat being tossed by huge waves, lightning flashing in the distance. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
It was now the middle of the night — between three and six in the morning. The storm was still raging all around them.
ሕጂ ፍርቂ ለይቲ ነበረ — ኣብ መንጎ ሰለስተን ሽዱሽተን ናይ ወጋሕታ። እቲ ማዕበል ገና ይነውጽ ነበረ።
Hiji firqi leyti nebere — ab mengo selestenin shidushtenin nay wegaHta. Iti ma'ibel gena yinewtsi nebere.
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Jesus stepping onto the surface of the stormy water from the mountain shore, His robes gently flowing, a peaceful expression on His face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then something amazing happened! Jesus came walking toward them — right on top of the water! He walked on the waves like they were solid ground.
ሽዑ ዘደንቕ ነገር ኮነ! የሱስ ናባታቶም ብእግሩ ይመጽእ ነበረ — ኣብ ልዕሊ ማይ! ኣብ ልዕሊ ማዕበላት ከም ጽኑዕ መሬት ይኸይድ ነበረ።
Shi'u zedenq neger kone! Yesus nabatatom bi'igru yimetsi' nebere — ab li'ili may! Ab li'ili ma'ibelat kem tsnu'i meret yikeyd nebere.
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Disciples in the boat with terrified expressions, pointing at a figure walking on the dark water toward them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When the disciples saw someone walking on the water, they were terrified! "It's a ghost!" they screamed in fear.
ደቀ መዛሙርቲ ኣብ ልዕሊ ማይ ዝኸይድ ሰብ ምስ ረኣዩ፡ ኣዝዮም ፈርሑ! "መንፈስ እዩ!" ብፍርሒ ጨደሩ።
Deqe mezamurti ab li'ili may zikeyd seb mis re'ayu, azyom ferHu! "Menfes iyu!" biFirHi chederu.
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Jesus walking calmly on the water with His hand raised in a calming gesture, His face kind and reassuring as He approaches the boat. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Jesus quickly called out to them, "Be brave! It is I. Don't be afraid!" His voice was warm and strong above the storm.
ግን የሱስ ቀልጢፉ ጸውዓም፡ "ጽንዑ! ኣነ እየ። ኣይትፍርሑ!" ድምጹ ካብ ማዕበል ንላዕሊ ውዑይን ሓያልን ነበረ።
Gin Yesus qeltifu tsew'am, "Tsni'u! Ane iye. Aytifrihu!" Dimtsu kab ma'ibel nila'ili wu'uyin Hayalin nebere.
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Peter standing up eagerly in the rocking boat, looking excitedly at Jesus on the water, other disciples watching nervously. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter was so excited! He called out, "Lord, if it's really You, tell me to come to You on the water!"
ጴጥሮስ ኣዝዩ ተሓጒሱ! "ጎይታ፡ ብሓቂ ንስኻ እንተ ኮይንካ፡ ኣብ ልዕሊ ማይ ናባኻ ክመጽእ ኣዝዘኒ!" ኢሉ ጸውዓ።
Petros azyu teHagisu! "Goyta, biHaqi niska inte koynka, ab li'ili may nabaka kimtsi' azzeni!" ilu tsew'a.
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Jesus smiling warmly with His hand extended toward Peter, standing on the water with gentle waves around His feet. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus smiled and said one simple word: "Come." He stretched out His hand toward Peter.
የሱስ ፍሽኽ ኢሉ ሓደ ቃል ጥራይ በለ፡ "ንዓ።" ኢዱ ናብ ጴጥሮስ ዘርጊሑ።
Yesus fishkh ilu Hade qal teray bele, "Ni'a." Idu nab Petros zergiHu.
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Peter with one foot stepping out of the boat onto the water surface, his eyes fixed on Jesus, a look of brave determination on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter took a deep breath and stepped out of the boat! His foot touched the water — and it held him up! He was standing on the water!
ጴጥሮስ ዓሚቝ ትንፋስ ወሲዱ ካብ ጃልባ ወጸ! እግሩ ነቲ ማይ ተንከፎ — ሓዚሉ ድማ! ኣብ ልዕሊ ማይ ደው ኢሉ!
Petros amiq tinfas wesidu kab jalba wetse! Igru neti may tenkefo — Hazilu dima! Ab li'ili may dew ilu!
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Peter walking on the water toward Jesus with a joyful amazed expression, waves gently lapping around his feet but not pulling him under. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Peter began to walk on the water toward Jesus! Step by step, he moved across the waves. As long as he looked at Jesus, he stayed on top!
ጴጥሮስ ኣብ ልዕሊ ማይ ናብ የሱስ ክኸይድ ጀመረ! ስጉምቲ ብስጉምቲ ኣብ ልዕሊ ማዕበላት ይኸይድ ነበረ። ናብ የሱስ ክሳብ ዝጥምት ኣብ ልዕሊ ነበረ!
Petros ab li'ili may nab Yesus kikeyd jemere! Sigumti bisigumti ab li'ili ma'ibelat yikeyd nebere. Nab Yesus kisab zitmit ab li'ili nebere!
Scene 16
[PICTURE: Peter turning his head to look at the large crashing waves around him, his expression changing from joy to fear, wind whipping his clothing. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But then Peter looked away from Jesus. He saw the big waves and felt the strong wind. He became very afraid!
ግን ሽዑ ጴጥሮስ ካብ የሱስ ኣረኣእያኡ ኣልወጠ። ነቲ ዓበይቲ ማዕበላትን ሓያል ንፋስን ረኣየ። ኣዝዩ ፈርሐ!
Gin shi'u Petros kab Yesus are'ay'ya'u alwete. Neti abeyti ma'ibelatn Hayal nifasn re'aye. Azyu ferHe!
Scene 17
[PICTURE: Peter sinking into the water up to his waist, his arms flailing above, mouth open crying out, waves crashing around him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
As soon as Peter took his eyes off Jesus and focused on the storm, he began to sink! "Lord, save me!" he cried out.
ጴጥሮስ ኣዒንቱ ካብ የሱስ ኣልዒሉ ነቲ ማዕበል ምስ ጠመተ፡ ክስጥም ጀመረ! "ጎይታ፡ ኣድሕነኒ!" ኢሉ ጨደረ።
Petros a'intu kab Yesus ali'ilu neti ma'ibel mis temete, kisitim jemere! "Goyta, adiHneni!" ilu chedere.
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Jesus immediately reaching down and grabbing Peter's hand firmly, pulling him up from the water with a loving but concerned expression. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Right away, Jesus reached out His hand and caught Peter! He pulled him up from the water. Jesus never let him drown.
ብቕጽበት የሱስ ኢዱ ዘርጊሑ ንጴጥሮስ ሓዞ! ካብ ማይ ኣልዒሉ። የሱስ ክጥሕል ኣይገደፎን።
Biqitsbet Yesus idu zergiHu niPetros Hazo! Kab may ali'ilu. Yesus kitiHil aygededfon.
Scene 19
[PICTURE: Jesus holding Peter's hand steady on the water, looking into his eyes with gentle kindness, speaking to him tenderly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus gently said to Peter, "You have so little faith. Why did you doubt?" He wasn't angry — He wanted Peter to trust more.
የሱስ ብልኽዕና ንጴጥሮስ "ኣታ ውሑድ እምነት ዘለካ፡ ስለምንታይ ተጠራጠርካ?" በሎ። ኣይተቖጥዐን — ንጴጥሮስ ዝያዳ ክኣምን ይደሊ ነበረ።
Yesus bilikhi'ina niPetros "Ata wuHud imnet zeleka, slemintay teteratirka?" belo. Aytequoti'en — niPetros ziyada ki'amin yideli nebere.
Scene 20
[PICTURE: Jesus and Peter walking together on the water back toward the boat, Peter looking relieved and holding Jesus' hand tightly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus and Peter walked together on the water back to the boat. Peter held tightly to Jesus' hand, feeling safe and loved.
የሱስን ጴጥሮስን ብሓንሳብ ኣብ ልዕሊ ማይ ናብ ጃልባ ተመለሱ። ጴጥሮስ ኢድ የሱስ ጽኑዕ ሒዙ፡ ውሑስን ፍቑርን ተሰሚዕዎ።
Yesusn Petrosn biHansab ab li'ili may nab jalba temelesu. Petros id Yesus tsnu'i Hizu, wuHusn fiqurin tesemi'iwo.
Scene 21
[PICTURE: Jesus and Peter climbing into the boat, the other disciples reaching out to help them aboard with amazed faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When Jesus and Peter climbed into the boat, something wonderful happened — the wind completely stopped! The storm was over!
የሱስን ጴጥሮስን ናብ ጃልባ ምስ ደየቡ፡ ዘደንቕ ነገር ኮነ — እቲ ንፋስ ብምሉኡ ደው በለ! እቲ ማዕበል ተወድአ!
Yesusn Petrosn nab jalba mis deyebu, zedenq neger kone — iti nifas bimlu'u dew bele! Iti ma'ibel tewede'a!
Scene 22
[PICTURE: The lake becoming perfectly calm and still like glass, the sky clearing with stars shining above, the boat floating peacefully. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The water became calm and peaceful. The sky cleared and the stars shone brightly. Everything was quiet and still.
እቲ ማይ ህዱእን ሰላማውን ኮነ። እቲ ሰማይ ጸረየ ከዋኽብቲ ድማ ብሩህ ኮነ። ኩሉ ነገር ህዱእን ጸጥታን ኮነ።
Iti may hidu'in selamawn kone. Iti semay tsereye kewakhbti dima biruh kone. Kulu neger hidu'in tsettayn kone.
Scene 23
[PICTURE: All the disciples bowing down and worshipping Jesus in the boat, some with hands raised, some kneeling, expressions of awe and reverence. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
All the disciples fell down and worshipped Jesus. "You truly are the Son of God!" they said with wonder and praise.
ኩሎም ደቀ መዛሙርቲ ተደፊኦም ንየሱስ ሰገዱሉ። "ብሓቂ ንስኻ ወዲ ኣምላኽ ኢኻ!" ብምስትንዓቕን ምስጋናን በሉ።
Kulom deqe mezamurti tedefi'om niYesus segedulu. "BiHaqi niska wedi Amlak ika!" bimistin'aqin misganain belu.
Scene 24
[PICTURE: The boat sailing smoothly across calm waters toward a shore with gentle hills, sunrise beginning to paint the sky in warm colors. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The boat sailed safely to the other side. The disciples learned that with Jesus, there is nothing to fear — even storms obey Him!
ጃልባ ብሰላም ናብቲ ማዶ በጽሐት። ደቀ መዛሙርቲ ምስ የሱስ ዝፍራህ ነገር ከም ዘየለ ተማሃሩ — ማዕበላት እውን ይእዘዝዎ!
Jalba biselam nabti mado betsHat. Deqe mezamurti mis Yesus zifrah neger kem zeyele temaharu — ma'ibelat i'wun yi'izeziwo!
Scene 25
[PICTURE: A child looking up at Jesus with trusting eyes, Jesus with His hand on the child's shoulder, calm waters and a rainbow in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Just like Peter, when we keep our eyes on Jesus and trust Him, we can face any storm in life. He is always there to catch us when we feel afraid!
ከም ጴጥሮስ፡ ኣዒንትና ኣብ የሱስ ኣቐሚጥና ምስ ንኣምኖ፡ ኣብ ህይወትና ዝኾነ ማዕበል ክንጻወር ንኽእል! ምስ ንፈርሕ ኩሉ ግዜ ክሕዘና ኣብኡ ኣሎ!
Kem Petros, a'intna ab Yesus aqemitna mis ni'amino, ab hiywetna zikone ma'ibel kintsawer nikhil! Mis nifirH kulu gize kiHizena ab'u alo!

The Moral of the Story

Keep your eyes on Jesus, not on your problems. Faith means trusting Him even in the middle of life's storms. When we focus on our fears, we begin to sink — but when we look to Jesus, He holds us up and carries us through.
