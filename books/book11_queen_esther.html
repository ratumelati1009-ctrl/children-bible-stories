Queen Esther's Courage

ትብዓት ንግስቲ ኣስቴር

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 11

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 11

For all the brave children who stand up for what is right, even when it is hard.

ንኹሎም ከቢድ ኣብ ዝኾነሉ'ውን ንቕኑዕ ዝቖሙ ተባዓት ቆልዑ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A beautiful young woman named Esther living in a humble home with her older cousin Mordecai, both looking kind. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Long ago, there lived a beautiful young woman named Esther. She was kind and good. Her older cousin Mordecai raised her like his own daughter after her parents died.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ቅድሚ ግዜ ኣስቴር ዝስማ ጽብቕቲ መንእሰይ ጓል ነበረት። ለዋህን ጽብቕትን ነበረት። ድሕሪ ሞት ወለዳ ዓቢ ሓዉ ሞርድኬ ከም ጓሉ ኣዕበያ።
(Pronunciation: ኣስቴር = Aster = Esther, ሞርድኬ = Mordeke = Mordecai, ጽብቕቲ = ts'ibiqti = beautiful)

Page 2

[PICTURE: A grand palace with golden pillars and colorful curtains, the Persian king sitting on a magnificent throne. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
In that land, there was a powerful king named King Xerxes. He lived in a huge, beautiful palace. One day, he decided to choose a new queen.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ'ታ ሃገር ንጉስ ኣሕሽዌሮስ ዝብሃል ሓያል ንጉስ ነበረ። ኣብ ዓባይ ጽብቕቲ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ይነብር ነበረ። ሓደ መዓልቲ ሓዳስ ንግስቲ ክመርጽ ወሰነ።
(Pronunciation: ንጉስ = nigus = king, ቤተ-መንግስቲ = beyte-mengisti = palace, ንግስቲ = nigisti = queen)

Page 3

[PICTURE: Many young women being brought to the palace, Esther among them looking humble and gentle, entering the palace gates. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Many beautiful young women came to the palace. Esther was one of them. She was nervous, but Mordecai told her, "Trust God. He has a plan for you."
TIGRINYA:
ብዙሓት ጽቡቓት መንእሰያት ደቂ ኣንስትዮ ናብ ቤተ-መንግስቲ መጽኣ። ኣስቴር ሓንቲ ካብኣተን ነበረት። ተረቢሻ ነበረት ግን ሞርድኬ "ንኣምላኽ እመኒ። መደብ ኣለዎ ንዓኺ" በላ።
(Pronunciation: ደቂ ኣንስትዮ = deqi anistiyo = young women, ተረቢሻ = terebisha = nervous, መደብ = medeb = plan)

Page 4

[PICTURE: King Xerxes smiling warmly at Esther, placing a golden crown on her head, Esther looking graceful and humble. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The king loved Esther more than all the others! He placed a beautiful golden crown on her head. Esther became the queen! Everyone in the kingdom celebrated.
TIGRINYA:
ንጉስ ንኣስቴር ካብ ኩለን ንላዕሊ ፈተዋ! ጽብቕቲ ወርቃዊት ዘውዲ ኣብ ርእሳ ኣቐመጠላ። ኣስቴር ንግስቲ ኮነት! ኩሉ ሰብ ኣብ'ታ መንግስቲ ኣብዓለ።
(Pronunciation: ዘውዲ = zewdi = crown, ወርቃዊት = werqawit = golden, ኣብዓለ = ab'ale = celebrated)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Mordecai sitting at the palace gate, watching over Esther from afar, looking proud and protective. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Mordecai was so proud of Esther! He sat near the palace gate every day to watch over her. He told Esther to keep a secret - she was Jewish, one of God's special people.
TIGRINYA:
ሞርድኬ ብኣስቴር ኣዝዩ ሕጉስ ነበረ! ነፍሲ ወከፍ መዓልቲ ኣብ ቀረባ ደገ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ኮፍ ኢሉ ይሕልዋ ነበረ። ንኣስቴር ምስጢር ክትሕዝ ነገራ - ኣይሁዳዊት ሓንቲ ካብ ፍሉያት ህዝቢ ኣምላኽ ምዃና።
(Pronunciation: ሕጉስ = Higus = proud, ምስጢር = mist'ir = secret, ኣይሁዳዊት = ayhudawit = Jewish)

Page 6

[PICTURE: A mean-looking man named Haman in fancy robes, looking arrogant and cruel, standing in the palace with evil eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
But there was a bad man in the palace named Haman. He was very proud and mean. The king made him the most important helper. Haman wanted everyone to bow down to him.
TIGRINYA:
ግን ኣብ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ሃማን ዝስሙ ሕሱም ሰብ ነበረ። ኣዝዩ ዕቡይን ጨካንን ነበረ። ንጉስ ኣዝዩ ኣገዳሲ ሓጋዚ ገበሮ። ሃማን ኩሉ ሰብ ንዕኡ ክስገድ ደለየ።
(Pronunciation: ሃማን = Haman = Haman, ሕሱም = Hisum = mean, ዕቡይ = 'ibuy = proud)

Page 7

[PICTURE: Mordecai standing tall while others bow to Haman, refusing to bow, looking brave and dignified at the palace gate. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Everyone bowed to Haman - except Mordecai! He would only bow to God. This made Haman furious! His face turned red with anger. He wanted revenge on Mordecai.
TIGRINYA:
ኩሉ ሰብ ንሃማን ይሰግድ ነበረ - ብዘይ ሞርድኬ! ንኣምላኽ ጥራይ'ዩ ዝሰግድ። እዚ ንሃማን ኣዝዩ ኣቖጥዖ! ገጹ ብቑጥዓ ቐይሐ። ኣብ ሞርድኬ ሕነ ክፈዲ ደለየ።
(Pronunciation: ይሰግድ = yisegid = bow, ኣቖጥዖ = aqot'i'o = angered him, ሕነ = Hine = revenge)

Page 8

[PICTURE: Haman whispering his evil plan to the king, a wicked smile on his face, the king looking thoughtful and unaware of the trick. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Haman made a terrible, evil plan! He tricked the king into making a law to destroy all of Mordecai's people - all the Jewish people in the whole kingdom!
TIGRINYA:
ሃማን ኣዝዩ ሕሱም ክፉእ መደብ ሰርሐ! ንኹሎም ህዝቢ ሞርድኬ - ኩሎም ኣይሁድ ኣብ ምሉእ መንግስቲ - ንምጥፋእ ሕጊ ንኽወጽእ ንንጉስ ኣታለሎ!
(Pronunciation: ክፉእ = kifu'e = evil, መደብ = medeb = plan, ኣታለሎ = atalelo = tricked him)

Page 9

[PICTURE: Mordecai in torn clothes weeping outside the palace gate, other Jewish people around him looking frightened and sad. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
When Mordecai heard about the evil plan, he was heartbroken! He tore his clothes and cried. All the Jewish people were terrified. Who could save them?
TIGRINYA:
ሞርድኬ ብዛዕባ'ቲ ክፉእ መደብ ምስ ሰምዐ ልቡ ተሰብረ! ክዳኑ ቐዲዱ ነበዐ። ኩሎም ኣይሁድ ኣዝዮም ፈሪሖም። መን ከድሕኖም ይኽእል?
(Pronunciation: ልቡ ተሰብረ = libu tesebere = heartbroken, ነበዐ = nebe'e = cried, ከድሕኖም = kedHinom = save them)

Page 10

[PICTURE: A messenger delivering a secret letter from Mordecai to Queen Esther inside the palace, Esther reading it with worried eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Mordecai sent a message to Queen Esther. "You must go to the king and beg him to save our people!" he said. "Only you can help us now!"
TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: መልእኽቲ = mel'ikhti = message, ህዝብና = hizbina = our people, ክትሕግዝና = kitHigzina = help us)

(Pronunciation: ፈሪሓ = feriHa = afraid, ሕጊ = Higi = law, ህይወታ = hiyiweta = her life)

(Pronunciation: ንኸምዚ ዝበለ ግዜ = nikemzi zibele gize = for such a time, ቃላት = qalat = words, ተንከፋ = tenkefa = touched)
