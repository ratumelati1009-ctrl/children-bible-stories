In the year that King Uzziah died, the nation of Judah was plunged into political crisis and uncertainty. Uzziah had reigned for fifty-two years, and his death left a void that no one knew how to fill. The surrounding nations—Assyria, Egypt, and Syria—watched like vultures, sensing weakness. The people feared what would come next, for their strong king was gone. Isaiah, a young nobleman who served in the courts, felt the weight of this national grief pressing upon his heart. Everything that had seemed stable and secure was suddenly shaken to its foundations.

[PICTURE: A young man with East African features wearing simple robes enters the grand temple of Jerusalem alone. His face shows grief and searching. Shafts of light filter through the temple pillars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Isaiah entered the temple in Jerusalem, seeking the presence of God in his time of mourning and confusion. The incense rose like silent prayers toward the heavens, and the great pillars stood as witnesses to centuries of worship. Isaiah did not come with answers—he came with questions, with grief, with a desperate need to hear from the God of his fathers. He understood that when earthly kings fall, only the eternal King remains. In the quietness of that sacred space, Isaiah opened his heart completely, holding nothing back from the Almighty. He did not know that this ordinary act of worship would become the most extraordinary moment of his entire life.
ኢሳይያስ ኣብ ግዜ ሓዘኑን ምድንጋረኡን ናብ ቤተ መቕደስ ኢየሩሳሌም ኣተወ፡ ኣምላኽ ክረኽቦ ደለየ። ዕጣን ከም ህድእ ጸሎት ናብ ሰማይ ይድይብ ነበረ፡ ዓበይቲ ኣዕኑድ ድማ ናይ ዘመናት ኣምልኾ ምስክራት ኮይኖም ቆሙ። ኢሳይያስ ብመልሲ ኣይመጸን—ብሕቶታት፡ ብሓዘን፡ ብጽኑዕ ድሌት ካብ ኣምላኽ ኣቦታቱ ክሰምዕ መጸ። ምድራውያን ነገስታት ምስ ዝወድቁ፡ ዘልኣለማዊ ንጉስ ጥራይ ከም ዝተርፍ ተረደኦ። ኣብቲ ህድኣት ናይቲ ቅዱስ ቦታ፡ ኢሳይያስ ልቡ ብምሉእ ከፈተ፡ ካብ ኩሉ ዝኽእል ገለ ኣይሓበአን። እዚ ተራ ናይ ኣምልኾ ተግባር ናይ ምሉእ ህይወቱ ዝዓበየ ህሞት ክኸውን ምዃኑ ኣይፈለጠን።

Scene 3 of 25
[PICTURE: A breathtaking heavenly throne room appearing above the temple. A majestic figure sits on an impossibly high throne radiating golden light. Isaiah looks upward in total awe, his body trembling. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then the vision broke open like thunder splitting the sky. Isaiah saw the Lord seated on a throne, high and lifted up, exalted beyond anything human imagination could conceive. This was not a dream or a metaphor—it was the most real thing Isaiah had ever experienced. The throne towered above everything, speaking of absolute sovereignty and unquestionable authority over all creation. The glory radiating from the Lord was not merely bright—it was overwhelming, terrifying, and beautiful all at once. Isaiah stood frozen, unable to move, unable to look away, caught between wonder and dread.
ድሕሪኡ ራእይ ከም ነጎዳ ሰማይ ዝሰንጥቕ ተኸፍተ። ኢሳይያስ ንእግዚኣብሄር ኣብ ዝፋን ተቐሚጡ ረኣዮ፡ ልዑልን ተላዒሉን፡ ካብ ሰብኣዊ ሓሳብ ንላዕሊ ዝበረኸ። እዚ ሕልሚ ወይ ምስላ ኣይነበረን—ኢሳይያስ ዝተመክሮ ዝበለጸ ሓቂ ነበረ። ዝፋኑ ካብ ኩሉ ንላዕሊ ቆመ፡ ፍጹም ልዑልነትን ዘይከሓድ ስልጣንን ኣብ ልዕሊ ኩሉ ፍጥረት ይዛረብ ነበረ። ካብ እግዚኣብሄር ዝወጽእ ክብሪ ብሩህ ጥራይ ኣይነበረን—ኣብ ሓደ ግዜ ዝስዕር፡ ዘፍርህን ዝጽብቕን ነበረ። ኢሳይያስ ረጊኡ ቆመ፡ ክንቀሳቐስ ኣይከኣለን፡ ክርሕቕ ኣይከኣለን፡ ኣብ መንጎ ተኣምርን ፍርሓትን ተታሒዙ።

Scene 4 of 25
[PICTURE: The massive, richly embroidered hem of a royal robe cascading down from the throne, filling the entire temple floor with flowing fabric of gold and deep purple. The scale is overwhelming. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The train of God's robe filled the entire temple—every corner, every alcove, every sacred space was consumed by the magnificent fabric of His garment. This single detail revealed that God's presence cannot be contained within any building made by human hands. The hem alone was enough to fill the greatest structure Israel had ever built. Isaiah understood in that moment that the temple was not a house for God—it was merely a meeting place where infinite glory condescended to touch finite humanity. The sheer scale of what he witnessed made the grandest human achievements seem like children's toys.

Scene 5 of 25
[PICTURE: Magnificent six-winged seraphim hovering above the throne. Each covers their face with two wings, their feet with two wings, and flies with two wings. They glow with fiery radiance. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Above the throne stood the seraphim—burning ones—angelic beings of breathtaking power and beauty. Each had six wings: with two they covered their faces, unable to gaze directly upon God's full glory even in their celestial perfection. With two they covered their feet, a posture of deepest reverence and humility before their Creator. With the remaining two they flew, suspended in eternal worship and service. These were not weak or timid creatures—they were mighty beings of fire and light, yet even they recognized their place before the Almighty. If such glorious beings must veil themselves before God, what hope has mortal flesh?
ኣብ ልዕሊ ዝፋን ሱራፌል ቆሙ—ነደድቲ—ዘደንቕ ሓይልን ጽባቐን ዘለዎም መላእኽቲ። ነፍሲ ወከፎም ሽዱሽተ ክንፊ ነበሮም፦ ብኽልተ ገጾም ሸፈኑ፡ ኣብ ሰማያዊ ፍጽምናኦም'ውን ናብ ምሉእ ክብሪ ኣምላኽ ቀጥታ ክጥምቱ ኣይከኣሉን። ብኽልተ ኣእጋሮም ሸፈኑ፡ ቅድሚ ፈጣሪኦም ዝዓመቐ ኣኽብሮትን ትሕትናን ብምርኣይ። ብዝተረፈ ክልተ ድማ ነፈሩ፡ ኣብ ዘልኣለማዊ ኣምልኾን ኣገልግሎትን ተንጠልጢሎም። እዚኣቶም ድኹማትን ፈራሃትን ፍጥረታት ኣይነበሩን—ሓያላት ናይ ሓውን ብርሃንን ፍጥረታት ነበሩ፡ ግን ንሳቶም'ውን ቅድሚ ኩሉ ዝኽእል ቦታኦም ኣፈለጡ። ከምዚኦም ዝበሉ ኽቡራት ፍጥረታት ቅድሚ ኣምላኽ ርእሶም ክሽፍኑ እንተደኣ ኣለዎም፡ ስጋዊ ሰብ እንታይ ተስፋ ኣለዎ?

[PICTURE: The seraphim calling out to one another with mouths open wide, sound waves visible in the air. And the seraphim called to one another with voices like rolling thunder: "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Almighty; the whole earth is full of His glory!" The triple repetition was not mere emphasis—it was the highest possible declaration in Hebrew, a superlative beyond superlatives. God is not merely holy; He is holy, holy, holy—infinitely set apart, infinitely pure, infinitely other. No other attribute of God is repeated three times in Scripture. Not "love, love, love" or "power, power, power"—but "holy, holy, holy." This is the foundation upon which all other attributes rest, the defining characteristic of the God who created everything.

What We Learn

Isaiah's vision teaches us that encountering God's holiness changes everything. When we truly see who God is—infinitely holy, utterly pure, completely sovereign—we cannot remain the same. His holiness exposes our sin, but His grace provides cleansing. And once we have been forgiven, the natural response is availability: "Here am I, send me!" God does not call the perfect; He calls the willing. He does not demand perfection before service; He provides purification that enables service. Our job is simply to be honest about our need, receive His grace, and respond with our whole lives.

Discussion Questions

  1. When have you felt overwhelmed by God's greatness or holiness? How did that experience change you, and what did it reveal about yourself that you hadn't seen before?
  2. Isaiah immediately confessed his specific sin—"unclean lips." Why is it important to be specific rather than vague when we confess our failures to God? What does honest confession require of us?
  3. The burning coal brought both pain and healing simultaneously. Can you think of times when God's grace felt uncomfortable or painful before it brought restoration? Why does true cleansing sometimes hurt?
  4. Isaiah said "Here am I, send me!" without knowing where he would go or what it would cost. What makes it difficult to offer God this kind of unconditional availability? What fears hold you back from saying "send me"?
  5. Isaiah was called to speak truth that people didn't want to hear. When is it hardest for you to speak truth? How do you balance faithfulness to God's message with compassion for those who may reject it?

ግን ጐይታ፡ ነዳዲ ፈሓምካ—ዘጽርይ ጸጋኻ—ክትልእኸልና ንሕተት—ክትትንክየናን ንጹሃት ክትገብረናን። ዘይኮነ ስለ ዝግብኣና ግን ስለ ዝሓዘንካ። ሓጢኣትና ሕደገልና፡ በደልና ኣርሕቐልና፡ ንኣገልግሎትካ ድሉዋት ግበረና።

ጐይታ፡ ብዘመናት ገና ዝጽውዕ ሕቶኻ ንሰምዕ፦ "ንመን ክሰድድ?" ከም ኢሳይያስ "እኔኹ፡ ስደደኒ!" ክንብል ትብዓት ሃበና! ሓቂ ብፍቕሪ ክንዛረብ፡ ንዝተሰብሩ ከነገልግል፡ ቅድስናኻን ጸጋኻን ናብ ዝመራሕካና ክንእውጅ ስደደና። ህይወትና ንጻውዒትካ መልስና ይኹን። ብስም የሱስ፡ ኣሜን።
