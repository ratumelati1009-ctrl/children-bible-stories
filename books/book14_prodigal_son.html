እቲ ጠፋእ ወዲ

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 14

For all the children who know that God always welcomes us home with open arms.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ ኣምላኽ ኩሉ ግዜ ብኽፉት ኣእዳው ናብ ገዛ ከም ዝቕበለና ዝፈልጡ።

[PICTURE: Jesus sitting under a tree telling a story to a group of people, everyone listening carefully with interest. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

TIGRINYA:
