Elijah Fed by Ravens

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A brave prophet Elijah standing before the wicked King Ahab in a grand palace throne room. Elijah wears a simple rough garment and points his finger boldly. King Ahab sits on a golden throne looking angry and proud. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Long ago in the land of Israel, there lived a brave prophet named Elijah. God sent him to speak to the wicked King Ahab, who did not follow God. Elijah was not afraid because God was with him.
ቀደም ኣብ ምድሪ እስራኤል ኤልያስ ዝስሙ ጅግና ነብዪ ነበረ። ኣምላኽ ንኣምላኽ ዘይስዕብ ንዝነበረ ኽፉእ ንጉስ ኣሃብ ክዛረቦ ለኣኾ። ኣምላኽ ምስኡ ስለ ዝነበረ ኤልያስ ኣይፈርሐን።
(Qedem ab midri Israel Eliyas zisimu jigna nebiy nebere. Amlak ni'Amlak zeysis'ib nizinebere khfu'e nigus Ahab kizarebo le'akho. Amlak mis'u sile zinebere Eliyas ayferhen.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Elijah standing boldly before King Ahab, delivering God's message with one hand raised toward heaven. The king's face shows shock and anger. Courtiers and guards look stunned in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elijah told King Ahab, "As the Lord God of Israel lives, there will be no rain or dew in this land for years — until I say so!" This was God's message to the wicked king.
ኤልያስ ንንጉስ ኣሃብ "ኣምላኽ እስራኤል ህያው ከም ዝኾነ፡ ኣነ ክሳብ ዝብል ንዓመታት ኣብዛ ምድሪ ዝናም ወይ ኣውሊ ኣይክህሉን'ዩ!" በሎ። እዚ ናይ ኣምላኽ መልእኽቲ ነቲ ኽፉእ ንጉስ ነበረ።
(Eliyas ninigus Ahab "Amlak Israel hiyaw kem zikhone, ane kisab zibil ni'ametat abza midri zinam wey awli aykilun'yu!" belo. Izi nay Amlak mel'ikhti neti khfu'e nigus nebere.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: King Ahab rising from his throne in fury, his face red with anger, pointing at Elijah. Guards begin to move toward Elijah. The palace is ornate with golden pillars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
King Ahab was furious! He did not want to hear God's message. But Elijah spoke bravely because he trusted God completely. The king wanted to punish Elijah, but God had a plan to protect His prophet.
ንጉስ ኣሃብ ኣዝዩ ተቖጥዐ! ናይ ኣምላኽ መልእኽቲ ክሰምዕ ኣይደለየን። ግን ኤልያስ ንኣምላኽ ምሉእ ብምሉእ ስለ ዝኣመኖ ብትብዓት ተዛረበ። ንጉስ ንኤልያስ ክቐጽዖ ደለየ፡ ግን ኣምላኽ ንነብዪኡ ክሕልዎ መደብ ነይርዎ።
(Nigus Ahab azyu teqot'e! Nay Amlak mel'ikhti kisemi'e aydeleyyen. Gin Eliyas ni'Amlak milu'e bimilu'e sile zi'ameno bitib'at tezarebe. Nigus ni'Eliyas kiqets'o deleye, gin Amlak ninebiy'u kihilwo medeb neyrwo.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: God speaking to Elijah in a soft glowing light. Elijah kneels with his head bowed, listening carefully. The words seem to come from a warm golden cloud above him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God spoke to Elijah and said, "Leave this place and go east. Hide by the brook called Cherith, near the Jordan River. You will drink water from the brook, and I will take care of you there."
ኣምላኽ ንኤልያስ ተዛረቦ፡ "ካብዚ ቦታ ውጻእ ንምብራቕ ኺድ። ኣብ ጥቓ ዮርዳኖስ ኣብ ዝርከብ ኮሪት ዝበሃል ርባ ተሓባእ። ካብቲ ርባ ማይ ክትሰቲ ኢኻ፡ ኣብኡ ድማ ክሕልወካ'የ።"
(Amlak ni'Eliyas tezarebo, "Kabzi bota wits'a'e nimbiraq khid. Ab tiqa Yordanos ab zirikeb Korit zibehal riba tehaba'e. Kabti riba may kitiseti ikha, ab'u dima kihilweka'ye.")
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Elijah walking along a dusty path through dry hills, carrying only a simple staff. He heads eastward toward a green valley in the distance. The sun is setting behind him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elijah obeyed God right away! He left the palace and walked eastward toward the brook Cherith. He trusted that God would take care of him, even though he was going to a lonely, hidden place.
ኤልያስ ብኡ ንብኡ ንኣምላኽ ተኣዘዘ! ነቲ ቤተ-መንግስቲ ገዲፉ ንምብራቕ ናብ ርባ ኮሪት ከደ። ናብ በይናዊ ስዉር ቦታ ይኸይድ'ኳ እንተነበረ ኣምላኽ ከም ዝሕልዎ ኣሚኑ።
(Eliyas bi'u nibi'u ni'Amlak te'azeze! Neti bete-mengisti gedifu nimbiraq nab riba Korit kede. Nab beynawi swur bota yikheyd'kwa intenebere Amlak kem zihiliwo aminu.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Elijah arriving at a beautiful brook flowing between rocky hills with green bushes around it. Clear water flows gently over smooth stones. Elijah kneels to drink with a look of relief and gratitude. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elijah found the brook Cherith! Cool, clear water flowed gently between the rocks. He knelt down and drank the fresh water. God had provided exactly what he needed. What a good God!
ኤልያስ ንርባ ኮሪት ረኸቦ! ዝሑል ጹሩይ ማይ ኣብ መንጎ ኣእማን ብህድኣት ይፈስስ ነበረ። ተምበርኪኹ ነቲ ጥዑም ማይ ሰተየ። ኣምላኽ ዘድልዮ ብልክዕ ሃቦ። ክንደይ ጽቡቕ ኣምላኽ!
(Eliyas nirba Korit rekebo! Zihul tsuruy may ab mengo a'iman bihid'at yifesis nebere. Temberkiku neti ti'um may seteye. Amlak ziedliyo bilki'e habo. Kindey tsibuq Amlak!)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Early morning at the brook. The sky is pink and orange with sunrise. Two large black ravens fly down toward Elijah carrying bread and meat in their beaks. Elijah looks up in amazement with outstretched hands. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Every morning, something wonderful happened! Big black ravens flew down from the sky carrying bread and meat in their beaks! They brought the food right to Elijah. God was feeding His prophet through birds!
ኩሉ ንግሆ ዘደንቕ ነገር ይኸውን ነበረ! ዓበይቲ ጸለምቲ ኳዃታት ኣብ መንቁሮም ንጌራን ስጋን ሒዞም ካብ ሰማይ ይወርዱ ነበሩ! ነቲ መግቢ ቀጥታ ናብ ኤልያስ ይሕዝዎ ነበሩ። ኣምላኽ ንነብዪኡ ብኣዕዋፍ ይምግቦ ነበረ!
(Kulu nigho zidenq neger yikhewn nebere! Abeytii tselemti kwakhatat ab menqurom nigera'n siga'n hizom kab semay yiwerdu neberu! Neti megbi qetita nab Eliyas yihizwo neberu. Amlak ninebiy'u bi'a'iwaf yimigbo nebere!)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Evening time at the brook. The sky is purple and blue with stars appearing. Ravens again fly down bringing more bread and meat to Elijah, who sits on a rock waiting faithfully. A small fire glows nearby. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
And every evening, the ravens came again! They brought more bread and meat for Elijah's dinner. Morning and evening, God faithfully provided food. Elijah never went hungry because God kept His promise.
ኩሉ ምሸት ድማ ኳዃታት ይምለሱ ነበሩ! ንድራር ኤልያስ ተወሳኺ ንጌራን ስጋን ይሕዙ ነበሩ። ንግሆን ምሸትን ኣምላኽ ብእምነት መግቢ ይህብ ነበረ። ኣምላኽ ቃሉ ስለ ዝሓለወ ኤልያስ ፈጺሙ ኣይጠመየን።
(Kulu mishet dima kwakhatat yimilesu neberu! Nidrar Eliyas tewesakhii nigera'n siga'n yihizu neberu. Nigho'n mishet'n Amlak bi'imnet megbi yihib nebere. Amlak qalu sile zihalehe Eliyas fetsiimu aytemeyen.)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Elijah sitting peacefully by the brook eating bread, with a raven perched on a nearby rock watching him. The scene is peaceful with green trees and flowers around the brook. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elijah sat by the brook, eating the bread and meat the ravens brought. He drank cool water from the stream. Even though he was all alone, he felt God's love and care all around him.
ኤልያስ ኣብ ጥቓ ርባ ኮፍ ኢሉ ኳዃታት ዘምጽኡሉ ንጌራን ስጋን ይበልዕ ነበረ። ካብ ርባ ዝሑል ማይ ይሰቲ ነበረ። በይኑ'ኳ እንተነበረ ፍቕርን ሓልዮትን ኣምላኽ ኣብ ዙርያኡ ይስምዖ ነበረ።
(Eliyas ab tiqa riba kof ilu kwakhatat ziemts'ulu nigera'n siga'n yibeli'e nebere. Kab riba zihul may yiseti nebere. Beynu'kwa intenebere fiqri'n haliyot'n Amlak ab zuryau yismi'o nebere.)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: The land around the brook becoming dry and brown. The sun beats down harshly. Trees are wilting. But the brook still flows with water, keeping Elijah safe and hydrated in his hidden spot. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Days and weeks passed with no rain. The land became very dry and brown. Plants wilted and the ground cracked. But the brook Cherith still had water flowing, and the ravens still came every day. God was faithful!
ዝናም ዘይብሉ መዓልታትን ሰሙናትን ሓለፉ። ምድሪ ኣዝያ ደረቐትን ሓመድ ቀለም ኮነትን። ተኽልታት ሓረሩ ምድሪ ድማ ተሰንጠቐት። ግን ርባ ኮሪት ገና ማይ ይፈስሶ ነበረ፡ ኳዃታት ድማ ኩሉ መዓልቲ ይመጹ ነበሩ። ኣምላኽ ኣሚን ነበረ!
(Zinam zeyiblu me'altat'n semunat'n halefu. Midri azya dereqet'n hamed qelem konet'n. Tekhiltat hareru midri dima tesenteqet. Gin riba Korit gena may yifesiso nebere, kwakhatat dima kulu me'alti yimetsu neberu. Amlak amin nebere!)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: The brook Cherith getting smaller and smaller. The water is now just a tiny trickle between dry, cracked rocks. Elijah looks at the disappearing water with concern but still trusts God. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But after many days without rain, the brook began to dry up. The water got less and less each day. Soon there was only a tiny trickle left. Elijah watched and wondered what God would do next.
ግን ድሕሪ ብዙሕ መዓልታት ዝናም ዘይምህላው እቲ ርባ ክነቅጽ ጀመረ። ማይ ኩሉ መዓልቲ ይውሕድ ነበረ። ቀልጢፉ ንእሽቶ ፈሰስ ጥራይ ተረፈ። ኤልያስ ይርኢ ነበረ'ሞ ኣምላኽ ደሓር እንታይ ክገብር ምዃኑ ይሓስብ ነበረ።
(Gin dihri buzuh me'altat zinam zeymihlaw iti riba kineqts jemere. May kulu me'alti yiwihid nebere. Qeltifu ni'ishto feses tiray terefe. Eliyas yiri'i nebere'mo Amlak dehar intay kigeber mikwanu yihasib nebere.)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: The brook completely dried up — only dusty rocks and cracked mud remain where water once flowed. Elijah kneels in prayer with hands raised to heaven, trusting God for his next step. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Finally, the brook dried up completely. There was no more water to drink. But Elijah did not panic! He prayed to God and waited. He knew that the same God who sent the ravens would provide again.
ኣብ መወዳእታ እቲ ርባ ብምሉኡ ነቐጸ። ዝስተ ማይ ኣይነበረን። ግን ኤልያስ ኣይሸበረን! ናብ ኣምላኽ ጸለየ'ሞ ተጸበየ። ኳዃታት ዝለኣኸ ኣምላኽ ከም ብሓዲሽ ከም ዝቕርብ ይፈልጥ ነበረ።
(Ab meweda'ita iti riba bimilu'u neqetse. Zistee may ayneberen. Gin Eliyas aysheberren! Nab Amlak tseleye'mo tetsbeye. Kwakhatat zile'akhe Amlak kem bihadish kem ziqirib yifelit nebere.)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: God speaking to Elijah again in a warm golden light from above. Elijah listens attentively with his head bowed and hands open in obedience. The dried brook is visible behind him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then God spoke to Elijah again: "Get up and go to Zarephath, a town near Sidon. I have commanded a widow there to feed you." God already had a new plan ready!
ሽዑ ኣምላኽ ንኤልያስ ከም ብሓዲሽ ተዛረቦ፡ "ተንስእ ናብ ጥቓ ሲዶን ዝርከብ ዘረፋት ዝበሃል ከተማ ኺድ። ኣብኡ ንዝርከብ ሓንቲ መበለት ክትምግበካ ኣዚዘያ ኣለኹ።" ኣምላኽ ድሮ ሓዲሽ መደብ ኣዳልዩ ነበረ!
(Shi'u Amlak ni'Eliyas kem bihadish tezarebo, "Tense'e nab tiqa Sidon zirikeb Zerefat zibehal ketema khid. Ab'u nizirikeb hanti mebeleet kitimigbeka azizeya alekhu." Amlak diro hadish medeb adalyu nebere!)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Elijah walking on a long dusty road through a dry, barren landscape toward a distant town. He carries his staff and walks with determination and faith. The town of Zarephath can be seen on the horizon. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elijah obeyed God immediately and set out on the long journey to Zarephath. The land was dry all around — no rain had fallen anywhere. But Elijah kept walking with faith in his heart, trusting God's word.
ኤልያስ ብኡ ንብኡ ንኣምላኽ ተኣዚዙ ነቲ ነዊሕ ጉዕዞ ናብ ዘረፋት ተበገሰ። ምድሪ ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ ደረቐት — ኣብ ዝኾነ ቦታ ዝናም ኣይዘንበን። ግን ኤልያስ ብቓል ኣምላኽ ኣሚኑ ብእምነት ኣብ ልቡ ምስጓም ቀጸለ።
(Eliyas bi'u nibi'u ni'Amlak te'azizu neti newih gu'izo nab Zerefat tebegese. Midri ab kulu bota dereqet — ab zikhone bota zinam ayzineben. Gin Eliyas biqal Amlak aminu bi'imnet ab libu misgwam qetsele.)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Elijah arriving at the gate of Zarephath and seeing a thin, tired-looking widow gathering sticks on the ground. She wears simple worn clothes. Her young son peeks from behind her. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When Elijah arrived at the gate of Zarephath, he saw a widow gathering sticks. She looked thin and tired. God told Elijah that this was the woman who would help him. He called out to her kindly.
ኤልያስ ኣብ ደገ ዘረፋት ምስ በጽሐ ዕንጸይቲ ትእክብ ዝነበረት ሓንቲ መበለት ረኣየ። ቀጢና ዝደኸመት ትመስል ነበረት። ኣምላኽ ንኤልያስ እዛ ክትሕግዞ ዝኾነት ሰበይቲ ምዃና ነገሮ። ብልዙብ ጸዊዑ ተዛረባ።
(Eliyas ab dege Zerefat mis betsihe intseyti ti'ikib zineberet hanti mebeleet re'aye. Qetina zidekhmet timesil neberet. Amlak ni'Eliyas iza kithigzo zikonet sebeyti mikwana negero. Bilzub tsewi'u tezareba.)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: Elijah speaking gently to the widow at the town gate, asking her for water. She holds a bundle of sticks in her arms. Her face shows tiredness but kindness. A clay water jar sits nearby. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elijah said to her, "Please bring me a little water in a cup so I may have a drink." The widow turned to go get him water. Then Elijah called out again with another request.

The Moral of the Story

God always provides for those who trust Him. He uses unexpected ways to care for His children — whether through ravens, a widow's jar, or miracles we never imagined. When we obey and trust God, He never lets us go without.
