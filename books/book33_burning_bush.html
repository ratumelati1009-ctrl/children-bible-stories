Moses and the Burning Bush

Scene 1
[PICTURE: Moses as a shepherd standing in the vast desert of Midian, holding a wooden staff, with sheep grazing around him under a golden sunset sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses lived in the desert of Midian. He was a shepherd, taking care of sheep every day. His life was quiet and simple.
ሙሴ ኣብ በረኻ ሚድያን ይነብር ነበረ። ጓሳ ኮይኑ ኣባጊዕ ይጥበቕ ነበረ። ህይወቱ ህድእትን ቀላልን ነበረት።
Muse ab bereka Midyan yinebir nebere. Gwasa koynu abagi' yit'beq nebere. Hiywetu hid'etin qelalin neberet.
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Moses walking with his flock of sheep near Mount Horeb, rocky desert landscape with dry bushes scattered around. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One day, Moses led his sheep near Mount Horeb, the mountain of God. He walked slowly, watching over his flock.
ሓደ መዓልቲ ሙሴ ኣባጊዑ ናብ ከረን ሆሬብ፡ እቲ ከረን ኣምላኽ መርሖም። ብዕረፍቲ እናኸደ ነተን ኣባጊዕ ይሕልወን ነበረ።
Hade me'alti Muse abagi'u nab keren Horeb, iti keren Amlak mer'Hom. Bi'erefti enakhede neten abagi' yiH'liwen nebere.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Moses looking up with wide surprised eyes at a bush engulfed in bright orange and yellow flames, but the green leaves remain untouched. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Suddenly, Moses saw something amazing! A bush was on fire, burning with bright flames. But the bush was not burning up!
ብድንገት ሙሴ ዘገርም ነገር ረኣየ! ሓንቲ ቊጥቋጥ ብሓዊ ትነድድ ነበረት። ግና እታ ቊጥቋጥ ኣይተቓጸለትን!
Bidnget Muse zegerm neger re'aye! Hanti qut'qwat' biHawi tinedid neberet. Gina ita qut'qwat' aytequats'eletin!
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Moses stepping closer toward the burning bush, his hand shielding his eyes from the bright light, looking curious and amazed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses said to himself, "This is very strange! I must go closer and see why this bush does not burn up."
ሙሴ ንርእሱ "እዚ ብጣዕሚ ዘገርም እዩ! ቀሪበ ስለምንታይ እዛ ቊጥቋጥ ዘይትቓጸል ክርኢ ኣለኒ" በለ።
Muse nir'isu "izi bit'a'imi zegerm iyu! Qeribe slemintay iza qut'qwat' zeytiqats'el kir'i aleni" bele.
Scene 5
[PICTURE: The burning bush glowing with heavenly golden light, and a voice calling from within the flames, with Moses standing nearby listening intently. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When God saw Moses coming closer, He called out from the bush, "Moses! Moses!" And Moses answered, "Here I am!"
ኣምላኽ ሙሴ ክቐርብ ምስ ረኣየ፡ ካብታ ቊጥቋጥ "ሙሴ! ሙሴ!" ኢሉ ጸውዖ። ሙሴ ድማ "እኔኹ!" ኢሉ መለሰ።
Amlak Muse kiqerib mis re'aye, kabta qut'qwat' "Muse! Muse!" ilu ts'ew'o. Muse dima "inekhu!" ilu melese.
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Moses bending down to remove his sandals, the burning bush glowing brightly before him on holy ground. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said, "Do not come any closer! Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy ground."
ኣምላኽ "ኣይትቕረብ! ሳእንኻ ኣውጽእ፡ ከመይሲ እዚ ዘለኻዮ ቦታ ቅዱስ መሬት እዩ" በለ።
Amlak "Aytiqreb! Sa'inka awts'i', kemeysi izi zelekayo bota qidus meret iyu" bele.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Moses barefoot with his face covered by his hands, kneeling in awe before the burning bush, afraid to look at God. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses quickly took off his sandals. He covered his face because he was afraid to look at God. He knelt down in awe.
ሙሴ ቀልጢፉ ሳእኑ ኣውጸአ። ናብ ኣምላኽ ክጥምት ስለ ዝፈርሐ ገጹ ሸፈኖ። ብፍርሃት ተምበርከኸ።
Muse qelt'ifu sa'inu awts'e'a. Nab Amlak kit'mit sle ziferHe getsu shefeno. BifirHat temberkeche.
Scene 8
[PICTURE: A vision of the Israelites working as slaves in Egypt, carrying heavy bricks under the hot sun, looking tired and sad. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said, "I have seen how my people are suffering in Egypt. I have heard them crying because of their slave masters."
ኣምላኽ "ህዝበይ ኣብ ግብጺ ክሳቐዩ ርእየ ኣለኹ። ብሰንኪ ጨቆንቶም እናበኸዩ ሰሚዐዮም ኣለኹ" በለ።
Amlak "Hizbeya ab Gibtsi kisaqeyu ri'iye alekhu. Bisenki cheqontom enabekeyu semi'eyom alekhu" bele.
Scene 9
[PICTURE: God's radiant light shining from the bush illuminating Moses, who is listening carefully with a look of compassion on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"I care about their pain," God said. "I have come down to rescue them and bring them to a good and spacious land flowing with milk and honey."
"ብቓንዛኦም ይግደሰኒ" በለ ኣምላኽ። "ከድሕኖምን ናብ ጽቡቕን ገፊሕን ጸባን መዓርን ዝውሕዛ ምድሪ ከብጽሖምን ወሪደ ኣለኹ።"
"Biqanz'aom yigdeseni" bele Amlak. "KediHnomn nab ts'buqin gefiHin ts'eban me'arin ziwiHza midri kebitsiHomin weride alekhu."
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Moses looking startled and pointing at himself in disbelief, with the burning bush still glowing before him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then God said, "I am sending YOU, Moses, to Pharaoh. You will lead my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt!"
ድሕሪኡ ኣምላኽ "ንዓኻ ሙሴ ናብ ፈርኦን ይልእኸካ ኣለኹ። ንህዝበይ እስራኤላውያን ካብ ግብጺ ክትመርሖም ኢኻ!" በለ።
DihHri'u Amlak "ni'aka Muse nab Fer'on yil'ikheka alekhu. NiHizbeya Isra'elawyan kab Gibtsi kitmer'Hom ika!" bele.
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Moses with his hands spread open in a gesture of doubt, looking small and humble before the mighty burning bush. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses was afraid. He said, "Who am I to go to Pharaoh? Who am I to bring the Israelites out of Egypt? I am just a shepherd!"
ሙሴ ፈርሐ። "ኣነ መን እየ ናብ ፈርኦን ክኸይድ? ኣነ መን እየ ንእስራኤላውያን ካብ ግብጺ ከውጽኦም? ጓሳ ጥራይ እየ!" በለ።
Muse ferHe. "Ane men iye nab Fer'on kikeyd? Ane men iye ni'Isra'elawyan kab Gibtsi kewts'iom? Gwasa t'ray iye!" bele.
Scene 12
[PICTURE: A gentle warm light wrapping around Moses like a comforting embrace, representing God's promise to be with him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God answered, "I will be with you! That is all you need. When you bring the people out, you will worship Me on this very mountain."
ኣምላኽ "ኣነ ምሳኻ ክኸውን እየ! እዚ ጥራይ እዩ ዘድልየካ። ነቲ ህዝቢ ምስ ኣውጻእካዮ ኣብዚ ከረን እዚ ከተምልኸኒ ኢኻ" ኢሉ መለሰ።
Amlak "Ane misaka kikewn iye! Izi t'ray iyu zedliyeka. Neti hizbi mis awts'a'ikayo abzi keren izi ketemlikheni ika" ilu melese.
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Moses gesturing with his hands asking a question, looking uncertain, with the burning bush casting a warm glow on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses said, "But if I go to the Israelites and say God sent me, they will ask, 'What is His name?' What shall I tell them?"
ሙሴ "ግና ናብ እስራኤላውያን ከይደ ኣምላኽ ልኢኹኒ እንተ በልኩዎም 'ስሙ መን እዩ?' ክብሉ እዮም። እንታይ ክብሎም?" በለ።
Muse "Gina nab Isra'elawyan keyde Amlak li'ikhuni inte belkuwom 'simu men iyu?' kiblu iyom. Intay kiblom?" bele.
Scene 14
[PICTURE: The burning bush blazing gloriously as God reveals His holy name, with rays of golden light spreading outward in all directions. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said to Moses, "I AM WHO I AM. Tell them: 'I AM has sent me to you.' This is My name forever."
ኣምላኽ ንሙሴ "ኣነ እቲ ዘለኹ እየ። 'ኣነ እቲ ዘለኹ' ልኢኹኒ በሎም። እዚ ንዘለኣለም ስመይ እዩ" በሎ።
Amlak niMuse "Ane iti zelekhu iye. 'Ane iti zelekhu' li'ikhuni belom. Izi nizele'alem simeya iyu" belo.
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Moses shaking his head with worry, making excuses, while the patient glow of the burning bush continues steadily. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Moses still doubted. He said, "What if they don't believe me? What if they say God did not really appear to me?"
ግና ሙሴ ገና ተጠራጠረ። "እንተ ዘይኣመኑኒ ከ? ኣምላኽ ብሓቂ ኣይተራእዮን ኢሎም እንተ በሉ ኸ?" በለ።
Gina Muse gena tet'erat'ere. "Inte zey'amenuni ke? Amlak biHaqi ayter'ayon ilom inte belu khe?" bele.
Scene 16
[PICTURE: Moses throwing his wooden staff to the ground, and it transforming into a wriggling snake, Moses jumping back in surprise. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said, "Throw your staff on the ground." Moses threw it down, and it became a snake! Moses ran away from it in fear.
ኣምላኽ "በትርኻ ናብ ምድሪ ደርብዮ" በለ። ሙሴ ደርበዮ እሞ ተመን ኮነ! ሙሴ ብፍርሃት ካብኡ ሃደመ።
Amlak "Betrika nab midri derbyo" bele. Muse derbeyo imo temen kone! Muse bifirHat kab'u hademe.
Scene 17
[PICTURE: Moses bravely reaching down to grab the snake by its tail, and it turning back into his wooden staff in his hand. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said, "Reach out and grab it by the tail." Moses obeyed, and when he grabbed it, it became a staff again! This was a sign from God.
ኣምላኽ "ኢድካ ዘርግሕ ብጭራኡ ሓዞ" በለ። ሙሴ ተኣዘዘ፡ ምስ ሓዞ ድማ ከም ብሓድሽ በትሪ ኮነ! እዚ ካብ ኣምላኽ ምልክት ነበረ።
Amlak "Idka zergiH bichiRa'u Hazo" bele. Muse te'azeze, mis Hazo dima kem biHadish betri kone! Izi kab Amlak milkit nebere.
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Moses putting his hand inside his cloak, then pulling it out showing it white as snow with leprosy, looking shocked. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God gave Moses another sign. "Put your hand inside your cloak," God said. When Moses pulled it out, his hand was white as snow with disease!
ኣምላኽ ንሙሴ ካልእ ምልክት ሃቦ። "ኢድካ ኣብ ክዳንካ እቶ" በለ ኣምላኽ። ሙሴ ምስ ኣውጸኣ ኢዱ ከም በረድ ጻዕዳ ብሕማም ኮነት!
Amlak niMuse kal'i milkit habo. "Idka ab kidanka ito" bele Amlak. Muse mis awts'e'a idu kem bered ts'a'ida biHimam konet!
Scene 19
[PICTURE: Moses putting his hand back in his cloak and pulling it out again, now completely healed and normal, smiling with relief. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"Now put it back," God said. Moses put his hand back inside his cloak, and when he pulled it out, it was completely healed! God's power is amazing.
"ሕጂ መልሳ እቶ" በለ ኣምላኽ። ሙሴ ኢዱ ናብ ክዳኑ መለሳ፡ ምስ ኣውጸኣ ድማ ፈጺማ ሓወየት! ሓይሊ ኣምላኽ ዘደንቕ እዩ።
"Hiji melsa ito" bele Amlak. Muse idu nab kidanu melesa, mis awts'e'a dima fets'ima Haweyet! Hayli Amlak zedenq iyu.
Scene 20
[PICTURE: Moses with a worried expression, touching his mouth and shaking his head, trying to explain to God that he cannot speak well. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Moses made another excuse. "O Lord, I am not a good speaker. I am slow of speech and tongue. Please send someone else!"
ግና ሙሴ ካልእ ምኽንያት ኣቕረበ። "ጐይታይ ጽቡቕ ተዛራቢ ኣይኮንኩን። ኣብ ዘረባን ልሳንን ደንጓዪ እየ። በጃኻ ካልእ ስደድ!"
Gina Muse kal'i mikinyat aqrebe. "Goytay ts'buq tezarabi aykonkun. Ab zereban lisanin dengwayi iye. Bejaka kal'i sided!"
Scene 21
[PICTURE: God's light from the burning bush intensifying with loving patience, as Moses listens with his head bowed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said, "Who gave people their mouths? Who makes them able to speak or hear? Is it not I, the Lord? Now go! I will help you speak and teach you what to say."
ኣምላኽ "ንሰብ ኣፉ ዝሃቦ መን እዩ? ክዛረቡ ወይ ክሰምዑ ዝገብሮም መን እዩ? ኣነ እግዚኣብሄር ኣይኮንኩን ድዩ? ሕጂ ኺድ! ክትዛረብ ክሕግዘካ ክትብሎ ዘለካ ድማ ክምህረካ እየ" በለ።
Amlak "Niseb afu ziHabo men iyu? Kizarebu wey kisem'u zigebrom men iyu? Ane Igzi'abHer aykonkun diyu? Hiji khid! Kitzareb kiHigzeka kitblo zeleka dima kimihireka iye" bele.
Scene 22
[PICTURE: Moses kneeling with clasped hands, finally surrendering to God's will, with a look of humble acceptance on his face before the glowing bush. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God also said, "Your brother Aaron speaks well. He will go with you and be your helper. I will tell you both what to do."
ኣምላኽ ውን "ሓውኻ ኣሮን ጽቡቕ ይዛረብ እዩ። ምሳኻ ክኸይድ እዩ ሓጋዚኻ ድማ ክኸውን እዩ። ንኽልቴኹም እንታይ ክትገብሩ ከም ዘለኩም ክነግረኩም እየ" በለ።
Amlak wun "Hawka Aron ts'buq yizareb iyu. Misaka kikeyd iyu Hagazika dima kikewn iyu. Nikhiltekhum intay kitgebru kem zelekum kinegrekum iye" bele.
Scene 23
[PICTURE: Moses standing tall with renewed courage, gripping his staff firmly, the burning bush fading behind him as he turns to begin his journey. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses finally accepted God's mission. He trusted that God would be with him. Even though he was afraid, he chose to obey.
ሙሴ ኣብ መወዳእታ ተልእኾ ኣምላኽ ተቐበለ። ኣምላኽ ምስኡ ከም ዝኸውን ኣመነ። ዋላ እኳ እንተ ፈርሐ ክእዘዝ መረጸ።
Muse ab mewed'aeta tel'ikho Amlak teqebele. Amlak mis'u kem zikhewn amene. Wala ikwa inte ferHe ki'izez merets'e.
Scene 24
[PICTURE: Moses and his brother Aaron embracing joyfully in the desert, both smiling, ready to go to Egypt together on God's mission. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses went back and met his brother Aaron. Together they would go to Egypt to tell Pharaoh: "Let God's people go!" God's plan was beginning.
ሙሴ ተመሊሱ ንሓው ኣሮን ረኸቦ። ብሓደ ናብ ግብጺ ከይዶም ንፈርኦን "ንህዝቢ ኣምላኽ ሕደጎም!" ክብሉዎ ነበሮም። ውጥን ኣምላኽ ይጅምር ነበረ።
Muse temelisu niHaw Aron rekhebo. BiHade nab Gibtsi keydom niFer'on "niHizbi Amlak Hidegom!" kibluw'o neberom. Wit'in Amlak yijmir nebere.
Scene 25
[PICTURE: Moses and Aaron walking together toward the horizon with Egypt's pyramids in the distance, staff in hand, guided by a warm divine light from above. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Moses learned that God uses ordinary people to do extraordinary things. When God calls you, He gives you everything you need. Trust Him and obey!

Moral of the Story / ትምህርቲ ታሪኽ

God calls ordinary people to do extraordinary things. He equips those He calls. Don't let fear stop you from obeying God. When God asks you to do something, He will always be with you and give you what you need to succeed.

ኣምላኽ ንተራ ሰባት ንዘይተራ ነገራት ክገብሩ ይጽውዖም። ንዝጸውዖም ይዕጥቖም። ፍርሃት ንኣምላኽ ካብ ምእዛዝ ኣይኹልኸኩም። ኣምላኽ ገለ ነገር ክትገብሩ ምስ ዝሓተኩም ኩሉ ግዜ ምሳኻትኩም ክኸውን እዩ ክትዕወቱ ዘድልየኩም ድማ ክህበኩም እዩ።

  2. Why was the burning bush special?
  3. What excuses did Moses make to God?
  4. What signs did God give Moses to show His power?
  5. How does God help us when He asks us to do something hard?

  4. ኣምላኽ ሓይሉ ከርኢ ንሙሴ እንታይ ምልክታት ሃቦ?
  5. ኣምላኽ ከቢድ ነገር ክንገብር ምስ ዝሓተና ከመይ ይሕግዘና?

Prayer / ጸሎት

Dear God, thank You for calling us to do great things for You. Help us to be brave like Moses and not make excuses when You ask us to help others. We know You are always with us and will give us what we need. Help us to trust You and obey, even when we feel afraid. In Jesus' name, Amen.

ዝኸበርካ ኣምላኽ፡ ንዓኻ ዓበይቲ ነገራት ክንገብር ስለ ዝጸዋዕካና ነመስግነካ። ከም ሙሴ ጀጋኑ ክንከውንን ንኻልኦት ክንሕግዝ ምስ ዝሓተካና ምኽንያት ከይንገብር ሓግዘና። ኩሉ ግዜ ምሳና ከም ዘለኻ ዘድልየና ድማ ከም ዝህበና ንፈልጥ ኢና። ዋላ ምስ ዝፈራሕና ክንኣምነካን ክንእዘዘካን ሓግዘና። ብስም ኢየሱስ ኣሜን።
