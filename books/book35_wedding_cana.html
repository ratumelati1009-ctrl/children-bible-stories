The Wedding at Cana

መርዓ ኣብ ቃና

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A beautiful village in Cana of Galilee with a wedding celebration being prepared. Colorful decorations hang between stone buildings. Villagers carry food and flowers. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In a little town called Cana, in the land of Galilee, a wonderful wedding was about to begin! The whole village was excited and getting ready for the big celebration.
ኣብ ቃና ዝትበሃል ንእሽቶ ከተማ ኣብ ምድሪ ገሊላ ጽቡቕ መርዓ ክጅምር ኢሉ ነበረ! ብምሉእ ዓዲ ተሓጒሶም ንዓቢ በዓል ይዳለዉ ነበሩ።
(Ab Qana zitbehal ni'ishto ketema ab midri Gelila tsibuq mer'a kijimer ilu nebere! Bimlu'e adi tehagwisom ni'abi be'al yidale'u neberu.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Mary, the mother of Jesus, arriving at the wedding wearing a beautiful blue robe. She is greeted warmly by the bride's family at the entrance. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mary, the mother of Jesus, was invited to the wedding. She was a dear friend of the family. She arrived early to help with the preparations and share in the joy.
ማርያም ኣደ የሱስ ናብቲ መርዓ ተዓዲማ ነበረት። ንሳ ናይታ ስድራ ቤት ቅርብቲ ዓርኪ ነበረት። ኣብ ምድላው ንምሕጋዝን ንሓጎስ ንምክፋልን ቀልጢፋ መጸት።
(Maryam ade Yesus nabtimeera te'adima neberet. Nisa nayta sidra bet qiribti arki neberet. Ab midlaw nimihigaz'n nihagos nimikfal'n qeltifa metset.)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Jesus arriving at the wedding with his disciples walking behind him on a dusty path. Jesus smiles warmly. The disciples look happy and excited. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus was also invited to the wedding, and He brought His disciples along too! They were happy to celebrate with the family on this special day.
የሱስ'ውን ናብቲ መርዓ ተዓዲሙ ነበረ፡ ደቀ መዛሙርቱ'ውን ሒዙ መጸ! ኣብዚ ፍሉይ መዓልቲ ምስታ ስድራ ቤት ምብዓል ሕጉሳት ነበሩ።
(Yesus'wun nabtimeera te'adimu nebere, deqe mezamurtu'wun hizu metse! Abzi filuy me'alti mista sidra bet mib'al highusat neberu.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: A joyful wedding feast with guests eating, laughing, and clapping. Musicians play drums and stringed instruments. Tables are filled with food and decorations. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The wedding celebration was full of joy! There was music playing, people dancing, delicious food on every table, and laughter everywhere. Everyone was having a wonderful time!
እቲ መርዓ ብሓጎስ ዝመልአ ነበረ! ሙዚቃ ይጻወት፡ ሰባት ይስዕስዑ፡ ጥዑም መግቢ ኣብ ኩሉ ጣውላ፡ ስሓቕ ድማ ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ ነበረ። ኩሎም ጽቡቕ ግዜ የሕልፉ ነበሩ!
(Iti mer'a bihagos zimel'a nebere! Muziqa yitsawet, sebat yis'is'u, ti'um megbi ab kulu tawla, sihaq dima ab kulu bota nebere. Kulom tsibuq gize yehlifu neberu!)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: The bride and groom sitting together on decorated chairs, smiling at each other. Guests surround them with gifts and flowers. Colorful fabrics drape the scene. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The bride and groom were so happy! Their families had worked hard to make this the best celebration. In those days, weddings lasted for many days, and everyone in the village came to enjoy the feast.
መርዓትን መርዓውን ኣዝዮም ሕጉሳት ነበሩ! ስድራ ቤቶም ነዚ ዝበለጸ በዓል ክገብርዎ ብዙሕ ጽዒሮም ነበሩ። ኣብቲ ዘመን መርዓ ንብዙሕ መዓልታት ይጸንሕ ነበረ፡ ኩሎም ኣብ ዓዲ ዝነበሩ ድማ ነቲ በዓል ክሕጎሱ ይመጹ ነበሩ።
(Mer'at'n mer'aw'n azyom highusat neberu! Sidra betom nezi zibelets'e be'al kigebrwo buzuh tsi'irom neberu. Abti zemen mer'a nibuzuh me'altat yitsenihi nebere, kulom ab adi zineberu dima neti be'al kihigosu yimetsu neberu.)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: A worried servant looking into empty wine jars, tilting them to check. His face shows panic. Other servants whisper to each other in concern in the background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But then something terrible happened — the wine ran out! The servants looked in every jar, but they were all empty. This was very, very embarrassing for the family!
ግን ሽዑ ሕማቕ ነገር ኮነ — ወይኒ ተወድአ! ኣገልገልቲ ኣብ ኩሉ ዕትሮ ረኣዩ፡ ግን ኩሉ ጥራሕ ነበረ። እዚ ንስድራ ቤት ኣዝዩ ኣዝዩ ዘሕፍር ነበረ!
(Gin shi'u himaqi neger kone — weyni tewede'a! Agelgelti ab kulu itro re'ayu, gin kulu tirah nebere. Izi nisidra bet azyu azyu zehifir nebere!)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Mary leaning toward Jesus and speaking quietly to him with a concerned but trusting expression. They are at a table slightly apart from the main celebration. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mary noticed the problem and went to Jesus. She said to Him quietly, "They have no more wine." She knew Jesus could help, even though He had not done a miracle yet.
ማርያም ነቲ ጸገም ኣስተብሃለት'ሞ ናብ የሱስ ከደት። ብህድኣት "ወይኒ ተወዲኡዎም" በለቶ። የሱስ ክሕግዝ ከም ዝኽእል ትፈልጥ ነበረት፡ ገና ተኣምር ዘይገበረ'ኳ እንተኾነ።
(Maryam neti tsegem astebhalet'mo nab Yesus kedet. Bihid'at "weyni tewedi'uwom" beleto. Yesus kihigiz kem zikhil tifelit neberet, gena te'amir zeygibere'kwa intekone.)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Jesus looking at Mary with a gentle but serious expression, his hand raised slightly as he speaks. Mary listens calmly with faith in her eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus answered His mother, "Dear woman, why do you involve me? My hour has not yet come." But Mary was not worried. She had faith that Jesus would do the right thing.
የሱስ ንኣዲኡ "ኣቲ ሰበይቲ ንዓይ እንታይ ኣገደሰኒ? ግዜይ ገና ኣይበጽሐን" ኢሉ መለሰላ። ግን ማርያም ኣይተሻቐለትን። የሱስ ቅኑዕ ነገር ክገብር ከም ዝኾነ እምነት ነይርዋ።
(Yesus ni'adiyu "ati sebeyti ni'ay intay agediseni? Gizeey gena aybetstsen" ilu melesela. Gin Maryam aytesha-qelettin. Yesus qinu'e neger kigeber kem zikhone imnet neyrwa.)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: Mary turning to the servants and pointing toward Jesus with confidence. The servants look at her attentively, ready to listen. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Mary turned to the servants and said something very important: "Do whatever He tells you." She trusted Jesus completely and wanted the servants to obey Him too.
ማርያም ናብቶም ኣገልገልቲ ግልጽ ኢላ ኣዝዩ ኣገዳሲ ነገር ተዛረበት፡ "ዝኾነ ዝብለኩም ግበሩ።" ንየሱስ ምሉእ ብምሉእ ትኣምኖ ነበረት፡ ኣገልገልቲ'ውን ክእዘዝዎ ትደሊ ነበረት።
(Maryam nabtom agelgelti gilts ila azyu agedasi neger tezarebet: "Zikhone ziblekum giberu." Niyesus milu'e bimilu'e ti'amino neberet, agelgelti'wun ki'izeziwo tideli neberet.)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: Six large stone water jars standing in a row near the entrance of the wedding house. They are tall, made of grey stone, and used for Jewish ceremonial washing. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Nearby stood six very large stone water jars. They were used for Jewish ceremonial washing. Each jar could hold twenty to thirty gallons of water — that's a lot!
ኣብ ጥቓ ሽዱሽተ ኣዝየን ዓበይቲ ናይ እምኒ ዕትሮታት ማይ ነበራ። ንኣይሁዳዊ ስርዓት ምሕጻብ ዝግልገላ ነበራ። ነፍሲ ወከፍ ዕትሮ ካብ ዕስራ ክሳብ ሰላሳ ጋሎን ማይ ትሕዝ ነበረት — እዚ ብዙሕ'ዩ!
(Ab tiqa shidushte azyen abeytii nay imni itrotat may nebera. Ni'Ayihudawi sir'at mihitsab zigilgela nebera. Nefsi wekef itro kab isra kisab selasa galon may tihiz neberet — izi buzuh'yu!)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Jesus standing near the six stone jars, gesturing toward them as he speaks to the servants. His expression is calm and authoritative. The servants lean in to listen carefully. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus looked at the six large stone jars and said to the servants, "Fill the jars with water." It seemed like a strange thing to ask when they needed wine, but Jesus had a plan!
የሱስ ነተን ሽዱሽተ ዓበይቲ ናይ እምኒ ዕትሮታት ረኣየን'ሞ ነቶም ኣገልገልቲ "ነዘን ዕትሮታት ብማይ ምልእወን" በሎም። ወይኒ ኣብ ዘድልየሉ ግዜ ዘገርም ሕቶ ይመስል ነበረ፡ ግን የሱስ መደብ ነይርዎ!
(Yesus neten shidushte abeytii nay imni itrotat re'ayen'mo netom agelgelti "nezen itrotat bimay mil'ewon" belom. Weyni ab ziedelyelu gize zigerim hito yimesil nebere, gin Yesus medeb neyrwo!)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Servants carrying heavy clay pitchers of water and pouring water into the large stone jars. Water splashes as they fill each jar to the very top. They work hard and fast. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The servants obeyed right away! They carried water from the well and poured it into each jar, filling them all the way to the very top — to the brim! They did exactly what Jesus told them.
ኣገልገልቲ ብኡ ንብኡ ተኣዘዙ! ካብ ዔላ ማይ ጹይቖም ኣብ ነፍሲ ወከፍ ዕትሮ ኣፍሰስዎ፡ ክሳብ ኣፍ ንኣፍ ሕንቅብ ኢሉ ክሳብ ዝመልእ መልእወን! የሱስ ዝበሎም ብልክዕ ገበሩ።
(Agelgelti bi'u nibi'u te'azezu! Kab ela may tsuyqom ab nefsi wekef itro afseswo, kisab af ni'af hinqib ilu kisab zimel'e mel'ewon! Yesus zibelom bilki'e geberu.)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Jesus calmly speaking to the servants while pointing toward the banquet hall. The servants hold a small pitcher, ready to draw from the jars. A soft golden glow surrounds the stone jars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Jesus said, "Now draw some out and take it to the master of the banquet." The servants looked at each other in surprise, but they remembered Mary's words — do whatever He says!
ድሕሪኡ የሱስ "ሕጂ ቑሩብ ቀዲሕኩም ናብ ሓላቓ ድግስ ውሰዱሉ" በለ። ኣገልገልቲ ብምግራም ተጠማምቱ፡ ግን ናይ ማርያም ቃላት ዘከሩ — ዝኾነ ዝብለኩም ግበሩ!
(Dihri'u Yesus "hiji qurub qedihkum nab halaqa digs wisedulu" bele. Agelgelti bimigram tetemamtu, gin nay Maryam qalat zekeru — zikhone ziblekum giberu!)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: A servant drawing liquid from one of the stone jars with a ladle. The water has turned into deep red wine. The servant's eyes are wide with amazement as he sees the color. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When the servants drew the water out, something amazing had happened! The water had become wine — beautiful, deep red wine! The servants could hardly believe their eyes!
ኣገልገልቲ ነቲ ማይ ምስ ቀደሑ ዘደንቕ ነገር ኮይኑ ነበረ! እቲ ማይ ወይኒ ኮይኑ — ጽቡቕ ጸሊም ቀይሕ ወይኒ! ኣገልገልቲ ነዓይኖም ክኣምኑ ኣይከኣሉን!
(Agelgelti neti may mis qedehu zidenq neger koynu nebere! Iti may weyni koynu — tsibuq tselim qeyih weyni! Agelgelti ne'aynom ki'amnu ayke'alun!)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: A servant carefully carrying a golden cup filled with wine to the master of the banquet, who sits at the head table. The servant walks with careful steps, still amazed. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The servants carefully carried the wine to the master of the banquet. He was the person in charge of all the food and drinks. He did not know where this wine had come from.
ኣገልገልቲ ነቲ ወይኒ ብጥንቃቐ ናብ ሓላቓ ድግስ ወሰድዎ። ንሱ ናይ ኩሉ መግብን መስተን ሓላፊ ነበረ። እዚ ወይኒ ካበይ ከም ዝመጸ ኣይፈለጠን።
(Agelgelti neti weyni bitinqaqe nab halaqa digs wesedwo. Nisu nay kulu megbi'n meste'n halafi nebere. Izi weyni kabey kem zimetse ayfeleten.)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: The master of the banquet tasting the wine from a cup, his eyebrows raised high in delighted surprise. His face shows pure amazement at how wonderful it tastes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The master of the banquet tasted the wine. His eyes grew wide! It was the most delicious wine he had ever tasted — the very best! He was absolutely amazed!
ሓላቓ ድግስ ነቲ ወይኒ ቀመሶ። ዓይኑ ገፍሐ! ካብ ዝቐመሶ ኩሉ ወይኒ ዝበለጸ ጥዑም ነበረ — ኣዝዩ ዝበለጸ! ብምሉእ ተገረመ!
(Halaqa digs neti weyni qemeso. Aynu gefhe! Kab ziqemeso kulu weyni zibelets'e ti'um nebere — azyu zibelets'e! Bimlu'e tegereme!)
Scene 17
[PICTURE: The master of the banquet calling the bridegroom over excitedly, gesturing with one hand while holding the wine cup in the other. The bridegroom looks confused but pleased. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The master called the bridegroom over and said, "Everyone brings out the best wine first, but you have saved the best until now! This is the finest wine of all!"
ሓላቓ ድግስ ንመርዓዊ ጸዊዑ "ኩሉ ሰብ ቅድም ዝበለጸ ወይኒ የቕርብ፡ ንስኻ ግን ንዝበለጸ ክሳብ ሕጂ ሓዚእካዮ! እዚ ካብ ኩሉ ዝበለጸ ወይኒ'ዩ!" በሎ።
(Halaqa digs nimer'awi tsewi'u "kulu seb qidim zibelets'e weyni yeqrib, niskha gin nizbelets'e kisab hiji hazi'ekayo! Izi kab kulu zibelets'e weyni'yu!" belo.)
Scene 18
[PICTURE: The bridegroom looking surprised and scratching his head, not knowing where the wine came from. In the background, the servants smile knowingly at each other. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The bridegroom was confused — he didn't know where this amazing wine came from! But the servants knew. They had seen the miracle with their own eyes. Jesus had turned plain water into the best wine!
መርዓዊ ተደናገረ — እዚ ዘደንቕ ወይኒ ካበይ ከም ዝመጸ ኣይፈለጠን! ግን ኣገልገልቲ ይፈልጡ ነበሩ። ነቲ ተኣምር ብዓይኖም ርእዮምዎ። የሱስ ተራ ማይ ናብ ዝበለጸ ወይኒ ቀዪርዎ!
(Mer'awi tedenagere — izi zidenq weyni kabey kem zimetse ayfeleten! Gin agelgelti yifeltu neberu. Neti te'amir bi'aynom ri'yomwo. Yesus tera may nab zibelets'e weyni qeyirwo!)
Scene 19
[PICTURE: Guests at the wedding joyfully drinking the new wine from cups, laughing and celebrating. The celebration continues with even more energy and happiness than before. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The celebration continued with great joy! There was plenty of wine for everyone now. The family was saved from embarrassment, and the wedding feast was even more wonderful than before!

Jesus cares about our everyday needs and celebrations. When we obey Him, He does amazing things. Trust and obey, even when you don't understand.
