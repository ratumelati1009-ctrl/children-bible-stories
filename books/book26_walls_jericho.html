Scene 1
[PICTURE: Joshua standing on a hill overlooking the promised land with the Israelites behind him, green valleys and hills stretching ahead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After Moses died, God chose Joshua to lead the Israelites into the promised land. Joshua was brave and strong.
ድሕሪ ሞት ሙሴ፡ ኣምላኽ ንኢያሱ መሪሕ እስራኤላውያን ናብ ምድሪ ተስፋ ክኸውን መረጾ። ኢያሱ ጅግና ን ሓያል ነበረ።
Dihri mot Muse, Amlak n'Iyasu meriH IsraElawiyan nab midri tesfa ki'khewn mereTso. Iyasu jigna n Hayal nebere.
Scene 2
[PICTURE: The Israelites crossing the Jordan River on dry ground, with water piled up on both sides. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God told Joshua, "Be strong and courageous! I will be with you wherever you go." The Israelites crossed the Jordan River on dry ground.
ኣምላኽ ንኢያሱ "ጽናዕ ን ተባዕ! ናብ ዝኸድካዮ ኩሉ ምሳኻ ክኸውን እየ" በሎ። እስራኤላውያን ንዮርዳኖስ ብንቑጽ መሬት ተሳገሩ።
Amlak n'Iyasu "TsinaE n tebaE! Nab ziKhedkayo kulu misaKha ki'khewn iye" belo. IsraElawiyan n'Yordanos bi'nQuTs meret tesageru.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: The great walled city of Jericho with tall thick walls and locked gates, viewed from outside. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When the Israelites reached Jericho, they saw a great city with very tall, thick walls. The gates were shut tight. No one could go in or out.
እስራኤላውያን ናብ ኢያሪኮ ምስ በጽሑ፡ ዓባይ ከተማ ምስ ነዋሕን ረጒድን ቅጽርታት ረኣዩ። ደጌታት ጽኑዕ ተዓጽዩ ነበሩ። ዝኣቱ ወይ ዝወጽእ ኣይነበረን።
IsraElawiyan nab Iyariko mis beTsHu, Abay ketema mis newaHn reggudn QiTsrtat re'ayu. Degeytat Tsnu'E te'aTsiyu neberu. Zi'atu wey ziweTsE ayneberenun.
Scene 4
[PICTURE: Joshua kneeling in prayer, looking up to heaven with a glowing light above him, camp of tents in background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Joshua prayed to God, asking how they could conquer the city. God had a special plan — it was unlike any battle plan anyone had ever heard!
ኢያሱ ነታ ከተማ ብኸመይ ክሰዓርዋ ከም ዝኽእሉ ናብ ኣምላኽ ጸለየ። ኣምላኽ ፍሉይ ውጥን ነበሮ — ካብ ዝኾነ ዝተሰምዐ ውጥን ውግእ ዝተፈለየ ነበረ!
Iyasu neta ketema bi'Khemey ki'seErwa kem ziKhElu nab Amlak Tseleye. Amlak filuy wiTin nebero — kab ziKhone zitesemEe wiTin wigE zitefelaye nebere!
Scene 5
[PICTURE: God speaking to Joshua with rays of light, Joshua listening attentively with hands clasped. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
God said to Joshua, "March around the city once each day for six days. Seven priests shall carry trumpets before the ark."
ኣምላኽ ንኢያሱ "ንሽዱሽተ መዓልቲ ኣብ ነፍስወከፍ መዓልቲ ነታ ከተማ ሓንሳብ ዙሩ። ሸውዓተ ካህናት ቅድሚ ታቦት መለኸት ይሓዙ" በሎ።
Amlak n'Iyasu "ni'shidushte meAlti ab nefsiwekef meAlti neta ketema Hansab zuru. ShewAte kahnat Qidmi tabot melekhet yiHazu" belo.
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Joshua addressing the Israelite people, pointing toward Jericho's walls, people listening with determination. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Joshua gathered the people and told them God's plan. "Do not shout or make any noise until I tell you. Then shout with all your might!" he said.
ኢያሱ ንህዝቢ ኣኪቡ ውጥን ኣምላኽ ነገሮም። "ክሳብ ዝብለኩም ኣይትጸውዑ ወይ ድምጺ ኣይትግበሩ። ሽዑ ብኹሉ ሓይልኹም ጨድሩ!" በሎም።
Iyasu n'hizbi akibu wiTin Amlak negerom. "Kisab ziblekum aytiTsewEu wey dimTsi aytigberu. ShiEu bi'Khulu Haylkhum chedru!" belom.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: A long procession of Israelite soldiers marching in line toward Jericho's walls on Day 1, with armed men in front. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
On the first day, the armed men marched in front. Behind them came seven priests blowing trumpets of rams' horns. Then came the Ark of the Covenant.
ኣብ ቀዳማይ መዓልቲ፡ ዕጡቓት ሰባት ኣብ ቅድሚት ሰለፉ። ድሕሪኦም ሸውዓተ ካህናት መለኸት ቀርኒ ደዓል እናነፍሑ መጹ። ብድሕሪኦም ታቦት ኪዳን መጸ።
Ab Qedamay meAlti, ETuQat sebat ab Qidmit selefu. DiHriom shewAte kahnat melekhet Qerni deAl ina'nefHu meTsu. BidiHriom tabot kidan meTse.
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Seven priests blowing shofar trumpets while walking around the walls of Jericho, the Ark carried behind them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The priests blew their trumpets loudly as they marched. The sound of the rams' horns echoed against the tall walls of Jericho. But the people stayed silent.
ካህናት እናኸዱ መለኸቶም ብድምቀት ነፍሑ። ድምጺ ቀርኒ ደዓል ኣብ ነዋሕ ቅጽርታት ኢያሪኮ ተደጋገመ። ግን ህዝቢ ስቕ በሉ።
Kahnat ina'Khedu melekhetom bi'dimQet nefHu. DimTsi Qerni deAl ab newaH QiTsrtat Iyariko tedegageme. Gin hizbi siQ belu.
Scene 9
[PICTURE: The Israelites completing their march around Jericho on the first day and returning to camp as the sun sets. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
After marching around the city once, they returned to camp. The people of Jericho watched from the walls, confused and afraid. What were these people doing?
ነታ ከተማ ሓንሳብ ዞሮም ድሕሪ ምውዳእ ናብ ሰፈሮም ተመልሱ። ህዝቢ ኢያሪኮ ካብ ቅጽርታት እናረኣዩ ተደናገሩን ፈርሑን። እዞም ሰባት እንታይ ይገብሩ ኣለዉ?
Neta ketema Hansab zorom diHri miwdaE nab seferom temelsu. Hizbi Iyariko kab QiTsrtat ina're'ayu tedenagerun ferHun. Ezom sebat intay yigberu alewu?
Scene 10
[PICTURE: The Israelites marching around Jericho on the second day, showing discipline and order in their ranks. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
On the second day, they marched around the city again — once — then returned to camp. They did the same on the third day, and the fourth day.
ኣብ ካልኣይ መዓልቲ ከም ብሓድሽ ነታ ከተማ — ሓንሳብ — ዞሩ ናብ ሰፈሮም ተመልሱ። ኣብ ሳልሳይን ራብዓይን መዓልቲ'ውን ከምኡ ገበሩ።
Ab kal'ay meAlti kem biHadish neta ketema — Hansab — zoru nab seferom temelsu. Ab salisayn rabiEayn meAlti'wun kemU geberu.
Scene 11
[PICTURE: The Israelites marching on the fifth day, children watching from the camp, people of Jericho looking nervous on the walls. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
On the fifth and sixth days, they marched around the city once each day. The people of Jericho grew more and more nervous. The Israelites obeyed God patiently.
ኣብ ሓሙሻይን ሻድሻይን መዓልቲ ኣብ ነፍስወከፍ መዓልቲ ሓንሳብ ነታ ከተማ ዞሩ። ህዝቢ ኢያሪኮ ኣዝዩ ይፈርሕ ነበረ። እስራኤላውያን ብትዕግስቲ ንኣምላኽ ተኣዘዙ።
Ab Hamushay shadishay meAlti ab nefsiwekef meAlti Hansab neta ketema zoru. Hizbi Iyariko aziyu yiferH nebere. IsraElawiyan bi'tiEgisti n'Amlak te'azezu.
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Early dawn on the seventh day, Joshua waking the people with excitement, stars still fading in the sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
On the seventh day, Joshua woke everyone very early, before the sun rose. "Today is the day!" he told them. "We will march around the city seven times!"
ኣብ ሻብዓይ መዓልቲ ኢያሱ ጸሓይ ቅድሚ ምውጻኣ ንኹሉ ኣተንሰአ። "ሎሚ እታ መዓልቲ እያ!" በሎም። "ነታ ከተማ ሸውዓተ ግዜ ክንዞራ ኢና!"
Ab shabiEay meAlti Iyasu TseHay Qidmi miwTsa'a n'Khulu atense'a. "Lomi ita meAlti iya!" belom. "Neta ketema shewAte gize ki'nzora ina!"
Scene 13
[PICTURE: The grand procession beginning on the seventh day, the full army, priests with trumpets, and the Ark moving together at sunrise. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The great procession began at sunrise. Armed men led the way, priests blew their trumpets, and the Ark of the Covenant was carried high. The people marched in faith.
እቲ ዓቢ ሰልፊ ጸሓይ ምስ ወጸት ጀመረ። ዕጡቓት ሰባት መርሑ፡ ካህናት መለኸቶም ነፍሑ፡ ታቦት ኪዳን ድማ ልዕል ኢሉ ተጸርሐ። ህዝቢ ብእምነት ሰለፉ።
Iti Abi selfi TseHay mis weTset jemere. ETuQat sebat merHu, kahnat melekhetom nefHu, tabot kidan dima liEl ilu teTserHe. Hizbi bi'imnet selefu.
Scene 14
[PICTURE: The Israelites completing their first round on the seventh day, counting on their fingers, continuing to walk around the city. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Around the city they went — one time, two times, three times. Still they marched in silence. The trumpets blasted, but no voice was heard from the people.
ነታ ከተማ ዞሩ — ሓንሳብ፡ ክልተ ሳብ፡ ሰለስተ ሳብ። ገና ብስቕታ ይኸዱ ነበሩ። መለኸት ይነፍሕ ነበረ፡ ግን ካብ ህዝቢ ድምጺ ኣይተሰምዐን።
Neta ketema zoru — Hansab, kilte sab, seleste sab. Gena bi'siQta yiKhedu neberu. Melekhet yinefH nebere, gin kab hizbi dimTsi aytesemEen.
Scene 15
[PICTURE: The Israelites on their fourth and fifth circuits, sweat on their brows, determination on their faces, walls towering above them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Four times, five times, six times they marched around. The sun grew hot, but no one complained. They trusted God's plan even though it seemed very strange.
ኣርባዕተ፡ ሓሙሽተ፡ ሽዱሽተ ግዜ ዞሩ። ጸሓይ ሃረረት ግን ዝማረረ ኣይነበረን። ውጥን ኣምላኽ ዘገርም ኮይኑ ክንሱ ተኣማመኑ።
ArbaEte, Hamushte, shidushte gize zoru. TseHay hareret gin zimarere ayneberenun. WiTin Amlak zegerm koynu kinsu te'amamenu.
Scene 16
[PICTURE: The Israelites starting their seventh circuit around the walls, anticipation building, Joshua raising his hand to signal readiness. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
As they began the seventh time around, Joshua told the people, "Get ready! When you hear the long trumpet blast, shout as loud as you can! God has given us the city!"
ሻብዓይ ግዜ ክዞሩ ምስ ጀመሩ ኢያሱ ንህዝቢ "ተዳለዉ! ነዊሕ ድምጺ መለኸት ምስ ሰማዕኩም፡ ብዝኽእልኹም ጨድሩ! ኣምላኽ ነዛ ከተማ ሂቡና!" በሎም።
ShabiEay gize ki'zoru mis jemeru Iyasu n'hizbi "tedaLewu! NewiH dimTsi melekhet mis semaEkum, bi'ziKhElkhum chedru! Amlak neza ketema hibuna!" belom.
Scene 17
[PICTURE: Priests blowing a long sustained trumpet blast, cheeks puffed, the sound waves visible in the air, dramatic moment. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The priests blew a long, powerful blast on their trumpets. The sound filled the air like thunder. Every man, woman, and child took a deep breath.
ካህናት ነዊሕን ሓያልን ድምጺ መለኸት ነፍሑ። ድምጺ ከም ነጎዳ ንኣየር መልኦ። ኩሉ ሰብኣይ፡ ሰበይትን ቆልዓን ዓሚቚ ትንፋስ ወሰዱ።
Kahnat newiHn Hayaln dimTsi melekhet nefHu. DimTsi kem negoda n'ayer melO. Kulu sebAy, sebeytIn QolEan AmiQu tinfas wesedu.
Scene 18
[PICTURE: All the Israelites shouting together with mouths wide open, hands raised, incredible energy and joy on their faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then all the people shouted with a great shout! The noise was so loud it shook the ground. Men, women, and children all cried out together with one mighty voice!
ሽዑ ኩሉ ህዝቢ ብዓቢ ድምጺ ጨደረ! ድምጺ ክንድኡ ሓያል ስለ ዝነበረ መሬት ኣንቀጥቀጠ። ሰብኡት፡ ኣንስትን ቆልዑን ብሓደ ሓያል ድምጺ ጨደሩ!
ShiEu kulu hizbi bi'Abi dimTsi chedere! DimTsi kindU Hayal sle zinebere meret anQeTQeTe. SebUt, anstin QolEun bi'Hade Hayal dimTsi chederu!
Scene 19
[PICTURE: The mighty walls of Jericho cracking and crumbling, large stones falling, dust clouds rising, a dramatic miraculous moment. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The great walls of Jericho began to crack and crumble! Stones fell everywhere as the mighty walls crashed to the ground. God had done an amazing miracle!
ዓበይቲ ቅጽርታት ኢያሪኮ ክስበሩን ክፈርሱን ጀመሩ! እምኒ ናብ ኩሉ ወደቐ ኣብ ዝሓያላት ቅጽርታት ናብ መሬት ዝወደቑ። ኣምላኽ ዘደንቕ ተኣምር ገበረ!
Abeyti QiTsrtat Iyariko ki'sberun ki'ferisun jemeru! Imni nab kulu wedeQe ab ziHayalat QiTsrtat nab meret ziwedQu. Amlak zidenQ te'amr gebere!
Scene 20
[PICTURE: The Israelite soldiers charging into the city through the rubble of fallen walls, dust settling, victorious expressions. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The Israelites charged straight into the city! Every man went forward through the fallen walls. God had given them the victory just as He promised.
እስራኤላውያን ቀጥታ ናብ ከተማ ኣተዉ! ነፍሲ ወከፍ ሰብ ብፍርስራስ ቅጽርታት ንቕድሚት ሰጎመ። ኣምላኽ ከም ዝተመባጸዐ ዓወት ሃቦም።
IsraElawiyan QeTta nab ketema atewu! Nefsi wekef seb bi'firsras QiTsrtat ni'Qidmit segome. Amlak kem zitemebaTs'Ee Awet habom.
Scene 21
[PICTURE: Rahab's house on the wall with a red cord hanging from the window, two Israelite spies approaching to rescue her and her family. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Joshua remembered Rahab, a woman who had helped the Israelite spies. She had hidden them and believed in God. "Bring Rahab and her family to safety!" Joshua commanded.
ኢያሱ ንራሃብ ንሰለይቲ እስራኤል ዝሓገዘት ሰበይቲ ዘከራ። ሓቢኣቶም ኣብ ኣምላኽ ድማ ኣሚና ነበረት። "ንራሃብን ስድራኣን ናብ ድሕነት ኣምጽእዋ!" ኢያሱ ኣዘዘ።
Iyasu n'Rahab ni'seleyti IsraEl ziHagezet sebyti zekera. Habi'atom ab Amlak dima amina neberet. "Ni'Rahabn sidra'an nab diHnet amTsiEwa!" Iyasu azeze.
Scene 22
[PICTURE: Rahab and her family being led safely out of the city by Israelite men, relief and gratitude on their faces. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Rahab and her whole family were saved because she had faith in God. She trusted the Lord and showed kindness to His people. God kept her safe.

Moral of the Story / ትምህርቲ ታሪኽ

Obedience to God leads to victory even when His plan seems unusual. The Israelites did not fight with weapons — they obeyed God's strange instructions and He gave them the victory. When we trust and obey God, He will always make a way for us.
ንኣምላኽ ምእዛዝ ውጥኑ ዘይልሙድ ኮይኑ ክንሱ ናብ ዓወት ይመርሕ። እስራኤላውያን ብኣጽዋር ኣይተዋግኡን — ዘይልሙድ ትእዛዝ ኣምላኽ ተኣዚዞም ንሱ ድማ ዓወት ሃቦም። ንኣምላኽ ኣሚንና ምስ እንእዘዝ ኩሉ ግዜ መንገዲ ይገብረልና።
