The Widow's Oil

ዘይቲ እታ ሰበይቲ መበለት

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A poor widow in simple worn clothing sitting in a small humble house, crying with her face in her hands. Two young boys stand beside her looking worried. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
A poor widow was crying in her small house. Her husband had died and left behind many debts. She had no money to pay what was owed. Her two young sons stood beside her, very worried.
ሓንቲ ድኽምቲ ሰበይቲ መበለት ኣብ ንእሽቶ ቤታ ትበኪ ነበረት። ሰብኣያ ሞይቱ ብዙሕ ዕዳ ገዲፉላ ነበረ። ነቲ ዕዳ ክትከፍል ገንዘብ ኣይነበራን። ክልተ ንኣሽቱ ኣወዳታ ኣብ ጥቓኣ ተሻቒሎም ደው ኢሎም ነበሩ።
(Hanti dikhimti sebeyti mebelet ab ni'ishto beta tibeki neberet. Seb'aya moytu buzuh ida gedifula nebere. Neti ida kitikefi genzeb ayneberan. Kilte ni'ashtu aweddata ab tiqaa teshaqilom dew ilom neberu.)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: An angry creditor man standing at the door of the widow's house, pointing his finger and demanding payment. The widow shields her two sons behind her protectively. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
A creditor came to her door. He was angry and demanded his money. "If you cannot pay," he said, "I will take your two sons as slaves!" The widow was terrified!
ሓደ ዋሃቢ ልቓሕ ናብ ማዕጾኣ መጸ። ሕሩቕ ኮይኑ ገንዘቡ ሓተታ። "ክትከፍሊ እንተዘይክኢልኪ" በላ "ንኽልተ ኣወዳትኪ ባሮት ገይረ ክወስዶም'የ!" እታ መበለት ኣዝያ ፈርሐት!
(Hade wahabi liqah nab ma'itso'a metse. Hiruq koynu genzebu hateta. "Kitikefi intezeykhilki" bela "nikilte awedatki barot geyre kiwesdom'ye!" Ita mebelet azya ferihet!)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: The widow running through a dusty village street with her two sons, heading toward the house of the prophet Elisha. She looks desperate but hopeful. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The widow remembered Elisha, the man of God! Her husband had been a faithful servant of the Lord. She ran with her sons to find the prophet Elisha and ask for help.
እታ መበለት ንኤልሳእ ሰብ ኣምላኽ ዘከረት! ሰብኣያ እሙን ኣገልጋሊ እግዚኣብሄር ነይሩ። ምስ ኣወዳታ ጎያ ንነብዪ ኤልሳእ ረድኤት ክትሓትት ከደት።
(Ita mebelet ni'Elsa'e seb Amlak zekeret! Seb'aya imun agelgali Egzi'abher neyru. Mis awedatta goya nineb'iy Elsa'e ridet'et kithatit kedet.)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: The widow kneeling before Elisha the prophet, crying and explaining her problem. Elisha sits listening with compassion. The two boys stand behind their mother. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The widow fell at Elisha's feet, crying. "My husband who served you has died!" she wept. "Now the creditor is coming to take my two boys as slaves! Please help us!"
እታ መበለት ኣብ ኣእጋር ኤልሳእ ወዲቓ በኸየት። "ሰብኣየይ ዘገልግለካ ሞይቱ!" ብኽያት በለት። "ሕጂ ዋሃቢ ልቓሕ ንኽልተ ኣወዳተይ ባሮት ገይሩ ክወስዶም ይመጽእ ኣሎ! በጃኻ ሓግዘና!"
(Ita mebelet ab a'igar Elsa'e wediqa bekheyet. "Seb'ayey zigelgileka moytu!" bikhyat belet. "Hiji wahabi liqah nikilte awedatey barot geyru kiwesdom yimets'e alo! Bejakha hagizena!")
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Elisha leaning forward with a kind expression, asking the widow a question. He has one hand raised gently. The widow looks up at him with tears on her face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elisha looked at her with kindness and asked, "How can I help you? Tell me — what do you have in your house?" It seemed like a strange question, but God had a plan!
ኤልሳእ ብለውሃት ረኣያ'ሞ "ከመይ ገይረ ክሕግዘኪ? ንገርኒ — ኣብ ቤትኪ እንታይ ኣሎኪ?" ሓተታ። ዘገርም ሕቶ ይመስል ነበረ፡ ግን ኣምላኽ መደብ ነይርዎ!
(Elsa'e bilewhat re'aya'mo "kemey geyre kihigizeki? Nigerini — ab betki intay aloki?" hateta. Zigerim hito yimesil nebere, gin Amlak medeb neyrwo!)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: The widow holding up a tiny jar of olive oil in her hands, looking at it sadly. The small jar looks almost empty. Elisha watches her with a thoughtful expression. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The widow looked down sadly. "I have nothing at all in my house," she said, "except one small jar of olive oil." It was just a tiny bit — barely enough to cook one meal!
እታ መበለት ብሓዘን ትሕቲ ረኣየት። "ኣብ ቤተይ ፈጺሙ ሓንቲ'ኳ የብለይን" በለት "ብዘይ ሓንቲ ንእሽቶ ዕትሮ ዘይቲ ኣውሊዕ።" ውሑድ ጥራይ ነበረ — ንሓንቲ መግቢ'ውን ዘይኣክል!
(Ita mebelet bihazen tihiti re'ayet. "Ab betey fetsimu hanti'kwa yebleyn" belet "bizey hanti ni'ishto itro zeyti awli'e." Wihud tiray nebere — nihanti megbi'wun zeyakil!)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Elisha standing up with authority, his hand raised as he gives instructions to the widow. His face shows confidence and faith. The widow and her sons listen intently. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elisha's eyes lit up! "Go to all your neighbors," he told her, "and borrow as many empty jars as you can find. Don't ask for just a few — get many, many jars!"
ዓይኒ ኤልሳእ በርሀ! "ናብ ኩሎም ጎረባብትኪ ኪዲ" በላ "ዝረኸብክዮ ዝበዝሐ ጥራሕ ዕትሮታት ተለቅሒ። ውሑድ ጥራይ ኣይትሕተቲ — ብዙሕ ብዙሕ ዕትሮታት ኣምጽኢ!"
(Ayni Elsa'e berihe! "Nab kulom gorebabetki kidi" bela "zirekhebkiyo zibezihe tirah itrotat teleqihi. Wihud tiray aytihiteti — buzuh buzuh itrotat amtsi'i!")
Scene 8
[PICTURE: The widow and her two sons going door to door in the village, asking neighbors for empty jars. A kind neighbor woman hands them several clay jars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The widow and her sons went from house to house, knocking on every door. "Please, may we borrow your empty jars?" they asked. The neighbors were kind and gave them jar after jar after jar!
እታ መበለትን ኣወዳታን ካብ ቤት ናብ ቤት ከዱ፡ ኣብ ኩሉ ማዕጾ ኩሕኩሕ ኢሎም። "በጃኹም ጥራሕ ዕትሮታትኩም ክንልቃሕ ንኽእል'ዶ?" ሓተቱ። ጎረባብቲ ለዋሃት ነበሩ'ሞ ዕትሮ ድሕሪ ዕትሮ ድሕሪ ዕትሮ ሃቡዎም!
(Ita mebelet'n awedatan kab bet nab bet kedu, ab kulu ma'itso kuhkuh ilom. "Bejakhum tirah itrotatkum kinliqah nikhil'do?" hatetu. Gorebabti lewhat neberu'mo itro dihri itro dihri itro habuwom!)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: The two boys carrying stacks of empty jars back to their small house. Jars of different sizes are piled up. The widow follows behind carrying more jars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The boys carried jar after jar back home. Big jars, small jars, tall jars, wide jars — they collected as many as they could! Soon their little house was filled with empty jars everywhere!
ኣወዳት ዕትሮ ድሕሪ ዕትሮ ናብ ቤት ጹይቖም። ዓበይቲ ዕትሮታት፡ ነኣሽቱ ዕትሮታት፡ ነዋሕቲ ዕትሮታት፡ ሰፋሕቲ ዕትሮታት — ዝኽእልዎ ዝበዝሐ ኣከቡ! ቀልጢፋ ንእሽቶ ቤቶም ብጥራሕ ዕትሮታት ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ መልአት!
(Awedat itro dihri itro nab bet tsuyqom. Abeytii itrotat, ni'ashtu itrotat, newahti itrotat, sefahti itrotat — zikhilwo zibezihe akebu! Qeltifa ni'ishto betom bitirah itrotat ab kulu bota mel'at!)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: The inside of the widow's small house completely filled with empty clay jars of various sizes lined up everywhere - on the floor, on tables, stacked against walls. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
There were so many empty jars! They filled every corner of the house — lined up against the walls, stacked on tables, crowded on the floor. The widow and her sons looked at all those empty jars and wondered what would happen next.
ኣዝዩ ብዙሕ ጥራሕ ዕትሮታት ነበረ! ኣብ ኩሉ ኩርናዕ ቤት መልአ — ኣብ መንደቕ ተሰሪዑ፡ ኣብ ጣውላ ተደራሪቡ፡ ኣብ ባይታ ተጸቒቑ። እታ መበለትን ኣወዳታን ነቲ ኩሉ ጥራሕ ዕትሮታት ረኣይዎም'ሞ እንታይ ከም ዝስዕብ ተገረሙ።
(Azyu buzuh tirah itrotat nebere! Ab kulu kurna'e bet mel'a — ab mendeq teseriru, ab tawla tederaribu, ab bayta tetseqiqu. Ita mebelet'n awedatan neti kulu tirah itrotat re'aywom'mo intay kem zis'ib tegeremu.)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Elisha speaking to the widow at her doorway, giving final instructions. He points inside the house. The widow nods with determination while her sons peek from behind her. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Elisha gave her one more instruction: "Now go inside with your sons, shut the door behind you, and start pouring oil from your little jar into all the empty jars." What a strange command!
ኤልሳእ ሓደ ተወሳኺ መምርሒ ሃባ፡ "ሕጂ ምስ ኣወዳትኪ ናብ ውሽጢ እተዊ፡ ማዕጾ ብድሕሬኺ ዕጸዊ'ሞ ካብ ንእሽቶ ዕትሮኺ ናብ ኩለን ጥራሕ ዕትሮታት ዘይቲ ኣፍስሲ ጀምሪ።" ኣዝዩ ዘገርም ትእዛዝ ነበረ!
(Elsa'e hade tewesaki memrihi haba: "Hiji mis awedatki nab wishti itewi, ma'itso bidihireki itsewi'mo kab ni'ishto itrooki nab kulen tirah itrotat zeyti afsisi jemri." Azyu zigerim ti'izaz nebere!)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: The widow closing the wooden door of her house firmly. Inside, her two sons stand ready among all the empty jars. Light comes through a small window. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The widow did exactly as Elisha said. She went inside with her two sons and shut the door tightly behind them. It was just the three of them, the empty jars, and one tiny jar of oil. She chose to obey God's prophet!
እታ መበለት ኤልሳእ ከም ዝበላ ብልክዕ ገበረት። ምስ ክልተ ኣወዳታ ናብ ውሽጢ ኣተወት'ሞ ማዕጾ ብጽኑዕ ብድሕሪኣ ዓጸወት። ሰለስቲኦም ጥራይ፡ ጥራሕ ዕትሮታት፡ ሓንቲ ንእሽቶ ዕትሮ ዘይቲ ነበሩ። ንነብዪ ኣምላኽ ክትእዘዝ መረጸት!
(Ita mebelet Elsa'e kem zibela bilki'e geberet. Mis kilte awedatta nab wishti atewet'mo ma'itso bitsinu'e bidihri'a atsewet. Selesti'om tiray, tirah itrotat, hanti ni'ishto itro zeyti neberu. Nineb'iy Amlak kit'izez meretset!)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: The widow carefully tilting her small jar of oil and beginning to pour into the first empty jar. Her sons watch with wide eyes. A golden glow emanates from the oil. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The widow picked up her tiny jar of oil and began to pour. The oil flowed out into the first empty jar — and it kept flowing! It didn't stop! Her eyes grew wide with amazement!
እታ መበለት ንእሽቶ ዕትሮ ዘይታ ኣልዒላ ከተፍስስ ጀመረት። ዘይቲ ናብታ ቀዳመይቲ ጥራሕ ዕትሮ ፈሰሰ — ምፍሳሱ ድማ ቀጸለ! ኣየቋረጸን! ብምግራም ዓይና ገፍሐ!
(Ita mebelet ni'ishto itro zeyta al'ila ketafsis jemeret. Zeyti nabt'a qedameyti tirah itro fesese — mifisasu dima qetsele! Ayequwaretsen! Bimigram ayna gefhe!)
Scene 14
[PICTURE: The widow pouring oil steadily from her small jar while one son holds an empty jar and the other moves a full jar aside. Oil flows in a golden stream that never stops. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"Bring me another jar!" the widow called to her sons. One boy held the jars steady while the other brought more empty ones. The oil kept pouring and pouring from that one little jar — it was a miracle!
"ካልእ ዕትሮ ኣምጽኡለይ!" እታ መበለት ንኣወዳታ ጸውዓቶም። ሓደ ቆልዓ ነቲ ዕትሮ ሒዙ ክንዲ ካልኣይ ተወሳኺ ጥራሕ ዕትሮ ይምጽእ ነበረ። ዘይቲ ካብ ሓንቲ ንእሽቶ ዕትሮ ይፈስስ ነበረ — ተኣምር ነበረ!
(Kal'e itro amtsi'uley! Ita mebelet ni'awedatta tsew'atom. Hade qol'a neti itro hizu kindi kal'ay tewesaki tirah itro yimts'e nebere. Zeyti kab hanti ni'ishto itro yifesis nebere — te'amir nebere!)
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Multiple jars now filled with golden olive oil, gleaming in the light. The widow continues pouring with joy on her face. Her sons smile excitedly as they watch jar after jar fill up. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One jar full! Two jars full! Three, four, five jars full! The oil just kept flowing and flowing. The widow laughed with joy as she watched jar after jar fill up to the top with beautiful golden oil!
ሓንቲ ዕትሮ መሊኣ! ክልተ ዕትሮ መሊኣ! ሰለስተ፡ ኣርባዕተ፡ ሓሙሽተ ዕትሮ መሊአን! ዘይቲ ይፈስስ'ሞ ይፈስስ ነበረ። እታ መበለት ዕትሮ ድሕሪ ዕትሮ ብጽቡቕ ወርቃዊ ዘይቲ ክሳብ ኣፍ ክመልእ ክትርኢ ከላ ብሓጎስ ሰሓቐት!
(Hanti itro meli'a! Kilte itro meli'a! Seleste, arba'ite, hamushte itro meli'an! Zeyti yifesis'mo yifesis nebere. Ita mebelet itro dihri itro bitsibuq werqawi zeyti kisab af kimel'e kitri'i kela bihagos sehaquet!)
Scene 16
[PICTURE: The two boys eagerly running back and forth, grabbing empty jars and lining up full ones. Their faces beam with excitement and wonder. Oil glistens in the filled jars. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The boys worked quickly! They grabbed empty jars and set them before their mother. As fast as they brought them, she filled them up! The room was filling with jar after jar of precious oil!
ኣወዳት ቀልጢፎም ይሰርሑ ነበሩ! ጥራሕ ዕትሮታት ሒዞም ቅድሚ ኣዲኦም የቐምጡ ነበሩ። ክንድ'ቲ ዘምጽኡ ቀልጢፋ ትመልኦም ነበረት! ክፍሊ ብዕትሮ ድሕሪ ዕትሮ ክቡር ዘይቲ ይመልእ ነበረ!
(Awedat qeltifom yiserihu neberu! Tirah itrotat hizom qidmi adi'om yeqemtu neberu. Kind'ti zimits'u qeltifa timle'om neberet! Kifli bi'itro dihri itro kibur zeyti yimel'e nebere!)
Scene 17
[PICTURE: The widow still pouring oil with a look of pure amazement and gratitude. Tears of joy stream down her face. Around her are dozens of jars already filled to the brim with golden oil. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Tears of joy ran down the widow's face as she kept pouring. God was providing for her family! Every jar she poured into filled right up to the very top. Not a single drop was wasted!

God cares about our problems, big and small. When we give God what little we have, He multiplies it! Obey God's instructions even when they seem unusual.
