The Tower of Babel

ግምቢ ባቤል

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 18

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 18

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

For all the children who celebrate the beautiful differences in our world.

ንኹሎም ኣብ ዓለมና ዘለዉ ጽቡቓት ፍልልያት ዘብዕሉ ቆልዑ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A beautiful green land with many people living together happily, all talking and understanding each other. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Long ago, after the great flood, all the people in the world spoke one language. Everyone could understand each other perfectly. They used the same words and the same speech.
TIGRINYA:
ቀደም ድሕሪ እቲ ዓቢ ማይ ኩሎም ሰባት ኣብ ዓለም ሓንቲ ቋንቋ ይዛረቡ ነበሩ። ኩሉ ሰብ ንሓድሕዱ ፍጹም ይረዳዳእ ነበረ። ሓደ ቃላትን ሓደ ዘረባን ይጥቀሙ ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ቋንቋ = qwanqwa = language, ይረዳዳእ = yiredada' = understand each other, ዘረባ = zereba = speech)

Page 2

[PICTURE: People traveling together as a group across a flat plain, arriving at a large open area in the land of Shinar. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The people traveled together and found a big flat plain in a land called Shinar. "Let us settle here!" they said. "This is a perfect place to build a great city!"
TIGRINYA:
ሰባት ብሓባር ተጓዒዞም ኣብ ሺንዓር ዝበሃል ምድሪ ዓባይ ጠፍሓይ ቦታ ረኸቡ። "ኣብዚ ንቐመጥ!" በሉ። "ዓባይ ከተማ ንምስራሕ ፍጹም ቦታ'ዩ!"
(Pronunciation: ሺንዓር = Shin'ar = Shinar, ጠፍሓይ = t'efHay = flat plain, ከተማ = ketema = city)

Page 3

[PICTURE: People making bricks from clay and baking them in fire, working together to prepare building materials. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The people learned to make bricks from clay and bake them hard in fire. "Come!" they said to each other. "Let us make bricks and build something amazing!"
TIGRINYA:
ሰባት ካብ ጭቃ ጡብ ክሰርሑን ብሓዊ ከጽንዕዎምን ተማህሩ። "ንዓዉ!" ንሓድሕዶም በሉ። "ጡብ ንስራሕ ዘደንቕ ነገር ድማ ንስራሕ!"
(Pronunciation: ጡብ = t'ub = bricks, ጭቃ = ch'iqa = clay, ዘደንቕ = zedenq = amazing)

Page 4

[PICTURE: People in a group discussion, some pointing up to the sky with proud expressions, planning their great tower. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The people had a proud idea. "Let us build a tower that reaches all the way up to heaven!" they said. "Then we will be famous! Everyone will know how great we are!"
TIGRINYA:
ሰባት ትዕቢታዊ ሓሳብ ሓሰቡ። "ክሳብ ሰማይ ዝበጽሕ ግምቢ ንስራሕ!" በሉ። "ድሕሪኡ ፍሉጣት ክንከውን ኢና! ኩሉ ሰብ ክንደይ ዓበይቲ ምዃንና ክፈልጥ'ዩ!"
(Pronunciation: ትዕቢት = ti'ibit = pride, ግምቢ = gimbi = tower, ሰማይ = semay = heaven, ፍሉጣት = filut'at = famous)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Construction beginning on a massive tower, people carrying bricks and mixing mortar, the base being laid. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
They started building! They stacked brick upon brick. They used tar to hold the bricks together. The tower grew bigger and taller every day. The people were very proud.
TIGRINYA:
ክሰርሑ ጀመሩ! ጡብ ኣብ ልዕሊ ጡብ ደርቡ። ነቶም ጡብ ንምጽናዕ ቅጥራን ተጠቐሙ። እቲ ግምቢ ኩሉ መዓልቲ ዓበየን ነውሐን። ሰባት ኣዝዮም ኩሩዓት ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ደርቡ = derbu = stacked, ቅጥራን = qit'ran = tar, ነውሐ = newHe = grew taller)

Page 6

[PICTURE: The tower rising high above the surrounding buildings, workers on scaffolding at different levels. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Higher and higher the tower grew! It was taller than any building they had ever made. But the people were not building it for God. They were building it for themselves.
TIGRINYA:
እቲ ግምቢ ልዕሊ ልዕሊ ዓበየ! ካብ ዝኾነ ዝሰርሑዎ ህንጻ ዝነውሐ ነበረ። ግን ሰባት ንኣምላኽ ኣይሰርሑዎን ነበሩ። ንገዛእ ርእሶም ይሰርሑዎ ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ልዕሊ = li'ili = higher, ህንጻ = hintsa = building, ገዛእ ርእሶም = geza'i ri'isom = themselves)

Page 7

[PICTURE: People boasting and showing off, flexing muscles and pointing at the tower with arrogant expressions. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
"We don't need God!" some people said. "Look how powerful we are! We can reach heaven by ourselves! We will make our own name great!" Their hearts were full of pride.
TIGRINYA:
"ኣምላኽ ኣየድልየናን!" ገለ ሰባት በሉ። "ክንደይ ሓያላት ምዃንና ርኣዩ! ባዕልና ናብ ሰማይ ክንበጽሕ ንኽእል! ስምና ባዕልና ከነዕብዮ ኢና!" ልቦም ብትዕቢት መሊኡ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ሓያላት = Hayalat = powerful, ባዕልና = ba'ilna = by ourselves, ትዕቢት = ti'ibit = pride)

Page 8

[PICTURE: A view from above showing God looking down at the tiny tower and tiny people, showing how small it really was. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
God looked down from heaven and saw what the people were doing. The tower they thought was so tall was tiny compared to God! God saw their proud and disobedient hearts.
TIGRINYA:
ኣምላኽ ካብ ሰማይ ጠሚቱ ሰባት እንታይ ይገብሩ ከም ዘለዉ ረኣየ። ኣዝዩ ነዊሕ ዝመሰሎም ግምቢ ብኣምላኽ ኣዝዩ ንእሽቶ ነበረ! ኣምላኽ ኩሩዕን ዘይእዙዝን ልቦም ረኣየ።
(Pronunciation: ጠሚቱ = t'emit'u = looked down, ንእሽቶ = ni'ishto = tiny, ዘይእዙዝ = zey'izuz = disobedient)

Page 9

[PICTURE: God in heavenly glory deciding what to do about the proud people and their tower. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
God said, "These people all speak one language and now they think they can do anything without Me. If they continue like this, their pride will only grow worse."
TIGRINYA:
ኣምላኽ "እዞም ሰባት ኩሎም ሓንቲ ቋንቋ ይዛረቡ ሕጂ ድማ ብዘይ ኣነ ኩሉ ክገብሩ ከም ዝኽእሉ ይሓስቡ። ከም'ዚ ምስ ዝቕጽሉ ትዕቢቶም ዝገደደ ጥራይ ክኸውን'ዩ" በለ።
(Pronunciation: ቋንቋ = qwanqwa = language, ብዘይ = bizey = without, ትዕቢት = ti'ibit = pride)

Page 10

[PICTURE: Swirling colorful winds coming down from heaven toward the people at the tower, representing God confusing their languages. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
So God confused their language! Suddenly, the people could not understand each other anymore. One person would say one thing, but the other heard something completely different!
TIGRINYA:
ስለዚ ኣምላኽ ቋንቋኦም ደበላለዎ! ብድንገት ሰባት ንሓድሕዶም ክረዳድኡ ኣይከኣሉን። ሓደ ሰብ ሓደ ነገር ይብል ግን እቲ ካልእ ፍጹም ዝተፈልየ ነገር ይሰምዕ ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: ደበላለወ = debelalewe = confused, ብድንገት = bidnget = suddenly, ክረዳድኡ = kiRedadu = understand each other)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Workers on the tower looking confused, one shouting orders and others shrugging with question marks, unable to understand. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
"Pass me a brick!" one worker shouted. But the others just stared at him confused. They could not understand his words! Everyone was speaking differently now.
TIGRINYA:
"ጡብ ኣሕልፈለይ!" ሓደ ሰራሕተኛ ጨዲሩ። ግን እቶም ካልኦት ተደናጊሮም ጠመቱዎ ጥራይ። ቃላቱ ክርድኡ ኣይከኣሉን! ኩሉ ሰብ ሕጂ ብዝተፈላለየ ይዛረብ ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ጡብ = t'ub = brick, ሰራሕተኛ = seraHtenya = worker, ተደናጊሮም = tedenagirom = confused)

Page 12

[PICTURE: Groups of people arguing and getting frustrated, pointing in different directions, complete chaos at the construction site. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Everything became chaos! People argued and got frustrated. Nobody could work together anymore. The building stopped completely. Bricks fell and tools were dropped everywhere.
TIGRINYA:
ኩሉ ነገር ዕግርግር ኮነ! ሰባት ተኻትዑን ተሸገሩን። ሓደ'ኳ ብሓባር ክሰርሕ ኣይከኣለን። እቲ ስራሕ ብምሉእ ደው በለ። ጡብ ወደቐ መሳርሒ ድማ ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ ተበታተነ።
(Pronunciation: ዕግርግር = 'igirgir = chaos, ተኻትዑ = tekati'u = argued, ደው በለ = dew bele = stopped)

Page 13

[PICTURE: Small groups of people finding others who speak the same new language, huddling together away from the tower. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Slowly, people found others who spoke the same language as them. They gathered in small groups. "I can understand you!" they said happily to those who spoke like them.
TIGRINYA:
ብዝሒ ሰባት ከምኦም ዝዛረቡ ካልኦት ረኸቡ። ብንኣሽቱ ጉጅለታት ተኣከቡ። "ክርድኣካ ይኽእል'የ!" ንዝመሰሉዎም ብሓጎስ በሉ።
(Pronunciation: ጉጅለታት = gujiletat = groups, ተኣከቡ = te'akebu = gathered, ክርድኣካ = kird'aka = understand you)

[PICTURE: Different groups of people packing their belongings and preparing to leave, heading in different directions. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The groups decided to leave. They could no longer live together because they could not understand each other. Each group packed their things and prepared to go their own way.

እቶም ጉጅለታት ክኸዱ ወሰኑ። ንሓድሕዶም ክረዳድኡ ስለ ዘይከኣሉ ብሓባር ክነብሩ ኣይከኣሉን። ነፍሲ ወከፍ ጉጅለ ኣቕሕኦም ዓሺጎም ባዕሎም ክኸዱ ተዳለዉ።

ENGLISH:
