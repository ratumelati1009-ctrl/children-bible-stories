Jesus Heals the Paralyzed Man

የሱስ ንዝሰለለ ሰብ ፈዊሱ

Written by Daniel Tesfamariam

Ages 3-10

Scene 1
[PICTURE: A paralyzed man lying on a mat looking sad in a small village house. His legs are still and he gazes out the window. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In a small town called Capernaum, there lived a man who could not walk. He lay on his mat every day, unable to move his legs.
ኣብ ቅፍርናሆም ዝተባህለት ንእሽቶ ከተማ፡ ክኸይድ ዘይክእል ሰብኣይ ነበረ። ኩሉ መዓልቲ ኣብ ዓራቱ ይድቅስ ነበረ፡ ኣእጋሩ ክንቀሳቐስ ኣይክእላን ነበራ።
Ab Qifirnahos zitebahlet ni'ishtoy ketema, kkeyd zeyke'il seb'ay nebere. Kulu me'alti ab 'aratu yidqis nebere, a'igaru kinqesaqes ayke'ilan nebera.
Scene 2
[PICTURE: Four kind friends sitting around the paralyzed man, talking and smiling at him encouragingly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But this man had four wonderful friends. They loved him very much and always came to visit him and keep him company.
ግን እዚ ሰብኣይ ኣርባዕተ ግሩማት ኣዕሩኽ ነበሩዎ። ብጣዕሚ ይፈትዉዎ ነበሩ፡ ኩሉ ግዜ ድማ ክበጽሑዎን ከዕልሉዎን ይመጹ ነበሩ።
Gin izi seb'ay arba'ite grumat a'irukh neberuwo. Bta'imi yifetiwuwo neberu, kulu gize dima kibetsḥuon ke'illuon yimetshu neberu.
Scene 3
[PICTURE: One friend rushing excitedly to tell the paralyzed man news, gesturing with hands wide. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One day, exciting news came to town. "Jesus is here!" said one of his friends. "He is teaching in a house nearby and healing people!"
ሓደ መዓልቲ ዜና ሓጎስ ናብ ከተማ መጸ። "የሱስ ኣብዚ ኣሎ!" በለ ሓደ ካብ ኣዕሩኹ። "ኣብ ጥቓና ኣብ ዘሎ ገዛ ይምህርን ሰባት ይፍውስን ኣሎ!"
Ḥade me'alti zena ḥagos nab ketema metse. "Yesus abzi alo!" bele ḥade kab a'irukhu. "Ab tiqana ab zelo geza yimihrn sebat yifewsn alo!"
Scene 4
[PICTURE: The four friends looking at each other with determination and hope, one pointing toward where Jesus is. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The four friends looked at each other with hope in their eyes. "We must take our friend to Jesus!" they said. "Jesus can heal him!"
እቶም ኣርባዕተ ኣዕሩኽ ብተስፋ ተጠማመቱ። "ንዓርክና ናብ የሱስ ክንወስዶ ኣሎና!" በሉ። "የሱስ ክፍውሶ ይኽእል እዩ!"
Itom arba'ite a'irukh bitesfa tetemametu. "Ni'arkna nab Yesus kinwesdo alona!" belu. "Yesus kifewso yikhe'il iyu!"
Scene 5
[PICTURE: Four friends carefully lifting the paralyzed man on his mat, each holding a corner. The man looks hopeful. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
So the four friends carefully picked up their friend on his mat. Each one held a corner. Together they carried him through the streets toward Jesus.
ስለዚ እቶም ኣርባዕተ ኣዕሩኽ ንዓርኮም ብጥንቃቐ ኣብ ዓራቱ ኣልዒሎም ኣልዓሉዎ። ነፍሲ ወከፍ ሓደ ኩርናዕ ሒዙ። ብሓንሳብ ብጐደና ናብ የሱስ ወሰዱዎ።
Silezi itom arba'ite a'irukh ni'arkom bitinqaqe ab 'aratu ali'ilom al'aluwo. Nefsi wekef ḥade kurna'i ḥizu. Biḥansab bigodena nab Yesus wesduwo.
Scene 6
[PICTURE: A house overflowing with people, crowd spilling out the doorway and windows, everyone trying to see Jesus inside. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When they arrived at the house, it was completely full of people! So many had come to see Jesus that there was no room left, not even near the door.
ናብ'ቲ ገዛ ምስ በጽሑ፡ ብሰባት መሊኡ ነበረ! ብዙሓት ንየሱስ ክርእዩ መጺኦም ነበሩ፡ ቦታ ኣይነበረን፡ ኣብ ጥቓ ማዕጾ'ውን ከይተረፈ።
Nab'ti geza mis betsḥu, bisebat meli'u nebere! Bizuḥat niYesus kir'iyu metsi'om neberu, bota ayneberenni, ab tiqa ma'itso'wun keytirefe.
Scene 7
[PICTURE: The four friends trying to push through the crowd at the door but unable to get in, looking frustrated but not giving up. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The friends tried to push through the crowd, but they could not get in. "What do we do now?" they asked. "We cannot get through the door!"
እቶም ኣዕሩኽ ብኻብ ሰባት ክሓልፉ ፈተኑ፡ ግን ክኣትዉ ኣይከኣሉን። "ሕጂ እንታይ ንግበር?" ተሓታተቱ። "ብማዕጾ ክንኣቱ ኣይንኽእልን!"
Itom a'irukh bikab sebat kiḥalfu fetenu, gin ki'atwu ayke'alun. "Ḥiji intay nigber?" teḥatatetu. "Bima'itso kni'atu aynikhiln!"
Scene 8
[PICTURE: One friend pointing up at the flat roof of the house with a big smile, having an idea. The others look at him with surprise. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then one friend had a clever idea! He pointed up at the roof. "We can go up to the roof!" he said. "We will make an opening and lower him down to Jesus!"
ሽዑ ሓደ ዓርኪ ጽቡቕ ሓሳብ ረኸበ! ናብ ናሕሲ ኣመልከተ። "ናብ ናሕሲ ክንድይብ ንኽእል!" በለ። "ክፍቲ ገይርና ናብ የሱስ ከነውርዶ ኢና!"
Shi'u ḥade 'arki tsibuq ḥasab rekebe! Nab naḥsi amelkete. "Nab naḥsi kindiyb nikhel!" bele. "Kifti geyrna nab Yesus kenewrdo ina!"
Scene 9
[PICTURE: The four friends climbing up stone steps on the outside of the house to reach the flat roof, carrying the mat with their friend. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The four friends carried their friend up the outside stairs to the roof. It was hard work, but they did not give up. Their love for their friend made them strong.
እቶም ኣርባዕተ ኣዕሩኽ ንዓርኮም ብደገ ብዘሎ መሳልል ናብ ናሕሲ ኣደየቡዎ። ከቢድ ስራሕ ነበረ፡ ግን ኣይሓደጉን። ፍቕሪ ንዓርኮም ሓይሊ ሃቦም።
Itom arba'ite a'irukh ni'arkom bidige bizelo mesalil nab naḥsi adeyebuwo. Kebid siraḥ nebere, gin ayḥadegunn. Fiqri ni'arkom ḥayli habom.
Scene 10
[PICTURE: The friends on the rooftop digging through the mud and straw roof, making a hole. Bits of roof material falling. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When they reached the roof, they began to dig! They broke through the mud, straw, and tiles. Pieces fell down into the house below as they made a big opening.
ናብ ናሕሲ ምስ በጽሑ፡ ክኹዕቱ ጀመሩ! ጭቃ፡ ሳዕርን ጡብን ሰበሩ። ዓብዩ ክፍቲ ክሳብ ዝገብሩ ቁራጺታት ናብ ውሽጢ ገዛ ወደቐ።
Nab naḥsi mis betsḥu, kiku'itu jemeru! Chiqa, sa'irn tubn seberu. 'Abiyu kifti kisab zigibru quratsitat nab wishti geza wedeqe.
Scene 11
[PICTURE: People inside the house looking up in surprise as dust and debris fall from the ceiling. Jesus looks up calmly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The people inside the house were very surprised! They looked up and saw the roof breaking open. Dust and pieces of roof fell down around them.
እቶም ኣብ ውሽጢ ገዛ ዝነበሩ ሰባት ብጣዕሚ ተገረሙ! ንላዕሊ ጠሚቶም ናሕሲ ክፍንጠቕ ረኣዩ። ሓመድን ቁራጺታት ናሕስን ኣብ ከባቢኦም ወደቐ።
Itom ab wishti geza zineberu sebat bta'imi tegeremu! Nila'ili temitom naḥsi kifenteq re'ayu. Ḥamedn quratsitat naḥsin ab kebabi'om wedeqe.
Scene 12
[PICTURE: The paralyzed man being lowered on his mat through the hole in the roof with ropes, the four friends holding the corners from above. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Slowly and carefully, the four friends lowered their friend down through the opening. They held the corners of the mat with ropes, bringing him down right in front of Jesus!
ብዕረፍትን ብጥንቃቐን እቶም ኣርባዕተ ኣዕሩኽ ንዓርኮም ብኽፍቲ ኣውረዱዎ። ብገመድ ኩርናዓት ዓራቱ ሒዞም፡ ኣብ ቅድሚ የሱስ ኣውረዱዎ!
Bi'ireftnn bitinqaqen itom arba'ite a'irukh ni'arkom bikifti awreduwo. Bigemed kurna'at 'aratu ḥizom, ab qidmi Yesus awreduwo!
Scene 13
[PICTURE: Jesus smiling warmly as he looks at the paralyzed man lying on the mat before him, then looking up at the friends peering through the roof hole. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus looked at the man on the mat, then looked up at the four friends peering through the roof. He saw how much faith they had. Their love and belief touched His heart.
የሱስ ነቲ ኣብ ዓራት ዝነበረ ሰብኣይ ጠመቶ፡ ድሕሪኡ ናብቶም ብናሕሲ ዝጥምቱ ዝነበሩ ኣርባዕተ ኣዕሩኽ ጠመተ። ክንደይ እምነት ከም ዘለዎም ረኣየ። ፍቕሮምን እምነቶምን ንልቡ ተንከዮ።
Yesus neti ab 'arat zinebere seb'ay temetO, diḥri'u nabtom binaḥsi zitmitu zineberu arba'ite a'irukh temete. Kindey imnet kem zelewo re'aye. Fiqromn imnetomn nilbu tenkeyo.
Scene 14
[PICTURE: Jesus bending down and speaking gently to the paralyzed man with compassion on his face, his hand reaching toward him. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus smiled at the man and said something surprising. "Son, your sins are forgiven," He said with love and kindness in His voice.
የሱስ ነቲ ሰብኣይ ፍሽኽ ኢሉ ዘገርም ነገር ተዛረበ። "ወደይ፡ ሓጢኣትካ ተሓዲጉልካ" በለ ብፍቕርን ለውሃትን።
Yesus neti seb'ay fishkh ilu zegerim neger tezarebe. "Wedey, ḥati'atka teḥadigulka" bele bifiqrn lewhatn.
Scene 15
[PICTURE: Religious leaders (scribes) sitting in the corner with angry, disapproving faces, whispering to each other. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Some religious leaders were sitting there. They were not happy! They thought to themselves, "Who can forgive sins but God alone? How dare He say that!"
ገለ መምህራን ሕጊ ኣብኡ ተቐሚጦም ነበሩ። ሕጉሳት ኣይነበሩን! ብልቦም ሓሰቡ፡ "ብጀካ ኣምላኽ መን ሓጢኣት ክሓድግ ይኽእል? ከመይ ኢሉ ከምዚ ይብል!"
Gele memhiran ḥigi ab'u teqemitom neberu. Ḥigussat ayneberun! Bilbom ḥasebu, "Bijeka Amlak men ḥati'at kiḥadig yikhel? Kemey ilu kemzi ybil!"
Scene 16
[PICTURE: Jesus turning to face the religious leaders with a knowing look, his hand raised gently as he speaks to them. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
But Jesus knew what they were thinking! He turned to them and said, "Why do you think these things in your hearts? Which is easier to say—your sins are forgiven, or get up and walk?"
ግን የሱስ እንታይ ከም ዝሓስቡ ፈለጠ! ናባታቶም ግልጽ ኢሉ፡ "ስለምንታይ ኣብ ልብኹም ከምዚ ትሓስቡ? ኣየናይ ይቐልል—ሓጢኣትካ ተሓዲጉልካ ምባል ወይ ተንሲእካ ኪድ ምባል?"
Gin Yesus intay kem ziḥasibu felete! Nabatatom gilts ilu, "Silemintay ab libkhum kemzi tiḥasibu? Ayenay yiqelil—ḥati'atka teḥadigulka mibal wey tensi'ika kid mibal?"
Scene 17
[PICTURE: Jesus standing tall with authority, one hand raised toward heaven and looking at the crowd with power and love. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Then Jesus said, "I will show you that the Son of Man has power on earth to forgive sins." He wanted everyone to know that He had authority from God.
ድሕሪኡ የሱስ "ወዲ ሰብ ኣብ ምድሪ ሓጢኣት ክሓድግ ስልጣን ከም ዘለዎ ከርእየኩም እየ" በለ። ካብ ኣምላኽ ስልጣን ከም ዘለዎ ኩሎም ክፈልጡ ደለየ።
Diḥri'u Yesus "Wedi seb ab midri ḥati'at kiḥadig siltan kem zelewo kir'iyekum iye" bele. Kab Amlak siltan kem zelewo kulom kifeltu deleye.
Scene 18
[PICTURE: Jesus looking directly at the paralyzed man and commanding him with outstretched hand, light radiating from Jesus. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Jesus looked straight at the paralyzed man and said with power: "I say to you, get up! Take your mat and go home!"
የሱስ ነቲ ዝሰለለ ሰብኣይ ቀጥታ ጠሚቱ ብሓይሊ ተዛረበ፡ "ተንስእ! ዓራትካ ኣልዒልካ ናብ ገዛኻ ኪድ!" በሎ።
Yesus neti ziselele seb'ay qetita temitu biḥayli tezarebe, "Tens'i! 'Aratka ali'ilka nab gezakha kid!" belo.
Scene 19
[PICTURE: The man's eyes growing wide with wonder, feeling tingling in his legs for the first time, his hands gripping the mat. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Immediately, the man felt something amazing happen! Strength flowed into his legs. His feet began to tingle. He could feel his toes moving for the very first time!
ብቕጽበት እቲ ሰብኣይ ዘደንቕ ነገር ተሰምዖ! ሓይሊ ናብ ኣእጋሩ ፈሰሰ። ኣእጋሩ ክንቀሳቐሳ ጀመራ። ንመጀመርያ ግዜ ኣጻብዕ ኣእጋሩ ክንቀሳቐሳ ተሰምዖ!
Biqits'bet iti seb'ay zedenq neger tesem'o! Ḥayli nab a'igaru fesese. A'igaru kinqesaqesa jemera. Nimejemirya gize atsabi'i a'igaru kinqesaqesa tesem'o!
Scene 20
[PICTURE: The man slowly sitting up on his mat, pushing himself up with his arms, a look of pure joy and disbelief on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The man slowly sat up. Then he pushed himself to his feet! His legs were strong. He stood up straight and tall. Everyone watched in complete amazement!
እቲ ሰብኣይ ብቐስታ ተንሲኡ ኮፍ በለ። ድሕሪኡ ደው በለ! ኣእጋሩ ሓያላት ነበራ። ቀጥ ኢሉ ደው በለ። ኩሎም ብምግራም ጠሚቶም ረኣዩ!
Iti seb'ay biqesta tensi'u kof bele. Diḥri'u dew bele! A'igaru ḥayalat nebera. Qet ilu dew bele. Kulom bimgiram temitom re'ayu!
Scene 21
[PICTURE: The healed man bending down to pick up his mat, rolling it under his arm with a huge smile, tears of joy on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The man bent down and picked up his mat! He rolled it up and tucked it under his arm. Tears of joy rolled down his cheeks. He was healed!
እቲ ሰብኣይ ጎንፊ ኢሉ ዓራቱ ኣልዓለ! ጠቕሊሉ ኣብ ትሕቲ ቅልጽሙ ሓዞ። ንብዓት ሓጎስ ካብ መላግቦታቱ ወረደ። ሓውዩ!
Iti seb'ay gonfi ilu 'aratu al'ale! Teqlilu ab tiḥti qilts'mu ḥazo. Nib'at ḥagos kab melagbotatu werede. Ḥawyu!
Scene 22
[PICTURE: The healed man walking through the amazed crowd toward the door, people parting and staring with open mouths, praising God. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The man walked right through the crowd! Everyone moved aside, staring in amazement. He walked out the door, praising God with every step he took.
እቲ ሰብኣይ ብማእከል ሰባት ሓለፈ! ኩሎም ብምግራም እናጠመቱ ጎኒ ጎኒ ኮኑ። ንኣምላኽ እናኣመስገነ ብማዕጾ ወጸ።
Iti seb'ay bimaekël sebat ḥalefe! Kulom bimgiram ina'temetu goni goni konu. Ni'Amlak ina'amesgene bima'itso wetse.
Scene 23
[PICTURE: The four friends running down from the roof and embracing the healed man outside, all jumping and celebrating together. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
His four friends rushed down from the roof and hugged him! They jumped and danced together. Their faith and love had brought their friend to Jesus, and Jesus had healed him completely!
ኣርባዕተ ኣዕሩኹ ካብ ናሕሲ ወሪዶም ሓቖፉዎ! ብሓንሳብ ዘለሉን ሰዓሰዑን። እምነቶምን ፍቕሮምን ንዓርኮም ናብ የሱስ ኣብጺሑዎ፡ የሱስ ድማ ብምልኡ ፈዊሱዎ!
Arba'ite a'irukhu kab naḥsi weridom ḥaqofuwo! Biḥansab zelelun se'ase'un. Imnetomn fiqromn ni'arkom nab Yesus abitsi'ḥuwo, Yesus dima bimilu'u fewisuwo!
Scene 24
[PICTURE: The crowd outside the house with hands raised praising God, faces full of wonder and joy, some kneeling. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
All the people were filled with wonder and praised God! They said, "We have never seen anything like this!" Everyone glorified God for what they had witnessed that day.
ኩሎም ሰባት ብምግራም መሊኦም ንኣምላኽ ኣመስገኑ! "ከምዚ ዝበለ ርኢና ኣይንፈልጥን!" በሉ። ኩሎም በታ መዓልቲ ንዝረኣዩዎ ንኣምላኽ ኣኽበሩ።
Kulom sebat bimgiram meli'om ni'Amlak amesgenu! "Kemzi zibele ri'ina aynifeltin!" belu. Kulom beta me'alti nizre'ayuwo ni'Amlak akberu.
Scene 25
[PICTURE: The healed man walking home on a path carrying his mat, his four friends walking beside him, all smiling under a beautiful sunset sky. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The healed man walked all the way home, carrying his mat. His four faithful friends walked beside him, smiling and laughing. What a wonderful day it was! Their faith had made the impossible possible.
እቲ ዝሓወየ ሰብኣይ ዓራቱ ሒዙ ክሳብ ገዛኡ ብእግሩ ኸደ። ኣርባዕተ እሙናት ኣዕሩኹ ጎኑ ጎኑ ተሰንዩዎ ብፍሽኽታን ስሓቕን ኸዱ። ክንደይ ዝበለጸ መዓልቲ! እምነቶም ንዘይከኣል ከኣል ገይሩዎ።
Iti ziḥaweye seb'ay 'aratu ḥizu kisab geza'u bi'igru khede. Arba'ite imunat a'irukhu gonu gonu tesenyuwo bifishktan siḥaqn khedu. Kindey zibeletse me'alti! Imnetom nizeyke'al ke'al geyruwo.

Moral of the Story

Good friends help each other get to Jesus. Faith and love can overcome any obstacle. When we believe and work together, nothing is impossible!
ጽቡቓት ኣዕሩኽ ንሓድሕዶም ናብ የሱስ ክበጽሑ ይተሓጋገዙ። እምነትን ፍቕርን ንዝኾነ ዕንቅፋት ክስዕር ይኽእል። ምስ እንኣምንን ብሓንሳብ ምስ እንሰርሕን ዘይከኣል ነገር የለን!
Tsibuqat a'irukh niḥadiḥdom nab Yesus kibetsḥu yiteḥagagezu. Imnetn fiqrn nizkhone 'inqifat kis'ir yikhel. Mis ina'aminn biḥansab mis iniserḥn zeyke'al neger yelen!
