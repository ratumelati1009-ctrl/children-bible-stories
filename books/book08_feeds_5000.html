Jesus Feeds 5,000 People

የሱስ 5,000 ሰባት መጊቡ

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 8

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 8

For all the children who share what they have, no matter how small.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ ዘለዎም ዝካፈሉ ንእሽቶ'ውን እንተ ኾነ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: Jesus teaching a large crowd of people on a green hillside, people sitting and listening attentively. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
One day, Jesus was teaching many people on a hillside. They came from many villages to hear Him speak. Everyone loved listening to Jesus.
TIGRINYA:

(Pronunciation: የሱስ = Yesus = Jesus, ኮረብታ = korebta = hillside, ይምህር = yimhir = teaching)
