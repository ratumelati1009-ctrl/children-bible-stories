Ruth and Naomi

ሩትን ናኦሚን

Little Believers Tigrinya Bible Stories - Book 12

ንኣሽቱ ኣመንቲ ትግርኛ ናይ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ዛንታታት - መጽሓፍ 12

For all the children who know that love and loyalty are the greatest gifts.

ንኹሎም ቆልዑ ፍቕርን ተኣማንነትን ዝዓበየ ህያብ ምዃኑ ዝፈልጡ።

Page 1

[PICTURE: A kind woman named Naomi with her husband and two sons standing in the land of Israel, looking at dry fields with no food. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
A long time ago, a woman named Naomi lived in Israel with her husband and two sons. But there was no food in the land. The family was very hungry.
TIGRINYA:
ኣብ ቅድሚ ግዜ ናኦሚ ዝስማ ሰበይቲ ምስ ሰብኣያን ክልተ ደቃን ኣብ እስራኤል ትነብር ነበረት። ግን ኣብታ ሃገር ምግቢ ኣይነበረን። እታ ስድራ ኣዝያ ጠምያ ነበረት።
(Pronunciation: ናኦሚ = Na'omi = Naomi, ስድራ = sidra = family, ጠምያ = t'emya = hungry)

Page 2

[PICTURE: Naomi's family walking on a long dusty road, carrying bundles, heading toward the land of Moab. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
So the family moved far away to a land called Moab. There was food in Moab. Naomi hoped her family would be safe and happy there.
TIGRINYA:
ስለዚ እታ ስድራ ናብ ሞኣብ ዝብሃል ርሑቕ ሃገር ገዓዘት። ኣብ ሞኣብ ምግቢ ነይሩ። ናኦሚ ስድራኣ ኣብኡ ድሕንትን ሓጎስን ክረኽቡ ተስፋ ገበረት።
(Pronunciation: ሞኣብ = Mo'ab = Moab, ገዓዘት = ge'azet = moved, ተስፋ = tesfa = hope)

Page 3

[PICTURE: Naomi looking very sad, sitting alone, tears on her face, after losing her husband. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
But very sad things happened in Moab. Naomi's husband died. She was left alone with her two sons. Naomi's heart was broken.
TIGRINYA:
ግን ኣዝዩ ዘሕዝን ነገራት ኣብ ሞኣብ ኮነ። ሰብኣይ ናኦሚ ሞተ። ምስ ክልተ ኣወዳታ ጥራይ ተረፈት። ልቢ ናኦሚ ተሰብረ።
(Pronunciation: ሞተ = mote = died, ተረፈት = terefet = was left, ልቢ = libi = heart)

Page 4

[PICTURE: Two young women Ruth and Orpah joining Naomi's family, a small wedding celebration in Moab. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Naomi's two sons grew up and married two women from Moab. One was named Ruth and the other was named Orpah. They were a happy family for some time.
TIGRINYA:
ክልተ ኣወዳት ናኦሚ ዓብዮም ክልተ ደቂ ኣንስትዮ ካብ ሞኣብ ተመርዓዉ። ሓንቲ ሩት ሓንቲ ድማ ዖርጳ ይብሃላ ነበራ። ንቑሩብ ግዜ ሕጉስቲ ስድራ ነበሩ።
(Pronunciation: ሩት = Rut = Ruth, ዖርጳ = Orpa = Orpah, ተመርዓዉ = temerA'wu = married)

Page 5

[PICTURE: Naomi weeping, both her sons have died, Ruth and Orpah comforting her with arms around her. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Then both of Naomi's sons died too. Now Naomi had lost her husband and both sons. She was so sad. Ruth and Orpah were very kind to her.
TIGRINYA:
ድሕሪኡ ክልቲኦም ኣወዳት ናኦሚ'ውን ሞቱ። ሕጂ ናኦሚ ሰብኣያን ክልቲኦም ኣወዳታን ሲኢና። ኣዝያ ሓዚና ነበረት። ሩትን ዖርጳን ኣዝየን ለዋሃት ነበራ።
(Pronunciation: ሞቱ = motu = died, ሓዚና = Hazina = sad, ለዋሃት = lewahat = kind)

Page 6

[PICTURE: Naomi standing on a road with a small bundle, telling Ruth and Orpah to go back to their families in Moab. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Naomi decided to go back home to Israel. She told Ruth and Orpah, "Go back to your mothers. You are young. Find new husbands in Moab."
TIGRINYA:
ናኦሚ ናብ ሃገራ እስራኤል ክትምለስ ወሰነት። ንሩትን ዖርጳን "ናብ ኣዴታትክን ተመለሳ። ንእስ ዝበልክን ኢኽን። ኣብ ሞኣብ ሓደስቲ ሰብኡት ርኸባ" በለተን።
(Pronunciation: ክትምለስ = kitimles = to return, ኣዴታት = adetat = mothers, ሰብኡት = seb'ut = husbands)

Page 7

[PICTURE: Orpah crying and hugging Naomi goodbye, then walking away back toward Moab. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Orpah cried and kissed Naomi goodbye. She loved Naomi but decided to go back to Moab. She walked away sadly, looking back one last time.
TIGRINYA:
ዖርጳ ነቢዓ ንናኦሚ ሰዓማ ተሰናበተታ። ንናኦሚ ትፈትዋ ነበረት ግን ናብ ሞኣብ ክትምለስ ወሰነት። ንመወዳእታ ግዜ ንድሕሪት እናረኣየት ብሓዘን ከደት።
(Pronunciation: ነቢዓ = nebi'a = crying, ሰዓማ = se'ama = kissed, ተሰናበተታ = tesenabetera = said goodbye)

Page 8

[PICTURE: Ruth holding Naomi's hands tightly, looking into her eyes with love and determination, refusing to leave. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
But Ruth would not leave Naomi! She held her hands and said the most beautiful words: "Where you go, I will go. Where you stay, I will stay."
TIGRINYA:
ግን ሩት ንናኦሚ ክትገድፋ ኣይደለየትን! ኣእዳዋ ሒዛ ኣዝዩ ጽቡቕ ቃላት ተዛረበት: "ናብ ዝኸድክዮ ክኸይድ'የ። ናብ ዝጸናሕክዮ ክጸንሕ'የ።"
(Pronunciation: ክትገድፋ = kitgedfa = to leave her, ቃላት = qalat = words, ክኸይድ = kikeyd = I will go)

Page 9

[PICTURE: Ruth and Naomi embracing warmly, both with tears of love, with the road to Israel stretching ahead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Ruth said, "Your people will be my people. Your God will be my God." Naomi was so touched. They hugged each other tightly with tears of joy.
TIGRINYA:
ሩት "ህዝብኺ ህዝበይ ክኸውን'ዩ። ኣምላኽኪ ኣምላኸይ ክኸውን'ዩ" በለት። ናኦሚ ኣዝያ ተተንከፈት። ብንብዓት ሓጎስ ተሓቛቚፈን።
(Pronunciation: ህዝብኺ = hizbiKi = your people, ኣምላኽኪ = AmlakKi = your God, ተሓቛቚፈን = teHaqwaqwufen = hugged)

Page 10

[PICTURE: Ruth and Naomi walking together on a long road, supporting each other, heading toward Bethlehem. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
So Ruth and Naomi traveled together to Bethlehem in Israel. It was a long journey but they helped each other. Ruth never left Naomi's side.
TIGRINYA:
ስለዚ ሩትን ናኦሚን ብሓንሳብ ናብ ቤትልሄም ኣብ እስራኤል ተጓዓዛ። ነዊሕ ጉዕዞ ነይሩ ግን ንሓድሕደን ተሓጋገዛ። ሩት ንናኦሚ ፈጺማ ኣይገደፈታን።
(Pronunciation: ቤትልሄም = Betlehem = Bethlehem, ጉዕዞ = gu'izo = journey, ተሓጋገዛ = teHagageza = helped each other)

Page 11

[PICTURE: Ruth and Naomi arriving at the small town of Bethlehem, people looking at them, simple houses in background. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
When they arrived in Bethlehem, the people were excited. "Is that Naomi?" they asked. Naomi was home again, but she was very poor now.
TIGRINYA:
ናብ ቤትልሄም ምስ በጽሓ ሰባት ተሓጎሱ። "ናኦሚ ድያ?" ሓተቱ። ናኦሚ ናብ ገዛኣ ተመሊሳ ግን ሕጂ ኣዝያ ድኻ ነበረት።
(Pronunciation: በጽሓ = bets'Ha = arrived, ተሓጎሱ = teHagosu = excited, ድኻ = diKa = poor)

Page 12

[PICTURE: Ruth waking up early in the morning, determined, telling Naomi she will go find food for them both. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Ruth said to Naomi, "I will find food for us." In those days, poor people could pick up leftover grain from farmers' fields. This was called gleaning.
TIGRINYA:
ሩት ንናኦሚ "ምግቢ ክረኽበልና'የ" በለታ። ኣብቲ ግዜ'ቲ ድኻታት ሰባት ካብ ግራት ሓረስቶት ዝተረፈ እኽሊ ክኣርዩ ይኽእሉ ነበሩ። እዚ ምእራይ ይብሃል ነበረ።
(Pronunciation: ምግቢ = migbi = food, ግራት = grat = fields, ምእራይ = mi'iray = gleaning)

Page 13

[PICTURE: Ruth bending down in a golden wheat field, carefully picking up stalks of grain, the sun shining warmly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Ruth went to the fields early in the morning. She worked very hard all day, picking up grain. She did not stop even when she was tired.

(Pronunciation: ንግሆ = nigho = morning, ብትግሃት = bitighat = hard work, እኽሊ = ikhli = grain)

[PICTURE: A kind wealthy man named Boaz arriving at his field, noticing Ruth working hard among the other gleaners. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
The field belonged to a kind man named Boaz. He saw Ruth working so hard. "Who is that young woman?" he asked his workers. They told him about Ruth.

(Pronunciation: ቦኣዝ = Bo'az = Boaz, ለዋህ = lewah = kind, መንእሰይ = men'esey = young)

Boaz was so kind to Ruth. He said, "Stay in my field. Drink water when you are thirsty. No one will bother you here." He gave her food to eat.

[PICTURE: Ruth looking surprised and grateful, bowing down to thank Boaz for his kindness to a stranger. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Ruth was surprised! "Why are you so kind to me?" she asked. "I am just a stranger from Moab." But Boaz knew about her love for Naomi.

[PICTURE: Boaz telling Ruth he has heard how good she has been to Naomi, blessing her with warm words. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
ENGLISH:
Boaz said, "I know how you left your family to care for Naomi. May God bless you for your kindness." Ruth's heart felt warm and thankful.

(Pronunciation: ምሕብሓብ = miHibHab = to care for, ይባርኽኪ = yibariKKi = bless you, ኣመስጋን = amesgan = thankful)

That evening, Ruth brought home so much grain! Naomi was amazed. "Where did you work today?" she asked. Ruth told her about the kind man Boaz.

Naomi smiled for the first time in a long time. "Boaz is our relative!" she said happily. "God has not forgotten us. God is taking care of us!"
