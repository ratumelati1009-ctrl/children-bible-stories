Scene 1
[PICTURE: Philip preaching to a crowd of people in a city, sharing the good news about Jesus. People listening eagerly. Ancient Middle Eastern city setting. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Philip was one of Jesus' followers who loved to tell others about God. He traveled to many places, sharing the good news that Jesus saves!
ፊሊጶስ ንኻልኦት ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ክነግር ዘፍቅር ሓደ ካብ ተኸተልቲ የሱስ ነበረ። ናብ ብዙሕ ቦታታት ተጓዒዙ የሱስ ከም ዘድሕን ጽቡቕ ዜና ኣካፊሉ!
(Pronunciation: Filipos nkalot bza'ba Amlak knegr zefqir hade kab tekhtelti Yesus nebere. Nab bzuh botatat tegwa'izu Yesus kem zedhn tsbuq zena akafilu!)
Scene 2
[PICTURE: An angel of God appearing to Philip in bright light, pointing toward a desert road. Philip looking attentive and obedient. Morning light. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
One day, an angel of God told Philip, "Get up and go south to the desert road that goes from Jerusalem to Gaza." Philip obeyed right away!
ሓደ መዓልቲ መልኣኽ ኣምላኽ ንፊሊጶስ "ተንስእ ናብ ደቡብ ናብቲ ካብ ኢየሩሳሌም ናብ ጋዛ ዝኸይድ ናይ በረኻ መንገዲ ኪድ" በሎ። ፊሊጶስ ብቕጽበት ተኣዘዘ!
(Pronunciation: Hade me'alti mel'ak Amlak n'Filipos "Tens'e nab debub nabti kab Iyerusalem nab Gaza zkhyd nay bereka mengedi kid" belo. Filipos bqts'bet te'azeze!)
Scene 3
[PICTURE: Philip walking on a dusty desert road alone. In the distance, an ornate chariot with horses approaching. Flat desert landscape with sparse bushes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Philip walked along the desert road. He didn't know why God sent him there. Then he saw a fancy chariot coming along the road!
ፊሊጶስ ኣብ ናይ በረኻ መንገዲ ተመላለሰ። ኣምላኽ ስለ ምንታይ ናብኡ ከም ዝሰደዶ ኣይፈልጥን ነበረ። ድሕሪኡ ግሩም ሰረገላ ኣብ መንገዲ ትመጽእ ረኣየ!
(Pronunciation: Filipos ab nay bereka mengedi temelалese. Amlak sle mntay nab'u kem zsededo ayfelTn nebere. Dhri'u grum seregela ab mengedi tmets'e re'aye!)
Scene 4
[PICTURE: A wealthy Ethiopian official sitting in an ornate chariot, reading a scroll. He is dressed in fine robes and looks important. His driver steering the horses. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
In the chariot sat an important Ethiopian man! He was an official of the Queen of Ethiopia. He was reading a scroll — the book of Isaiah from the Bible!
ኣብ ሰረገላ ኣገዳሲ ኢትዮጵያዊ ሰብ ኮፍ ኢሉ ነበረ! ናይ ንግስቲ ኢትዮጵያ ሰበስልጣን ነበረ። ጥቕላል — ናይ መጽሓፍ ኢሳይያስ ካብ መጽሓፍ ቅዱስ ይነብብ ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Ab seregela agedasi Ityopyawi seb kof ilu nebere! Nay ngsti Ityopya sebesselTan nebere. Tqlal — nay metshaf Isayas kab metshaf qdus ynebb nebere!)
Scene 5
[PICTURE: The Holy Spirit represented as a dove/light near Philip, prompting him to go to the chariot. Philip starting to run toward the chariot with purpose. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The Holy Spirit told Philip, "Go near that chariot!" Philip ran to it. He could hear the man reading Isaiah out loud. This was why God sent him here!
መንፈስ ቅዱስ ንፊሊጶስ "ናብታ ሰረገላ ቅረብ!" በሎ። ፊሊጶስ ጎየየ ናብኣ። እቲ ሰብ ኢሳይያስ ብዓውታ ክነብብ ሰምዐ። ኣምላኽ ናብዚ ዝሰደዶ ስለ'ዚ ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Menfes Qdus n'Filipos "Nabta seregela qreb!" belo. Filipos goyeye nab'a. Iti seb Isayas b'awta knebb sem'e. Amlak nabzi zsededo sle'zi nebere!)
Scene 6
[PICTURE: Philip running alongside the chariot, calling up to the Ethiopian man. The man looks down with a curious and welcoming expression. Desert road stretching ahead. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Philip called out, "Do you understand what you're reading?" The Ethiopian man replied, "How can I? I need someone to explain it to me! Please come sit with me!"
ፊሊጶስ "ትንብቦ ዘለኻ ትርድኦ ዲኻ?" ኢሉ ጸውዓ። ኢትዮጵያዊ "ብኸመይ ክእለት? ዝገልጸለይ ሰብ የድልየኒ! በጃኻ ምሳይ ኮፍ በል!" ኢሉ መለሰ።
(Pronunciation: Filipos "Tnbbo zelekha trda'o dikha?" ilu tsew'a. Ityopyawi "Bkemey kh'elet? Zgeltseley seb yedlyeni! Bejaka msay kof bel!" ilu melese.)
Scene 7
[PICTURE: Philip sitting beside the Ethiopian in the chariot, both looking at the scroll together. Philip pointing to a passage and explaining eagerly. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Philip climbed into the chariot. The man was reading about someone who suffered like a sheep. "Who is this about?" he asked. Philip smiled — he knew the answer!
ፊሊጶስ ናብ ሰረገላ ደየበ። እቲ ሰብ ከም በጊዕ ብዛዕባ ዝተሳቐየ ሰብ ይነብብ ነበረ። "እዚ ብዛዕባ መን'ዩ?" ሓተተ። ፊሊጶስ ፈሰመ — መልሱ ይፈልጥ ነበረ!
(Pronunciation: Filipos nab seregela deyebe. Iti seb kem begi' bza'ba ztesaqeye seb ynebb nebere. "Izi bza'ba men'yu?" hatete. Filipos feseme — melsu yfelT nebere!)
Scene 8
[PICTURE: Philip enthusiastically explaining the scripture, his hands gesturing passionately. The Ethiopian listening with wide eyes and growing understanding. Light of comprehension on his face. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
"This is about JESUS!" Philip explained. He told the Ethiopian all about Jesus — how He came to save everyone, how He died and rose again, and how He loves all people!
"እዚ ብዛዕባ የሱስ'ዩ!" ፊሊጶስ ገለጸ። ንኢትዮጵያዊ ኩሉ ብዛዕባ የሱስ ነገሮ — ንኩሉ ሰብ ከድሕን ከም ዝመጸ ከም ዝሞተን ዝተንስኤን ንኩሉ ሰብ ከም ዘፍቅርን!
(Pronunciation: "Izi bza'ba Yesus'yu!" Filipos geletse. N'Ityopyawi kulu bza'ba Yesus negero — nkulu seb kedhn kem zmetse kem zmoten ztense'en nkulu seb kem zefqirn!)
Scene 9
[PICTURE: The Ethiopian man's face lighting up with joy and understanding. He is deeply moved, pressing his hand to his heart. Tears of happiness in his eyes. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
The Ethiopian man's heart was filled with joy! He believed in Jesus! Everything Philip said made perfect sense. God's love was for him too!
ልቢ ኢትዮጵያዊ ብሓጎስ መልአ! ብየሱስ ኣመነ! ፊሊጶስ ዝበሎ ኩሉ ፍጹም ርድኢት ሃበ። ፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ንዕኡ'ውን'ዩ!
(Pronunciation: Lbi Ityopyawi bhagos mel'a! B'Yesus amene! Filipos zbelo kulu ftsum rd'it habe. Fqri Amlak n'u'wn'yu!)
Scene 10
[PICTURE: The chariot approaching a small body of water/pool along the desert road. The Ethiopian pointing excitedly at the water. Lush green around the water. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
As they rode along, they came to some water. The Ethiopian said excitedly, "Look! There is water! What is stopping me from being baptized right now?"
ክጎዓዙ ከለዉ ናብ ገለ ማይ በጽሑ። ኢትዮጵያዊ ብሓጎስ "ረአ! ማይ ኣሎ! ሕጂ ክጥመቕ እንታይ ዝኽልክለኒ ኣሎ?" በለ።
(Pronunciation: Kgo'azu kelewu nab gele may betshu. Ityopyawi bhagos "Re'a! May alo! Hji kTmeq intay zkhlekeleni alo?" bele.)
Scene 11
[PICTURE: Philip and the Ethiopian walking together into the water for baptism. Both smiling with joy. The chariot and driver waiting on the road. Beautiful desert oasis. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Philip said, "If you believe with all your heart, you can!" The Ethiopian replied, "I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God!" They stopped the chariot and walked into the water together.
ፊሊጶስ "ብምሉእ ልብኻ ምስ እትኣምን ትኽእል!" በለ። ኢትዮጵያዊ "የሱስ ክርስቶስ ወዲ ኣምላኽ ምዃኑ ይኣምን!" ኢሉ መለሰ። ሰረገላ ኣጠጊዖም ብሓባር ናብ ማይ ኣተዉ።
(Pronunciation: Filipos "Bmlu'e lbkha ms it'amn tkhel!" bele. Ityopyawi "Yesus Kristos Wedi Amlak mkwanu y'amn!" ilu melese. Seregela ategi'om bhabar nab may atewu.)
Scene 12
[PICTURE: Philip baptizing the Ethiopian in the water, gently lowering him. Water splashing. Heavenly light shining down. A moment of great spiritual joy. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Philip baptized the Ethiopian man in the water! It was a beautiful moment. The man became a follower of Jesus right there in the desert!
ፊሊጶስ ነቲ ኢትዮጵያዊ ኣብ ማይ ኣጥመቖ! ጽቡቕ ህሞት ነበረ። እቲ ሰብ ኣብኡ ኣብ በረኻ ተኸታሊ የሱስ ኮነ!
(Pronunciation: Filipos neti Ityopyawi ab may aTmeqo! Tsbuq hmot nebere. Iti seb ab'u ab bereka tekhetali Yesus kone!)
Scene 13
[PICTURE: The Ethiopian coming up out of the water with his arms raised in praise, water dripping, pure joy on his face. Philip smiling beside him. Sunlight creating rainbow in water drops. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
When they came up from the water, the Ethiopian was so happy! His whole life had changed! He had found the truth about God through Jesus!
ካብ ማይ ምስ ወጹ ኢትዮጵያዊ ኣዝዩ ተሓጎሰ! ምሉእ ህይወቱ ተቐይሩ! ብየሱስ ብዛዕባ ኣምላኽ ሓቂ ረኺቡ!
(Pronunciation: Kab may ms wets'u Ityopyawi azyu tehagose! Mlu'e hywetu teqeyiru! B'Yesus bza'ba Amlak haqqi rekhibu!)

Then something amazing happened — God took Philip away! He suddenly appeared in another town to tell more people about Jesus. The Ethiopian didn't see Philip again.
ድሕሪኡ ዘደንቕ ነገር ኮነ — ኣምላኽ ንፊሊጶስ ወሰዶ! ብድንገት ኣብ ካልእ ከተማ ንዝያዳ ሰባት ብዛዕባ የሱስ ክነግር ተራእየ። ኢትዮጵያዊ ንፊሊጶስ ከም ብሓድሽ ኣይረኣዮን።
(Pronunciation: Dhri'u zedenq neger kone — Amlak n'Filipos wesedo! Bdnget ab kal'e ketema nzyada sebat bza'ba Yesus knegr tera'eye. Ityopyawi n'Filipos kem bhadsh ayre'ayon.)

[PICTURE: The Ethiopian continuing his journey in his chariot, but now with a huge smile and radiant joy. He's singing praises. The road ahead is bright and hopeful. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]

(Pronunciation: Gna Ityopyawi dhri hji Filipos ayedlyon nebere. Yesus ab lbu nebero! Nab gezu'u gu'zu'u qetsele b'abi hagos inazameren n'Amlak ina'amesgenen!)

(Pronunciation: Ityopyawi nab gezu'u Afrika ms temelese nkulu seb bza'ba Yesus negere! Tsbuq zena Yesus nab hageru zemts'e'a qedamay seb kone!)

ሎሚ ኣብ ኢትዮጵያን ኤርትራን ኣማኢት ሰባት ንየሱስ ይፈትዉ! ኩሉ ዝጀመረ ሓደ ሰብ ኣብ ናይ በረኻ መንገዲ ንፊሊጶስ ምስ ረኸቦ ነበረ። ሓደ ሰብ ለውጢ ክገብር ይኽእል!

Philip obeyed God even when he didn't understand why. He trusted God's plan. When we obey God, amazing things happen — even if we can't see the reason yet!
ፊሊጶስ ስለ ምንታይ ከም ዘይተረድኦ'ውን ንኣምላኽ ተኣዘዘ። ውጥን ኣምላኽ ኣመነ። ንኣምላኽ ምስ ንእዘዝ ዘደንቕ ነገራት ይኸውን — ምኽንያት ዘይንርእየሉ'ውን!

Scene 23

God's love is for ALL people — every country, every color, every language! Just like God sent Philip to the Ethiopian, He wants everyone to know His love!
ፍቕሪ ኣምላኽ ንኩሉ ሰብ'ዩ — ንኩላ ሃገር ንኩሉ ሕብሪ ንኩላ ቋንቋ! ኣምላኽ ንፊሊጶስ ናብ ኢትዮጵያዊ ከም ዝሰደዶ ኩሉ ሰብ ፍቕሩ ክፈልጥ ይደሊ!
(Pronunciation: Fqri Amlak nkulu seb'yu — nkula hager nkulu hbri nkula qwanqwa! Amlak n'Filipos nab Ityopyawi kem zsededo kulu seb fqru kfelT ydeli!)
Scene 24
[PICTURE: A family reading the Bible together at home, just as the Ethiopian was reading scripture. Parents and children gathered around God's Word. Cozy home setting. East African/Eritrean features. Warm watercolor children's Bible illustration.]
Like the Ethiopian who was reading God's Word, we should read the Bible too! And when we don't understand, we can ask for help. God wants us to learn about Him!

(Pronunciation: Zekhru: Amlak nhywetkum'wn fluye wuTn alewo! Kem Filipos dluwat kunu — Amlak ms ztsw' iwe belu! Nay men hywet nzele'alem kem tqeyiru aytfelTun!)

🌟 Moral of the Story 🌟

English: God has a plan for each of us. When we listen to Him and obey, He can use us to change the world — one person at a time. The good news of Jesus is for everyone, everywhere!

ትግርኛ: ኣምላኽ ንነፍሲ ወከፍና ውጥን ኣለዎ። ንዕኡ ምስ እንሰምዕን ምስ እንእዘዝን ዓለም ክንቅይር ክጥቀመልና ይኽእል — ሓደ ሰብ ኣብ ሓደ ግዜ። ጽቡቕ ዜና የሱስ ንኩሉ ሰብ ኣብ ኩሉ ቦታ'ዩ!
